*Confidence: established · Source: [[world|World notes]]*

**Triple Dog Dare origin — ESTABLISHED:** dreamed up by Doug Layne (Adopted Acres) with Marcus Klinger of The Barbershop Duet "while spitballing ideas for Blizzard Battles" (retailer copy, The Shave Supply Halloween 86 listing, quoting Doug Layne). Marcus is "the unofficial creative director of Adopted Acres Soap Co." per the same copy. **Adopted Acres Soap Co. background — ESTABLISHED:** founded 2024 by Doug Layne (and wife Cara); beef tallow rendered from suet from Cara's family Angus cattle farm in Ohio; six adopted children, several with disabilities; dream of a brick-and-mortar near Ashville, Ohio (MULLY'S PLACE artisan spotlight + ShaveSplash interview, 2025). House bases: EIEIO (farm-built) and Grand Champion 2.0.
