**TSM** = The Shave Mercantile (retailer; "Catie's Bubbles Le Marché du
Rasage TSM edition" = retailer exclusive). Established. [catalog]
- 2026-11-04 CORRECTION + AMBIGUITY: this entry conflated two entities.
**(a) The Shave Market (Facebook group)** — the expansion behind
"TSM Fougère" and every 2016-era "TSM" group reference, per Shawn
Maher's own 2016-08-29 Sharpologist interview; **(b) The Shave
Mercantile** (theshavemercantile.com, Nashville TN, run by Ethan;
ACTIVE 2026) is a separate retailer that posters may also abbreviate
TSM. In product strings "TSM Fougère" = (a) always; bare "TSM" is
AMBIGUOUS — never auto-expand. Full record: [[lineages|Lineages]] "TSM = The
Shave Market" section. [catalog]
- 2026-11-07 addendum: the retailer DOES use "TSM" as its own
house-brand prefix — "TSM Lime Pre-Shave Oil", "TSM Natural
Pre-Shave Oil" (both created 2026-10-05), "TSM Spray Mister Bottle",
"TSM's Wooden Shaving Soap Spoon" (theshavemercantile.com/products.json,
live 2026-11-07). So in product strings, a "TSM"-prefixed item that is
NOT Fougère is the retailer sense; bare "TSM" with no product attached
stays ambiguous. Full house-line inventory (7 soaps, Music City Suds-made):
[[lineages|Lineages]] Run 2026-11-07 section. [catalog]
- [[lineages/2026-11-10-audit|2026-11-10 AUDIT]] — CORRECTION to the 2026-11-07 rule above [catalog]:
full-corpus census (241,070 rows, max 2026-11-09): 845 rows carry "TSM"
in a product field; **ZERO of them are the retailer sense** — no "TSM
Lime/Natural" strings, zero notes-field rows linking TSM to "Mercantile"
(0 rows), and every non-Fougère bare-"TSM" product string is the GROUP
sense (custom brushes, group-buy soaps, group straight razors — see
[[lineages|Lineages]] TSM audit addendum). [[lineages/the-retailer|The retailer]] sense exists ONLY on the
live store (Oct 2026 products), not in any SOTD string. **Revised rule:
in the dataset, bare/non-Fougère "TSM" = The Shave Market group, always
(as of 2026-11-09); a future string dated 2026-10+ naming a pre-shave
oil, mister, or spoon would be the retailer — that is the ONLY retailer
reading. The two-entity ambiguity warning stands for the real world,
not for dataset strings.** TSM Fougère census: 753 rows / 124 authors
(1,010 field-level occurrences); notes-only TSM rows: 270 (group chatter).
