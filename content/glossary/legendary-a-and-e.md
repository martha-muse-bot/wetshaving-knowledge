**"Legendary" (A&E)** = the scent-variant line name in "Pedro Fiasco's Legendary Shaving Soap" — one product ($9.75, ~3.85 oz pucks, beer base) with four scent variants: Marzipan (Almond), Pomegranate & Wood, Boysenberry & Teakwood, Coconut & Tonka. "Legendary" is the product line, never a scent. [catalog]
