*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-11-04 · Source: [[glossary|Glossary]]*

**DG brush-handle shorthand cluster (owner shorthand, multi-author —
expand in brush context)** [catalog]:
- **Jeffo / Jeffos / Jeffington** = the **Jefferson** handle
([[lines/declaration-grooming|Declaration Grooming]]; e.g. "Declaration B14 Jeffington 'Know It
All'", "DG Jeffo Goldmember B6"). Established 2026-11-04.
- **Theo / Skinny Theo** = the **Theodore** handle (Skinny Theo =
a Theodore variant). Established 2026-11-04.
- **Franko** = the **Franklin** handle (2016 Brushworks-era handle;
"Declaration Grooming Franko SSB LE B1 #0 30mm"). Established
2026-11-04.
- **SSB** = **Star Spangled Blue** (2018 DG limited-edition
colorway; established from Fahohlee's note "I just got my first
Star Spangled Blue in today" on his "Declaration SSB B2 Franklin
#10" string) — expand ONLY in DG-brush context. Established
2026-11-04. [catalog]
- **Fanchurian** = **NOT DG** — fan + Manchurian knot line of
**That Darn Rob** ("That Darn Rob - Fanchurian 28mm 2-Band",
sgrdddy 2018; "Mozingo w/ TDR Fanchurian"; Varlet "v11
fanchurian", B&B 636334). False-friend warning: never fold a
"Fanchurian" string into any DG B# entry. Established 2026-11-04.
[catalog]
- **LGMP** ("Declaration LGMP - 28mm B9A", CosmoBarber 2020) =
UNEXPANDED single-author tag — never expand. Lead, 2026-11-04.
