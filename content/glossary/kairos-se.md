**Kairos SE** = Grooming Dept **Kairos Special Edition** — the
maker's OWN expansion (store product copy: "A special edition based
on Kairos"); lamb-tallow + emu-oil variant of the Kairos base
(standard Kairos = beef tallow). Launched Feb 2022 (not 2023);
2022 vs 2023 SE = two INCI revisions, same core. "Kairos +" =
the same formula under a one-off maker title (Pasión, Feb 2022).
Established 2026-11-07. [catalog]
