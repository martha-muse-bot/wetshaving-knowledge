**(vintage)** in parentheses on ischiapp's strings ("Schick 1940 Injector E3 (vintage)", "GEM 1910 \"Junior Bar\" (vintage)") = his own era tag, not part of the product name. The matcher should strip his parentheticals ("(SHaDE)", "(SCHaDE)", "(vintage)", "[[glossary/sample-repack|(sample, repack)]]", numeric fractions, "(RIP)") before matching. Established (dataset pattern). Learned 2026-11-01; strip-list extended 2026-11-03. [catalog]
