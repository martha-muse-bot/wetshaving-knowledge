**C&R / CR** = Carnavis & Richardson (artisan brush maker — aluminum
Victorian handles, 24mm customs; dataset 2017–2022). Also the collab
partner on Catie's Bubbles "Porch Drinks" soap (CB × C&R) and Chatillon
Lux "Les Boissons sur le Porche" aftershave (CL × C&R) — one scent,
composed by [[people/shawn-maher|Shawn Maher]]. EldrormR flickers "C&R"/"CL"/"CR" prefixes for
the same aftershave bottle; "C&R Les Boissons sur le Porche" = the
CL-collab aftershave, never a standalone C&R product. Established
(dataset + maker blog). Learned 2026-11-02. [catalog]
