*Confidence: established · Source: [[glossary|Glossary]]*

**Numeric fraction tags** = ischiapp's usage-tracking notation in notes and
some fields ("(29/27)" ×53, "(28/27)" ×14, "(14 / 22 / 38)", "(31/27)",
"(18 / 27)", "(14 / 27)", "(30/58)"): appended to his SOTD notes blocks and
occasionally to strings ("FrankenRazor PP1489 ... 12x41x100mm, 56g
(29/27)"). Exact meaning unconfirmed (likely shave/rotation counters —
his blades carry separate "#66"-style use counts, e.g. "Sapphoo Red #52").
AMBIGUOUS even within his own posts; never product identity, strip.
CAUTION: genuine inch-fraction straight-razor widths ("6/8"", "7/8"",
"+17/16"") look similar but are real measurements — the distinction is the
absence of the inch mark and the notes-block context. Established as
author-scoped (dataset pattern, 2026-11-03). [catalog]
