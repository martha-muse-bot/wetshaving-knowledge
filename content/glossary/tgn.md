**TGN** = **The Golden Nib** (knot supplier for handle makers; owner
"Tony" per B&B 175855 "have used no less than 50 of Tonys knots").
"TGN Silvertip Premium", "TGN Finest", "TGN Silvertip Grade A" = their
knot grades. Established (B&B + dataset 2016–2026, 55 authors).
