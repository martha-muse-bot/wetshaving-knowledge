*Confidence: established · Source: [[glossary|Glossary]]*

**The Club** = the house line of **The Shaving Shop Club**, Ariana & Evans' membership club (shavingshopclub.com; VIP-member-exclusive scents, A&E-made; "for VIP members" product URLs; "The Back Room" = the members' exclusive area). A bare "The Club" with no scent is a channel/brand reference, never a product. Established (B&B 614265 firsthand + live store read 2026-11-08 + dataset). **"The Club Black" is TWO scents, never one entry:** "Black Birch Stout" (2019–2020, oatmeal-cookie/stout scent) vs "BLACK" (2022–, pepper/tobacco/oud/amber/dark chocolate/rum).
