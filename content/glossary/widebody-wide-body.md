*Confidence: established · Source: [[glossary|Glossary]]*

**widebody / wide body** (Karve CB) = the optional CB top cap
COVERING the blade tabs (vs the original exposed-tab cap; offered by
MARCH 2020 — dataset first attestation cteriyaki 2020-03-24; exact
announcement unpinned). Top-cap era attribute on the CB entry — never
a model split; a bare pre-2020 CB string is NOT assumed widebody.
Ambiguity guard: "widebody" alone on a non-Karve string is not Karve
evidence. Established (B&B + dataset; 2026-11-03). [catalog]
