**"Psycho"** (razor context) = the **Gillette Security Razor** — collector nickname (alongside "Prison" and "Asylum"). Sharpologist: never sold to the public; developed for the US Army in 1949 (locking-nut hollow handle needing a special key), then used by mental hospitals, prisons, and other government entities — hence the nicknames. **AMBIGUOUS-DANGEROUS**: (a) "Psycho" is ALSO used as an owner nickname for any aggressive-feeling razor — macG70's 2017 dataset razor field "Grafco (Merkur) Psycho OC" parses as Grafco + (Merkur) + Psycho (owner nickname) + OC (open comb); never treat "Psycho" there as a model name; (b) no Merkur "Psycho" model exists (targeted search negative). Other authors' "Psycho" strings (e.g. "Tomac Psycho Razor") are the Gillette Security Razor, never Grafco. Established (Sharpologist, Gillette Archives via The Gillette Blade; 2026-11-03). [catalog]
