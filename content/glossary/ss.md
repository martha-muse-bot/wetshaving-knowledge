**SS** (soap context) = **AMBIGUOUS — never auto-apply**: (1) **S**trop
**S**hoppe — "SS Baker Street SE" (Baker Street was a Strop Shoppe
Special Edition), "SS Alpine Frost LE" (_neutrino_'s note glosses it
"Strop Shoppe LE"; Alpine Frost was their menthol LE); B&B Strop Shoppe
threads routinely say "the SS soaps"; (2) **S**udsy **S**oapery — "SS
Rose and Black Pepper" (ohm_mho's note names Sudsy Soapery outright),
"SS Peppermint Lavender", "SS Delor de Treget"/"SS DdT" (the CL-collab
scent — see DdT below). Resolve per-scent, never by default. **RESOLVED
2026-10-31 (author-scoped LEAD): "SS Cola" (Eric--M, 2017-08-28, soap
field, "Soda theme" note) = Sudsy Soapery Cola.** Rationale: the author's
two other 2017 soap strings are full-form "Sudsy Soapery" strings; Sudsy
Soapery Cola is a real product (first full-form dataset attestation 2018,
fuckchalzone; TryThatSoap collection 884 — "The Sudsy Soapery - Cola";
still in use 2026, Breadheater9876); zero Strop Shoppe Cola products
exist anywhere, and Strop Shoppe closed ~2016. Validator: fold into the
Sudsy Soapery Cola entry as an author-scoped abbreviation — never
resolve other 2017 "SS" strings from this one (the namespace ran
Strop Shoppe by default). Established 2026-10-30 (dataset
notes + B&B); SS-Cola resolution added 2026-10-31 (dataset +
TryThatSoap). [catalog]
