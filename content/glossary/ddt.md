*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-10-30 · Source: [[Glossary]]*

**DdT** = **D**elor **d**e **T**reget — the [[Chatillon Lux]] × Sudsy Soapery
collaboration scent (named for Clement Delor de Treget, founder of
Carondelet — a St. Louis reference, like CL's Colbeck and Taum Sauk).
TWO products, never conflated: (a) the **Sudsy Soapery soap** ("SS
Delor de Treget", "SS DdT"; released Jan 15 — B&B 2018 sabbatical
thread); (b) Chatillon Lux's **own aftershave** ("CL DdT",
Eighchops 2016 — the CL prefix is CORRECT here, not an error).
Eighchops's "DdT" is his author abbreviation (aymanem's "[DDT]" imgur
link text confirms the initials). Per P-16: soap carries joint
Sudsy-Soapery×CL credit, aftershave is CL's alone. Established
2026-10-30 (dataset + B&B + SharpRazorPalace review). [catalog]
