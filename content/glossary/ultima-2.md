**Ultima 2** = Ariana & Evans' merged shaving-soap base (2026), combining
K2e + Ultima ("bringing together the best qualities of its K2e and Ultima
formulations into one new shaving soap base" — The Lather Ledger, Nov 10,
2026, the A&E owner's own publication). A base tag, never a scent; do not
retag pre-2026 K2e/Ultima tubs. Established. Learned 2026-11-15. [catalog]
