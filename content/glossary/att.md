*Confidence: established · Source: [[Glossary]]*

**ATT** = Above The Tie (razor maker; Kronos/Atlas/Calypso/S1/S2/Windsor
handles). Established (dataset razor-field usage). [catalog]
- 2026-11-03 addendum: in the Blackland era (2024+), **Colossus** = the
new ATT handle (semi-hollow, 102mm; maker site); **S1** = the slant
line with BOTH Safety Bar and Open Comb plates (the old S2 name is
retired in the new line); **Windsor M/R/H** = the original plate
names restored on the 2025 Windsor re-launch (vs SSMR/SSRH on the
pre-2024 plates).
