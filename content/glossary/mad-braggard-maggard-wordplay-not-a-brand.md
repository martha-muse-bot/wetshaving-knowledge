Day 8 theme ("Mad Braggard Day" — brag about your Maggard Razors
purchases; pun on Maggard). **AMBIGUOUS-DANGEROUS — looks like a
brand name, is a joke**: any future dataset string like "Mad Braggard -
<product>" is almost certainly a poster goof. u/VisceralWatch's
2025-11-27 "the artist formerly known as Mad Braggard" is a Prince
joke about a person, not a product. Established 2026-10-23 (dataset
