**SHD** = **AMBIGUOUS — never auto-apply**: (1) Maggard **S**uper
**H**igh **D**ensity badger knot ("Maggard 24mm SHD"; B&B spells it out:
"Maggard's Super High Density (SHD) 22mm/48mm loft"); (2) Pearl
**SHD-42** razor model ("Pearl SHD-42 Open Comb", NoPantsJake, bought
off the bazaar). Also a Lather Games scavenger-hunt tag (#SHD).
Established 2026-10-30 (dataset + B&B). [catalog]

letter-transposition typo of SHD, never a distinct knot — full-corpus
census: 9,353 SHD vs 8 SDH brush-field rows; every SDH author writes
SHD far more often, including for the SAME brush (Old_Hiker's Rubberset

strings into the SHD knot family; never a separate knot entry. [catalog]
