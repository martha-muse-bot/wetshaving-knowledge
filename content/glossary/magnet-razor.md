*Confidence: established · Source: [[Glossary]]*

**"Magnet Razor"** = the **Everyday Shaving "Stinger"** — the
magnetic double-sided DE razor (magnets hold the blade cover in
place; solid-bar side + scalloped side; 40 g; launched Jan 2024;
maker-archived price $34.99 Feb 2024 vs B&B-reported $70→$95 —
price unresolved). Dataset: 6 razor-field strings, 2 authors
(iamsms ×5, IntroductionMajor655 ×1, 2024), all passaround uses;
pinned by the dataset's own narration ("Everyday Stinger (aka that
weird square handled magnet razor)" — jimm262 2024-04-01).
**[[CORRECTION 2026-11-05]]:** not a plate-swapping design — maker copy
describes a double-sided razor; do not call the plates
interchangeable.
**AMBIGUITY GUARD:** "Magnet"/"Magnetic" on straight razors = steel-
grade descriptors (Genco Magnetic Steel, Yankee Cutlery Magnetized
#910, Böker Damascus Magnetic Steel, Griffon "Carbo-Magnetic 60",
Lion "The Magnetic Razor", Licht Werk Magnetic Blue Steel) — never
the Stinger. Established (dataset + B&B 648098, 2026-11-05). [catalog]
