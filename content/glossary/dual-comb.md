**Dual Comb** = iKon's dual-function head design (OC + SB in one head) — MalthusTheShaver's term for the 101 ("IKON 101 Dual Comb", 2021-06-10). Applies to the 101/OG1 (aluminum) and the B1 OSS (stainless) — same design, different lines, never fold across them. [catalog]
