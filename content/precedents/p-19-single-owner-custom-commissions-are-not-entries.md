*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** A custom-commissioned item made for one owner (one physical
item, one poster) is not a catalog entry, however many times that owner
posts it. Its strings resolve at brand level.
**Established by:** Böker §6 ruling (2026-10-28, user) — Marquis90's
custom-designed Böker 6/8 (~283 posts, one razor).
**Contrast:** Koraat §5 named designs WERE cataloged — they recur across
owners and community runs. Single-owner + single-item fails that test.
