*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** When posters' strings cannot distinguish two label variants, they
share one entry with both variants recorded; splitting would manufacture counts
that are guesses dressed as data.
**Established by:** §A9 Gillette Rubie / Rubie Platinum Plus (2026-10-21).
**Relied on by:** §A11 Van der Hagen — one entry recording both real variants
(Basics/China and Ice Tempered/Germany) because bare strings cannot be sorted
between them (2026-10-21).
