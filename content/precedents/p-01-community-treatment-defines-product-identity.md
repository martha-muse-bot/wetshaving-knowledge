*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** Where the community consistently treats two things as different
products, they are separate entries, even with shared lineage or manufacture.
**Established by:** Polsilver vs Wizamet separation; Personna Med Prep kept separate
from Lab Blue (2026-10-20).
**Relied on by:** §A2 Personna Red kept separate from Platinum (2026-10-21);
§A6 Rex/Tatra (with P-04).
