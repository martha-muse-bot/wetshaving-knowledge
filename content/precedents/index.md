The graph of catalog decisions and the precedents they establish. Rules of the system
(user directive, 2026-10-21):

1. **Precedents bind.** Every discuss-queue presentation and every catalog opinion must
   cite the governing precedent(s) below. A recommendation that deviates from a standing
   precedent must say so explicitly and justify the deviation.
2. **Precedents can be overturned — by strong evidence, not by preference.** An overturn
   is recorded here (status changed, never silently edited) with the evidence that
   forced it.
3. **Overturns cascade.** When a precedent falls, every decision listed under its
   "Relied on by" is queued for re-review; those decisions stand until re-reviewed, but
   they are flagged, not trusted blindly.
4. **New rulings join the graph.** Each user ruling either relies on existing precedents
   (recorded under them) or establishes a new one (added here with its establishing
   decision).

Status key: STANDING · OVERTURNED · SUPERSEDED.
