*Status: overturned · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement (as held):** An unbranded reseller's blades (Ted Pella's white
dispenser) alias to the manufacturer's entry, since no brand exists.
**Established by:** §A3 first ruling (2026-10-21).
**OVERTURNED by:** §A3 reversal (2026-10-21, same session) under P-04 — posters
consistently record the purchase as "Ted Pella"; unbranded packaging does not
defeat brand of record. Ted Pella is its own entry, Personna manufacture noted.
**Dependents re-reviewed:** §A3 itself (the only dependent) — reversed and applied.
