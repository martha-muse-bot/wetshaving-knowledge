*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** A maker's named model is ONE entry. Sequential revisions of
that model (AlumiGoose V1→V2), and its eras, handles, finishes, countries,
and base-plate variants, are attributes of the model entry — recorded as
variant aliases on the entry — not separate entries. This preserves
model-level popularity counts instead of diluting them across versions, and
avoids manufacturing version precision the posts don't support (99% of
AlumiGoose posts are bare). Separate entries only for distinct model names,
distinct makers, and distinct designs sold in parallel.
**Established by:** Mongoose/AlumiGoose ruling (2026-10-28, user) +
Gillette Tech analysis (2026-10-28).
**Refines:** P-03 — P-03's geometry-is-identity rule survives for distinct
models and parallel designs (Timeless Slant, WR1 vs WR2); it no longer splits
sequential revisions or variant dimensions of one named model.
**Relied on by:** Mongoose 2-entry structure (razor/mongoose-mongoose,
razor/mongoose-alumigoose, 2026-10-28); Tech attribute-fold project (queued
2026-10-28).
**Open boundary:** Ranger Tech / Milord — sold under distinct model names
but Tech-based; user call still owed. Other makers' Tech-styles (Baili,
RazoRock, iKon) stay separate entries.
