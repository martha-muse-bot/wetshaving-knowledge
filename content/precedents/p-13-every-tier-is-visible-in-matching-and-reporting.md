*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** The matcher indexes every catalog tier. Resolution precedence:
verified (and needs-review) > attested/corroborated (their own middle tier) >
unverified clusters. A product must never disappear from usage reports because
its evidence improved; reports label each line with its tier.
**Established by:** matcher-extension ruling (2026-10-21), prompted by the
Crystal §A8 consolidation (attested entry resolving zero posts) and the Rubie
§A9 flag — ~7,800 attested/corroborated entries were matcher-invisible.
**Relied on by:** all coverage/usage reporting from 2026-10-21 on.
