*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** When a bare name could be two variants and one is clearly more
popular, the bare entry defaults to the popular one (bare "Astra" = Superior
Platinum green; bare "Minora" = Minora Stainless; bare "Wilkinson Sword" =
Edgewell Classic unless the post says Gillette; bare "Feather" in DE context =
Hi-Stainless; bare "Schick" resolves per the razor named).
**Established by:** standing convention (2026-10-20).
**Relied on by:** bare "Timeless" → standard-head entry (2026-10-21); bare "Dorco
Stainless" → ST300 line (§A5); §B6 "Feather Pro" → AC Professional (2026-10-21;
naming-behavior asymmetry — Pro Super users say "Super" — reinforced the
volume default).
**Application note (2026-10-21):** P-07 survives as the leader-selection rule
only — its outputs are applied as P-15 loose aliases per the universal ruling
(see P-15, "UNIVERSAL APPLICATION").
