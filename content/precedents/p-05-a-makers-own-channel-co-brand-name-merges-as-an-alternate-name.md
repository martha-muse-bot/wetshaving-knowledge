*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** When the second name is the maker's (or its distributor's) own
channel brand for the identical product — evidenced by a dual-branded listing — the
names merge: one entry, second name as alternate. Boundary with P-04: an independent
brand sourcing manufacture is a separate entry (P-04); a co-brand of the same
maker's product merges (P-05).
**Established by:** Personna Lab Blue = AccuTec AccuForge (2026-10-20).
**Relied on by:** §A1 Fromm hair shaper → Personna Hair Shaper (SalonCentric's
dual-branded `fromm-personna-…` listing), 2026-10-21.
