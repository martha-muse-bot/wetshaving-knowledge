*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** Catalog identity follows the brand the user intends — the brand of
record — because a brand owner can move production between manufacturers and the
product-as-bought stays the same. Manufacture and lineage are recorded as
attributes/notes, never as identity.
**Established by:** §A4 Parker Injector (AccuTec-made, Parker entry) and §A6
Rex/Tatra (Czech Blades manufacture in notes), both 2026-10-21, plus the user's
stated rationale.
**Relied on by:** §A8 Crystal (brand Crystal, ASR manufacture noted); §A3-REV Ted
Pella (see Overturned, below).
