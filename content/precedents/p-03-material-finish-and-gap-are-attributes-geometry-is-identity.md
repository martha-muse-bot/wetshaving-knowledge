*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** For razors, the same geometry in different materials, finishes, or
gaps is ONE entry with attributes (user example: Blackland Blackbird SS/Ti/polished).
A meaningfully different geometry is a different razor (user example: Timeless
Aluminum vs Timeless Aluminum Slant).
**Established by:** §0b-3 Timeless restructure (2026-10-21).
**Relied on by:** Timeless 4-entry structure (2026-10-21).
