*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** When a fragrance house and a soap maker collaborate, brand of
record follows how the product was labeled and sold, not who poured it. The
soap carries JOINT collab credit with the fragrance house primary (the maker
as manufacture attribute); the aftershave/fragrance line stays the fragrance
house's alone. Consequence for set cross-validation: same-scent soap +
aftershave pairs are NEVER same-maker sets in this pattern — cross-brand by
design — even when the same soap maker also made the scented aftershave (the
set rule propagates within one brand's line, and the aftershave line is the
fragrance house's).
**Established by:** [[Chatillon Lux]] × [[Declaration Grooming]] ruling (2026-10-22,
user). Evidence: DG sold the soaps on declarationgrooming.com (CL's site never
listed them; CL's June 2020 announcement made DG "sole provider of Chatillon
Lux fragrances in shaving product bases"); tubs carried DG's milksteak-era
label artwork with the Chatillon Lux name prominent; CL's own copy: "soap in
the Icarus base from Declaration Grooming, as well as *our own* aftershave and
toner". Dataset 2018–2026: 84% of CL soap posts name both brands (82% of those
CL-first); aftershave posts are 100% "Chatillon Lux" alone.
**Relied on by:** Chatillon Lux soap/aftershave entries; the standing
cross-brand set-exclusion note in the knowledge base; Cerberus entries
(soap/aftershaves/fragrance first re-attributed HoM-primary 2026-10-22, then
REVISED to label/retail-brand primary — DG×HoM / NO×HoM with HoM as scent
creator — same day after EdP label photos showed DG-only branding).
