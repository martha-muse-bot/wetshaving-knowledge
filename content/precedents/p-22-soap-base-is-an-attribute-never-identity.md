*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** A soap's base (tallow, vegan, Donkeybase, Maxima, Optimus,
and the like) is an attribute of the scent entry, never its identity.
Base alone never splits a soap entry: one maker + one scent = one entry
within the soap category, with base transitions recorded as eras when a
post specifies them. Bare posts match the entry regardless of base.
Applies uniformly across brands; no brand-specific base treatment.
**Established by:** Aion base discussion (2026-10-28, user-approved) —
user observed that splitting by base breaks comparison of a long-lived
scent across bases and leaves most posts unassignable, since users
rarely name a base.
**Boundary:** A difference in name or scent is a different product
regardless of base. [[Barrister and Mann]] Roam vs Roam II stay separate:
Roam II (June 2024 limited edition, vegan base) is a different scent
profile — refined gentleman-cowboy, without the smoke-forward
true-to-trail nature of original Roam — not Roam in a new base.
Base-as-attribute folds only records differing by base and nothing
else (e.g. Roam Excelsior vs Roam Glissant).
**Contrast:** P-17 (model attributes) — same logic extended from models
to soap scents: revisions and eras are attributes of one entry. P-03
(material as attribute) — same principle: a variant dimension is not
identity. B&M Spice (§0b-1) — base already treated as an era attribute
there; P-22 generalizes it.
**Matcher implication:** Entries keyed by maker + scent; base recorded
as an era attribute when specified. Existing base-differentiated
records (e.g. B&M Roam Excelsior vs Glissant, both corroborated) fold
into single scent entries under this rule. Entry ids embedding a base
(e.g. soap/aion-skincare-optimus-shaving-soap-reverence) want renames
to the base-free form at application.
