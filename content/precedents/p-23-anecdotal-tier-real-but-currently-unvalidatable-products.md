*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** Products that are real — credible poster testimony of
existence and use — but unvalidatable *at this time* (primary evidence
cannot exist yet in principle, not merely hard to find) get the entry
status `anecdotal`, below corroborated. The loop spends no further
research slots on them — revisiting is futile *until something changes*.
The door stays open by design: if genuine primary evidence surfaces
later (the prototype goes into production, a maker page appears), the
entry promotes normally; nothing about the tier blocks that. The entry
persists so its strings keep matching (matching doesn't require
validatability), but anecdotal entries are never published in the
encyclopedia.
**Qualifying test:** (a) credible poster testimony that the thing existed
and was used, AND (b) a positive indication it was never retailed / no
listing could exist — typically the poster's own description as
prototype, one-off, test batch, or "never-born," plus no retail trace.
"We searched and found nothing" alone stays unverified; the positive
unvalidatability finding is what qualifies.
**Established by:** Baybarry discussion (2026-10-30, user-approved) —
Green Mountain × Savannah Bee prototype c.2016 ("il sapone mai nato"),
15 uses / 1 author, never retailed. User observed the dataset holds a
real category of prototypes, one-offs, and experiments and proposed the
terminal "anecdotal" state; the shape (terminal, still matches, never
published, below corroborated) was agreed in discussion.
**Boundary:** A sample *of* a real product (e.g. HoM avocado sample,
Meißner Tremonia sample) folds to the product entry — the unvalidatable
thing must be the entity itself. Multi-author prototypes (artisan test
batches sent to several people) qualify the same way; author count is
irrelevant, unvalidatability is what matters. The open door is
deliberate, not an afterthought: a prototype that later goes into
production (cf. Wolfman Guerrilla) promotes out of anecdotal normally —
"leave open" is a matter of permission (nothing blocks promotion), not
monitoring (nothing watches for it); the realistic reopening paths are
opportunistic enrichment or a human noticing.
**Contrast:** P-19 (custom commissions → not entries, resolve at brand
level) — anecdotal keeps the entry for matching fidelity instead of
dissolving it; a named prototype resolving to an anecdotal entry beats a
generic brand-level match. not_a_product — reserved for strings
referring to nothing real (jokes, misparses); anecdotal is for things
believed real.
**Matcher implication:** `anecdotal` added to MID_STATUSES in
match_all.py (2026-10-30) so anecdotal entries keep matching, ranking
below verified and alongside attested/corroborated, above unverified.
**Applied:** 2026-10-30 — `unverified/soap/green-mountain-baybarry-savannah-bee-prototype-2016`
→ anecdotal; Q007314 resolved.
