*Status: standing · Source: [[Precedents]]*

**Statement:** When a maker renames a product in place, it is one entry; the current
name is canonical and the former name becomes an alias. (User rationale: a buyer of a
new tub today reports the name on the tub.) Product bases/eras are attributes.
**Established by:** §0b-1 B&M Spice / Reserve Spice (2026-10-21).
**Relied on by:** the whole Barrister's Reserve line application — Spice, Waves,
Cool, Fern, Lavender across soap/aftershave/fragrance (2026-10-21).
