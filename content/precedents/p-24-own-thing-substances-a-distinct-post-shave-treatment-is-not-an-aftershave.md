*Status: standing · Source: [[precedents|Precedents]]*

**Statement:** A substance posters use as its own distinct post-shave step — a mineral-salt block/stick applied and rinsed off, antiseptic/astringent — is its own thing, not an aftershave. Bare strings for it stay on their own attested entry; they never fold into a branded product of that substance, and the substance is never deleted as a non-product. Branded products (Osma is one brand among several — Parker, Proraso, Omega also make alum blocks) are entries of their own, not merge targets for the bare string.
**Established by:** User ruling 2026-11-15 (Q000980): "Alum is not an aftershave. It's its own thing. Osma is one popular brand of Alum but there are several." Source: TRC alum-block guide (potassium/aluminum sulfate, antiseptic + astringent, applied after the shave, rinsed off).
**Boundary:** This is about identity, not the dataset field — bare "Alum" strings arrive in the aftershave field and the entry stays matchable there; the ruling does not create a new catalog category by itself. Whether alum (and its branded blocks) gets its own category is a separate structural decision, still open.
**Contrast:** P-20 (generic buckets for non-products) — Alum is not a bucket for clones; it is a real substance with its own use. Witch-hazel exclusion at build (ingredient/additive) does not extend to a substance posters use as a standalone step.
**Applied:** 2026-11-15 — Q000980 resolved; `unverified/aftershave/alum` annotated, stays attested.
