**Era rule [catalog]:** a parallel-format sibling is not automatically
current — Sir Irisch Moos cream (discontinued ~2012) and Nivea Sensitive
cream (US-discontinued ~2016) both need the era on the entry; the 244
"(current)" gloss on Irisch Moos was wrong.
