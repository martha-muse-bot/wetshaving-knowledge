**TTS cream-classification rule [catalog]:** TryThatSoap lists soft artisan soaps under product_type=cream. A TTS-markdown alias with product_type=cream does NOT prove a cream product exists — posters paste the TTS link for the soap. The alias only becomes a cream-string claim when the poster's own text says 'cream'. Applies to DG Albizia, all 15 Catie's Bubbles entries, Stirling, Noble Otter, Chicago Grooming Co., B&M, A&E, Spearhead, Captain's Choice, Taconic, The Holy Black — routing there is CORRECT, no moves. The distinguishing test is maker-side: does the maker sell the scent in both formats? (DRH/Palmolive/Tabac/M&W do; DG/Catie's/Stirling/Noble Otter/CGC/B&M/A&E do not.)
