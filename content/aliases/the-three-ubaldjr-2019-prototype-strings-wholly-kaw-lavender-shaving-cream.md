The three UbaldJr 2019 prototype strings ("Wholly Kaw Lavender Shaving Cream prototype (aka "La Supérieure")" 2019-11-23; "Wholly Kaw Lavender Shaving Cream prototype" 2019-12-08; "Wholly Kaw Lavender Tallow Shaving Cream prototype" 2019-12-28) are the La Supérieure CREAM line's pre-history, never the WCS soap's — the prototype was a tallow cream, the WCS soap is a vegan soap, and the maker describes the shipped cream as a from-the-bottom-up reformulation after multiple prototypes. Route prototype strings to the cream line's history, not to any soap entry.
