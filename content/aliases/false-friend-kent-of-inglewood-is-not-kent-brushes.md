dataset string "Kent Of Inglewood Shaving Soap Sample" names the
Canadian retailer Kent of Inglewood (Edmonton) — never fold it
into Kent Brushes / MWF entries. Source: dataset string +
retailer identity; established as a distinction, the sample's own
maker unpinned.
