**Maker pinned: Ogallala Bay Rum, not Summer Break Soaps.** rocketk455's own 2021-06-14 note narrates the commission chain: a club member first arranged it with Ballenclaugh ("the other Nebraska soap maker... an OG artisan"), who backed out ("a disagreement... he is not going to do the exclusive because it's not worth the stress"); the member asked rocketk455, who passed ("in the middle of the very first Cannonball release"); "Ogallala, yet another Nebraska soap maker, steps in to shave the day." His 2019-06-17 soap FIELD states it outright: "This Ogallala Bay Rum soap was an exclusive made for the short lived Nebraska Wet Shaving Club after Ballenclaugh decided not to make the soap."
