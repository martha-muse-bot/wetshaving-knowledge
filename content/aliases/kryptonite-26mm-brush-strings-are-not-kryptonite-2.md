[catalog] **"Kryptonite 26mm" brush strings are NOT Kryptonite 2.** "Chisel & Hound x HoM Kryptonite 26mm v27 Fanchurian" (InfernalInternal, Just_Bit2698) is the v1-scented Fanchurian knot in a 26mm handle — the "2" is the millimeter size. Never fold brush-field "Kryptonite 26mm" rows into any Kryptonite 2 entry; they belong to the C&H × HoM brush line (cf. the unverified `brush/turn-n-shave-x-mammoth-kryptonite-tip-28mm-v5` family).
