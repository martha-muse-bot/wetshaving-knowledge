**"The Handlebar Supply" ≠ Ethos Grooming Essentials (caution,
2026-10-22; CORRECTED 2026-10-26 — see [[Lineages]] correction).**
The 2021 note never claimed an Ethos rename: "He is carried by The
Shave Supply, formerly The Handlebar Supply" — the rename belongs
to the RETAILER (The HandleBar Supply → The Shave Supply, before
June 2021, corroborated by the retailer's own self-description).
The old caution's conclusion (do NOT create an Ethos alias) stands,
but its "poster confusion" reasoning is retired: the poster was
precise, the learner mis-parsed the sentence. Do NOT alias Ethos
to any Handlebar name; DO fold "The HandleBar Supply" → "The
Shave Supply" as one retailer group ([[World notes]]).
**UPDATE 2026-10-28 — "The Handlebar Supply Caribbean Snow"
RESOLVED [catalog]:** maker = **Queen Charlotte Soaps (QCS)** —
fold "The Handlebar Supply Caribbean Snow" strings into a QCS
Caribbean Snow entry; NEVER a retailer house-brand entry, and
never an Ethos product. Evidence: two Aug 2013 B&B Head
Shaver's Check-In posts (thread 259639, pp. 52/55) by posters
holding shop samples — "a sample of QCS Caribbean Snow" picked
up "at The Handlebar Barbershop (KodiakAttack's shop)" and
"The HandleBar Caribbean Snow (by QCS)" with scent notes
(mentholated firm croap, rich coconut base, sweet lime on top).
Dataset scope corrected: 21 soap-field strings, ALL darkfox45,
Jul 12–Aug 9, 2016 (20 plain + 1 combo string with Dapper
Dragon Signature / LASSCo Vert Noir); his notes call it a
"Barbershop soap" and he uses it up by Aug 9 — consistent with
a shop-distributed QCS sample/tub, not a house formula. Open
sub-question (does not block the fold): whether Caribbean Snow
was a QCS catalog scent or a shop exclusive made by QCS.
"Handlebar Shaving Company Dali" (single-author razor strings)
remains a separate open lead, not an alias of anything yet.
