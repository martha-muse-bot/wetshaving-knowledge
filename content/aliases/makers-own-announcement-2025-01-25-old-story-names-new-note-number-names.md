Maker's own announcement (2025-01-25): old story names → new
note+number names. Canonical = the NEW name; old name = alias.
**Tabac No. 01 = Hunting Lodge; Cèdre No. 06 = 1776; Tabac No. 04 =
Brown Bess; Épicé No. 07 = Bay Rhum; Chypre No. 03 = Voyageur;
Boisé No. 09 = Saranac; Menthe No. 05 = Hivernant.** Numbers are
arbitrary (maker: "rollout, rank, because I like the number") —
never treat them as version numbers. Dataset variants to fold:
"06-Cèdre", "Cèdre No. 6", "Tabac no. 3" (beard balm naming in
dataset — appears to follow the same scheme), "Long Rifle - Bay
Rum: Tobacco & Leather" (dataset 2026; likely the renamed Bay Rhum
presentation). AMBIGUITY WARNING: "Tabac No. 01" must NOT match
Mäurer & Wirtz Tabac; the number is the discriminator. Note "Bay
Rhum" (old) vs "Bay Rum" spellings were already mixed in-dataset.

**CORRECTION 2026-10-31 (scent-family of "Bay Rum: Tobacco & Leather"
— the 2026-10-23 "likely Épicé No. 07 / Bay Rhum" gloss was WRONG):**
the 2026 string folds to **Tabac No. 01 / Hunting Lodge**, not
Épicé No. 07 / Bay Rhum. Maker's own Hunting Lodge copy is
"Tobacco, leather, and bay rum combine to make the best manly scent
around" (Naked Armor's Long Rifle partnership page, relaying maker
copy; corroborated by Creaky Bottom Bracket 2017-07-18 quoting the
maker site: "bay rum with the addition of leather and tobacco
scents"). Bay Rhum is the LIME-forward scent — "The Bay is a lime
forward scent" (B&B tankerjohn 2018), "a fabulous warm-weather
rendition, light & breezy with a splash of lime" — with no
tobacco/leather notes anywhere. The dataset string (aftershave
field, sgrdddy, 2026-06-23, single post) mirrors the Hunting Lodge
note order ("Bay Rum: Tobacco & Leather" = bay rum + tobacco +
leather). Validator guidance [catalog]: fold the string into the
Hunting Lodge → Tabac No. 01 scent family as an author-scoped
descriptor alias; the bottle could be old-stock Hunting Lodge tonic
or the new Black Label Tabac No. 01 (both sold as "Long Rifle"),
so do NOT use the string to date the Black Label transition —
bottle-era unpinned (sgrdddy's gear video/gear-pic, live-image
job, is the only remaining route).
