"Pinaud", "Clubman", and "Pinaud Clubman" are ONE brand group (the US
Clubman line, now owned by American International Industries) — never
split "Clubman" off as a separate brand, and never fold French
Parfums Pinaud products into it if any appear. The Clubman line
itself dates to the 1940s American branch, not to founder Édouard
Pinaud (d. 1868); the brand's "since 1810" is marketing — the house
was founded 1830. Full lineage in [[Lineages]]. [catalog]
