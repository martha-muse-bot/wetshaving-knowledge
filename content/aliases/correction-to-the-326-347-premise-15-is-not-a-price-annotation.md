**CORRECTION to the (326)/(347) premise — "| 15" is NOT a price
annotation:** the 'Musgo Oakmoss in a tube | 15' row (silentisdeath,
2016-06-15) carries the Lather Games day number — its notes read
"Day's Completed 15 / Unique Fragrances: 15". The 03:06 run's
"price annotation" gloss and the (347) lead's wording are both
retired. The whole soap day-number family is Lather Games June
day-counts, typed bare ("15") or as "Product | N" / "N/N" halves.
