the 8 scent-less "Lime" rows (STG210 x7 2018–2019, FudgeShaver 2022

"Limes"/"Extract of Limes" but not bare "Lime". Add "Geo F Trumper
Lime" (+ the accented "Géo F. Trumper Lime") as aliases; the rows
should then route to Limes. "Lime" here = Extract of Limes / West
