**[catalog] Dusy Kabinett = Klar Kabinett reincarnated.** B&B
corroboration ×3: "It is indeed the very same soap as the legendary
Klar Kabinett" (thread 531697 #27); "Klar Kabinett was reincarnated
under the name Dusy Kabinett several years ago" (B&B "RIP products"
thread, staff member nemo); "I am told that Klar Dusy is almost
identical" (thread 548343 #50). Nuance from #27: "They've changed
one of the oils in it" — treat formula as era attribute. The 2022
dataset notes ("essentially, a rebranding of Klar Kabinett... a
rose-scented, vegetable-based soap with a bit of a cult following";
"supposedly has the same scent as Klar Kabinett") match. Klar
Kabinett was the German barber-block rose soap; Dusy is the
professional-barber supply brand that continued it. Established.
