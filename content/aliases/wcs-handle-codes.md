[catalog] **WCS handle codes — number = weight in grams, letter = finish (pattern-established).** Dataset census (razor field, WCS + number-letter code): 84S 42 rows/5 authors (2019–2026), 84B 12/3a (2020–21), 84BR 6/2a (2020, 2025 — Ramjet615 ×5 2020, Lob-Star 2025-12-24), 84BL 2/1a (Ramjet615 2020); also 78S 51, 78B 10, 78BL 5 (reddeckwinning only), 88S 53, 77S 3, 175S 8, 110S 6, 110B 3, 47B 2. Decode: **number = weight in grams; letter = finish.** Evidence: Sharpologist "West Coast Shaving Razors" (2016-12-02): "175B" Midnight handle, "four inch handle and a weight of 175 grams"; Classic = 303 stainless steel, Midnight = 303 stainless with black powder coating; six handle styles mated to scalloped SB or OC heads (zinc alloy, chrome-plated). **S = stainless (Classic); B = black (Midnight; pilgrim32 2018-06-21: "WCS Midnight Collection Razor 110B, Black Stainless Steel"); BL = blue titanium** (reddeckwinning 2020-05-14: "RazoRock German 37 Slant with WCS 78BL Titanium Handle"; pietroconti 2020-09-30: "Handle-[[aliases/wcs-84|WCS 84]] in blue"); **BR = brass** (pattern + mcqueen4 2018-02-16 "Gillette NEW SC / WCS brass handle" — but NO poster ever spells out "84BR = brass" in so many words, so BR=brass is pattern-established with that caveat). Catalog also has `wcs-84tb-black-titanium` / `wcs-84bt-black-titanium` clusters (TB/BT = black titanium, two orderings) and `wcs-84m` / `wcs-78m` clusters (M = unpinned). Catalog: `unverified/razor/wcs-classic-collection-razor-84s-stainless-steel-closed-comb` (corroborated), `wcs-78b` (corroborated), `wcs-84tb-black-titanium` (corroborated, "Multi Titanium Collection Razor"), plus many unverified 84S/84B/84BL/84BR/84BT clusters. WCS product page was not fetchable by text search; maker-page confirmation of BR=brass + the M code remains a residual lead. See [[Hardware lore]] for the full entry.
