*Confidence: lead · Source: [[aliases|Product aliases]]*

[catalog] **Collision guards:** "Pearlescent Parfums - Baklava" is a different brand's Baklava — never fold into A&E/Club Baklava. "Smitten Soapery - Morning Wood" is a different brand's Morning Wood — never fold into Club Morning Wood. "[[glossary/the-club|The Club]] - The Wood" (PennyStonks, Feb 2020, soap + splash) is a Club scent, not a generic woods reference — but it is single-author/one-week, so it stays a lead until a second author or a listing firms it. "A&E / The Club / Charky's Midnight Special" is NOT B&M Midnight Special (a 2023 B&M scent) — the shared words are coincidence.
