*Confidence: lead · Source: [[aliases|Product aliases]]*

**[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]:** a "live-browser job" label on a backlog lead can be stale — the March 2025 Coast homepage capture and both Feb 2025 Etsy listing captures read fine from the text sandbox via Wayback's `id_` raw-capture URL with curl. The 403s that blocked earlier runs were on the framed `web/` replay URLs, not the raw captures — try the `id_` URL before dispatching a browser task for a Wayback read. Also: a listing page's "you may also like" rail is other shops' stock — names there looked like scent variants in a filtered grep and were related items; always read the option list in document order before claiming variants.
