**"Mäuer & Wirtz" (missing the second r) is a pervasive dataset
typo** for Mäurer & Wirtz — dozens of strings across 2022–2026
("Mäuer & Wirtz - SIR Irisch Moos - Soap" etc.). Normalize to
Mäurer & Wirtz; never create a "Mäuer" brand.
