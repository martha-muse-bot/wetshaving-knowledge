**CORRECTION of a standing backlog guess: "Burn the Ships" is a
Hoffman's Shave & Soap Co. scent, NOT a [[Declaration Grooming]] scent.**
The backlog's vintage/misc item ("Burn the Ships is likely a DG
scent") was wrong. ESTABLISHED from the dataset itself (primary
data): dozens of soap strings 2023–2026 across many authors, always
under Hoffman name forms ("Hoffman's Shave & Soap Co. – Burn the
Ships", "Hoffman's Grooming - Burn the Ships (Hoffman Base)");
product forms include soap and deodorant. Zero DG "Burn the Ships"
strings in any year. Scent = Old Spice (Shulton) dupe — multiple
owners (dataset strings tag "(Shulton Old Spice dupe)"; WSDB
lead-page lists classic Old Spice-family notes: nutmeg, lemon,
orange, star anise, aldehydes, carnation, cinnamon, ambergris,
benzoin, cedar, vanilla, tonka, musk).
