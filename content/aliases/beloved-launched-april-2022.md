[catalog] **Beloved launched April 2022 — Fragrantica's "2026" is a database-addition artifact, never cite it as a launch year.** Maker listings created 2022-04-20 (EdP published 2023-02-03, $94); first EdP use 2022-05-07. Fragrantica added HoM scents in a 2026 backfill (its Missing Fragrance board shows Shire requested Feb/Apr/Aug 2026) and stamped addition years as launch years. Standing rule: a Fragrantica launch year for an artisan house needs a maker-side date.
