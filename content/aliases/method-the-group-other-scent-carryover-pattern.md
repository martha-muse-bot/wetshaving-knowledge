[catalog] **METHOD — the "GROUP - <other scent>" carryover pattern.** A single-author string of the form "<group> - <scent B>" that appears adjacent in time to the same author's genuine "<group> - <scent A>" string is a template carryover until a second author or a maker source says otherwise. Evidence bar for an exclusive: second author, or a maker/retailer listing naming the group + scent. Applies here to both (c1) and (c3); the maker interview (Sharpologist 2016-08-29) naming TSM Fougère as the sole TSM scent is the negative control. Same pattern family as the "Gillette Airline Tech" provenance-descriptor fold.
