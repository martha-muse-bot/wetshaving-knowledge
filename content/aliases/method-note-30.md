**[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]:** a pre-announcement ("getting ready to launch") is not a launch — when a product exists only in a pre-announcement and no launch/follow-up coverage appears in 8 years, the silence is the verdict (never-shipped), not an open question. Also: a Shopify products.json can omit a live product page (Imperial vegan brush absent from the 42-product list) — the per-product `.js` read is the authority, same lesson as TRC 2026-11-08.
