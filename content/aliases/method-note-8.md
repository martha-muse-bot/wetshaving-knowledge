**[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]:** a contract-maker claim that names only a city ("made in Chandler, Arizona") is checkable by fingerprint, not just by asking who is in that city — the identical INCI list + marketing phrases against the candidate maker's own current copy is the decisive match, and it works even when the retailer page never names the maker. Same discipline as the Bigfoot run: re-read the source page's own words (the splash page's "matching shaving soap" line) before accepting a backlog gloss ("2017 only") that was built on an absence.
