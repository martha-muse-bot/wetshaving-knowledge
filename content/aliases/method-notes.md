**[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]s:** (1) sample the earliest census rows before citing a first-use date — the raw first hit can be another brand (Purely Skinful Tobacconist) or a different product (Mood Indigo vs Indigo is the same family, but the name form matters). (2) A catalog LIKE query with a concatenated slug ("dinosmores") misses hyphenated IDs — query `%dino%` or the spaced form too. (3) A substring census on a common word ("mull", "beloved") returns plain-English false positives — field-scope + sample before citing counts.
