**[catalog] "Big Ben" is TWO products, split by FIELD, never by
name.** In the BLADE field, "Big Ben" / "Big Ben Super
Stainless" is Lord's Egyptian blade (hundreds of dataset
strings). In the RAZOR field, "Big Ben" is Souplex Ltd's
c.1935 Bakelite travel razor (V&A M.59-1984, Morecambe;
"BIG BEN" on head and case lid) — which has ZERO razor-field
dataset strings to date. A matcher keyed on the bare name
alone will misfile every Lord blade as a Souplex razor;
field decides. Full Souplex entry in [[Hardware lore]].
