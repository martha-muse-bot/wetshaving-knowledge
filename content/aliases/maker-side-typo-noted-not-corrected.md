**Maker-side typo, noted not corrected:** the Succès EDP variant list spells one option "Pineapple **Nior**" (variant created 2026-05-27) beside "Extra Pineapple" — a maker typo for Noir; do not treat "Nior" as a distinct scent. The "Extra Pineapple" EDP variant (2026) is a line extension, not a separate product.
