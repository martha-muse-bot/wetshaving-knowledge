[catalog] **Serial strings are plate+batch+sequence:** "ST-01-084" = Standard plate, batch 01, sequence 084 (maker: "each base plate is etched with a serial number representing the model, batch number, and sequence"). Batch-2 serial format unpinned (etching was ground off) — do not assume batch-2 plates carry serials.
