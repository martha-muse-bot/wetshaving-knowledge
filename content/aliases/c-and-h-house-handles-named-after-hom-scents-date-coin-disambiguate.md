[catalog] **C&H house handles named after HoM scents — date + coin disambiguate.** C&H sells unknotted handles "5610GC Fu Dao" (Aug 25, 2025), "5599RC Voices" (Aug 25, 2025, mango wood), "6165RC Kryptonite" (Sep 17, 2026), "5826GC/5837GC Shire" (Feb 2026). A scent-named C&H brush string WITHOUT a Mammoth coin number (xxxxMC), or dated after Aug 2025, may be a house handle with a Fanchurian knot — not the collab brush. The v16 Voices collab brush (3505–3508mc, Oct 2022, mango wood) vs the v28 "5582GC Voices" (readicculus5, Aug–Oct 2025, house handle) is the worked example: never fold the v28 strings into the v16 entry.
