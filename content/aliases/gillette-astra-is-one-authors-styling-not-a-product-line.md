**"Gillette Astra" is one author's styling, not a product line.**
Dataset: 63 blade strings pairing "Gillette" with Astra
Stainless/Blue, 49 of them by Glass_Procedure7497 (2024–2025:
"Gillette Astra Stainless" ×22, "Gillette Astra Stainless Blue" ×13,
"Gillette Astra Superior Stainless" ×6); the rest are one- or two-off
other authors. Fold all of them into the Astra Superior Stainless
(blue) entry as an author-scoped styling — never a separate product,
never evidence of a distinct "Gillette Astra" SKU. The underlying
Gillette ownership is real (Astra is a Gillette/P&G brand; some
market packs carry the Gillette name — B&B 562199 discusses which
Gillette-family blades do and don't print it), but the string form is
author-scoped.
