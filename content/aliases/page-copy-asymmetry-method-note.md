[catalog] **Page-copy asymmetry (method note):** Everett's 2024-09-12 and You & I's 2024-11-10 captures carry NO cooling copy at all — neither the menthol line nor the note. Z's and Hygge's pre-flip captures DID carry the menthol line, which the note replaced. On a page that never carried cooling copy, absence of the note proves only that the page hadn't been edited — it says nothing about the formula. Do not read "no note in capture" as "still cooled" on Everett-type pages.
