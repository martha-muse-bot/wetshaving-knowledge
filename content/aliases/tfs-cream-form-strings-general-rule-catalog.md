**TFS cream-form strings — general rule [catalog]:** TFS soft soaps are
croaps; retailers label the same SKU 'soap' or 'cream' ('crema per barba').
NEVER create a cream entry on TFS 'Cream' label wording alone; fold
cream-form strings into the soap entry. Confirmed per-scent: YOLO (248),
Purissima (this run). No cream-form sibling owed for any TFS scent.
