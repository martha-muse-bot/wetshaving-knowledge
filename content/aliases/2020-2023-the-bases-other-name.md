**2020–2023: the base's other name.** Community base ranking (B&B GD
club #2,082): "Nai Base (New Aion Soap)" — Nai literally expands to
"New Aion". DFS "All things Grooming Dept" members use "Aion/Nai"
interchangeably for the base. So "Aion" alone in 2020–2023 context
usually means the Nai vegan base (era attribute), not a scent.
