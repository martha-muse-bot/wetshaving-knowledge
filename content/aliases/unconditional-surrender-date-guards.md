[catalog] **Unconditional Surrender date guards.** Fragrantica's "2017" is the fragrance-listing year, never the launch (scent ≤ Sep 2016, EdT ≤ Nov 2016). The DG/L&L soap's first appearance is a Feb 2017 LIMITED EDITION (bison tallow, "V3A"); the Apr 21, 2018 Daily Lather release is the general release — never cite Apr 2018 as the soap launch. Yuzu/Rose/Patchouli: aftershave from May 2016; cafleurebon's "(2018)" dates the EdT.
