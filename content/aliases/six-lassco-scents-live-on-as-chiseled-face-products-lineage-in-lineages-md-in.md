*Confidence: lead · Source: [[Product aliases]]*

SIX LASSCo scents live on as Chiseled Face products (lineage in
[[Lineages]]), in two waves. They are CONTINUATIONS under a new
brand, NOT renames — keep separate catalog entries with lineage
links (same pattern as B&M Samhain ← SW Samhain). Maker framing =
COLLABORATION ("Chiseled Face / Los Angeles Shaving Soap Co collab
scent", Black Rose EdP page) — not a purchase; the OnionMiasma
"bought the scents" detail stays single-community-sourced.
- **Wave 1 (~2021, maker-attested):**
- **Chiseled Face "Black Rose"** ← LASSCo **"The Black Rose"**
(vegan → CF tallow; CF's own page credits LA explicitly).
- **Chiseled Face "Blackfern"/"Black Fern"** ← LASSCo "Blackfern"
(vegan → CF tallow; CF soap page: "originally made popular by
the Los Angeles Shaving Soap Co"; aftershave splash: "brought
to life by the creative genius of [[John Brown]]"). Note-stack:
lavender/geranium/patchouli/labdanum/clove/eucalyptus/orange/
peppermint. Two spellings in the wild ("blackfern" 2021: 9 CF-
branded hits; "black fern" 2021: 17) — fold both spellings.
- **Wave 2 (~2023, lead-grade continuation — note-stack + dataset
timing + maker-attested launch dates, NO on-page LA credit):**
two sub-waves per the maker's own Instagram (@chiseled_face,
2026-11-01): **Woody Lavender launched Feb 23, 2023** ("will be
available on the 23rd!", post 2023-02-20); the other three went
live **June 9, 2023** as the "new all essential oil based
scents" batch (reel: "Printing new labels... being posted
today!").
- **CF "Woody Lavender"** ← LASSCo "Woody Lavender" (same note
stack: lavender / ho wood / amyris / West Indian bay, 100%
essential oils).
- **CF "Topanga Fougère"** ← LASSCo "Topanga Fougere" (CF notes:
lavender/geranium/ho wood/balsam tolu/clary sage/vetiver/
patchouli/sweet myrrh vs LA's lavender/sage/patchouli/balsam/
myrrh/vetiver — strong match). TENSION: CF's own page frames
it as "an homage to the grandeur of Houbigant's Fougère
Royale... Paul Parquet... 1882" — no LA credit anywhere.
- **CF "Bespoke #1"** ← LASSCo "Bespoke #1" (CF: vetiver/amber/
frankincense/opopanax/clove/pepper vs LA's amber/cedar/clove/
frankincense/opoponax/pepper/vetiver — strong match).
- **CF "Santa Monica Bay Rum"** ← LASSCo "Santa Monica Bay Rum"
(pimenta racemosa + dark rum both sides; CF full stack adds
vanilla/orange extracts, balsam tolu, clary sage, opopanax,
lemon, anise, ho wood; LA's 2013 core = "actual bay EO and
dark rum", B&B 211273).
- **CF "Vanilla Eucalyptus & Mint"** ← LASSCo "Vanilla Eucalyptus
Mint" — STRONG LEAD, not yet a wave member: CF current-line
soap + splash = Vanilla Extract + Eucalyptus EO + SPEARMINT
EO; LA's 2013 original = vanilla/eucalyptus/spearmint
(B&B 359413; 2017 "Vanilla/Spearmint/Eucalyptus" string pins
the mint). Launch window ~June 2023 → May 2024 (first CF use
kind_simian 2024-05-05; no IG post; Maggard Meetup 2024
sample packs).
Dataset: first CF-branded strings — WL 2023-06-14; SM Bay Rum
2023-06-09 (rocketk455, launch day); Topanga 2023-08-26 samples
(_walden_); Bespoke #1 2023-08-26 samples (_walden_) — matching
the maker's Feb + June 2023 launch calendar.
