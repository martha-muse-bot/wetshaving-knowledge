**The Gillette "Blue" blade family — FOUR distinct products share one name
space** [catalog] (researched 2026-10-21, discuss §B4):
1. **Gillette Silver Blue** — stainless DE, PPI St. Petersburg (Russia).
Current, ubiquitous; "GSB" is its universal abbreviation. Verified
catalog entry `DE/gillette-silver-blue`.
2. **Gillette Super Blue (Shanghai)** — modern stainless DE from the
Shanghai Gillette joint-venture plant (blade printed "Super Stainless –
Shanghai China"); blue packs sold domestically in China and cheaply via
AliExpress/eBay (~$2). Packs are often titled "Super Gillette Blue
Blades." Catalog entry `DE/gillette-super-blue` (verified on a post
photo). NOT the same blade as Silver Blue (B&B insider plant list:
"Gillette Super Blue (Shanghai JV)" vs "Gillette Silver Blue (PPI)").
Counterfeits of the Shanghai blades circulate (RunWei "FakeBlues").
3. **Gillette Blue Blade (US vintage)** — carbon steel, blued finish,
1930s–1970s, wax-paper envelopes and metal dispensers, date-coded
(e.g. "1948 T4"). Discontinued; NOS still traded. Dataset uses that are
explicitly dated/NOS ("1948 T4 Gillette Blue Blade", "NOS Gillette
Blue Blade") are THIS product.
4. **Gillette Super Blue Blade (US vintage)** — introduced 1958–1960 as
Gillette's first coated blade: silicone-coated (later PTFE-type)
CARBON steel, sold in metal dispensers; later French production
(~1990). A different product from both the plain Blue Blade and the
modern Shanghai Super Blue — same name as (2), nothing else in common.
**"Gillette Blue" bare is NOT a product name** — established from dataset
evidence 2026-10-21: posters use it as shorthand, overwhelmingly for
Silver Blue. Proof cases: Sleezey-Sleeze's blade run "Gillette Blue"
(uses 1–7) continues as "GSB" (use 8) with a note "I just don't think
GSBs are my blade"; SirKravsALot wrote "Gillette Blue Silver" (his
transposed Silver Blue, 108 posts, 2023-01–2024-06) then shortened it to
"Gillette Blue" (146 posts, 2024-06–2026-10, same Above the Tie S1 razor
throughout). The matcher currently reports bare "Gillette Blue" as
AMBIGUOUS between Silver Blue and Super Blue — do NOT blanket-alias it.
**"Gillette Blue Silver" = transposed shorthand for Silver Blue** (poster
word-order error; resolves to `DE/gillette-silver-blue`).
