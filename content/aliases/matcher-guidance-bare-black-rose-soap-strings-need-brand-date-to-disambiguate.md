Matcher guidance: bare **"Black Rose"** soap strings need brand +
date to disambiguate (LA pre-2021; CF 2021-04-13+; "The Black Rose"
= LA house style). Bare **"Woody Lavender"** 2023+ is most likely
the CF soap (LA wound down ~2022/23), BUT the aftershave field still
carries genuine LA destash (frankieIVfingers, 2025-02-01: CF soap +
LA aftershave in the same post) — use the field (soap vs
aftershave) and the brand word when present; never fold a
"Los Angeles Shaving Soap Company" aftershave string into the CF
entry. "LA Woody Lavender" as written in notes (OnionMiasma,
2025-02-01) = the CF product in practice.
