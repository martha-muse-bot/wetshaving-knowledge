[catalog] **HoM "Mull" — etymology RESOLVED, maker-attested.** "Mull" is a deliberate double meaning: (1) "to mull" = to ponder/reflect — maker's own fragrance-descriptions page: "Mull illustrates how reflection requires us to sit with the unfamiliar until it becomes part of us. It's a scent that calls us to linger and contemplate..."; (2) "mulled wine" — "...in the same way a pot of wine and spices develops into something deep and delicious over low heat and with time" (spiced wine + orange + clove + tobacco + patchouli + Malaysian oud). **The Isle of Mull reading is RETIRED — zero support anywhere; never cite an island origin.** Fits HoM's concept-word naming pattern (Sonder, Embrace, Shire, Indigo, Hygge, Jitterbug, Uitwaaien — all concept words, not places, except the specific Dammusi/Pantelleria reference).
