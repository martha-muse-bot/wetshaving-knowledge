**"Grooming Dept / MAOL - Frankenlime"** (dataset 2018+) is NOT a
brand string to create — it is the Grooming Dept × **Maol Grooming**
collaboration (soap by GD in the donkey base + aftershave by Maol,
~Oct 2018). Maol Grooming is a SEPARATE Canadian aftershave brand
(Hanover, Ontario), not a GD successor or rebrand. Learned 2026-10-22.
