**"Mühle R106" (13u/1a + 8u/1a + 9u/1a "Mühle  R106") = REAL** — the
black closed-comb (R89-head family). Never fold into R41 (the miner's
target). **CORRECTION 2026-11-02 (supersedes the 2026-11-02 mystery-run
attribution of the R108): "Muhle R108" (6u/2a) = the TORTOISESHELL-handle
CLOSED-comb (R89-head family) — fold to the R89 entry with
tortoiseshell-handle attribute per P-17, never treat as a typo.** The
"black open-comb" reading was a lineup-convention overreach; the R1xx
series codes the open comb as the R101 only: bestmensshaver's R101
review — "the Mühle R101 has the exact same head as the R41... open
comb... This also has the same high-grade black resin handle as the
R106"; their R107 review — "a similar head to the closed comb R89...
If you like the less aggressive Mühle R106 but you don't like the black
handle, this white handled version (Mühle R107)"; B&B Mühle Mondays
2023-05-22 firsthand — "Razor: R108 (R89 with tortoiseshell handle)
with 1 shim"; and the dataset itself — rmic_ 2020: "I won the Muhle
R108 with the tortoise handle in the Wheeler raffle." Scheme: R101 =
open (R41 head) black resin; R106 = closed (R89) black resin; R107 =
closed (R89) white resin; R108 = closed (R89) tortoiseshell resin.
**Validators: UNDO any R108→R41 fold — it goes to the R89 family.**
