**[[aliases/method-notes|[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]s]]:** (1) A product's published_at reset across a whole scent line + zero pre-reset Wayback captures is a re-launch signature — read it as "dark until the reset date" only with the capture-absence caveat stated, and check the re-launch snapshot for stock. (2) A Maggard snapshot's embedded product JSON ("available":false, SKU, price) settles sold-out-at-capture even when the rendered page is ambiguous — read the JSON, not the button text. (3) The CL price check in the diff script matches exact titles ("Confluence") but live titles carry the "Eau de Toilette" suffix — match on handle/token, not exact title.
