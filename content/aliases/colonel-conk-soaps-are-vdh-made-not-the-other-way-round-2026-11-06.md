*Confidence: established · Source: [[aliases|Product aliases]]*

**[catalog] Colonel Conk soaps are VDH-made, NOT the other way round
(2026-11-06).** Van Der Hagen Enterprises makes Col. Conk's glycerin
soaps to Conk's specifications (B&B 62695 #8, B&B 139331 VDH-rep quote,
B&B 70533 — three independent threads + B&B Wiki: "The current company
does not manufacture its own products, and merely sells rebranded
products"). The old "VDH mug/brush are rebranded Conk" lead (from a
misread of B&B 520948 #40, which says only "the puck is made by the same
people that make Col Conk") is RETIRED — direction reversed. Catalog:
Conk soap entries carry VDH as a manufacture attribute (P-04); never
fold Conk into VDH (different scents/formulas). **"Country Uncle" is
NOT a Conk brand** — it is AmazingShaving.com's house brand, also
VDH-made with a slightly different recipe (lanolin + aloe; Sharpologist
2014-08-06, retailer-confirmed); zero dataset strings. **Surrey** is
VDH's predecessor (VDH bought Surrey; formula changed at the transition
— see [[lineages|Lineages]] VDH addendum); vintage Surrey brush strings (96 rows,
nearly all brush field) are their own predecessor-brand entries, never
folded into VDH. **Conk brush = rebranded Vulfix** is a single-voice
lead (B&B 139331 p.3); VDH boar brush shares a Chinese OEM with ASR's
Burma Shave / Ever Ready brushes (B&B 16801 p.2, lead-grade).
Established for the VDH→Conk direction and the Surrey buyout; leads as
noted for the brush claims.
