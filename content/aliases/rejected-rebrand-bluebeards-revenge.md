are NOT [[Mitchell's Wool Fat]] under license (resolved 2026-10-27,
see [[Lineages]] MWF addendum).** BBR's only lather product is a
Decelerine shaving CREAM (stearic/myristic cream, no lanolin,
marketed Vegan Friendly) — a different product class from MWF's
hard puck in both formula eras; Dreadnought is BBR's US brand
for the same cream. Dataset: all BBR soap strings are the cream,
zero BBR pucks, zero Dreadnought soap/cream strings. Never fold
BBR/Dreadnought cream strings into MWF entries; the claim's sole
source was one hedged forum "was it?" (pjgh, B&B 661231).
