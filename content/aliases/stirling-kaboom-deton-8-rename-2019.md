*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product aliases]]*

**[catalog] Stirling "Kaboom" → "Deton-8" (rename, ~2019).**
"Stirling - Kaboom" strings only in 2018 (27 uses, first 2018-12-02);
"Deton-8" from 2020 on (21 aftershave uses, first 2020-09-23).
Explicit 2023 note: "After that initial launch, Stirling's Kaboom
was renamed to 'Deton-8'" (sgrdddy). Maker's current scent page
lists **Deton-8** ("inspired by Spicebomb by Viktor&Rolf" —
stirlingsoap.com/pages/scent-descriptions). Canonical = Deton-8;
Kaboom alias (likely a trademark-avoidance rename — Kaboom is also a
household-cleaner brand). Note: Noble Otter ALSO has a scent called
"Kaboom!" (2018) — different product, never merge. Established.
