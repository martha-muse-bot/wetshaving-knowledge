**"Merkur 42C" (12u/4a) / "Merkur 42c" (7u/3a) = REAL** — the 1904-family
closed-comb (short hex handle; scharferladen.de lists 42C among the
mild closed-comb models; multiple reviews). Never fold into Merkur 23C
(the miner's target). Companion to the 41C=1904-OC guard entry: 42C =
the closed-comb 1904 sibling (one review source calls it open-comb —
conflict noted, closed-comb per scharferladen + shavingadvisor).
