"Barbershop Oil"), dict-typed 'other' fields (Beard Oil subfield), and notes mentions; string counts vs row counts differ (526 strings vs 500 rows all-years) — always state the unit. (2) A WAF 403 surviving a 3-attempt backoff + a second pass 40 min later is a wall, not a flake — stop after the retry pass, diff only what fetched cleanly, and NEVER diff partial paginated pages (false delists); NA p1–p5 totals are not comparable to full-catalog ei totals. (3) "Last product ever created" is a watch claim one night's listing can break — date it to its snapshot, never as a permanent fact.
