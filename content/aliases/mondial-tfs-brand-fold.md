**Mondial ≠ TFS brand fold:** Mondial/Antica Barberia soaps are TFS-MADE
(manufacture attribute), but Mondial entries stay Mondial — same-scent
equivalence is scent-by-scent, not blanket (one poster rates Mondial Tabacco
Verde's performance below TFS TV). Never fold a Mondial entry into a TFS
entry on brand alone.
