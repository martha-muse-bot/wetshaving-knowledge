**[catalog] "The Freedberg" = RazoRock Emperor (former name, 2026-11-06).**
RazoRock's 2014 Aventus-dupe soap "The Freedberg" (named for YouTuber Michael
Freedberg; B&B 3017 logs show 29 shaves Oct 2014) was renamed **Emperor** —
B&B thread 538000 (2018-02): "Freedberg was renamed to Emperor" (accepted
by the OP). Same scent profile both sides (Emperor retailer copy: pineapple,
bergamot, black pepper, smoke & leather, Creed Aventus-inspired); Freedberg
vanishes from the market after ~2015 while Emperor is the current Aventus
soap in the line (vshod listing, 150ml, Super Tallow). Canonical = Emperor,
"The Freedberg" = former-name alias; catalog `soap/razorock-emperor`
verified 2026-11-06 (Q017984/Q038888/Q038987) with the aftershave
cross-validated from it. Established (community rename statement +
scent/timeline corroboration + current retailer listing); maker-side
rename confirmation still a lead.
