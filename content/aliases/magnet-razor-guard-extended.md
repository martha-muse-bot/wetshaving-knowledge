[catalog] **[[glossary/magnet-razor|"Magnet Razor"]] guard, extended:** the Stinger is not the only magnetic-plate DE in the dataset — the Adopted Acres Chisel Plow is also magnetic-plate (interchangeable plates). "Magnet Razor" strings fold to the Stinger ONLY when the string/author context is the Everyday passaround (iamsms, IntroductionMajor655); a "magnet" note under another razor's field name belongs to that razor. The 2025 "loan wave" is retired: JoboozeRum's 2025 row is the Chisel Plow; the only 2025 Stinger rows are OnionMiasma Mar 7–21 (kept razor, not a second passaround).
