**[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]:** an audit that counts only one channel's branded rows can report "zero strings" for a product whose strings carry the other channel's brand. When a program has dual branding (Club VIP vs A&E retail), census the product name across ALL brand prefixes before reporting a zero — igord's "A&E - Singles" rows were invisible to a Club-branded count.
