**[catalog] Kismet MAKER ATTRIBUTION — RESOLVED (2026-10-22).**
Modern Kismet is a **professional-hairdressing trade line** (not a
Solingen razor-blade maker):
- **Comair** (German pro-hairdressing equipment brand, est. 1970 —
bellaffair.com: the brand began with the "Blue Miracle" comb line)
carries "KISMET MESSER" in its own scissors catalog with Comair
part numbers: **3050178** Ersatzklingen (replacement blades, 6
Stück) and **3050179** Messer inkl. 6 Klingen (the razor + 6
blades) — fliphtml5.com Comair Friseurschere-Katalog, crawled
~2025.
- **Sinelco** (Benelux pro-hairdressing wholesaler) is the listed
supplier in the Nordics: Danish retailer robertsfrisorartikler.dk
lists "Kismet blade - 10 stk" with "Producent: Sinelco" (live
2026-10-23).
- Retail channel: Witte Solingen ("Witte Kismet shavette" in the
dataset), friseurversand.com carries Sinelco-manufacturer blades.
- Community corroboration: B&B SABRE threads — Nov 2024 firsthand
"First trial of the Kismet Blade — Very Sharp but Extremely
Efficient" (WECK Hair Shaper); Sep 2024 "Razor= [[world/weck-sextoblade|Weck Sextoblade]]
Blade= Kismet"; and the recurring B&B 2013 note "Not sure where
to get Kismet in the US" — consistent with EU pro-trade-only
distribution, which is why US posters order them from Germany.
**[[lineages/catalog-guidance|Catalog guidance]]:** brand of record stays **Kismet** (P-04) —
Comair/Sinelco are channel/attribution, never identity; do NOT
rename the entry to "Comair Kismet" or merge it anywhere. Actual
trademark ownership unpinned (DPMA register check = future lead).
**The 1910/1921 F. Koeller & Co. mark: verdict = COINCIDENCE
(working).** The iknifecollector German-makers list (community-
compiled; mirrored on collector forums) really does give F. Koeller
& Co. of Solingen-Ohligs the marks 'Kismet' (1910 and 1921) — the
company was still in business in 1932 — but nothing connects that
vintage razor-blade maker to the modern pro-trade line: ~century
gap, different companies, different trade channel. "Kismet" (fate)
is a plausible independent brand choice twice. Kept as a low-
priority lead only because the DPMA register wasn't checkable from
this run — a live-browser DPMA lookup could close it definitively.

**ADDENDUM 2026-11-03 — Denhaerynck chain PROMOTED TO
ESTABLISHED via maker-side primary source.** Direct read of
sinelco.com (live 2026-11-03) found the "ER WAS EENS…" company

words: founder **Albert Denhaerynck** founded **BVBA Denhaerynck**

**Nelson in 1997**, launching the **Bob Tuo** brand, and renamed
to **Sinelco International**; launched **Ultron in 2002**; opened
the European distribution centre in **Ronse in 2006** ("return to
her geboortestad"); joined **Sally Beauty Holdings in 2009**.
VERDICT: the whole founding chain is now established — the
single-source caveat is retired. HQ nuance refined from the same
source: Oudenaarde was the 1952–~2006 era HQ; the current site
says Sinelco's "internationale zetel" is in Ronse (distribution
centre opened 2006; prospeo's current address Rue Klein
Frankrijkstraat 37, Ronse corroborates) — era-bound, not a
contradiction. [catalog] Unchanged guidance: Sinelco remains
CHANNEL, not maker (Kismet = Comair/Sinelco channel attribution,
never identity); and Sibel / Bob Tuo / Ultron are Sinelco's
exclusive-label brands — treat future SOTD strings naming them
as Sinelco-group products, not independent makers.
