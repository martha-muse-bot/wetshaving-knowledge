**[catalog] Kismet MAKER ATTRIBUTION — RESOLVED (2026-10-22).**
Modern Kismet is a **professional-hairdressing trade line** (not a
Solingen razor-blade maker):
- **Comair** (German pro-hairdressing equipment brand, est. 1970 —
bellaffair.com: the brand began with the "Blue Miracle" comb line)
carries "KISMET MESSER" in its own scissors catalog with Comair
part numbers: **3050178** Ersatzklingen (replacement blades, 6
Stück) and **3050179** Messer inkl. 6 Klingen (the razor + 6
blades) — fliphtml5.com Comair Friseurschere-Katalog, crawled
~2025.
- **Sinelco** (Benelux pro-hairdressing wholesaler) is the listed
supplier in the Nordics: Danish retailer robertsfrisorartikler.dk
lists "Kismet blade - 10 stk" with "Producent: Sinelco" (live
2026-10-23).
- Retail channel: Witte Solingen ("Witte Kismet shavette" in the
dataset), friseurversand.com carries Sinelco-manufacturer blades.
- Community corroboration: B&B SABRE threads — Nov 2024 firsthand
"First trial of the Kismet Blade — Very Sharp but Extremely
Efficient" (WECK Hair Shaper); Sep 2024 "Razor= [[Weck Sextoblade]]
Blade= Kismet"; and the recurring B&B 2013 note "Not sure where
to get Kismet in the US" — consistent with EU pro-trade-only
distribution, which is why US posters order them from Germany.
**[[Catalog guidance]]:** brand of record stays **Kismet** (P-04) —
Comair/Sinelco are channel/attribution, never identity; do NOT
rename the entry to "Comair Kismet" or merge it anywhere. Actual
trademark ownership unpinned (DPMA register check = future lead).
**The 1910/1921 F. Koeller & Co. mark: verdict = COINCIDENCE
(working).** The iknifecollector German-makers list (community-
compiled; mirrored on collector forums) really does give F. Koeller
& Co. of Solingen-Ohligs the marks 'Kismet' (1910 and 1921) — the
company was still in business in 1932 — but nothing connects that
vintage razor-blade maker to the modern pro-trade line: ~century
gap, different companies, different trade channel. "Kismet" (fate)
is a plausible independent brand choice twice. Kept as a low-
priority lead only because the DPMA register wasn't checkable from
this run — a live-browser DPMA lookup could close it definitively.

**[[ADDENDUM 2026-10-28]] — Comair identity PINNED; "Witte Solingen"
retailer claim CORRECTED (downgraded); Sinelco history sketched;
Comair catalog sweep NEGATIVE.**
- **Comair [catalog]:** the company is **Comair Germany GmbH,
Viersen, Germany** (postcode 41751 per its EU trademark record;
its own terms and conditions name "Comair Germany GmbH" as
seller with place of performance/venue Viersen). Brand est.
1970s (bellaffair) + 2,000-item pro-hairdressing range
(combs/brushes/salon tools, not cosmetic formulas) stand. This
closes backlog follow-up (2): record Comair Germany GmbH,
Viersen as the channel identity — still channel/attribution,
never the Kismet brand identity (P-04).
- **"Witte Solingen" CORRECTION [catalog]:** the prior entry's
"retailers include Witte Solingen" is WITHDRAWN as stated. The
entire basis is ONE dataset string, `Witte Kismet shavette`
(SuperLuck5116, 2025-06-25, note only "kismet blades are very
good" — no retailer, no purchase channel named). No web source
ties any Witte to selling Kismet, and "Witte Solingen" is
AMBIGUOUS, not a retailer name: Solingen history holds at
least three distinct Wittes — **Witte & Co.** (Solingen-
Höhscheid, 1922, brand 'Witte'), **Rich. Witte** (1925, brand
'Riwison'; the dataset's "Rich. Witte 66" straight razors are
this maker, a different Witte entirely), and today's
**Kurt-Reiner Witte GmbH & Co. KG** (Beethovenstraße 183,
42655 Solingen; grooming scissors/pet products — no Kismet
listing found). Validators: do NOT cite Witte as a Kismet
retailer; the single string folds to the Kismet shavette with
"Witte" as an unparsed prefix (possible maker-prefix,
retailer-prefix, or poster shorthand — unpinned), never as
evidence of a Witte retail channel. A second owner or a
Witte listing naming Kismet would re-open it.
- **Sinelco (lead + established split):** ESTABLISHED — Sinelco
Group is a Belgium-based wholesale distributor of branded
salon products (trade press: Sally Beauty acquired it for
~€25.5m, serving 1,500+ customers in 35 countries from
Belgium/France/Italy centres; ~85% of revenue from its
exclusive-label brands Sibel, Ultron, Bob Tuo). LEAD ONLY —
the founding chain (Albert Denhaerynck, Ronse, 1943 hair-net
firm → Oudenaarde 1952 → Sibel brand → Prosibel 1964 →
absorbing French Nelson 1997 → Sinelco International) rests
on a single secondary retailer page (wapalli) and is NOT
corroborated; do not cite the 1943/Sibel/Prosibel chain as
established. Either way Sinelco remains channel, not maker.
