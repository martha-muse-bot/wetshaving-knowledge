**[catalog] "SIR" = "Sir".** Dataset strings "SIR - Irisch Moos",
"SIR Irisch Moos", "Sir Irisch Moos", "Sir – Irisch Moos" are
one product line, not a "SIR" sub-brand — the brand's own styling
is "Sir Irisch Moos" and posters abbreviate it. Fold all of them.
