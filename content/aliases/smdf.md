**"SMdF" / "SMDF" = RazoRock Santa Maria del Fiore shaving soap
(established, catalog merge 2026-10-23).** The wetshaving abbreviation
for RazoRock's Santa Maria del Fiore "Firenze" sapone da barba; retailer
vshod.com lists the product as "RazoRock Santa Maria Del Fiore ( SMDF )".
Dataset usage concentrates 2016–2018 (343 of 360 "smdf" hits across all
matched years). Bare "SMdF"/"SMDF" folded as aliases of verified
soap/razorock-santa-maria-del-fiore (Q045134). NOTE: Santa Maria Novella
(SMN) is a different maker — do not expand "SMdF" to Novella.
