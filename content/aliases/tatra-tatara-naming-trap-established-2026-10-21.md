**Tatra ≠ Tatara (naming trap, established 2026-10-21)**: TATRA Platinum is
Czech Blades s.r.o.'s house brand (maker page czechblades.cz). TATARA blades
are Tatara Razors' (Portugal) own branded DE blades (maker page
tatararazors.com/product/tatara-blades; user-supplied 2026-10-21). Two real
brands, near-identical names — never merge or reassign on string resemblance;
a "Tatara" string is the Tatara product unless evidence says otherwise.
**"Matura" = misspoken Tatara (established 2026-10-30):** Sharpologist's
Wet Shaving Talk 2026-04-06 episode says the Parker AeroShave "looks
like the Matura razor from Spain or Portugal" — but his own written
AeroShave review (2026-02-19) names the same comparison as "Tatara
razors," and B&B independently says the AeroShave "looks a lot like
the Tatara Masamune" (Jan 2026). Ta-ta-ra → Ma-tu-ra is a speech
slur; no "Matura" razor exists anywhere in wetshaving, and zero
"Matura" strings appear in any SOTD structured field. [catalog]:
never create a "Matura" entry; a future "Matura" string folds to the
Tatara Masamune (loose, podcast context only).
[catalog] **Corroboration added 2026-10-22 (Astra-geography run):** B&B
thread 422882 names Tatra among the Jevíčko remnant's brands — Czech Blades
s.r.o. (the company that kept the old Astra/Gillette carbon-blade plant
after stainless production moved to Russia in 2004) sells Tiger, Tatra,
Leon, and Sokol, "mostly sold in Africa."
