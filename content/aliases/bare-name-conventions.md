**[catalog] Bare-name conventions** (community usage, catalog-applied):
bare "Astra" = Superior Platinum (green); bare "Minora" = Minora
Stainless; bare "Wilkinson Sword" = the Edgewell Classic unless the
post says Gillette; bare "Feather" in DE context = Hi-Stainless.
