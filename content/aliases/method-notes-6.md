**[[aliases/method-notes|[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]s]]:** (1) A same-handle pre/post Wayback pair settles "reformulation vs relabel" directly — compare the ingredient SET, not the naming style; INCI rewriting alone is not a reformulation, and a split "Emulsifying Wax" into two INCI components is the same ingredient. (2) When a re-publish wave touches two product types in one day, check BOTH — here the balm was a relabel and the oil a real reformulation inside the same event. (3) A scent-level "reformulated" note carried across all formats of one scent is a fragrance change, not a base change — read which noun the note attaches to. (4) A "no X yet" watch verdict must be checked against the FULL catalog, not the current-year wave — the Deck the Balls soap existed since Dec 2025 and was missed by looking only at 2026-created items.
