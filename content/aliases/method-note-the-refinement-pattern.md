**Method note (the refinement pattern):** a poster who says "it was called
X before they rebranded it as Y" is testifying to BRAND succession, not
formula sameness. The 2008 B&B side-by-side (scent, ingredients, lather
all differ) is the stronger identity evidence. "Rebrand" claims from
community notes default to brand-level truth, formula-level LEAD —
verify against a dated side-by-side or the maker's own rename notice
before asserting same-product. Recorded 2026-10-30.
