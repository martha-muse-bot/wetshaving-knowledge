**[[aliases/method-note|Method note]]:** the backlog premise ("single author each") was an artifact of counting spellings, not products — when a mystery is framed as "X vs Y, one author each", census the PRODUCT (all spelling variants, product fields only) before theorizing two products; author continuity across spellings (Aresmsu, 3 days apart) settles it faster than any web search. The maker's products.json then confirmed the canonical spelling in one read, including the negative (zero "Success" products) — a complete-store read turns an absence into a finding.
