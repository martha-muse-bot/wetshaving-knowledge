[catalog] **Brand-of-record rule for the line:** label brand is **[[glossary/the-club|The Club]]**, maker A&E is the attribute (P-04) — tub reads 'THE CLUB BLACK'. Parallel A&E-branded entries for the same scents (Novelist, Grecian Horse) are the retail branding; never merge Club↔A&E entries on a name match alone. "The Club" is also the store's branding ("The Back Room", membership) — a bare "The Club" string with no scent is a channel reference, never a product.
