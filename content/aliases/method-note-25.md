*Confidence: unknown · Source: [[aliases|Product aliases]]*

**[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]:** a collapsed CDX query (`collapse=urlkey`) can silently return EMPTY for URLs that uncollapsed queries then prove have captures — this run: boca-negra + amber-rose returned zero collapsed on the Club, 11 and 70 captures uncollapsed. Never log a collapsed empty as "never captured"; re-run uncollapsed. Also: a Wayback `id_` capture that parses as zero product links may be gzip-compressed (HoM rule) — AND a capture can be the wrong page entirely (the 2025-11-05 aegrooming.com/products capture is the /collections index, a JS shell with no product data). Check the title/og:url before concluding anything from a capture.
