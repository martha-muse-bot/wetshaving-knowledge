**B&B auto-censors "A.S.S." — read "***" as Astra Superior Stainless
in B&B quotes.** B&B 637908 #32 (Sept 2023): "I'm an *** man myself,
or at least I was before the war" — the asterisks are the forum's
profanity filter eating the A.S.S. initialism (confirmed by #33:
"Apparently I can't abbreviate Astra Superior Stainless here, as it
is a naughty word"). The same initialism appears UNcensored in SOTD
strings ("Astra Superior Stainless (A.S.S.)" ×5, r/Wetshaving). When
mining B&B text for Astra SS mentions, "***" in a blade context is a
censored A.S.S., not a redaction — never drop the hit.
