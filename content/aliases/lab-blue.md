*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product aliases]]*

**[catalog] "Lab Blue"** is the community name for Personna Comfort
Coated DE blades. **Personna Med Prep is a SEPARATE product** (same
Verona blade + final cleaning step; now sold under AccuThrive) — the
community treats and prices them differently, so they are never merged.
Source: catalog homework + user ruling 2026-10-20. Established.
**Nickname origin ESTABLISHED 2026-10-31:** coined by B&B posters
buying the blue-boxed blades from a hospital/medical-lab supplier
under a separate "lab use" catalog number (B&B 658492 p.2); "Comfort
Coated" was always the maker/retail name. Note: one Oct-2026 voice
(B&B 673805 p.18) claims CC ≠ Lab Blue as blades — single anecdote,
not followed (see [[Lineages]] dissent note).
