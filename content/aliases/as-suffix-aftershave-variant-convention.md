[catalog] **"AS" suffix = aftershave-variant convention:** "SV Tundra Artica AS" (40u), "T&S Sicily AS" (31u+14u), "WK Tempest AS" (14u), "CB Blugère AS" (17u), "T+S Morning Coffee in the Canadian Wilderness AS" (21u), "DG Sweet Lemon AS" (50u), "B&M Leviathan AS" (36u). These denote the maker's AFTERSHAVE, never the soap — match to the aftershave entry (Sicily/Tempest/Blugere all have verified aftershave entries; T&S Morning Coffee AS has no aftershave entry yet — gap noted). Never collapse AS-suffixed strings into the soap entry.
