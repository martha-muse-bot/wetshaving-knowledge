**[catalog] "HEMA" DE blades = private-label Personna made in Israel**
(learner run 2026-11-01; resolves the backlog's "[[Hema blades]]" mystery
and the struck-through blanket claim in the Oct-24 entry). HEMA is the
Dutch department-store chain (est. 1926) that sold its own-branded
10-pack DE blades; the manufacturer is **Personna (American Safety
Razor / Edgewell), Nazareth Israel plant** — four independent
community voices spanning 2009–2026: B&B 112995 p.2 (2009, "I think
the Hemas are re branded Personnas"), B&B 308102 (~2013, Dutch
tester: "I have read here on B&B that it is made in Israel by
Personna"), B&B User:Sabre's collection wiki ("Hema x2
(ASR/Personna/Bond - Israel)" — pack-origin classification, grouped
with his Tesco blades in the same private-label slot), and **Greyzer's
own SOTD note 2026-03-22: "Hema blades (rebranded Personna)"**
(firsthand). Dataset blade scope: `Hema blade` ×2 (handoverthecoffee,
2017/2019), blade-field `HEMA` (VenkHeerman, 2023-10-20, budget-shave
theme), `Hema blades (rebranded Personna)` (Greyzer, 2026-03-22).
Catalog: HEMA gets its OWN blade entry (P-04 — brand of record is the
HEMA retail brand; Personna Israel/Nazareth as manufacture attribute),
never folded into Personna Reds or Crystal — the exact Israeli variant
(platinum chrome "reds" vs Super+/Crystal) is unpinned. Era note:
pre-Nazareth-closure stock (Edgewell shuttered the plant ~2022–2023),
so 2024+ uses are NOS/destash. Method caution parked with this find:
the 2013 tester separately guessed an Egyptian (Lord) factory origin
from dullness alone — retired; dullness is not factory evidence.
