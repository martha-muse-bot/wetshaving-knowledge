[catalog] **VERDICT: the buyout is TRUE IN SUBSTANCE, WRONG AS FRAMED.** Minora WAS an old South African blade brand Gillette acquired — two independent B&B voices agree (B&B 598248 #17: "Minora was an old South African brand that Gillette acquired... Gillette started manufacturing operations in South Africa in 1954"; "Around the World in 80 Blades" journal, Blade #48: "before Gillette bought the brand, and were made in Gillette's factory there in the 1950s"). It was a BLADE brand, acquired in the 1950s era — NOT a 1930s–40s razor company, and NOT the maker of NEW long-comb variants. The Minora-stamped English NEW razors are Gillette-made razors carrying the Minora brand stamp (same regional-stamp pattern as the Polish "Grom" NEW). The source note is VisceralWatch, 2020-06-24 (the backlog's "2022" dating was wrong) — his "one of the many razor companies that Gillette absorbed back in the day" conflates Gillette's real 1930s absorptions (Autostrop/Probak 1930, Rotbart 1926–27) with the 1950s Minora brand acquisition. Exact acquisition date unpinned (by the 1950s at latest).
