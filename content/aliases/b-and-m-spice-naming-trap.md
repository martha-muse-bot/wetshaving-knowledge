*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product aliases]]*

**B&M "Spice" naming trap** [catalog] (researched 2026-10-21):
[[Barrister and Mann]] has sold exactly ONE "Spice" shaving soap.
2017–2021 it was **Barrister's Reserve Spice** (Reserve base);
from ~2022 it is the same product in the Omnibus base, renamed
simply **"Spice"** by the maker (same product page/URL, same
scent copy). Plain dataset strings "B&M Spice" / "Barrister and
Mann Spice" are shorthand for it in BOTH eras — dataset mining
(sotd_2016–2026_full.json, soap field): zero B&M Spice strings
in 2016; 2017–2021 posters write "Reserve Spice" ~93% of the
time (426 explicit vs a handful of plain); from 2022 the plain
form takes over (2024: 81 plain vs 18 explicit; 49 strings
explicitly tagged "(Omnibus)"). There is NO pre-Reserve or
regular-line "Spice" — do not split by era or by string form.
Distinct products NOT to merge into it: **Winter Spice**
(Mar 2013 standard-line scent, sweet orange/amaretto/cinnamon,
discontinued — dataset has ~6 strings), **Stirling Spice**
(Stirling Soap Co.), and Old Spice itself. Confidence:
established. Sources: Daily Lather B&M history + 2017-06-09
review; barristerandmann.com live product page (fetched
2026-10-21); dataset mining.
