**Amazon's "Burn The Ships" ≠ Hoffman's "Burn the Ships".**
The Amazon brand "Burn The Ships" sells "Inferno" shave soap
(goat milk; Himalayan cedar/vetiver/grapefruit/frankincense/
myrrh/lavender/cassia/clove per its own product copy) — a
separate men's-grooming brand that adopted the phrase as its
NAME. Hoffman's scent = Shulton Old Spice dupe. Cross-brand
fold is forbidden; record as a false friend (cf. the Florena/
Nivea caution pattern).
