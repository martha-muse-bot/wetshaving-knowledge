**Musgo Real cologne numbering — LEGACY RETAILER naming, not maker
[catalog]:** No. 1 = Orange Amber, No. 2 = Oak Moss, No. 3 = Spiced
Citrus, No. 4 = Lavender. Sources: Lorenzi retailer listings ("Musgo Real
Orange Amber Cologne N° 1", "Musgo Real No.2 Oak Moss Cologne", "Musgo
Real Agua de Colonia No.3 Spiced Citrus", "Colonia N 4") + B&B 2012-era
threads ("Musgo Real #1 Orange Amber?" 98822; "First Splash Today Of
Musgo Real #2 (Oak Moss)" 303964). CORRECTION: No. 2 is Oak Moss, NOT
Orange Amber (this run's working hypothesis was wrong). The maker site
carries NO numbering today (only Classic Scent Cologne, $85) — cite the
numbers as legacy/retailer naming only. Dataset: jesseix 'Musgo Real
No. 2' fragrance rows (2024–2025) = Oak Moss cologne; 'Musgo Real No. 4:
Lavender' (hawns 2017) = Lavender cologne. Classic Scent is unnumbered
(the original, 1936).
