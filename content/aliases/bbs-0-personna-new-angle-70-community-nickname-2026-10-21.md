*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product aliases]]*

**[catalog] "BBS-0" = Personna "New Angle 70" (community nickname,
2026-10-21).** Real vintage English Personna DE razor (1960s);
nicknamed "BBS-0"/"BBS Zero" because it is reported to be an
inspiration for the BBS-1. Dataset strings collapse: `Personna -
New Angle 70 (BBS-0)`, `Personna "BBS-0"`, `Personna BBS-0` are all
the one product (336 razor uses, 12 authors, 2020–2026). AMBIGUITY
WARNING: a BARE "BBS-0" without "Personna" attached is ambiguous —
it also reads as a hypothetical pre-BBS-1 Wolfman product; never
auto-apply without the Personna qualifier. Source: Sharpologist
podcast 2022-03-02 + dataset mining. Established (identity). Full
entry in [[Hardware lore]].
**[[CORRECTION 2026-10-26]] (supersedes the "inspiration" rationale
above, not the identity):** the Personna did NOT inspire the BBS-1 —
that was a single hedged 2021 B&B post's gloss on the nickname.
Sharpologist's 2018 BBS-1 history (the article that coined
"BBS-0") names the Baili BR131 — a Gillette Tech knock-off — as
THE inspiration razor, with the Tech as the root; the Personna's
documented relation to the BBS-1 is similar dimensions + parts
interchangeability only. Keep using "BBS-0" as the community alias;
never cite an inspiration link. Full correction in [[Hardware lore]].
