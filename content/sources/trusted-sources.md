A store product page from any source listed here counts as PRIMARY evidence
(maker pages likewise). Check these before open-web searching; attribution
questions especially should start at Maggard. Sources NOT listed as trusted —
wetshavingdatabase.com / wsdb.pages.dev, trythatsoap, Fragrantica, Parfumo,
the nmrs catalog — are leads/corroboration only, never evidence.

## Tier 1 — user-endorsed (Jim, 2026-10-21 unless noted)

- **Maggard Razors** — maggardrazors.com. The attribution gold standard:
  product pages carry reliable brand attribution across a wide artisan range.
  Check FIRST for brand/product attribution questions. (Its Ted Pella listing
  verified that entry, 2026-10-21.)
- **Razor Emporium** — razoremporium.com. Modern + vintage razors and blades;
  strong on razor model identification. Caveat: some lookups are effectively
  browser-only — if a fetch fails, log "needs parent browser check."
- **The Razor Company** — therazorcompany.com. Broad modern catalog, good
  blade coverage (its Tatra Platinum listing is a project source).
- **West Coast Shaving** — westcoastshaving.com. Long-standing retailer;
  broad brand range, reliable product pages.
- **Pasteur Shaving** — pasteurshaving.com (user-endorsed 2026-10-21). The
  NYC institution; very deep catalog across mainstream and artisan brands.
- **Top of the Chain** — topofthechain.ca (user-endorsed 2026-10-21).
  Canadian retailer with a strong artisan soap/aftershave range — a good
  first check for North American artisan lines.
- **BullGoose Shaving** — bullgooseshaving.com (user-endorsed 2026-10-21).
  Long-standing US retailer; broad hardware and software range.
- **Razor Blades Club** — razorbladesclub.com (user, 2026-10-20). The blade
  specialist: international blade product pages found nowhere else. Caveat:
  its own site search needs the live browser (parent-only task); individual
  product pages are poorly indexed.

## Tier 2 — proven in this project

- **Maker stores generally** — a maker's own product page is the strongest
  single source (e.g. timelessrazor.com for Timeless geometry;
  czechblades.cz for Tatra/Tiger; stirlingsoap.com). Defunct makers (Mickey
  Lee, Cold River) fall back to retailer pages here + Wayback captures.
- **Italian Barber** — italianbarber.com. RazoRock's store; settled the
  RazoRock BBS question. Caveat: old product URLs age out (a 2014 Supersteel
  link 404s) — navigate from the current catalog, don't trust old links.
- **SalonCentric** — saloncentric.com. Salon-channel source; its dual-branded
  Fromm/Personna listing settled the Fromm merge (§A1).
- **Naked Armor** — nakedarmor.com. Retailer whose standalone Parker Injector
  blade page carried that entry's verification (§A4) when the maker's own
  store only listed a bundle.
- **Gentle Shave** — gentleshave.com. Zenith brush code chart settled the
  Zenith brush line.

## Community sources (corroboration / identity color, NOT primary evidence)

- **Badger & Blade** and **DFS forums** — tester consensus, sharpness indexes,
  pack-photo threads. Forum pack photos CAN qualify as community-photo
  evidence when the pack is clearly identifiable; forum *claims* are leads.
- **Daily Lather** — brand histories (B&M Reserve timeline; Mickey Lee
  closure). History color, not product evidence.
- **Sharpologist** — hands-on reviews; useful geometry/product color
  (Timeless ALSB45), secondary only.
