*Status: open · Source: [[Open questions]]*

Enders residuals (new, 2026-10-28, spun off the Speed Shaver
resolution): (1) Speed Razor (1930s) vs Speed Shaver (1940s)
head geometry — is there any documented difference beyond
the square handle end, or is it one design with two names?
(2) 1935-succession vs 1940-merger date conflict — pin
against a primary source (corporate record / period ad
naming Durham-Enders) before citing either year flatly.
(3) Enders blade generations — greyhound-etched vs unetched:
which is earlier, and does the etching track the
Simmons-era vs Durham-Enders-era production? (4) jrhunter25's
single Enders Speed Shaver post — second-owner context
check (does he also run Proline B-20, or NOS Enders blades?).
(5) The "Enders by Christy" pre-lawsuit razors — do any
dataset strings name Christy, and do they take the same
Speed blade?
**RESOLVED 2026-10-28 ([[Hardware lore]] Enders addendum +
[[Product aliases]] three-way split) [catalog]:** NO dataset
string says "Enders by Christy". The 3 dataset "Christy"
strings (all luvmy07subie 2021) are Christy Co.'s OWN
Massage Bar SE (Fremont OH; 1924 massage-bar patent;
4.5 in, 41 g, open comb, lather catcher, rivets) taking
half-DE/injector HACK blades — never Enders family, never
an Enders blade. Separately, B&B 466447 #1 (firsthand)
establishes the pre-lawsuit Enders-by-Christy razors DO
take Speed Razor blades — so a future "Enders by Christy"
string folds to the Speed family; bare "Christy" does not.
(6) [["Enders' Dollar Razor"]] (Jimtasticness 2020–2021, with
an "Enders' razor blade") — a distinct Enders model or a
nickname for the Speed razor?
**RESOLVED 2026-10-28 — DISTINCT MODEL, same blade system
([[Hardware lore]] Enders addendum) [catalog]:** the ONLY
structured Dollar post is Jimtasticness, 2020-06-29
thread (razor "Enders' Dollar Razor", blade "Enders'
razor blade"; note: from u/fuckchalzone, injector possible
with a mod, but he shaved with a NOS Enders blade).
PRECISION vs the earlier bullet: Jimtasticness 2021-06-29
is a generic "The Enders single edge" post, not a second
Dollar post — one Dollar post + one generic Enders post,
same author. (A same-run misattribution to Ythin, from a
chunking error that picked up the next post's author
field, was caught by a late full scan + direct
author-field read and corrected in [[Hardware lore]].)
Externally: cased Dollar Razor (Proxibid #597 lot 150,
NYC imprint, signature engraving), auction lots listing
Speed & Dollar separately, and a Feb 28, 1931 Collier's
ad ("trade a dollar for an Enders razor and six [blades]",
105 W. 40th St NYC) — Dollar = early-1930s $1 price-point
model, pre-Mystic era. Catalog: fold Dollar strings to
the Enders family with Dollar as model attribute; never
into a "Speed Shaver" name; Dollar-vs-Speed head geometry
still unpinned. NEW LEADS spun off: (a) Dollar-vs-Speed
head comparison (photo/measurements — is Dollar a
cheaper Speed head or its own tooling?); (b) the 1985–86
Plane Talk note claiming an "Enders Dollar Razor by
Enders Sales Company, New York" — distributor imprint vs
Enders Razor Co., or collector confusion with the
St. Louis WM. Enders/Oak Leaf tool line?; (c) what blade
did the Christy Massage Bar SE take originally
("Christy-style blades" per the Price patent note) — a
Christy-blade dimensions note for [[Hardware lore]].
(7) "Last Shadow's injector
blade adapter" (esa1975 2022) — a community adapter maker
for Enders, worth a people/hardware note. (8) luvmy07subie
2021 pairs a Speed Razor with Gillette Permasharps / "1/2
Permasharp" — modified DE blade, mis-tag, or a different
razor?
**RESOLVED 2026-10-28 — DOCUMENTED MOD ([[Hardware lore]]
Enders addendum) [catalog]:** 2021-06-16 + 2021-09-15
posts; his June note says it outright — a Matt Hilton
tribute mod, 1/2 DE blade cut with kitchen scissors,
"never seen someone use one this way". Second Speed
workaround alongside Proline B-20; a DE blade field on a
Speed post is this mod, never native DE fit. (9)
