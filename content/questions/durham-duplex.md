**RESOLVED 2026-10-28 ([[Hardware lore]] Enders section)
[catalog]:** Enders is its OWN family with its OWN
proprietary blade — Speed razor c. 1930, Enders Razor Co.
(Simmons/Christy patents, US1791552/53), one-piece design,
blade 37.37 mm edge / 44 mm back / 15 mm deep per scanner
measurement, vs the 63.5 mm Durham blade: dimensionally
incompatible, full stop. Durham-Duplex succeeded to Enders'
business/rights Mar 13, 1935, Durham-Enders Corp by 1947
(B&B wiki; a Shaveden-derived page says merger 1940 —
caveated, B&B timeline preferred), but the blade system
stayed Enders'. Modern use = Schick Proline B-20 injector
blades propped by a dulled original Enders blade — exactly
what the dataset shows: 73 "Enders Speed Shaver" mentions,
72 by 120inna55 (2019–2021 + 1 in 2024) + 1 by jrhunter25,
all as notes-template posts pairing Speed Shaver with
Proline B-20, never a Durham blade. Catalog: own Enders
entry (none exists yet); these posts' blade field is
Injector, not Durham Duplex; "Durham-Enders" is the
corporate-era brand slot, not a format claim. Speed Razor
(1930s) vs Speed Shaver (1940s) stays ONE family, model as
attribute (blade interchange mostly confirmed, narrow-bed
variance noted). (c) Du Barry / Dorset / T-Type hoe-type
models — dataset scope check;
**RESOLVED 2026-10-28 (same [[Hardware lore]] section)
