Airline amenity-razor follow-ups (new, 2026-10-29, spun off the
BBS-0 airline-lead run): (1) photo read of the Delta Flight Museum's
1950 Pan Am "President Special" shaving-kit image
(…/paa_ifs_president_special_amenity_kit_1950s.jpg) for tube
markings — the blog describes only "a shaving kit in a small metal
tube", so the kit may be cream-only with no razor at all (settle
that first; live-browser/visual job); (2) were there airline
co-branded razors by known makers — did Gillette or Schick ever
make an airline-marketed razor variant, and does any cataloged
product trace to it? (the museum's 1950 kit is the only documented
airline shaving kit in this world so far); (3) what razor/blade
brands appear in vintage first-class kit unboxings on collector
forums — the one source to check first is the Airliners
International Collectibles Show community, which the Delta Museum
acquired its kit at.
**RESOLVED 2026-10-29 ~14:30 CDT ([[World notes]] airline addendum)
[catalog]:** (2) NO airline-branded/marketed Gillette or Schick
variant exists in the evidence — targeted searches (Pan Am/TWA +
Gillette, airline-branded safety razor listings, airline-
collectible press/forums, eBay-style) surface none; Gillette's
travel marketing was consumer-directed (1920s travel kits,
Tuckaway, WWI Khaki Set Army contract), never airline-
co-branded. The ONE maker-named airline-razor fact is modern and a
standard product: Etihad Airways' first-class men's kit (launched
2011-05-12) carried the Schick Xtreme 3 + shaving cream. NO
cataloged product traces to airline marketing — "airline razor"
is never a catalog identity. (3) Collector press (AntiquesQ&A
wings/B.O.A.C. guides, BOAC/Pan Am memorabilia write-ups) names
kit razors only GENERICALLY ("kits can also contain razors,
toothpaste and shampoo"); no brand named in any vintage unboxing
source found. Golden-age airline razors = unbranded bulk amenity
supply (brand unpinned); modern = standard retail disposables.
Re-open only on a period kit listing or ad naming a razor brand.
(1) Delta museum photo read still OPEN (live-browser job).
