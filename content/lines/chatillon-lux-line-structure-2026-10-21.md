**[[Chatillon Lux]] line structure (2026-10-21).** Three distinct lines:
(1) **Provisions** — the shaving line (toners, aftershaves, EdTs in
shaving-adjacent scents; 2015–2020 Maher-made, 2020–2026 DG-made, now
ended — see [[Lineages]]). (2) **Parfums** — pure-fragrance EdT line
(La Petite Prairie, Treget, Weinstrasse, Confluence, Rose Santal,
Lost Valley, Admiral, Envoi, Can't Stand the Rain, Sunrise on
LaSalle), sold as 2oz/$70 with 2mL samples; as of Oct 2026 four EdTs
carry "...To Be Discontinued" titles — LEAD: is the whole Parfums
line migrating to [[Maher Olfactive]]? (3) **Side Project Series** —
limited fragrance releases (Biblio, 3SHEETS, Nefertiti; Biblio made
CaFleureBon 2019 best-of lists). PLUS the separate
**Maher Olfactive** line (own site; vetiver/tuberose accord and
"Red Skies" in the works per Fragrantica 2026). Catalog implication:
a "Chatillon Lux X" scent name can exist as BOTH a shaving toner/AS
(Provisions) and an EdT (Parfums) — format disambiguates, never
assume the form from the scent name alone. [catalog] Established
(line names from maker's own copy + DFS quotes; Maher Olfactive
separate site).
