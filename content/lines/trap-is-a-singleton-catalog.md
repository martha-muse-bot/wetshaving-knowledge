**Trap is a SINGLETON [catalog]:** exactly ONE soap-field row in the
whole 241,569-row corpus carries a trumpers.com URL — the K1986 GFT row
itself (2020-06-04, "[Geo F. Trumper Soft Shaving Cream]
(https://www.trumpers.com/products/fragrance-ranges/gft/
gft-soft-shaving-cream/)"), routing to
`soap/geo-f-trumper-eucris-shaving-cream` by subset-rev. There are NO
other maker-URL-linked cream strings in the dataset. Corpus-wide
trumpers.com URLs: 2 rows total — the soap row + K1986's fragrance-field
"GeoF. Trumper Lavender Water" (same post, honest unmatched to the
unverified cluster, not a mis-route).
