**Aion Skincare-era products (post-rename, ~2024+, see [[Lineages]]
[catalog]).** Skincare line: **Nourishing Balm** (2023 breakout
product — dataset post-shave fields explode with it; maker DFS post
links the groomingdept.com product page), **Hydrating Gel**
(ingredient listing "Grooming Department Aion Skincare Hydrating
Gel", inkeedecoder, 2023), **Botanical Toner** (dataset 2025–2026).
**New soap base: Maxima** (ESTABLISHED 2026-10-27, supersedes the
lead above). The Aion-era tallow base, launched ~Nov 24, 2023
(Iris as the first release, Black Friday timing — Leisureguy
Feb 2024: "first released one year ago, on November 24"; DFS
2023-11-11 already anticipating the "new AION Skincare Maxima
base" as a refinement of Kairos SE 2023). Formula (Leisureguy,
quoting the maker list): aloe vera juice, green tea extract,
illipe/cupuacu/tucuma/murumuru butters, **lamb tallow**, emu oil,
sacha inchi oil, ceramides (NP/AP/EOP), lanolin — a long
skincare-heavy list distinct from every earlier GD base.
At the Black Friday 2024 sale it was the outgoing base:
Leisureguy reports "the last Maxima soap that will be made
with tallow (he's switching to a vegan formula for Maxima)"
alongside one Mallard soap, with all other sale soaps in the
new Optimus formula. **RESOLVED 2026-10-27 — the promised
vegan Maxima DID ship, but as ONE product, not a line
conversion (maker site, live-browser check 2026-10-27):**
"Aion Skincare Maxima Vegan Unscented Shaving Soap - Tacit"
(6.5 oz, sold out, $0.00 listing; product image labeled
"TACIT / MAXIMA - VEGAN"). The standard Maxima line stayed
tallow — the maker Formulas page describes Maxima as lamb
tallow + duck fat + emu oil — and the current shaving-soap
collection (11 products, all sold out, homepage banner
"Optimus — Evolved formula. Improved performance.") is
Optimus-only. So the BF 2024 picture resolves three ways,
not one: tallow Maxima continued as legacy listings, ONE
vegan Maxima (Tacit, unscented) shipped, and Optimus became
the house standard. Catalog rule [catalog]: Tacit is its OWN
scent+base product (vegan Maxima, unscented); never fold it
into tallow Maxima, and never read "vegan Maxima" as a
whole-line conversion. Maker-site Maxima scent list
(all sold out, $29–29.99): Wonderland, Lavender, After The
Fire, Unum, Iris, KAI, Vetiver, Tobacco Oud, Ténèbres,
Trésor (+ Tacit vegan unscented) — note "Vetiver" here vs
dataset "Vetyver" (spelling variant, same scent family).
Dataset: ZERO "Maxima" soap-field strings through Oct 2026
(word-boundary scan 2026-10-27; the earlier substring hits
were prose "maximal"), and ZERO notes combining "Maxima"
with "vegan"/"Optimus" — posters do not tag Maxima the way
they tag Kairos/Mallard, so Maxima-era scents will usually
need dating by release, not by a base tag. RESIDUAL LEADS:
(a) Tacit's ingredient list (the maker page shown had no
ingredient list — vegan status rests on title + label image,
not ingredients); (b) 2019 "Grooming Dept Tacit" soap strings
(2 hits: MentholMurph 2019-04-09, LOCO_X 2019-06-12, no base
tag) — is Aion Tacit a carry-forward of that 2019 scent, or a
name reuse for a new unscented product? Do NOT fold the 2019
strings into the vegan Maxima Tacit entry until the base is
pinned. [catalog]
**Optimus** (ESTABLISHED 2026-10-27 — answers the backlog's
"base vs line name" question: it is a BASE, and it is in the
maker product titles). The Aion-era soap formula that succeeded
Maxima as the house standard at the Black Friday 2024 sale
(Leisureguy Dec 2024: Pomp "uses the new Optimus formula",
vs his older Donkeybase Pomp; same month's sale report: "all
the other soaps are the new Optimus formula" once the last
tallow Maxima and one Mallard are excepted). Maker-page
evidence: "Aion Skincare Optimus Shaving Soap - Reverence"
(aionskincare.com, live 2026-10-27, $24.99, sold out, 5 oz).
Optimus formula (Reverence page ingredient list) is visibly
NOT Maxima: beef tallow (not lamb), cupuacu butter, kukui
nut oil, pumpkin seed oil, goat milk, silk amino acids,
oatmeal flour, slippery elm — no ceramides, no emu oil, no
sacha inchi. Catalog rule [catalog]: Maxima-era and
Optimus-era versions of the same scent are DIFFERENT
scent+base products (same rule as Kairos/Mallard); never
fold Optimus strings into Maxima, and never read "Optimus"
in a maker title as a line name. **RESOLVED 2026-10-27 (see Maxima entry):
the promised vegan Maxima shipped as ONE product (Tacit, vegan unscented),
NOT as a line conversion — Optimus is the house standard that succeeded
tallow Maxima, and Tacit is the separate vegan-Maxima product.** Current
maker collection (2026-10-27, all sold out): 11 Optimus soaps — Tentazione,
Kölnisch, Tidal, Chypre Muguet, etereo, Spa, Foin Coupé, Jasmine,
Barbershop Americana, Bergamot, Clarus (tallow unscented) — plus search-only
Optimus titles Aion, POMP, Tuberose, Mandarin Bay, Cedarwood, Fleur d'Iris,
Reverence. Optimus example ingredient list (Tentazione, maker page):
beef tallow, cupuaçu, babassu, kukui, pumpkin seed, goat milk, silk amino
acids — tallow-based, NOT vegan; never confuse Optimus with the vegan Maxima.
