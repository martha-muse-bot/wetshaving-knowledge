*Confidence: established · Source: [[lines|Product lines]]*

**Grooming Dept base system (2026-10-22).** The "lines" ARE the bases —
catalog-relevant because posters treat scent×base as the product
identity (see the base-as-identity backlog item). Community-reported
bases (B&B GD club threads, DFS discussions — all community-sourced,
ranked by posters for post-shave): **Vegan** (2016-17 era, "100% palm
free" tester); **Tallow & Duck Fat** (early Before the Fire era);
**Mallard** (duck fat); **Janus** (tallow + vegan variants); **Lusso**
(first seen on Coattails); **Donkey Milk** incl. the "Donkey Combo
Base" (donkey milk + duck fat + lamb tallow); **Angel** (lamb tallow,
one last release after the consolidation); **Primus** (dataset
"primos" = misspelling); **Beef Tallow**; **Fortis** (value line,
2019–~2020; licensing verdict 2026-10-27, see note below);
**Nai** ("New Aion Soap", vegan, post-consolidation —
expansion confirmed 2026-10-23 via B&B GD club #2,082, "Nai Base (New
Aion Soap)"; community used "Aion/Nai" interchangeably for the
base);
**Kairos** (tallow, post-consolidation; **[[glossary/kairos-se|Kairos SE]]** = **Special
Edition** — maker-attested, see addendum 2026-11-07 below; lamb
tallow + emu oil vs standard Kairos beef tallow; community
distinguishes OG/"non SE" Kairos vs SE, and 2022 vs 2023 SE);
**Asinus** (unscented final
DonkeyBase release). ~2020 consolidation kept only Nai / Kairos /
Mallard (+ Angel + Asinus finals). **Version numbers matter as era
attributes, not products**: Kairos v3.3 / v4.7 / v4.8 / 5.0, Mallard
reformulations — a scent in "original Mallard" vs later Mallard is
the same product line; do NOT split entries by version. Validator
rule: key GD products as scent + base; version = attribute.
[catalog] Established (bases list + consolidation + version-era
practice from B&B/DFS; Kairos SE = Special Edition, Feb 2022
launch — see addendum below).

**Kairos SE run 2026-11-07 [catalog]:** RESOLVED — "SE" =
**Special Edition**, ESTABLISHED from the maker's own store
(aionskincare.com/products.json, live 2026-11-07): every Kairos SE
soap page carries "Formula - A special edition based on Kairos"
(Ousia, Omnia, Pasión, Luxor, Ketoret, Coattails, Ousia II,
Mumtaz). CORRECTION to the standing entry: SE launched **February
2022, not 2023** — Pasión created 2022-02-01 (titled "Kairos +"),
Ousia created 2022-02-22, Omnia 2022-03-07 (both titled "Kairos SE
( Lamb Tallow + Emu Oil )"); first dataset attestation 2022-04-18
(B_S80, Ousia, via the SE product URL); B&B community use by Aug
2022 ("GD Omnia Kairos SE", 2022-08-24). The Feb 2023 B&B review
(636699) reviewed the Jan 2023 drop (Luxor etc.), not the launch.
Formula = lamb tallow + emu oil (maker titles verbatim); standard
Kairos = beef tallow (+ coconut milk, goat milk). TWO
maker-published INCI revisions, both lamb+emu at core: (A) 2022
list — Ousia/Omnia/Pasión/Ousia II (phenoxyethanol preservative;
hydroxyethylcellulose); (B) Jan 2023 list — Luxor (created
2023-01-05) + Mumtaz re-release (tagged Jan2023Release):
preservative swapped to sodium dehydroacetate + honeysuckle
(Lonicera) extracts, thickener to tara gum, + oat oil,
polyglyceryl-4 oleate, bis-diglyceryl polyacyladipate-2; no
phenoxyethanol, no hydroxyethylcellulose. The B&B poster's "2022
Kairos SE vs 2023 Kairos SE" distinction is therefore REAL — year
tags are release/revision tags, not just drop dates. **"Kairos +"**
is a real maker title form: Pasión alone carries "Kairos + Lamb
Tallow + Emu Oil" (same 2022 INCI list as Ousia) — treat "+" as an
SE title variant, never a separate base. RETROACTIVE-RETITLE TRAP
[catalog]: the maker retitled older products to "Kairos SE"
(L'avventura created 2019-07-18, Coattails Redux 2019-03-28,
Mumtaz 2020-12-07) — a product's created_at does NOT date its SE
status, and pre-2022 "Kairos SE"-titled pages prove nothing about
what shipped then; the Jan 2023 Mumtaz re-release is the SE
shipment. "Special Edition" ALONE is ambiguous: Ambrosia (2019)
is tagged/formula'd "Special Edition" on a DIFFERENT beef-tallow +
duck-fat formula — never read a bare "Special Edition" tag as
Kairos SE. The live Formulas page
(groomingdept.com/pages/formulas, read 2026-11-07) no longer
documents Kairos SE at all (Maxima / Kairos one-liner / Optimus /
Mallard only) — products.json is the maker record. [[lineages/dataset-scope|Dataset scope]]:
exactly 3 genuine "Kairos SE" soap-field strings in the whole
corpus (B_S80 Ousia 2022-04-18; Ali4451 "GD Kairos SE 2023 Luxor"
2023-02-16; RedMosquitoMM "Ardent - Kairos SE" 2023-12-02) —
posters mostly write the scent and let the product URL carry the
base. Wayback CDX was "Temporarily Offline" this run — no snapshot
check was possible. NEW NARROW LEADS: (a) Coattails Jan-2023 INCI
(list B presumed, unread); (b) the "Kairos+" community mention
(B&B 635274: "the Kairos base for Chamomile & Lemon had been
updated some") — the Chamomile & Lemon product is a PRE-SHAVE, so
check whether "+" ever labeled a pre-shave formula or is
soap-only; (c) Mo's own announcement of the SE launch / 2023
revision (email list or Facebook, Feb 2022 / Jan 2023) — would pin
the launch moment better than the Feb 1 product-creation date.

**Fortis note, 2026-10-27 [catalog]:** Fortis = GD's affordable
value base (5 oz, $15 at launch per B&B GD club p.72 #1,423;
shorter ingredient list, simpler fragrance-oil scents; Sharpologist
'rona-savings review gives the full Fortis ingredient list:
beef tallow, shea, avocado, cow milk, lanolin). Dataset scope is
THIN — 16 hits total (2019: 2, 2020: 11, 2022: 3), 8 authors —
scents: Spa, Rainforest, Incense & Oud, Ingress, Boomer, Le Tabac
(+ generic "GD Fortis Sample" ×5, shaveSymptomatic 2020). The
Oleo/WCS licensing question is RETIRED (see [[lineages|Lineages]] addendum):
the idea was one poster's hope, Fortis was dropped in the ~2020
consolidation, and no licensed Fortis product exists — validators
must keep Fortis scents as GD-made, scent + Fortis-base products,
and must NOT attribute any WCS/Oleo soap to the Fortis formula.
