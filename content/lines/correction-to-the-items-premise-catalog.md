**CORRECTION to the item's premise [catalog]:** the seven were NOT all "created after the tallow era." Waves and Full Measure of Man (both created 2021-02-12) LAUNCHED IN TALLOW — the 2021-03-04 captures (3 weeks after creation) print the original tallow INCI verbatim, identical on both: "Tallow (Beef), Water, Coconut Oil, Sodium Hydroxide, Castor Oil, Glycerin, Shea Butter, Fragrance, Vitamin E", 5.25 oz. Both stayed tallow through 2021-10-19 (Waves: tallow 2021-03-04, 2021-08-05, 2021-10-19; FMM: 2021-03-04, 2021-05-08, 2021-10-19), months after Seville's switch bracket opened — the tallow→goat change was per-scent and staggered, never line-wide. Tallow→goat brackets: Waves (2021-10-19, 2022-07-02], FMM (2021-10-19, 2022-07-02] (both goat by 2022-07-02). Goat→coconut brackets: Waves (2024-09-13, 2024-12-02] — goat 2024-09-13, coconut 2024-12-02, matching Bay Rum / Cologne Russe exactly; FMM (2024-05-26, 2024-09-13] — goat 2024-05-26, coconut by 2024-09-13.
