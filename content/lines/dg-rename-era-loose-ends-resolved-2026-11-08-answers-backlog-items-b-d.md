(b) **Pre-bison L&L vegan formula — DISCONTINUED at the bison
launch, never run in parallel.** Bison retail launch = early–mid
July 2016: first dataset bison strings 2016-07-08–12 (twagshot
2016-07-12 "my new arrival: L&L Marshlands Bison Tallow"; beslayed

post-July-2016 vegan string is a LEGACY puck/sample, each narrated
as such: hughmonstah 2016-07-24 "L&L Grooming Harmony (Vegan
Base)" (his own pre-bison samples, "trying out my L&L samples");

("Man I bought this soap back in April 2016... Too bad it'll never

soap-field L&L/DG vegan strings: 10 across 2016–2020, all accounted

soap_vegan-linked uses are the original vegan puck. **RULE:** a

pre-bison puck, never a parallel vegan line; TTS's preserved

retailer's category for the old formula, not evidence of current
vegan stock. The "run in parallel" reading is retired.
