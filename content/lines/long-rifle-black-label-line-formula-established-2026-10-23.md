**Long Rifle "Black Label" line/formula (established 2026-10-23).**
As of the maker's Jan-2025 "Big Changes" announcement, ALL Long
Rifle shaving soaps were reformulated to the **Black Label wool-fat
formula** (lanolin-forward; maker: "Shavers deserve the thick lather
provided by lanolin"), and ALL shave tonics to the Black Label
tonic formula (botanicals + jojoba). Pre-2025 base was locally-
sourced beef suet tallow (maker's 2020 Earth Day post); June 2025
dataset ingredient lists for Hivernant still show tallow = the
transition was in progress through 2025. [catalog]: base = era
attribute on Long Rifle entries (pre-2025 tallow era vs 2025+
Black Label wool-fat era); "Black Label" is the formula name, not
a sub-brand. Candles' rename partner **Big White Yeti** (candle
maker) noted for lineages.
