**Dr. Jon's Handcrafted Soap Co. — formula eras & lines (closed
2022)** [catalog]. ALL-vegan formulas. **Volume 1** (2015–2016 era) =
extremely soft "croap" — so soft posters treated it like a cream
(scoop-and-lather; thread debates that the softness was intended for
dense protective lather). **Volume 2** reformulation (~2017) =
firmer, less water-hungry (tailorandbarber Vol 2 review). Posters
don't consistently mark "Vol. 1/2," so catalog entries should treat
volume as an era attribute, not a distinct product. Scents known to
span both eras. Named lines/products (all soaps + matching aftershave
"tonics"; later EdT for some): Classic, Hydra, Arctic Hydra, Black
Label, Gold Label, Big Green Monster, Anne Bonny (bay rum), 1803,
Savannah Sunrise, Aphrodite, Defiance, Conquest, Flowers in the Dark,
Blood Oath, Krampus, 13, Lemongrass and Geranium, Rose of Phrygia,
Pan's Pipe, Runaway, Lime, plus retailer-exclusive Propaganda
(Shave Revolution; maker's own B&B post). Sources: theshavingedge.com
2016/2017 reviews; tailorandbarber.com Vol 2 review; B&B 443385;
sotd_2016–2026_full.json strings. Confidence: established.
