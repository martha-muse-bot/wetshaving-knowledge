*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product lines]]*

**[catalog] Asylum Shave Works — "English Collection" shaving
creams (established 2026-10-27).** A CREAM line inside the
BullGoose house brand, distinct from the Asylum tallow soaps
(Colonia / Flying Mango / Frankincense & Myrrh / Old Virginia /
Royal Fougere) and from the later Spanish triple-milled Levante
soap — do NOT fold cream strings into any soap entry, and do
NOT read "English Collection" as a maker (it is the line name;
brand of record stays Asylum Shave Works, sold via BullGoose).
Established lineup (at least these three scents, all three in
BullGoose's bestseller list as quoted by Sharpologist): **Charcoal,
European Barbershop, Bay Rum** — full product title form:
"Asylum English Collection Shaving Cream (<scent>)". The line
was already a BullGoose bestseller by Sharpologist's article
published 2020-11-13 — so the line predates Nov 2020; launch
date itself is unpinned (lead).
Dataset presence is thin but real: exactly ONE full-form string
in 2016–2026, KerblimeySkal 2024-01-27, "Sensitive Skin |
Asylum English Collection Shaving Cream - Charcoal", plus the
same author's shortened 2024-06-12 "Asylum Shave Works -
Charcoal" (same cream, almost certainly — fold the shortened
form into the Charcoal cream entry, not into a soap). PARSER
CAUTION [catalog]: the leading "Sensitive Skin |" is
KerblimeySkal's own skin-type prefix, NOT part of the product
name — he prefixes the same string to unrelated soaps
(Moonsoaps Havana, Amaretto Speciale, Spearhead Seaforth!,
Macduff's Santa's Cheer, Nov 2023–Jan 2024); strip author
skin-type prefixes before matching. The 2024 poster's note is
firsthand color only: bought "their branded soap" from
BullGoose (he had just learned BullGoose was local to him)
and found it "kind of burn-y" — NOT evidence the cream is
marketed for sensitive skin, and not a formula claim.
Contract maker UNPINNED (lead): unlike Levante (Nice Pack,
Spain) and the tallow soaps (SV / WSP), no source yet names
who makes the English Collection creams, and "English" in the
line name is NOT evidence of English manufacture — do not
assume it. Sources: Sharpologist "What Is The Most Popular
Lathering Shave Cream?" (published 2020-11-13, BullGoose
bestseller list #3/#5/#9); sotd_2024_full.json (KerblimeySkal
2024-01-27 + 2024-06-12). Confidence: line existence +
three scents + BullGoose house-brand channel established;
launch date, full scent list, and contract maker = leads.
