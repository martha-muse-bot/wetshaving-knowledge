*Confidence: unknown · Source: [[lines|Product lines]]*

**Bare-cream alias trap [catalog]:** the Classic entry's "Musgo Real
cream" / "Musgo Real Shaving Cream" aliases subset-match scented
variants (Orange Amber x7, 'Musgo Real Porto Citrus Spice shaving
cream' x1) to Classic. Bare strings with no scent token
(frankieIVfingers 'Claus Porto - Musgo Real - Cream' x2) stay a
loose default to Classic — the original scent (P-15-style).
