**[catalog] Envy Shave — the "Envy White" knot-maker brand (established
2026-11-01).** Envy Shave was a mid-2010s artisan that sold KNOTS,
HANDLES, SOAPS, and complete brushes — but the dataset legacy is
overwhelmingly the knot: "Envy White" = their 2-band badger knot,
set in other makers' handles (Carnavis & Richardson "C&R 2XL Envy
White" ×92 + ~56 more C&R variants, Wolf Whiskers, Doug Korn,
Mozingo ×11, Boucher, Nathan Clark, Elite Razor, Declaration
"Envy B14" strings, Rudy Vey customs...). Knot line: Envy White
(2-band), Envy White Supreme (pricier grade; $57 for a 24mm per one
B&B reviewer), Luxury Silvertip Premium (~$44/24mm, c. 2017),
"Black Wolf Fan Knot" (synthetic, sold as the '8 Ball' brush).
Handle line: aluminum chalice, Alluminati, Dynasty, "8 Ball".
Soap line (few strings): Senbò, Winter Forage, Sakura Sen
(envyshave.com product URL attested in a dataset string).
Fitting note: "Envy knots tend to run a bit larger" (B&B knot-buying
guide — size up the hole). Dataset: 591 Envy field hits in 2016
(peak) → 184 (2017) → 46 → 31 → 24 → 43 → 24 → 10 → 12 (2024),
61 authors — classic 2016-17 artisan boom then legacy/NOS tail.
