*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product lines]]*

**[catalog] [[Declaration Grooming]] — base eras: Bison / Icarus /
Milksteak (established 2026-10-24).** Posters routinely tag the base —
"Declaration Grooming - Chaipre (Milksteak)", "Declaration Grooming
*Dirtyver* (Icarus)", "L&L Grooming - Darkfall (Bison base)" — and the
SAME scent exists in multiple bases (dataset: Darkfall in all three;
Chaipre in Milksteak + Icarus; Unscented in Milksteak + Bison), so
catalog key for DG soaps = **scent + base**, version = era attribute.
Era sequence: **Bison** (the original bison-tallow base, continuous
from L&L Grooming through the Nov 2017 rename to Declaration Grooming —
one formula, July 2016 – Sept 2019; see continuity addendum below) →
**Icarus** (~2018; bison tallow + lamb tallow + goat's milk
+ colloidal oatmeal per B&B comparison thread; had its OWN scent
lineup — "Icarus didn't have the same scents as the bison base" per
B&B 575718 #20) → **Milksteak** (launched Sept 2019; maker's own FB
announcement relayed on B&B 574293 #40: "the Icarus and the older
bison base are now officially dead and are being replaced with this
new Milksteak base"; "The BASE has changed, not the scents" — B&B
575718 #15). Milksteak is bison-tallow + dairy: Massacre of the
Innocents packaging (via EWG) lists Bison Tallow, Yogurt, Buttermilk,
Egg Whites, Coconut Milk, Goat's Milk — the name reflects the dairy.
Dataset first-uses match: milksteak 2019 (44), icarus 2018 (94),
bison 2016+. Validator cautions: (1) "(Bison)" is an ERA tag (the
pre-Milksteak base), not an ingredient tag — ALL THREE bases contain
bison tallow, so post-2019 "(Bison)" strings are legacy pucks, not
evidence of a fourth base; (2) Icarus-era scent lineup was distinct —
don't assume an Icarus string implies a Milksteak equivalent; (3)
post-2019 untagged DG strings are almost certainly Milksteak (only
production base) but per P-11 don't auto-default. Sources: B&B
575718 (2019-10-22; maker-change relay + base comparison #9),
B&B 574293 p.2 #40 (2019-09-26; maker's FB Milksteak announcement),
B&B 547858 (Icarus ingredient recollection), EWG packaging listing
for DG Massacre of the Innocence, sotd_2016–2026_full.json base-tag
counts (milksteak 226 / icarus 272 / bison 190 soap hits).
**ADDENDUM — bison-base continuity at the L&L → DG rename RESOLVED
2026-10-29 (answers backlog item (3)) [catalog].** VERDICT: the bison
formula did NOT change at the rename — one continuous bison era from
its July 2016 launch (L&L) to the Sept 2019 Milksteak deprecation.
RENAME DATE CORRECTION: the rename was announced **~mid-Nov 2017**,
not "2018" as this entry previously said — dataset's first
"Declaration Grooming" strings are 2017-11-20 (jdubba, urfrendlipiro,
CombatCarl13), already phrased "Declaration Grooming (formerly L&L
Grooming)". Evidence for formula continuity: (1) the rename landed as
a pure name change — community adopted the "(formerly L&L Grooming)"
framing from day one, treating it as the same company; (2) the
maker himself deprecated "Bison and Icarus" as ONE continuous base in
the Sept 2019 Milksteak announcement (Sharpologist 2020-03-27 quotes
the maker's statement: "an incredibly difficult decision to deprecate
Bison and Icarus"); (3) the bison ingredient list is identical across
the pre-rename record — B&B 530210 (Optimum Lather table, purchase
Aug 11, 2017) and the early-2017 B&B 522030 L&L-vs-WK shootout quote
the same list: Stearic Acid, Water, Potassium Hydroxide, Avocado Oil,
Vegetable Glycerin, Bison Tallow, Mango Seed Butter, Castor Oil,
Fragrance, Sodium Hydroxide, Lanolin, Bentonite Clay, Sea Buckthorn /
White Willow / Grape Seed extracts, Phenoxyethanol, Caprylyl Glycol,
Sorbic Acid; (4) negative evidence — this community flags every base
change loudly (Icarus got its own thread B&B 540135 in Mar 2018;
Milksteak a maker announcement relayed B&B 574293 #40), yet ZERO posts
anywhere claim a formula change at the rename (negative dataset-note
scan 2017–2018 + web search 2026-10-29); (5) 2018 threads casually
treat the bison base as one thing across the name ("I'll just keep
buying the old bison tallow base" — B&B 556912, Dec 2018; "the old
bison tallow base that doesn't have EDTA in it" distinguishes it from
Icarus on preservatives). Catalog: validators may treat ALL bison-era
DG/L&L soap strings (July 2016 – Sept 2019) as the same base formula;
"(Bison)" tags on L&L-name and DG-name strings are the same era tag.
Pre-bison note: L&L launched with a VEGAN formula before the bison
base ("This is the vegan formula before bison hit" — NoLogonServAvailable,
2016 dataset note on L&L After the Rain Vegan), so pre-July-2016 L&L
strings are a DIFFERENT base — do not fold into the bison era.
Sources: dataset rename mining (sotd_2017_full.json); B&B 530210,
522030, 540135, 556912, 575718; Sharpologist DG After The Rain review
(2020-03-27, maker's Milksteak statement); dailylather 2016-11-01
(bison launch, early July 2016).
**ADDENDUM — Icarus scent lineup RESOLVED 2026-10-28 (answers backlog
item (2)) [catalog].** Dataset mining (sotd_2016–2026_full.json, soap
field, base-tag cross-tab) + B&B release threads give the Icarus-era
lineup as a CURATED SUBSET of the bison catalog, not a full carryover
— which is what reconciles the two 2019 B&B claims ("the BASE has
changed, not the scents" vs "Icarus didn't have the same scents as
the bison base"): the scents that DID move kept their names across
bases, but many bison scents never shipped in Icarus at all, and
several Icarus scents were new. Release order, B&B-dated: **Santal
Auster** first (Mar 14, 2018, [[Chatillon Lux]] Parfums collab, $31/4 oz,
B&B 540135 — "only available in Santal Auster" at launch; a one-time
run per B&B 575718 #18) → **Sweet Lemon, Shore, Contemplation**
(the 2018 sample trio, B&B 545809) → **Chaipre + Scrumtulescent**
(Dec 19, 2018, B&B 556912; Dirtyver already "no longer available at
the DG website" that week). Dataset Icarus-tagged first uses add, in
order: Harmony (Sep 2018), Dirtyver + Darkfall + La Forêt de Liguest
(Oct 2018), Vide Poche (Aug 2018), Sellout (Dec 2018), Lamplight
Penance + Chaipre (Jan 2019), Sni Mato (Feb 2019), 88 Chestnut +
Taxes + Nefertiti + Fourth and Pine (Apr–May 2019), Mayflower +
Taum Sauk (May–Jul 2019), Admiral (Jul 2019); Weinstrasse and Try
This Soap 2.0 Icarus strings are Jan 2020 legacy-puck uses, not new
Icarus production (post-Milksteak-launch tags = legacy stock).
Icarus-attested scent set (soap-field Icarus tags, 300 hits):
Santal Auster, Sweet Lemon, Shore, Contemplation, Dirtyver,
Darkfall, Vide Poche, La Forêt de Liguest, Harmony, Sellout,
Lamplight Penance, Chaipre, Scrumtulescent, Sni Mato, 88 Chestnut,
Taxes, Nefertiti, Fourth and Pine, Mayflower, Taum Sauk, Admiral,
Weinstrasse, Try This Soap 2.0. Positive Milksteak-tag carryovers
from that set: Darkfall (19), Chaipre (14), Shore (4), Mayflower
(4), Weinstrasse (1). Bison scents that SKIPPED Icarus (zero
Icarus tags) but reached Milksteak: Yuzu-Rose-Patchouli (8
Milksteak tags), Massacre of the Innocents (9), Gratiot League
Square (5), Cuir et Epices (4). Validator cautions: (a) post-2019
UNTAGGED DG strings are almost certainly Milksteak per the entry
above, so absence of a Milksteak TAG does not prove a scent never
shipped in Milksteak — the lineup above is the Icarus-attested
set, and only the positive Milksteak tags are carryover evidence;
(b) two single-hit anomalies are LEADS, not carryovers: one
Milksteak-tagged Dirtyver (Dganjo, 2020-03-30) and one
Milksteak-tagged Contemplation (wallygator88, 2023-04-15) — a
later re-release or a legacy-puck mis-tag, unpinned; (c) two
apparent Icarus tags are CONTAMINATION, not lineup evidence: the
single "Marshlands | ... Icarus was vast progress" string (note
text inside the soap field) and the strikethrough "~~Icarus
Mayflower~~ Bison Cuir et Epices" string (a correction, not an
Icarus Cuir); (d) Dganjo is the dominant Icarus chronicler — many
first uses are single-author, so treat first-use dates as
earliest-observed, not release dates, except where B&B dates them.
Sources: dataset cross-tab above; B&B 540135 (2018-03-14),
545809 (samples), 556912 (2018-12-19), 575718 #15/#18/#20
(2019-10-22).
