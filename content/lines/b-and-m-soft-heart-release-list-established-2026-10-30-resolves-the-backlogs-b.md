*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product lines]]*

**[catalog] B&M Soft Heart release list (established 2026-10-30;
RESOLVES the backlog's "B&M Soft Heart release list" item).**
PP8 = Soft Heart, confirmed as the internal/test name: the PP8 test
batch circulated among 4 dataset posters Sep 16, 2018 – Apr 16, 2019
("PP8 tester" / "PP8 test batch", 15 strings) during Excelsior
development, and poster strings tie PP8 to the released base
directly ("B&M Big Leap Soft Heart (aka PP8)"; "[[Barrister and Mann]]
- Paganini's Violin (Soft Heart Series) in PP-8"; "B&M Hallows
PP-8"; "Barrister & Mann Sandalwood PP8"; "Barrister and Mann
PP8 Oceana"). The public "Soft Hearts" series launched Dec 2019 —
ZERO Soft-Heart-tagged soap strings exist before 2019-12-05 (468
hits, 180 distinct strings, sotd_2016–2026 soap fields).
ERA ARC: (1) Sep 2018–Apr 2019: PP8 test batch; testers preferred it
but Excelsior (harder, longer-lasting) won the house-base slot
(B&B 613527 quoting Will's video). (2) Dec 2019–Sep 2021: limited
"Soft Hearts" series — mechanism per B&B 594535 p.2 (Sep 2020):
"the occasional release of old discontinued [scents]" plus
seasonals and Unscented. (3) Sep 2021: Excelsior retired; Will
confirmed on Facebook that all non-Reserve production moves to an
updated (hardened) SoftHeart base (B&B 613527). (4) Sep 28, 2021:
Omnibus debuts with Leviathan and replaces Reserve + Excelsior +
Soft Heart (maker's own statement via B&B 614345) — a weeks-long
modified-Soft-Heart standard window. (5) Post-2021 SH strings are
destash of phase-2 tubs (every post-2021 string is a phase-2 scent).
RELEASE LIST (dataset positive base-tags; external corroboration
where noted): **Taiga** (Dec 2019 launch, TTS product_type=soap_le;
Latha-scent revival), **Le Petit Chypre** (Dec 2019, TTS soap_le;
Latha-scent revival), **Amazelnut** (Jan 2020; Daily Lather: "one of
our favorite releases from the Soft Heart series" + Nov 2021 Winter
return), **Big Leap** (Mar 2020 Spring), **Unscented** (Apr 2020+,
48 hits / 8 authors), **Diamond** (May 2020; BEER soap — poster
notes "the beer added in Diamond makes the base even softer"),
**Paganini's Violin** (Aug–Oct 2020), **Ferox** (Sep–Oct 2020),
**Hallows** (Oct 2020 Fall seasonal — B&B 594535 p.2 pre-order
note), **Sandalwood** (Dec 2020 SH-series LE, the Fougère-Gothique-
EdP sandalwood accord — NOT Latha Sandalwood; returned Apr 2022 LE
— Daily Lather), **Brew Ha-Ha** (Dec 2020), **Roam** (Dec 2020
Christmas release — B&B 600350 review of "SoftHeart Roam"; OG Roam
scent, not Roam II), **Rhapsody** (Feb/Mar 2021 Spring), **Oceana**
(Mar/Apr 2021 seasonal — Maggard product URL
"...-oceana-artisan-shaving-soap-4oz-soft-heart-base-seasonal";
Latha-scent revival), **Figarose** (Sep 2021+, 11 hits / 4
authors — straddles the modified-SH standard window).
SINGLE-AUTHOR LEADS (positive tags, treat as unconfirmed releases):
**Petrichor** (Dec 2021–Jan 2022, MalthusTheShaver ×2; the two
2021-06-17 "petrichor" hits are Roam notes containing the word,
not the scent), **42** (2022–2023, sahenders ×2 — could be the
Sep 2021 modified-SH window, since 42 was in production then),
**Cool** (2025, Ramjet615 ×3 — the same author who mislabeled
Lavanda as "Latha Lavender"; possible mis-tag, never cite).
VALIDATOR CAUTIONS: (1) "PP8" strings = Soft Heart; "PP8 tester" /
"PP8 test batch" = the 2018–2019 test era, never a product. (2)
The Excelsior squiggle ALSO appears on Soft Heart (already
recorded, B&B 614345 p.50) — with this list, squiggle + Taiga /
Hallows / Rhapsody / etc. = Soft Heart, resolving the symbol's
ambiguity for these scents. (3) Post-2021 untagged strings for
these scents are NOT SH by default (P-11; Omnibus replaced SH).
(4) TTS product_type=soap_le on the Dec 2019 Taiga/LPC strings =
Limited Edition confirmation for the launch. Sources: dataset
census above (counts: Sandalwood 180/23+ authors, Unscented 48/8,
Taiga 45, Rhapsody 34, Oceana 24, Hallows 24, Amazelnut 15+, Big
Leap 14, Diamond 14, Le Petit Chypre 12, Figarose 11, Paganini's
Violin 10, Roam 8, Ferox 6, Brew Ha-Ha 6); B&B 594535 p.2; B&B
613527 (Sep 12, 2021); B&B 614345 (maker Omnibus statement); B&B
600350 (2020-12-25 Roam review); Daily Lather History of B&M
(Amazelnut + Sandalwood rows); Maggard Oceana product URL.
