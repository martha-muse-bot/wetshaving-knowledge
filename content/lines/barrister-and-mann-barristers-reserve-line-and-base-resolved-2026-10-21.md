**[[Barrister and Mann]] — Barrister's Reserve®: line AND base, resolved
2026-10-21** [catalog]. "Reserve" is both a product LINE name and
the name of the lanolin-free soap BASE exclusive to that line.
Maker FAQ (quoted on Badger & Blade): Reserve soaps "are offered
only in the Barrister's Reserve® scents (Classic, Cool, Spice,
Lavender, Fern, and Waves) and are restricted specifically to that
line." Line launched **June 9, 2017** (soap $17.99; matching
splashes); beta formula debuted in the one-off **Soapmakers of
Awesometown** (Apr 1, 2017). Launch scents (Jun 2017): **Classic,
Cool, Spice**; **Fern** and **Lavender** added Sep/Dec 2017;
**Waves** added Nov 2018. All six are recreations of vintage
fragrances (Spice = 1957 Shulton Old Spice; Classic = Gillette
Sun Up; Cool = Flöid Blue; Lavender = Caldey Island Lavender;
Fern = Houbigant Fougère Royale). Base exceptions: Fougère
Gothique (Oct 2017) and a one-off Reddit-exclusive Seville used
the Reserve base without joining the line. Sources: Daily Lather
"History of Barrister and Mann" (updated 2025-05-05) Reserve
table; Daily Lather review 2017-06-09; B&B threads 527966,
587797, 621964. Confidence: established.
