**RETAILER BODIES ARE FOSSIL COPIES (method finding, [catalog]):** Naked Armor "(Vegan)" listings split 4 coconut vs 5 goat INCI tails (Bay Rum, Lavanille, Spice, Seville, Lavender still goat) DESPITE the "(Vegan)" titles — NA copied the maker body at listing creation and never updated it; creation dates are NOT switch dates. Maggard's hand-body listings show the same pattern (only Fougère Gothique, listed 2025+, is coconut; Maggard's Waves title still says "5.25oz"). Only the maker's own page edits track the formula. Corollary: the Fougère Gothique NA listing shows the stale-prose trap again (prose "goat milk powder", INCI coconut). Also note the maker live bodies are inconsistent: Bay Rum / Seville / Lavender products.json bodies contain NO ingredients section at all, while Cologne Russe / Lavanille / LGC / Fougère Gothique do — a missing INCI in products.json is a template quirk, not a formula statement.
