*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product lines]]*

**[catalog] Wholly Kaw — base progression (established 2026-10-21/22).**
Posters treat the base as an identity tag, so base-era is an attribute
on the product entry, not a separate product. Sequence:
**Vegan** (launch ~2014–15 — Sharpologist ~Oct 2015: "a vegan shaving
products maker, that released its first soaps in roughly the last
year"; formula "fairly similar to" LASSCO/Catie's Bubbles) →
**Donkey Milk** ("WKDM" shorthand, ~2018; tallow + donkey milk) →
**Bufala** (~2019; adds water buffalo milk — B&B "Wholly Kaw fans" p.2:
"new Bufala soaps") → **Siero** (~2021; "siero" = whey in Italian —
donkey-milk whey + water buffalo-milk whey proteins; Maggard copy
spells out the full composition; Sharpologist Bare Siero matchup ~Nov
2021) → **Crème Fraîche** (newest; in the founder's 2026 lineup
statement alongside Tallow, Bufala, Siero). KEY BASE FACT: all four
dairy bases are beef-tallow + WHOLE donkey milk per the founder's
on-record statement (~Aug 2026) — "Tallow", "Bufala", "Siero",
"Crème Fraîche" differ by dairy additions, not by tallow-vs-dairy.
The vegan line is formulated separately (no beef tallow, no animal
milk or whey). Validators: a bare "Wholly Kaw - <scent>" string without
a base tag usually means whatever the current base was when posted —
do NOT default it to Siero or Tallow (cf. P-11 no-false-precision);
"Bufala"/"Siero"/"WKDM" strings carry the era. Naming convention:
the fougère-adjacent **\*Concerto** series (Fern Concerto, La Fougère
Parfaite, Chypre Rose Concerto — Daily Lather Dec 2016; B&B 2018).
Sources: Sharpologist ~Oct 2015 + ~Nov 2021 reviews; B&B 539727,
560031 p.2, 547986; Maggard product copy (Siero composition);
Prism News founder statement (~Aug 2026); whollykaw.com (live).
