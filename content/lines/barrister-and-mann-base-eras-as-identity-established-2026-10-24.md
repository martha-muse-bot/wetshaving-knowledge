*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product lines]]*

**[catalog] [[Barrister and Mann]] — base eras as identity (established
2026-10-24).** Posters routinely tag the base — "B&M Seville
(Excelsior)", "B&M Leviathan (Glissant)", "B&M Leviathan (Omnibus)",
"B&M Reserve Spice (omnibus)" — and the same scent spans bases
(Seville in Reserve, Glissant, Excelsior, Omnibus; one poster owned
"42 in every base"), so catalog key for B&M soaps = **scent +
base**, version = era attribute. Era sequence (maker history via
nebrito brand story + B&B announcement threads): **White Label +
Black Label** (original lines) → **Glissant** (2015; slicker) →
**Excelsior** (Nov 2018; dev code "Purple Possum"; Vespers was the
launch scent; "a mix of the formula Glissante and the Reserve",
LANOLIN-bearing; maker's Excelsior squiggle symbol on all Excelsior
soaps except Vespers) → **Omnibus** (debut 2021-09-28 with Leviathan;
replaced Reserve + Excelsior + Soft Heart; lanolin-free). Parallel
budget/vegan lines: **Latha** (lanolin-free, ~2015–2018; 790 dataset
soap hits — its own scent lineup: Oceana, Taiga, Sandalwood etc.),
**Tre Citta** (vegan base, 2016–2018 per maker: developed alongside
Excelsior; dataset: First Snow, Lavanille, Arctique), **Soft Heart**
(2019–2021; one Excelsior-development iteration sold as its own line
"Soft Hearts" — Taiga, Diamond, Brew Ha-Ha, Le Petit Chypre; 454
dataset hits). Dataset first-uses match the era boundaries: glissant
2016–2018, excelsior 2018+ (13 in 2018, 76–82/yr 2019–2020), omnibus
2021+ (36 in 2021, 129–178/yr 2022–2024). Validator cautions: (1) the
Excelsior squiggle ALSO appeared on Soft Heart — symbol is not
Excelsior-alone evidence (B&B 614345 p.50); (2) tub-ID as secondary
evidence: Glissant = older clear-bottom tubs, Excelsior = white-bottom
tubs, Omnibus = OMNIBUS printed on side label (same thread); (3) the
Reserve line's scents were re-released in Omnibus with "Reserve"
dropped from the name (see the Reserve/Omnibus entries above) —
"Spice (Omnibus)" = the former Reserve Spice, not a distinct scent;
(4) post-2021 untagged "B&M <scent>" strings are almost certainly
Omnibus but don't auto-default per P-11. Sources: nebrito.com brand
story; B&B 555036 p.5 (Vespers/Excelsior launch, Nov 2018);
barbearclassico (maker's Excelsior announcement quoted verbatim);
B&B 613527 (Omnibus announcement 2021-09-21, Leviathan debut 9/28);
B&B 614345 p.50 (label/tub identification); B&B 587797 p.2 #26
(Seville across Reserve/Glissant/Excelsior);
sotd_2016–2026_full.json (glissant 213 / excelsior 329 / omnibus 545 /
latha 790 / soft heart+softheart 454 / tre citta 31 / white label 247 /
black label 70 soap hits).
