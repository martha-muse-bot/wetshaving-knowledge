**The four scents (maker copy, verbatim notes):**
- **Kanno** — homage to Yoko Kanno (Cowboy Bebop; "almost single-handedly dragged Western jazz and blues into the world of Japanese anime"); shiso leaf, grapefruit, pink pepper, geranium; "a spectacular twist on the classic Eau de Cologne structure"; rated highest of the four in blind smelling at the April meetup. Dataset: 4 rows / 4 authors, all Oct 21–Nov 6, 2026 (DealeyLama, RenegadeLamp, Tetriside, mpark6288). Soap + splash both attested.
- **King** — tribute to B.B. King, "the man and his guitars" (all guitars named "Lucille"); leather, vetiver, sandalwood, oak, amber. Dataset: 6 genuine rows / 5 authors, Oct 15–Nov 9, 2026. Soap + aftershave both attested.
- **Two Walters** — Big Walter (Horton) + Little Walter (Jacobs), harmonica players, "no relation"; rosemary, cedar, sea salt, caramel, wood smoke; Will's personal favorite ("If you can only choose one, this is the one to try!"). Dataset: 2 rows / 2 authors (gcgallant 2026-10-10, Tetriside 2026-11-08). Soap + aftershave both attested.
- **Waits** — Tom Waits, "the Bard of the Bizarre"; roasted coffee, balsam fir, pine sap, black salt. Dataset: 2 rows / 2 authors, both 2026-10-09 (gcgallant, _walden_). Soap + aftershave both attested. The Barbershop Duet reviewed the Waits soap (S1 E871, ~Nov 7, 2026).
