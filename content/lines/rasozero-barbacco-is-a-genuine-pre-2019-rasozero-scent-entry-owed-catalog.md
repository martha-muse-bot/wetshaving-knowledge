**Rasozero Barbacco is a GENUINE pre-2019 Rasozero scent, entry OWED
[catalog]:** B&B firsthand use 2021-01-31 ('Soap: [[lineages/tcheon-fung-sing|Tcheon Fung Sing]]
Rasozero Barbacco') + Agrumella thread ('I have all three rasozeros...
the spiffero and the tobacco... I will get Barbacco next... Barbacco smells
like a marshmallow. They claim that the scent is tobacco'). Original trio
= Spiffero (mint/eucalyptus), Agrumella (lemon), Barbacco (tobacco).
Relaunch (discontinued 2019, 'reborn with new formulas and fragrances',
Super Safety Razors) = Sensitive Skin / Pelli Delicate, Refreshing /
Rinfrescante (citrus+menthol, green), Protective / Protettivo (anise/
licorice). Relaunch names are NOT the old names — do NOT fold Barbacco
into the verified Rasozero Pelli Delicate entry (different scent,
different era). The 3 dataset rows (Sensitive-Jump-3041, Sept–Oct 2022)
are old-stock use. Entry evidence route: retailer listing / label photo
(B&B firsthand use is community attestation, not primary).
