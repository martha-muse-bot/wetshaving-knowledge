**[catalog] DG addendum, 2026-11-07 — the two Milksteak singletons
RESOLVED as carryovers.** Both single-hit anomalies from the
2026-10-28 addendum graduate to positive Milksteak carryovers.
(1) Dirtyver: Dganjo's single "[[lines/declaration-grooming|Declaration Grooming]] Milksteak -
Dirtyver" (2020-03-30) sits inside a March–May 2020 restock
cluster of 10 Milksteak tags — Darkfall 2020-03-11, Massacre of the
Innocents 2020-03-16, Unscented 2020-03-26, Cuir et Epices
2020-03-29, Dirtyver 2020-03-30, Cuir 2020-03-31 + 04-23 + 05-03,
Unscented 2020-04-11, Darkfall 2020-05-04 — from an author who in
the same year tags Icarus legacy pucks (Contemplation, Vide Poche,
Chaipre, Taum Sauk) and Bison legacy pucks (After the Rain, Cuir,
Unconditional Surrender) correctly. The Cuir precedent closes the
case: B&B 575718 #17 (2019-10-22) said Cuir et Epices was "none of
which have been released in Milksteak yet", yet Dganjo's 4x
Milksteak Cuir tags (Mar–May 2020) show it reached Milksteak in
that wave — the rollout was gradual and unannounced, and Dirtyver
rides it. His Dirtyver uses run TTS-form 2020-01-01 → 2020-03-01,
the Milksteak tag 2020-03-30, TTS-form again 2020-05-18 →
2020-08-30 (the TTS link is his default paste; the base tag appears
when he consciously notes it). Corroborating color: w3strnwrld's
"Declaration Grooming Dirtyver (the new one)" (2021-11-30).
(2) Contemplation: wallygator88's single "Declaration Grooming
Contemplation Milksteak" (2023-04-15) is use #21 of one continuous
tub: 44 uses 2021–2025 — 17 TTS-form (2021) + 1 (2022) + the
Milksteak tag + 2 bare (2023-04-28/29) + numbered uses (22)
2023-05-10 → (44) in 2025. He tags bases elsewhere (After the Rain
Bison x8, 2019–2020) and never tags this tub Icarus; first use 2021
= Milksteak era. (3) Maker-side Wayback CDX check (2026-11-07,
declarationgrooming.com/collections/shaving-soaps/products/*,
2019–2022, statuscode:200, collapse=urlkey): 65 distinct product
URLs, NOT truncated. Both scents appear ONLY as Icarus-slug pages
— Dirtyver `dirtyver-shaving-soap-icarus-base-4oz-autumn-seasonal`
(2019-11-20) and Contemplation
`contemplation-shaving-soap-icarus-base-4oz` (2021-11-08); no
Milksteak-slug page for either. This is NEUTRAL, not negative: the
same list shows the maker kept the original slug through base
changes — Darkfall (19 dataset Milksteak tags), Chaipre (14), Shore
(4), Mayflower (4) and Weinstrasse (1) all ship Milksteak on

(2021-03-22) despite the Milksteak review (Sharpologist, March
2020). A Milksteak slug exists only for scents that never had an
Icarus page (Massacre, Tribute, B-Cubed, Dayman, Nightman,
Opulence, etc.) or new products — Chaipre 2.0 is a distinct
Milksteak product (slug `chaipre-2-0-shaving-soap-milksteak-base-4oz`,

alone; the slug dates the original listing. The CDX inventory also

saved in goal hidden_files
