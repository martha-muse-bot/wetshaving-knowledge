**[[Barrister and Mann]] — Omnibus transition (fall 2021)** [catalog].
Will Carius discontinued the Reserve, Excelsior, and Soft Heart
bases and consolidated on the new **Omnibus** base (debut: the
fall 2021 Leviathan drop). The Reserve SCENTS survived: they were
re-released in Omnibus (Cool first, late 2021; Spice followed),
and the maker DROPPED "Reserve" from the product names — e.g. the
soap is now sold simply as **"Spice Shaving Soap"** at the SAME
product URL whose slug still reads
`barristers-reserve-spice-shaving-soap` (live page fetched
2026-10-21: title "Spice Shaving Soap", $24.99, Omnibus
ingredient list, scent copy verbatim from 2017: carnation,
nutmeg, vanilla, tonka). Retailer listings render it "Barrister
and Mann Spice Shaving Soap (Omnibus Base)" (Naked Armor).
Sources: B&B thread 613527 (Omnibus replacement announcement
discussion, 2021); B&B thread 551830 ("Reserve Cool is already
in Omnibus", late 2021); barristerandmann.com product page;
dataset strings 2022+ ("B&M Reserve Spice (omnibus)", "Spice
(Omnibus)"). Confidence: established.
