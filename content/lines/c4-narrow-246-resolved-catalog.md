*Confidence: unknown · Source: [[lines|Product lines]]*

**(c4-narrow-246) RESOLVED [catalog]:** the reverse direction is SMALLER and mostly benign — 104 literal-'soap' poster strings across all 8 cream-form entries carrying soap aliases (7 named in 244 + Nuavia Nera, which carries the identical alias pattern but was not named there), vs 244's ~549 mis-attributed cream strings. Only ONE validator call (Speick Men merge, below). Two of the 244 note's "cream-form" classifications were NAME-based, not evidence-based, and are CORRECTED here: Nuavia Pannacrema is a soft SOAP in a 200ml tub (vshod: "PANNACREMA NUAVIA SHAVING SOAP - Verde - 200ml") — "crema" in the name is not a format; Oatcake Almond Cream IS a shaving soap named "Almond Cream" (Slickboys: "OATCAKE ALMOND CREAM SHAVING SOAP (100G); tin label readable"). Their soap aliases are CORRECT; no action. Catie's Revolution (14 strings): maker is soap-only; "Luxury Cream" is the maker's soap name — routing correct. Moon Soaps Amaretto Speciale (46) + Tobacco Flower (4): the maker identifies BOTH as creams (extra-soft, butter consistency; "Moon Soaps identifies Amaretto Speciale as a shaving cream" — Razor Company); the line's other scents (Old School, Astoria, Bixby, Koa Mahina, Santa Cruz, Union, Warmest Regards, Trade Winds) are "Shaving Soap 6oz" entries — the catalog already splits the line correctly by scent, and no per-scent soap+cream parallel exists, so the 50 "soap" strings are TTS-label + community loose usage routing to the one product per scent; soap aliases stay as routing aliases. The B&B 2019 Amaretto review documents the loose usage ("The soap (or in the case of Amaretto Speciale, the cream)"; "this particular product is a cream and is thus really soft" while calling it a soap throughout).
