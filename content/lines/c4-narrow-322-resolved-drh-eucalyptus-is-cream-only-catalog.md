no hard soap exists. Two independent full-range retailer matrices agree
exactly on the six DRH soap scents (Almond, Arlington, Lavender,
Marlborough, Sandalwood, Windsor) with Eucalyptus absent from every soap
format: Maggard lists Eucalyptus ONLY as "Eucalyptus Shaving Cream in

it ONLY as cream bowl 150g + cream tube 2.65 oz. The maker site returns
zero Eucalyptus shaving-soap pages (site:drharris.co.uk), and the
maker's own blog treats Eucalyptus as a tube cream explicitly contrasted
with hard soaps. Dataset: 14 rows, 13 cream-worded, 1 bare, 0
soap-worded, 8 authors, 2016–2025 (three rows cite TTS collection 761,
product_type=cream). TESC's shaving-soap page lists DRH soaps as
Marlborough/Lavender/Sandalwood refills only — consistent, but a partial
range (no Arlington/Windsor refills though they exist), so corroboration
only. Rose is the twin case: cream-only, own verified cream entry.
