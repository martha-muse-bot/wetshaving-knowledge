**RETAILER-SET SYNTHESIS — all four surviving retailer LGC soap pages are now content-read [catalog]:** Maggard Nov 22, 2015 (plain-"Chypre" title, $25.00, sold out, no INCI, no "Glissant"); Badger Dec 1, 2015 ($33.50 CAD ≈ $25 USD, sold out, v1 INCI, no "Glissant"); MenEssentials Dec 3, 2015 ($25.00, out of stock, v1 INCI, "Glissant" YES); Italian Barber Jan 13, 2016 ($25.95, sold out, v1 INCI minus one ingredient, "Glissant" YES). Established cross-page facts: (a) **the "500 jars" figure appears on NO retailer page — it is maker-page-only attestation** (maker Nov 23 snapshot); never cite a retailer page for the 500 count. (b) **"Limited Edition" is title-only and inconsistent** — Maggard "(Limited Edition)" + IB "Limited Edition" vs MenEssentials "SE" only vs Badger plain title; the LE status rests on the maker page + Maggard/IB titles, not on uniform retailer wording. (c) **"Glissant" naming is retailer-copy variance, not formula variance** — named on MenEssentials + IB, absent on Maggard + Badger, while every INCI-bearing page (Badger, MenEssentials, IB) is Glissant v1 (sweet almond, no safflower); a page's silence on "Glissant" proves nothing about the base. (d) **IB's Sodium Tallowate omission is a COPY VARIANT, not a second formula** — one dropped ingredient + a parenthesis rewording, against three independent v1 lists (maker Nov 23, Badger Dec 1, MenEssentials Dec 3) that all include it; no second batch or second formula is evidenced. Do not split a v1/v1b entry on it. (e) **Sell-out confirmed at all four retailers by their capture dates** — Maggard by Nov 22 (2 days post-drop), Badger by Dec 1, MenEssentials by Dec 3, IB by Jan 13; the 500-jar run was gone from retail within ~2 weeks. (f) **Price ladder: $25.00 (Maggard, MenEssentials) / $25.95 (IB) / $33.50 CAD (Badger ≈ $25 USD)** — one product, three price points, currency explains the outlier.
