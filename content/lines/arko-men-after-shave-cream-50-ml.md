*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product lines]]*

**[catalog] Arko Men After Shave Cream (50 ml) — pre-2015 line vs
2015 relaunch (established 2026-10-26).** Arko is Evyap's men's
brand (Turkey; Evyap also makes Duru, per B&B 422929). The US
distributor catalog (Kev and Cooper, Jan 2016) states the whole
Arko Men line was **relaunched in 2015 with a new formula and
design**: After Shave Cream 50 ml in **4 varieties — Cool,
Comfort, Fresh, Sensitive**; After Shave Balm 150 ml in the same
4; After Shave Cologne 250 ml in 5 (Cool, Comfort, Fresh,
Platinum Protection, Gold Power). The pre-2015 naming was
messier (B&B 422929, Sept 2014): creams described as Cool, Extra
Sensitive, Maximum Comfort; balms/colognes as Adventure, Aqua,
Ice Mint. Dataset attestations of the older names: "Arko Aqua
balm" / "Arko Balm Aqua" (2016), "Arko Ice Mint Balm" (2017),
"Arko Classic Aftershave Cream" (2017), "Arko Adventure splash"
(2020), and if0rg0t2remember's "Arko Citrus After Shave Cream"
(2021-01-25).
- **Date correction to the dataset note:** if0rg0t2remember
dates his hoarded tubes' discontinuation "some time around
2013". The documented change is the 2015 relaunch replacing
the old formula/packaging — his "~2013" is approximate at
best, and may date the ShoeBox clearance sale rather than
Arko's change. Do NOT cite 2013 as the old line's end date;
bracket it as "pre-2015 line, replaced 2015". It was also NOT
a full discontinuation: the four core names (Cool/Comfort/
Fresh/Sensitive) continue in the new packaging — a B&B
"sad to see go" poster (Dec 2021) puts it the same way:
"They still make like one or two scents but it's in a new
packaging, costs more, and I have no idea if it's the same
formula. I still have four tubes of the old scents."
- **[[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]:** (1) Cool/Comfort/Fresh/
Sensitive across the 2015 boundary = ONE product each, relaunch
= era attribute, never a product split; (2) Adventure, Aqua,
Ice Mint, Classic, Action, Citrus are pre-relaunch names —
keep them as their own entries, do NOT fold them into Cool/
Comfort/Fresh/Sensitive without evidence; (3) "Arko Citrus"
is single-author and unpinned — it may be a scent name or a
descriptor (Adventure was described by a B&B poster as a
strong artificial citrus) — do NOT alias Citrus → Adventure;
(4) "Arko Cool Cream" / "Arko Comfort Cream" are AMBIGUOUS
between the After Shave Cream and the Shaving Cream tubes
(both exist in Cool/Comfort) — field and poster context
decide, never auto-apply; (5) the 2022 dataset spike (41
"Arko Comfort" aftershave hits) is TWO authors, not a
revival — one heavy user, not market activity.
Sources: Kev and Cooper Arko catalog (Jan 2016, distributor);
B&B 422929 (Sept 2014); B&B 617846 p.2 #36 (Dec 2021);
sotd_2016–2026_full.json (Arko aftershave-field counts +
if0rg0t2remember 2021-01-25 note). Confidence: 2015 relaunch
+ 4-variety lineup established (distributor catalog); old-name
list established (B&B + dataset); Citrus identity = lead.
