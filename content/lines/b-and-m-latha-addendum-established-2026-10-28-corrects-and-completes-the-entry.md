*Confidence: established · Source: [[Product lines]]*

**[catalog] B&M Latha addendum (established 2026-10-28; corrects
and completes the entry above).** Latha is a lanolin-free TALLOW
base — the B&B Oceana-thread claim that Latha was "the non-tallow
line" is WRONG (corrected on the same thread: the non-tallow base
was Tre Citta; Latha's ingredient list carries potassium/sodium
tallowate, argan oil + shea butter superfats, sodium citrate,
sodium lactate, and NO lanolin, coconut milk, or silk — all three
of which Glissant has). Launched ~July 2015 (Limon and Oceana both
dated Jul 2015 in the Daily Lather history table); discontinued
by maker announcement **February 15, 2018** ("the end of a two and
a half year run"; reason: fine-fragrance direction + unsustainable
production; retailers stocked up and final stock sold through).
All post-Feb-2018 dataset uses are destash/remainder — the 2020
bump (158 soap hits) is two heavy users (BourbonInExile 66,
Lambaar12 28), not a relaunch; the tail runs through Oct 2026.
Canonical lineup (Daily Lather Latha table, 10 scents, each with
a matching alcohol-free aftershave): **Latha** (classic barbershop:
lavender, vanilla, oakmoss, carnation, geranium, heliotrope,
musk), **Lavanda**, **Le Petit Chypré** (coumarin chypre; splash
mentholated), **Limon** (Jul 2015; orange/lemon/rose), **Oceana**
(Jul 2015; aquatic/bergamot/jasmine/sandalwood), **Sandalwood**
(black currant/sandalwood/amber), **Taiga** (pine/balsam/cedar),
**Figgy Puddin'** (seasonal; green fig/oak/cinnamon/clove/berry),
**Unscented**, **Velvet** (for women: violet/iris/cherry/praline).
Dataset (soap-field substring over sotd_2016–2026 yearly files:
872 hits, 179 authors; the entry above's "790" was a narrower
scope): Taiga 145/41 authors, Limon 132/28, Oceana 90/48,
Sandalwood 78/30, Lavanda 75/26, Unscented 52/7, "Latha Original"
49/19, Le Petit Chypre 36 (+14 "Le Petite" misspellings, 11 "LPC"),
Figgy Puddin(g) ~25, Velvet 16/5. CORE-LINE OVERLAP (scent+base
identity consequence): **Taiga** returned in Soft Heart (45 SH
strings from Dec 2019) and as a core Winter scent Dec 2021
("originally created as a member of our Latha line");
**Oceana** returned in Soft Heart (~2021; 25 SH strings
2021-03-31–2024-07-31) and as a core Summer scent Jul 2023;
**Le Petit Chypre** returned in Soft Heart (Dec 2019 strings).
Limon, Lavanda, Figgy Puddin', Velvet, and barbershop Latha show
NO core return in the Daily Lather table. Validator cautions:
(1) **Sandalwood name reuse — do NOT fold**: the 2021 Soft Heart
Sandalwood (180 SH strings from 2020-12-05; Apr 2022 Limited
Edition entry) claims a DIFFERENT origin (accord developed for
the Fougère Gothique EdP) and different character ("most
natural-smelling sandalwood") from Latha Sandalwood
(black currant twist) — same name, separate scent+base products;
(2) **"Latha Original" = the barbershop Latha scent**, not
Unscented (Unscented is separately tagged; "Original" is
posters' disambiguator for the line's namesake scent);
(3) **"Latha Lavender" (7 soap hits: ChrisVanMeer 2022,
Ramjet615 2023–2025 + AS) = Lavanda mislabel** — no Lavender
scent exists in the Latha table, and Reserve Lavender is a
different line; fold into Lavanda. Other spellings: Lavenda /
Lavada, Le Petite Chypre, Tiaga, Sandlewood. (4) **"Latha
Osmanthus" is NOT a product** — frankieIVfingers' DIY
(2018 "B&M x Me Latha Osmanthus"; 2020 posts explicit: "Latha
Unscented + Osmanthus Absolute" / "with Osmanthus Absolute");
Rdthedo/wallygator88 2020 strings reference his creation. Fold
into Latha Unscented with a mod note, never a new entry.
(5) **Velvet strings never carry "Latha"** ("[[Barrister and Mann]]
Velvet", 16 hits) — matcher must still map them to Latha Velvet;
no core-line Velvet exists. LEAD only: "Latha was the test bench
for Glissant" (B&B Oceana thread #7, single forum claim) —
chronology is thin (Latha Jul 2015 vs Glissant's Nov 2015 Le
Grand Chypre debut) and the formulas differ on lanolin/coconut
milk/silk; do not cite as lineage. Sources: dailylather.com
History of Barrister and Mann (updated May 5, 2025; Latha section
+ ingredient lists + standard-table return entries); B&B 577827
(Oceana thread, incl. the non-tallow correction + Soft Heart
Maggard description); B&B 595249 (Oceana Soft Heart return);
dataset counts above.
