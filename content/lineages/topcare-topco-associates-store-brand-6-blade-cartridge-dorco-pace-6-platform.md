Dorco Pace 6 platform (established 2026-10-25; see [[Product aliases]]

regional grocers/pharmacies (SmartLabel "About Topco Associates").

SmoothShield strips, open-flow cartridge) and states "Blades made in
South Korea". Same P-04 treatment as DSC: TopCare-branded entry,
Dorco manufacture attribute, and the existing catalog cluster
`unverified/razor/top-care-rebranded-dorco-pace-6` stands. Note the
label's own limit: "Fits only TopCare 6-blade system handles" —
the platform is shared, the docking claim is not. The live

available" on 2026-10-25 (search-index snapshot is the surviving
