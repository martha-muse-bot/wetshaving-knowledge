**[catalog] Beaver WoodWright → [[Black Ship Grooming]] Co. (RENAME, resolved
2026-10-21).** Founder: **[[Kelly Hogan]]**, Beaver, PA — started as a
woodworking business (pens, rolling pins); "Beaver WoodWright" = his
hometown + his woodworking trade. Rename announced in the maker's OWN
email ~early July 2016 (quoted verbatim on B&B thread 491622, "Beaver
Woodwright" p.2; post edited Jul 26, 2016: "like a 3 weeks ago they
sent a email about them changing their name from BWW to Black Ship
Gromming Co."): "Sadly after many years as the Beaver WoodWright we
have moved on... we can be found at www.blackshipgroomingco.com. Our
soaps will remain the same formula as well as our aftershaves." Same
formulas/aftershaves, new site; motivation was the pirate-themed
shaving focus (shows feedback that the old name "just didn't fit";
decided with his wife). Dataset corroboration: earliest "Beaver
Woodwright" string 2016-05-11; earliest "Black Ship Grooming"
string 2016-07-19 (typed "Black Ship Grooming Co.?" — new to the
community that week); all post-2016 [["Beaver Woodwright"]] strings are
old-tub references ("OG Beaver Woodwright," the Dr. Jon's × Beaver
WoodWright "Bandit" collab, one poster's "Beaver Woodwright – Davey
Jones" aftershave signature line). NOTE: wettheface's 2017 Ghost Ship
review claims the change happened in 2015 — CONTRADICTED by the
maker's own email + dataset timing; treat that claim as lead/retired,
established date is July 2016. Sources: B&B 491622 p.2 (maker's email
quoted); blog.wettheface.com/2017/09/review-black-ship-grooming-ghost-ship.html;
Sharpologist WSN minicast with Kelly of Beaver Woodwright (Maggard
meetup); sotd_2016_full.json string dating. Confidence: established
(rename, date, founder, reason); formulas-carried-over per maker's own
statement. Catalog implication: one brand group — "Beaver Woodwright"
/ "Beaver Wood Wright" / "Black Ship Grooming" / "Black Ship Grooming
Co." fold into ONE brand group with attested-name variants (same
company, renamed). Validator note: Bandit-collab strings
("Dr. Jon's/Beaver WoodWright - Bandit" etc.) are the joint Dr. Jon's
× Beaver WoodWright special edition — same product, not two makers'
separate Bands.
