**(c4-narrow-37) RESOLVED — Kryptonite 2 is a DISTINCT maker product, and the verified fragrance entry is its EdP under the v1 name [catalog]:** the maker's own fragrance-descriptions page heads the two entries **"Kryptonite (original soap and aftershave only)"** and **"Kryptonite 2"** — the parenthetical is the maker's own scope statement. v1 notes: lime, litsea cubeba, balsam fir, cedar, cypress leaf, howood. v2 copy: "This scent was never been available as a perfume until now (May 2025). It's fresh and citrusy, green and clean, uplifting and easy to wear." v2 notes: lime, lemongrass, lime blossom, cypress, fir, cedar, oakmoss, clean notes. The v2 balm listing adds the maker's own line: "The fragrance in this product is a brand new fragrance as of May 2025." Different note stacks + "brand new fragrance" + distinct maker naming = a different scent, distinct product — same boundary as Roam vs Roam II (P-22).
