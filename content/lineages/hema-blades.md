**HEMA blades — Dutch department-store house brand; Personna-Israel
attribution UNCORROBORATED (lead, not established).** HEMA is the
Dutch department-store chain (Hollandsche Eenheidsprijzen
Maatschappij Amsterdam; first store Amsterdam, 4 November 1926),
and "HEMA" on a blade means the retailer's house brand, not a
maker — the same supermarket-private-label pattern as Tesco/TiP
in the B&B "common blades around the world" thread (439365 #3).
Dataset presence is MINIMAL: a field-specific scan of
sotd_2016–2026_full.json finds exactly ONE HEMA blade-field string
— "HEMA", VenkHeerman, 2023-10-20 (Yaqi TFC adjustable; the same
post also uses a HEMA aftershave cream, so field attribution
matters). HEMA is therefore a near-zero catalog item, not a queue
driver, whatever its maker turns out to be. The backlog's
"[[Hema blades]] = rebranded Personna" claim traces to ONE second-hand
source: B&B thread 308102 "DE blade testing diary" #13 (thread
published 2012-08-28, updated 2014-03-26), a Netherlands-based
tester writing "I have read here on B&B that it is made in Israel
by Personna" — he does not claim firsthand knowledge, names no
pack marking, and no second source was found in web search
2026-10-25 (no pack photo, no retailer copy, no Sharpness
Research Project measurement, no hema.nl listing surfaced).
Two cautions against promoting it: (1) "made in Israel by
Personna" cannot by itself pick a product — the Nazareth plant
made BOTH the Personna Platinum Chrome ("Israeli Reds") AND the
Super+/Crystal family, which this file already records as
different blades (see Crystal entry above), so HEMA must not be
folded into either without its own evidence; (2) the diary's own
performance report cuts against the Reds reading — #13 calls
HEMA "the dullest and the one that tugged the most" of his test
set, and #99 (sixth use) again reports "noticeable drag and some
tugging" — the opposite of the Reds' sharp/smooth reputation,
though a single tester's beard/technique is weak evidence either
way and is recorded here only as a tension, not a refutation.
Current retail status UNPINNED: no current HEMA DE listing was
found in search (negative search, not discontinuation proof);
the 2023 dataset use may be old stock. [[Catalog guidance]]
[catalog]: keep any HEMA blade entry as its OWN HEMA entry with
maker UNPINNED; do NOT record Personna/Israel as manufacture
attribute until a pack/wrapper photo or a measurement lands.
Verification routes, in order: (a) a HEMA pack or blade-wrapper
photo showing origin/coating marks; (b) a B&B Sharpness Research
Project measurement (mass/thickness/cutting force) matching a
known Nazareth blade; (c) a Dutch-market listing naming the
supplier. Learned 2026-10-25. Confidence: HEMA = Dutch
department-store house brand established; Personna-Israel
attribution LEAD (single second-hand community source).
