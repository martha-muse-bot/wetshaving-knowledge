**Tcheon Fung Sing — founder history (Sharpologist + retailer maker-text,
corroborated):** founder Tcheon Fung Sing, born China, moved to Italy 1930,
interned in a WWII refugee camp, married an Italian woman, started the Turin
company in 1945 making vegetable-based shaving soaps in the artisan
tradition; also sold Solingen three-hole safety-razor blades under the TFS
brand in the early years (HairMaker copy). Handed down three generations:
daughter Gabriella Tcheon, now niece Paola Barile (master soapmaker). Gold
Medal for shaving products, first Turin fair, Oct 7, 1951 (B&B 561844,
quoting maker history). Original scent range: almond, sandalwood, tobacco;
later Bergamot & Neroli, citrus peel, cologne-like scents. Still family-run,
Turin.
