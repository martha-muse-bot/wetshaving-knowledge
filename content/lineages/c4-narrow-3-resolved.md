**(c4-narrow-3) RESOLVED — the White→Glissant transition is now maker-attested [catalog]:** the maker's own Le Grand Chypre soap page, Wayback-captured **2015-11-23**, names Glissant, quotes the maker's explanation verbatim ("we christened the new soap 'Glissant'... after 16 separate iterations"), lists 500 jars / ~350 available for direct sale (launched by Nov 23, 2015), and carries the v1 sweet-almond INCI + allergy warning. Daily Lather's Nov 2015 table date is corroborated by a second, primary source. Standing arc refined: White Label (through Sep 2015) → Glissant v1, sweet almond (Le Grand Chypre LE, Nov 2015) → Glissant v2, safflower (Fireside, winter 2015–16). B&B 467993 p.2 #31 is the third corroboration of the sequence (debut with LGC LE; Fireside first tweaked release; Night Music second). The Fireside page captured 2016-01-31 carries the safflower copy + INCI — maker evidence for the v1→v2 tweak. Guards recorded: "June 2015" prose date stays retired; Fragrantica/Silloria "2014" is the EdT, never the soap; B&B #31's post date is unrecoverable from text fetch (cite for sequence only); Nov 23 is first capture, not launch day.
