**Shire 2 corroboration (no new lead):** the same maker page says of Shire, "I've rebuilt the fragrance from the ground up with the same notes and inspiration, but better perfumery skills and materials. Always improving. Shire 2 notes: violets, fresh air, vetiver, spice, and tobacco." The catalog ALREADY holds verified `soap/aftershave/fragrance/house-of-mammoth-shire-2` entries — the maker copy corroborates the rebuild; nothing owed. Note the contrast with Kryptonite: Shire 2 is a rebuild of the SAME notes (one scent, new perfumery), Kryptonite 2 is a NEW fragrance with different notes — which is why Kryptonite 2 gets its own entry while Shire 2's split was the catalog's existing call.
