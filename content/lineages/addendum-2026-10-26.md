**ADDENDUM 2026-10-26 — Nazareth→Germany switch, dated; Solingen
plant link DOWNGRADED to lead; pack-colour trap [catalog].** This
resolves the backlog item "Nazareth→Germany: when German-made
Personna Platinums replaced Israeli stock at US retailers."
(1) TIMELINE (established): Israeli production of the Platinum
Chrome ended in late 2020 (B&B 631567: "They stopped making them
in late 2020 so it is all NOS"), and German-made stock was already
in posters' hands by Feb–Mar 2021 — B&B 603804 (published
2021-02-27, updated 2021-03-04): "The platinum chrome blades are
now being produced in Germany"; the dataset's first
German-tagged strings are scarpux 2021-02-04 ("Personna Platinum
(Germany?, EDIT: Maybe Israel?)" — hedged, the switch week
itself) then confident from 2021-03-09 ("Personna Platinum
(Germany - Blue)", blue packs), with cowzilla3's "German
Personna Platinums" running from 2021-06-02. German-tagged
strings continue through 2026 (Gerry7070 Feb 2026, Kelvinator_61
Aug 2026), while "Israeli Red" strings after 2021 are NOS/destash
naming, not current production. Current retail confirms the
German era is still current: Edwin Jagger's live listing sells
"Personna Platinum DE Razor Blades … Manufactured in Germany,
dual coating of platinum and chrome."
(2) CORRECTION to the 2026-10-24 entry above: that entry stated
German production was at "the Wilkinson Sword plant" as if
established. The source actually says only "most likely in the
same facility that makes the Wilkinson Sword Classic blades"
(B&B 589707 #18) and "I suspect at least some of the machinery
from the Nazareth plant was relocated to the plant in Solingen,
Germany … I have no conclusive evidence" (B&B 603804). The
Solingen/Wilkinson-plant link and the machinery-relocation story
are therefore LEADS, not established facts — Germany as country
of manufacture is established; the specific plant is not.
(3) PACKAGING TRAPS [catalog]: pack colour does NOT date a
blade in the German era. Israeli era: red packs = Americas
(packaged in Mexico), blue packs = Europe/UK, same blade. German
era: first the blue/gold packs in Europe, then red boxes in the
US "that look the same as the old Israeli-made packs" (B&B
631567 #15) — a German blade in a red tuck is current stock,
not an Israeli Red. Also: vendor photos lag — "vendors still
show pictures of the Personna plastic tucks with the red
lettering, but what you receive are the current German-made
ones with the gold on blue logo" (same thread). And carton
copy lags: a 2023 Belgian Delhaize carton still said "Made in
Israel" while its inner container was stamped "PROD 23/01/23"
and Carrefour sold the same blades as "Made in Germany" (B&B
658492 #28). Validators: read the origin PRINT on the pack/tuck
in hand, never the pack colour, the vendor photo, or the
carton. (4) BLADE IDENTITY: one product across the switch —
official name unchanged ("Personna Platinum" / "Platinum
Chrome"); blades carry identical printing across eras (B&B
658492 #32, 659158 #10), so era is an ATTRIBUTE (P-04), never
a product split; do not record a shave-quality verdict — firsthand
reports split both ways (German sharper vs Israeli smoother),
i.e. YMMV. (5) CONFLICT NOTED, NOT RESOLVED: Ethical
Consumer's Feb 2026 Edgewell summary says the company's 2018
10-K "stated that it had closed its production facility in
Israel" — which sits uneasily with late-2020 end-of-production
in the community record (a 2018 filing line could describe a
restructuring announcement, a different facility, or a
misreading; the 2015/2017 10-K subsidiary lists still carry
Personna International Israel Ltd.). Do NOT cite 2018 as the
closure year until the filing text itself is read. LEADS left:
the Edgewell filing language; Solingen-plant confirmation
(pack photo naming a plant, or an Edgewell statement); the
Czech-packaging leg (Personna International CZ s.r.o. is in the
10-K subsidiary lists, consistent with the existing
"packaged in Czech Republic" note, but no pack photo was
checked this run). Learned 2026-10-26. Confidence: switch
timeline + current German production established; plant link
lead; 2018 filing claim unresolved.
