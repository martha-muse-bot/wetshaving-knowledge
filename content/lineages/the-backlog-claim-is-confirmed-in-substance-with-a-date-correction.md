**The backlog claim is CONFIRMED in substance, with a date correction.**
The 2019 dataset note ("started out in France but got quietly bought
out by an American company 30+ years ago", USS-SpongeBob) is right on
both halves: the brand is French in origin and is now owned by the
American company American International Industries (AII), Los Angeles.
The maker's own brand copy, repeated verbatim across distributors,
says AII "acquired the brand and its Clubman line over 30 years ago"
(clubman.com / clubmanonline.com history page). One independent
reviewer dates the buyout to the 1980s (Fragroom). Confidence:
established (maker copy + independent dating).
