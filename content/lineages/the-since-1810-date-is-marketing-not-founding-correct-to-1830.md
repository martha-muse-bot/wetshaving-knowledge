**The "since 1810" date is marketing, not founding — correct to 1830.**
Édouard Pinaud was BORN 29 December 1810 (Abbeville); he founded the
house in 1830, buying the Paris shop À la Corbeille Fleurie (230 rue
Saint Martin; Bespoke Unit and Wikipedia agree on 1830; Dumbarton Oaks
via Bay Bottles gives the same 1830 founding). The "1810" in brand
copy conflates his birth year with the Legrand perfume house he later
took over, which dated from ~1810. Validators: do NOT cite 1810 as the
founding year; cite 1830, with 1810 as the brand's own (incorrect)
marketing date. Confidence: established.
