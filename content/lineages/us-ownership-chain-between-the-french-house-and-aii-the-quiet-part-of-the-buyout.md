**US ownership chain between the French house and AII (the "quiet"
part of the buyout).** 24 June 1947: Pinaud, Inc. transferred its entire
business to Ed. Pinaud, Inc. (then Barbara Alice, Inc.), owned by
persons unrelated to Pinaud, Inc., with a 15-year exclusive agency
(court records, Perfumers Manufacturing Corp. v. Commissioner).
1949: the Joubert group (owner of Ed. Pinaud, Inc.) merged into
Nestle-LeMur, keeping the Nestle-Le Mur name. Ed. Pinaud, Inc.
continued as a Nestle-Le Mur subsidiary through the early 1980s
(still at 220 East 21st Street in a Sept 1980 ad). December 1983:
Nestle-LeMur merged into a subsidiary of Kleer Vu Industries. The
brand is now owned by AII (Bay Bottles). So the AII acquisition falls
AFTER December 1983 — consistent with the 1980s dating and the
maker's "over 30 years ago". The exact AII acquisition year is
UNPINNED — record as a lead, not a date. Confidence: chain
established (court records + merger announcements + period ads, via
Bay Bottles); AII year lead.
