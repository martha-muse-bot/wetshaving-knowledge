**"[[glossary/the-club|The Club]] Black" = TWO different scents — never conflate [catalog]:**
- **(a) "The Club Black Birch Stout"** — VIP-exclusive soap + aftershave, 2019–2020. Full-corpus census (240,951 rows): **11 product-field rows / 5 authors** (robemtnez ×3, EldrormR ×3, Arymo95 ×2, youarebreakingthings ×2, colt_45s_with_lando ×1), 2019-05-03 → 2020-09-16. Scent: **oatmeal cookies with a stout dry-down** (robemtnez verbatim ×3: "I smell like cookies!", "could still smell oatmeal cookies", "smells like oatmeal cookies and when the aftershave dries down you get more of the stout"). Maker attribution: youarebreakingthings "A&E/ The Club Black Birch Stout"; colt_45s_with_lando 2020-06-27 links the VIP product page (shavingshopclub.com/products/black-birch-stout-shaving-soap-for-vip-members) and says he bought his on r/Shave_Bazaar ("picked this one up on the bazzar basically the day I found out it existed"). **Live store check 2026-11-08: the product page is GONE (live 404, browser-confirmed) and no "Black Birch Stout" product exists anywhere on the live store** (site search returns only unrelated VIP products). **Catalog: ZERO Birch Stout entries exist** (black-birch grep = 0) — soap + aftershave entries are OWED, with the dataset + the dead VIP URL as evidence. Spelling guard: "Birtch" (colt_45s) is a typo, never a variant.
- **(b) "The Club – BLACK"** — a separate, later scent, launched ~April 2022. Engineered_Shave 2022-04-09 (first use, "this new" soap) gives the full label: "BLACK – The Club | Black Pepper, Tobacco, Oud, Amber, Dark Chocolate, and Rum. Very edgy yet smooth, like the BBS finish." Later rows add bourbon and sandalwood. **"BBS finish" here = baby-butt-smooth (glossary BBS), not a product.** Engineered_Shave 2022-04-16: "these firmer A&E soaps which have recently come out" — A&E-maker confirmation. Soap verified in the catalog (`soap/the-club-black`, photo evidence, tub reads 'THE CLUB BLACK'); aftershave by June 2022, Skin Food Sep 2022, splash Dec 2022 (spazztik88). The unverified long-label soap cluster (Engineered_Shave 2022-04-09, 1 use) is the SAME product's scent description — **fold into the verified entry**, never a separate product.
- **"Sadly there is no EdP with this" — STANDS [catalog]:** Engineered_Shave's Apr 2022 note says no EdP exists. The four 2024 `unverified/fragrance/the-club-black` rows (Engineered_Shave, 2024-02-01 / 04-16 / 07-04 / 12-24) are the BLACK SOAP's scent carried in the fragrance field — each pairs with the BLACK soap + an unscented splash, and the notes describe the soap ("dark chocolate aromas along with the rum"). **Do NOT promote the fragrance cluster to an EdP entry.** A BLACK EdP would need a bottle/label sighting.
