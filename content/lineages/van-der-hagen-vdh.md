**[catalog] Van Der Hagen (VDH) — mass-market drugstore brand; no rename,
ownership change in 2016 (resolved 2026-10-22, backlog hardware item).**
Origin lore: a Dutch "Van Der Hagen" family making homemade shaving soaps
in Lith, Holland, emigrating to the US in the early 1900s (brand/PR story
via third-news.com V-Razor launch piece, 2025 — treat as brand lore, not
independently verified). Corporate: **Van Der Hagen Enterprises was sold
to Universal Beauty Products Inc. in 2016** (Sharpologist, VDH luxury
soap review — established); shavers then noticed "Scented Luxury Shave
Soap" displacing the older "Deluxe Shave Soap" (Shea-butter Deluxe →
glycerin/mango/shea Luxury = formula evolution as era attribute). CEO in
2015: **[[Marty van der Hagen]]** (company press for the Men's Health
Grooming Award, Shave Butter product of the year; see [[People]]). Channels:
Walmart, Target, Walgreens, CVS/pharmacy, Rite Aid, Bartell/Kinney/Duane
Reade. **OEM model — catalog impact:** the TTO razors are rebranded
**Weishi 9306**-platform razors (see [[Hardware lore]] — do NOT merge VDH
into Baili); the mug/brush are claimed rebranded **Colonel Conk** products
(single B&B source, lead); blades are "manufactured in Solingen, Germany"
per VDH's own site (Sharpologist speculates Merkur — lead). Per P-04,
manufacture is an ATTRIBUTE: one VDH brand group with Weishi-made
hardware, Solingen-made blades (attribution open), and house/Conk
soap+brush lines. Launch note: VDH's own "V Razor" released 2025
("Available Now" per third-news.com) — watch for dataset strings.
Confidence: **established** for the 2016 sale + drugstore presence +
Weishi-platform attribution; origin lore and Col Conk/Merkur attributions
are leads.
