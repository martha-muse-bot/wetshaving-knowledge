*Confidence: established · Source: [[lineages|Lineages]]*

**TFS-MADE BRANDS [catalog] — manufacture attributes, never brand folds:**
TFS produces 'dedicated productions for the companies that want personalized
products, without imposing great orders' (maker-side copy via HairMaker.gr
Mandorla page). Established by multiple independent B&B attestations:
(a) **RazoRock La Famiglia** — 'The new line of Soaps that RazoRock is
bringing to market are the [[lineages/tcheon-fung-sing|Tcheon Fung Sing]] Soaps. They are being made by
TFS and branded with the RazoRock label for the North American market.'
(B&B 288827); 'It's only the RazoRock La Famiglia line (soaps and A/S
balms) which are made by TFS.' (B&B 310388). (b) **Mondial / Antica
Barberia Mondial** — 'The same TFS soap is sold under the Mondial label, I
believe' (B&B 598070); 'The Mondial soft soap/cream is the Tcheon Fung
Sing I referenced. Rebranded.' (B&B 639572); 'Several years ago Mondial
soaps were always produced by TFS' (B&B 666091). Caveat: same-scent
equivalence is scent-by-scent, not blanket (one poster rates Mondial
Tabacco Verde's performance below TFS TV). (c) **Abbate y la Mantia
ORIGINAL formula** was TFS (dataset firsthand note + B&B 595365: TFS
produces 'both branded TFS and for other brands (Razorock, Abbate Y La
Mantia and many others)'). Later Abbate iterations are Abbate's own.
LEADS (single source each, not established): **Matador** ('Matador is just
Tcheon Fung Sing', B&B 412569) — lead (317); **Figaro** (dataset 'Figaro
Monsieur "Fruits" ( made by Tcheon Fung Sing)' + 'Figaro Red (TFS Barbe
Rosse; same as Razorock Classic)') — lead (316).
