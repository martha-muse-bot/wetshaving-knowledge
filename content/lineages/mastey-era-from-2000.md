*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**Mastey era from ~2000.** Finley ran the company until 2000, when
Jean Pierre Mastey acquired it (Mastey's own account in the MR
interview: he knew Finley through his father's Beverly Hills salon,
"within ten minutes, we had a deal"). ~2004 refocus: the blue-bottle
packaging and a shift out of department/beauty-supply distribution
into independent menswear stores (Fred Segal, Barneys, Colette Paris,
Isetan Japan). The **Baxter Finley Barber & Shop** in West Hollywood
dates to this era — maker's timeline gives both "launched 2008" and
"opens on La Cienega 2010" (minor inconsistency: possibly launch vs
La Cienega premises; do not cite a single year without the caveat).
**Shave Tonic** (2014) was the first barber-tested product out of the
shop. Confidence: established (Mastey interview + maker timeline).
