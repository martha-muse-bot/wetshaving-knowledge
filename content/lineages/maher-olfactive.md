**[catalog] Maher Olfactive — [[people/shawn-maher|Shawn Maher]]'s fragrance line (catalog
thread resolved 2026-10-31; NEW BRAND for the catalog queue).**
Second brand of **Shawn Maher** (St. Louis; [[lineages/chatillon-lux|Chatillon Lux]] founder),
launched **April 28, 2020** — the first release was Crystal Moon,
Maher's own birthday gift to himself (cafleurebon draw post, pub
Apr 13, 2020). Rationale (cafleurebon): a separate space with the
goal "Art for Art's Sake — unconstrained by budgetary concerns such
as cost of materials"; Fragrantica interview (Maher): "my newer
brand, and its scents are a bit edgier and riskier and utilize more
natural components." FRAGRANCE-ONLY line (dataset: 132 field hits
2024–2026, ALL fragrance field; zero soap/aftershave strings) —
sold via maherolfactive.com (maker's own Scent Notes blog) and
American Perfumer (St. Louis retail shop, e.g. Treachery launch Jan
18, 2020: "through not only Maher Olfactive, but also American
Perfumer"). Dataset-attested scent list (2024–2026, many authors):
Santal Auster, Treachery (+ Barrel Aged Treachery), Sun Soaked,
Velouria, Fog Cowers (The Fog Cowers, extrait, created 2024, per
Surrender to Chance), Waking Dream, Nefertiti, Red Skies (rel. Apr
28, 2022), Orris Forest (2021), Tempo Rubato, Crystal Moon, Legato
(limited-edition oud extrait, Oct 2022, 10 mL), Lamplight Penance,
Madame Chouteau, Sagan Dalya, Benton Park, Just Like Honey, Vetiver
(A/B prototypes, 2024). MIGRATION LINK: several Chatillon Lux
Parfums scents live on under Maher Olfactive — Santal Auster (CL
2018 Icarus collab → MO Parfum; poster string "Chatillon Lux /
Maher Olfactive – Santal Auster", 2025-03-05) and Lamplight Penance
(MO Parfum in dataset) — so the CL Parfums wind-down below is a
PARTIAL migration, not a wholesale one; the four "To Be
Discontinued" CL EdTs have no MO equivalents. **[catalog]:
the catalog has ZERO Maher Olfactive entries as of 2026-10-31 —
a live fragrance line with 132 dataset uses 2024–2026; create
brand + scent entries (fragrance format).** Sources: dataset
mining sotd_2024–2026_full.json (132 field hits, 40+ distinct
strings); Fragrantica "History as Perfume" interview; cafleurebon
(Crystal Moon draw, Sun Soaked review, Velouria review);
scent-notes.com (Maher's own blog: Treachery, Legato);
surrendertochance.com (Fog Cowers); thescentbase.com (25-entry
Maher archive — full-list pull is a follow-up lead).
Confidence: established (line exists, founded 2020, fragrance-only);
per-scent dates: press-dated ones established, dataset-only
attestation otherwise.
