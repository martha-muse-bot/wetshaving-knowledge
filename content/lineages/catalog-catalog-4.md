**[[lineages/catalog-catalog|Catalog [catalog]]]:** the 7 variant clusters fold into ONE Legendary product with four scent variants — `unverified/soap/ariana-evans-pedro-fiasco-s-legendary-shaving-soap-marzipan`, `unverified/soap/pedro-fiasco-marzipan`, `unverified/soap/pedro-fiasco-s-shave-soap-marzipan` (Marzipan) + `unverified/soap/ariana-evans-pablo-fiasco-s-legendary-shaving-soap-boysenberry-and-teakwood`, `unverified/soap/ariana-and-evans-pablo-fiasco-boysenberry-teakwood-soap` (Boysenberry; "Pablo" is the typo) + `unverified/soap/a-e-pedro-fiasco-s-pomegranate-wood-beer-soap`, `unverified/soap/a-e-pedro-faisco-s-pomegranate-wood-shaving-soap` ("Faisco" typo) (Pomegranate & Wood). The "beer soap" in two Pomegranate cluster names is the Legendary base itself (beer is in the INCI) — not a separate beer-soap product. **Do NOT fold** `unverified/soap/catie-bubbles-bergmont-bay-pedro-fiasco-s-pomegranate-wood-artisan-beer-soap-bas` into the Legendary entry — that is a Catie's Bubbles-made beer soap carrying the scent (same pattern as Catie's Bubbles-made Francisco); different maker, separate product. No Coconut & Tonka cluster exists yet — the single SilverRavenSo row is the seed for one. The verified Santorini/Limoncello 5.3 oz creams stay separate (different format). The Legendary line is absent from BOTH live stores as of 2026-11-10 (aegrooming.com 151 products; shavingshopclub.com 272 products) — delisted at retail, era-closed at the 2023-02-06 last capture.
