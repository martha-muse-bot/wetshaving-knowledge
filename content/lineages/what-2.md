*Confidence: unknown · Source: [[lineages|Lineages]]*

**What:** Dallas-based DTC brand-consolidator **Foundry Brands** (backed by LightBay Capital + Monogram Capital Partners, ~$100M debt-free equity at launch July 2021) acquired men's grooming/shaving brand **Supply** (Supply Co), announced **2022-09-30** (Global Cosmetic Industry / Dallas Innovates) with coverage 2022-10-02 (BeautyMatter) and an October 2022 Beauty Independent feature. Terms undisclosed. The entire Supply team stayed in place post-transaction. Follow-up coverage (Beauty Independent 2023; Cart.com partnership press 2026-03-24) confirms Supply remains in the Foundry portfolio alongside Blu Atlas, Stryx, Benevolence LA, and Craft & Kin.
