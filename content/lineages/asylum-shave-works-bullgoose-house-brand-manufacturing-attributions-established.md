*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**[catalog] [[Asylum Shave Works]] (BullGoose house brand) manufacturing
attributions (established 2026-10-21, from the retailer's own copy).**
- Asylum Half Hive brushes (Mistura / Premium Boar / Alchemy Fiber):
MADE BY SEMOGUE IN PORTUGAL — BullGoose's own homepage copy:
"Semogue in Portugal made these brushes for Asylum Shave Works,
our house brand." [catalog]
- Asylum Levante triple-milled soap (refill/wood bowl): "made for us
by a top tier shaving soap manufacturer in Spain" — made in SPAIN,
maker UNNAMED (lead: identify the Spanish contract manufacturer).
- Asylum Rx (incl. V2): "designed exclusively for BullGoose Shaving
by a product engineer in Los Angeles" — that's [[Larry Calderon]] (met
Phil in a Pasadena IHOP, summer 2012); stainless, Product of USA.
- Asylum Injector (V3 handle): 316L stainless, milder than the Rx,
Product of USA (BullGoose × Calderon program, per the Sharpologist
Asylum Injector review quoting BullGoose). Sources:
bullgooseshaving.com homepage fetched 2026-10-21 (copy is the
house brand's own claim = near-primary); Sharpologist Asylum
Injector review. Confidence: established (attributions + USA/Spain/
Portugal origins); the unnamed Spanish soap maker is a lead.
- ADDENDUM 2026-10-23 — the Spanish soap maker is IDENTIFIED: **Nice
Pack** (nicepacksoap.com), the same Spanish third-party soap
company that makes LEA soap. Per B&B user dave1269, who asked Phil
(BullGoose) directly ("As per Phil, Levante soap is made by the
same 3rd party Spanish company that makes LEA soap. Weird name
'nice pack'"); community-corroborated on the same thread
("already known and had been widely believed for several years").
Source: badgerandblade.com thread 622046 (2022-03-18). Confidence:
established (owner-sourced via member + multi-member corroboration).
Catalog: brand of record stays Asylum Shave Works; Nice Pack is the
manufacture ATTRIBUTE (P-04). The earlier Asylum soaps were NOT the
same maker — they were scent-by-scent contract products:
Saponificio Varesino, Italy (Colonia, incl. beta 4.1/4.3; Flying
Mango — "Asylum Shave Works SV Colonia or Flying Mango beta 4.3",
B&B Steve56) and WSP, USA (Royal Fougere — "WSP/Asylum Shave Works -
Royal Fougere" dataset strings 2017–2018; B&B: "The Asylum soaps
were made by WSP, but they were made specifically for Bullgoose").
Frankincense & Myrrh (wegianwetshaving's 2017 "tallow soaps";
dataset strings 2020–2023) and Old Virginia (2017 only) — contract
maker UNPINNED (lead: Wayback/live read of the old BullGoose
product pages; IA was offline 2026-10-23). Dataset: "Levante" soap
strings first appear 2022 (2) + 2026 (1) — the Spanish/Nice Pack era
is the late era, and Nice Pack is a private-label manufacturer, so
"Levante" formula =/= the tallow-era formulas. [catalog]
- ADDENDUM 2026-10-27 — a THIRD Asylum lather format, the
"English Collection" shaving CREAMS (Charcoal, European
Barbershop, Bay Rum; BullGoose bestsellers by Nov 2020):
full entry in [[Product lines]] [catalog]. Cream strings
must not fold into the tallow soaps or Levante; contract
maker unpinned; the dataset's only full-form string is
KerblimeySkal 2024-01-27 (Charcoal), whose leading
"Sensitive Skin |" is his own skin-type prefix, not the
product name.
