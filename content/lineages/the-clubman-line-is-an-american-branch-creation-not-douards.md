**The Clubman line is an AMERICAN-branch creation, not Édouard's.**
Pinaud's US presence: imports from the mid-1840s, a New York agency
from the late 1870s, a branch house under Emile Utard from May 1890,
the Pinaud Building (84–90 Fifth Avenue) opened 1903, US business
renamed Pinaud, Inc. ~1927 (220 East 21st Street, manufacturing there).
The Clubman range itself was introduced in the 1940s (aftershave,
talc, hair tonics, shampoos, soaps; Bespoke Unit, estimated 1940) by
the American branch, which formulated cheaper alternatives for the US
market. Édouard died 1868, so no Clubman product is his own
composition — except arguably Lilac Vegetal (1880, "Lilas de France"
origin), which predates the Clubman range. Confidence: established
for the branch timeline (Bay Bottles, from Merck Report 1893,
Dumbarton Oaks, court records, period ads); 1940s Clubman launch is
Bespoke Unit's estimate — treat the decade as established, the exact
year as a lead.
