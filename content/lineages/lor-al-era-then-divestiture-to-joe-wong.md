*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**L'Oréal era, then divestiture to Joe Wong.** Baxter was owned by
L'Oréal Group as of January 2021 (PETA/Cosmetics Business reporting
on L'Oréal's animal-hair brush ban names Baxter as the L'Oréal-owned
brand at issue). By March 2025 Baxter was OUT of L'Oréal: Beauty
Independent and Premium Beauty News (both reporting L'Oréal's March
3, 2025 Carol's Daughter divestiture) state that finance veteran
**Joe Wong** "previously acquired" Baxter of California (with Ambi,
AcneFree, Dermablend) from L'Oréal. So current ownership = Wong's
independent vehicle, brand ALIVE (maker site live, shave consumables
still retailed — e.g. MASC Canada lists Super Close Shave Formula
and After Shave Balm as current stock). The exact L'Oréal acquisition
year (a review site says 2013) and the exact Wong divestiture date
are UNPINNED — record both as leads, not dates. Confidence:
L'Oréal ownership 2021 + Wong divestiture pre-Mar-2025 established;
2013 acquisition year lead (single weak source).
