**Catalog verdict [catalog]:** (1) `fragrance/house-of-mammoth-kryptonite` (verified) is mislabeled AS BUILT: its attestation is 20 uses / 4 authors / years [2025, 2026] only, and **8 of its 17 aliases are v2 strings** ("House of Mammoth - Kryptonite 2", "House Of Mammoth - Kryptonite v2", "House Of Mammoth - Kryptonite V2 EDP.", "House of Mammoth - Kryptonite 2025 - Eau de Parfum", "House of Mammoth - Kryptonite 2 perfume.", the jwoods23 v2-explainer string, etc.), while its evidence is set cross-validation with the v1 SOAP. A v1 fragrance entry cannot rest on v2-era attestation: v1 was "soap and aftershave only" per the maker, and the only pre-May-2025 fragrance strings are tester-era ("Kryptonite Tester EdP", Jul 2024). (2) Owed: `fragrance/house-of-mammoth-kryptonite-2` (maker EdP listing = primary evidence, created 2025-05-16); `aftershave/house-of-mammoth-kryptonite-2` (v2 balm listing live; v2 SPLASH strings exist — 89 aftershave-field K2 rows, first 2025-07-13 — but no maker splash listing found, so the splash half is lead-grade until a listing or label photo surfaces); `soap/house-of-mammoth-kryptonite-2` (126 rows / 5 authors, first 2025-06-19; retail listing unpinned — Maggard search negative; Tusk-sample variant corroborates). (3) Strip the v2 aliases out of the v1 entries: fragrance 8 aliases, soap 1 ("House Of Mammoth - Kryptonite V2 (green) - Croap"), aftershave 1 ("House of Mammoth - Kryptonite 2 splash."). The aftershave entry's "Kryptonite v1"/"(v1)" aliases are correctly v1 and stay. (4) The v1 fragrance entry's disposition is a validator call: keep it as a tester-era entry (tester strings only) or retire it — do NOT keep it as the v2 EdP's home. The v1 soap + aftershave entries are genuine (2019–, 601/476 rows) and untouched by this.
