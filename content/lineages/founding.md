**Founding:** **Imperial Barber Grade Products** founded **2009** by master barbers **Scott Serrata and Pedro Zermeno**, out of Razorbacks Barber Shop, Long Beach, California. First product: **Classic Pomade** (2009) — "the strongest water based pomade in America" per the maker's own copy; the line then expanded into Gel/Fiber/Matte/Blacktop/Freeform pomades and a shave collection. Maker's own About page (imperialbarberproducts.com/pages/about, live 2026-11-08). Hair/skin products "made in the U.S.A., along with the jars they go into" (maker copy).
