**Brand of record [catalog]:** the tub label reads **'THE CLUB BLACK'** (photo evidence on verified `soap/the-club-black`, imgur BEHZ4PM, Reddit u4rbi0 2022-04-16) — brand of record is **[[glossary/the-club|The Club]]**, maker A&E as attribute (P-04). The catalog already does this for Black / Grecian Horse / Novelist; the parallel A&E-branded entries (e.g. `soap/ariana-evans-the-novelist`, `fragrance/ariana-evans-a-amp-e-novelist`) are the same scents under the maker's retail brand — never merge across the two brandings without a label check, and never fold a Club scent into an A&E retail entry on a name match alone.
