**Acquired by Mäurer & Wirtz from Procter & Gamble at end-2006 /
in 2007**, together with 4711, Tosca, and Extase (Basenotes M&W
overview: "at the end of 2006"; Moodie Davitt trade press: "in
2007"; TheShavingEdge 2023, quoting the M&W site: "In 2007 Mäurer
& Wirtz acquired Sir Irisch Moos (along with 4711, Tosca, and
Extase) from Proctor and Gamble"). So the brand's ownership arc
is Mülhens → P&G → Mäurer & Wirtz (2006/07). All SOTD dataset
strings (2016+) are M&W-era, so M&W attribution for catalog
entries is correct — but any pre-2007 product history belongs to
the Mülhens/P&G era. Context: M&W itself was founded in Stolberg
in 1845 by Michael Mäurer and Andreas August Wirtz as a soap
maker, and is the fragrance/cosmetics division of the
Dalli-Werke–Mäurer & Wirtz family company (Basenotes overview).
Confidence: established.
