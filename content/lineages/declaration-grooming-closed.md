**[catalog] [[Declaration Grooming]] CLOSED — announced Dec 2025, shut down
January 31, 2026.** Post-closure dataset uses (attestation through
2026) are destash use, not new stock. Source: Sharpologist "Wet
Shaving Talk For 29 December 2025" (via catalog validation research,
2026-10-21). Confidence: lead — confirm with a primary announcement
before treating closure dates as established.
**ADDENDUM 2026-10-31 — owner named, announcement channel + dates
FIRMED to established.** Sharpologist's Dec 29, 2025 episode page
states: "Declaration Grooming LLC (formerly L&L Grooming and
Declaration Brushworks) owned and operated by **Scott Stewart** is
closing down shop. Scott made the announcement last month and will
completely shut down on January 31st." The transcript adds: the
announcement went out in Scott's own email to clients ("he put all
the reasonings in the email that he sent out to his clients");
reasons = rising costs, softening demand ("burnout"); remaining site
stock sold through Jan 31, all custom brush orders to be fulfilled.
Corroborated: Smallflower's Declaration brand page ("owned and
operated by Scott Stewart out of Morenci, Michigan"). Brush lore
from the same episode: B1–B18 hand-tied knot batches (B1 most
collectible, B3 densest/most controversial); Scott was "the only
American brush maker that hand-tied his own knots" circa 2015–16.
Recorded [[People]] (Scott Stewart). Confidence: established.
