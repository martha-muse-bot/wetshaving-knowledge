*Confidence: established · Source: [[lineages|Lineages]]*

**[catalog] [[lines/declaration-grooming|Declaration Grooming]] CLOSED — announced Dec 2025, shut down
January 31, 2026.** Post-closure dataset uses (attestation through
2026) are destash use, not new stock. Source: Sharpologist "Wet
Shaving Talk For 29 December 2025" (via catalog validation research,
2026-10-21). Confidence: lead — confirm with a primary announcement
before treating closure dates as established.
**ADDENDUM 2026-10-31 — owner named, announcement channel + dates
FIRMED to established.** Sharpologist's Dec 29, 2025 episode page
states: "Declaration Grooming LLC (formerly L&L Grooming and
Declaration Brushworks) owned and operated by **Scott Stewart** is
closing down shop. Scott made the announcement last month and will
completely shut down on January 31st." The transcript adds: the
announcement went out in Scott's own email to clients ("he put all
the reasonings in the email that he sent out to his clients");
reasons = rising costs, softening demand ("burnout"); remaining site
stock sold through Jan 31, all custom brush orders to be fulfilled.
Corroborated: Smallflower's Declaration brand page ("owned and
operated by Scott Stewart out of Morenci, Michigan"). Brush lore
from the same episode: B1–B18 hand-tied knot batches (B1 most
collectible, B3 densest/most controversial); Scott was "the only
American brush maker that hand-tied his own knots" circa 2015–16.
Recorded [[people|People]] (Scott Stewart). Confidence: established.
**ADDENDUM 2026-11-04 (learner brush-line census — "Declaration
Brushworks" confirmed as the brush line's 2016–2018 name):**
dataset-side confirmation that the Brushworks name was the brush
line's marketing name 2016–2018: 92 uses of "Declaration Brushworks
Washington #1" (2016–17), "Declaration Brushworks 30mm Franklin
'Red Zero'" + "The Franklin #7" (2016), "Batch 3 Declaration
Brushworks Jefferson Nebula brush" (B&B 523499, 2018-03, "Declaration
Brushworks site"). So the sequence is: L&L Grooming (2016) → brushes
marketed as **Declaration Brushworks** 2016–2018 while soaps were
Declaration Grooming → all Declaration Grooming. This does NOT change
the rename date (~mid-Nov 2017, first community strings) — the two
name forms overlapped in the market. Catalog: fold all "Declaration
Brushworks" brush strings into the DG brand group, never a separate
maker ([[hardware|Hardware lore]] 2026-11-04 census [catalog]).

resolved; [[lines|Product lines]] DG addendum) [catalog]:** (d) "DFS 2017"

debuted — the first "Declaration Grooming (formerly L&L Grooming)"
strings (2017-11-20) are DFS 2017 soap strings because TryThatSoap
