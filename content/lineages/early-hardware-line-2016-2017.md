**Early hardware line (2016–2017):** Stirling sold its own-branded DE razors by July 2016 (Sharpologist Stirling badger review) — the [[aliases/stirling-slant|"Stirling Slant"]] (~$12, B&B firsthand; $13 factory-seconds price per a Sep 2016 Reddit first-shave review) is the early line's slant; 20 dataset strings, 4 authors, 2017–2019; catalog `unverified/razor/stirling-slant` (attested) carries the promotion evidence. The slant's OEM is UNPINNED (Wayback CDX for "slant" returned nothing in any year — the maker page, if it existed, was never archived; a Reddit user calls it a Merkur 37C clone, user claim only). **[[lineages/correction-2026-11-05|CORRECTION 2026-11-05]]:** the live store still sells DE razors (DE3P1S $25.95, in stock) — only the slant is delisted, not the razor line.
