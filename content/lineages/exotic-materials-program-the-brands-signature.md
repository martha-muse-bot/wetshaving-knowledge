**Exotic-materials program (the brand's signature — running LE/custom series, ~1/month cadence):** Damascus (stainless; "Shiva" full-Damascus 2020, $1,200), Mokume-Gane (Sean claims the ONLY mokume-gane razor ever released), Titanium Damascus/Damtanium (Grade 2 + 5 custom-forged Ti Damascus), 316L+coper hybrid, copper (Cx-Cu, store 2022-03-30), brass (Cx-Brass, store 2022-12-08), carbon fiber + titanium hybrids (**Cx-Titan** 32–34 g w/ Perlage cap; **Cx-Atlas** ~30 g, USA-forged Ti + USA carbon fiber — the lightest Cx to date; **Cx-Graphite** ~31 g per maker blog / 38 g per the Sean interview — weight conflict noted, cite with source), 18K gold inlays (Red/Yellow, one with G VS diamond), 2-tone finishes, DLC coatings. Named LEs are ONE-OFF or small runs (V42/Ronin/Templar/Hada/Kojin/Osiris/Sparta/Marine/Crown/Apex/Lowe/Niwa/Takeda/Avem/GII/Iliad/Carbide — each razor carries either a unique serial number or a unique NAME, per the gallery page); the "X of 6" series (Atrum 2/6, Templar 3/6, Hada 4/6, V42 5/6, Lowe 6/6) are six-piece named editions; 1-of-1 customs (Cx-Blue, Cx-Black, rose-gold-inlay Ti) exist. **Dataset LE names that fold to the Cx entry:** Apex (2021, Jgalaxy), Red (2021, Jgalaxy), Atlas (2021, _walden_/wallygator88 — "courtesy /u/mr_onemoretime"), G2 (2021), Utah (2022, Zimora), Fuso (2025, brokenjaw622, Mokume-Gane — "Fuso" is the maker's name), 7Q-89 (2025, Ramjet615 — maker serial-name convention, LEAD-grade detail), Crown/Kojin/Ronin/Lowe/V42/Templar/Hada/Atrum/Niwa/Takeda/GII/Avem/Sparta/Osiris/Marine/Carbide (maker-side named editions; zero or near-zero dataset strings — future-use watcher).
