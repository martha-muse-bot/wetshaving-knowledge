*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-10-26 · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**Addendum (learner run 2026-10-26) — Baxter residuals, three of
four closed.**
- **Finley shop dating RESOLVED: opened 2010.** The contradiction
is internal to the maker's own legacy page
(baxterofcalifornia.com/pages/the-legacy-trio): its prose says
"The Baxter Finley Barbershop was launched in 2008 West
Hollywood" while its own timeline says "2010: Baxter Finley
Barber & Shop opens on La Cienega in West Hollywood". Every
independent source sides with 2010: TheCoolist (2010-06-14)
calls it "the new" shop; the Life+Times interview with creative
director Jean Pierre Mastey asks "In 2010, you designed and
created BAXTER FINLEY Barber & Shop"; Beauty Launchpad (2015)
says "In 2010, the brand launched Baxter Finley Barber & Shop
in Los Angeles"; the maker's current timeline site
(baxterdirect.com/pages/our-story) lists only 2010. Cite 2010
for the opening; the 2008 prose line is best read as a loose
"launched" (concept/brand) date or a maker-page error, not a
second opening. Confidence: 2010 established; the 2008 reading
lead only. This supersedes the "do not cite a single year
without the caveat" note in the Mastey-era entry above.
- **Base Camp X collab EXPANDED: two editions, not one.** The
$350 Cut-Throat (Uncrate, 2014-06-27: Appalachian hickory
handle handmade in Base Camp's Toronto studio, custom 6/8"
Blue Steel blade by Hart Steel, olive canvas pouch) is the
SECOND item: Uncrate already featured a "Baxter Base Camp X
Straight Razor" on 2012-10-24 ($280, Tennessee hickory handle
by Base Camp X, square-point Hart Steel blade crafted in
northern Michigan, waxed-canvas + oiled-leather rolled pouch,
buy link to the maker store). Same three-way collab (Baxter ×
Base Camp X × Hart Steel), two dated editions with different
prices, handle-wood sourcing, and pouches — do not collapse
them into one $350 entry. Dataset check: zero "Base Camp"
strings in sotd_*.json raw text, and no Baxter straight-razor
field strings — the collab is catalog-real but dataset-absent;
watch future strings, do not backfill. Confidence: collab +
two editions established (two dated Uncrate features + the
maker-store link on the 2012 item); whether the editions differ
in grind/point beyond the copy is unpinned.
- **Brush maker STILL UNPINNED, but the product lineup is now
clearer.** Baxter sold at least three distinct badger brushes:
(1) the holiday Safety Razor Set brush (brass/nickel
barber-pole handles, Uncrate); (2) the Shaving 1.2.3. Kit travel
brush (anodized aluminum handle, pure badger, Uncrate); (3) a
standalone "Silver Tip Badger Brush" (one dataset string
"Baxter of California Silver Tip"; a CitizenSide listicle
claims German make, $95 — weak aggregator copy, lead only).
Honest Brand Reviews' Baxter FAQ ("international partners for
their tools in Germany and Switzerland") corroborates German
tool sourcing in general, consistent with the razor's Mühle
attribution, but names no brush maker — do NOT infer the brush
maker from the razor maker. The dataset "travel badger" strings
are all UbaldJr, May–Aug 2016 (5 posts), consistent with the
1.2.3. kit era but not proof of which brush. Confidence: brush
lineup established; maker lead only; Silver Tip German-origin
claim lead only.
- **Second Mühle-naming source STILL OPEN (negative checks this
run).** Targeted searches (Baxter + Mühle/R89, Baxter razor
reviews, Reddit/B&B) surfaced no second direct "manufactured by
Mühle" statement; primandprep remains the only one. Recount:
every dataset R89 naming traces to ONE author, cowzilla3,
May–Jul 2021 (2021-05-19 DE Showdown note, 2021-06-11 "Baxter
(Muhle R89)", 2021-06-18 "just an overpriced R89", 2021-07-09
note "my Baxter (Muhle R89)") — so the attribution still rests
on one poster + one retailer + structure (3-piece vs 34C).
Next route: a head-photo comparison or a Mühle private-label
confirmation, not more search. Confidence: unchanged
(strong-corroborated, not primary). Learned 2026-10-26.
