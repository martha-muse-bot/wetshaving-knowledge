**[catalog] Vikings Blade — brand group settled, NO merge with Baili
(resolved 2026-10-22, backlog item; see [[Hardware lore]] for the full
story).** Design house, Vullion Group Pty Ltd, Melbourne, Australia
(Sharpologist 2019); formerly traded as the "Julian Vue" company;
brand name now Vikings Blade (maker's own site). Three signature
razors: Chieftain (original design; Baili built the brass mold from
VB's CAD — Baili simultaneously launched its own lookalike BD179),
Godfather (mild TTO), Vulcan; later adjustable Emperor Augustus
(possibly QShave OEM — one B&B lead, unverified). Catalog rule
applied: Baili is at most a contract manufacturer on VB designs;
brand of record stays Vikings Blade; BD179 is Baili's own product
and a separate entry. Learned 2026-10-22. Confidence: established
(maker's own page, fetched live; corroborating forum firsthands).
