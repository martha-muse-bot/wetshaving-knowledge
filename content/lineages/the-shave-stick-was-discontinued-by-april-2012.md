*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**The shave stick was discontinued by ~April 2012** — this settles
the backlog item. A Badger & Blade thread titled "Irisch Moos
Shave Stick Discontinued?" (published 2012-04-29, 120 posts of
stock-up discussion, last edited 2012-05-12) treats the stick as
gone; later dataset notes corroborate ("wish they still made the
stick", kaesees 2019-10-28; "the shaving soap has been
discontinued for a long time now", VisceralWatch 2022-06-28).
No maker discontinuation announcement was found, so "by April
2012" is the pin, not an exact end date — the stick may have
ended slightly earlier. The stick was a tallow soap, 100 g
(ClassicShaving review: "this shaving soap stick contains
tallow... $15.00 USD for a 100 gram / 3.5 ounce shaving stick").
Every post-2012 stick string in the dataset is NOS/destash, not
current production. Confidence: discontinued-by-2012 established
(contemporaneous forum + later dataset corroboration); exact end
date a lead.
