**[catalog] Williams Mug Shaving Soap DISCONTINUED — effective November
2021; Combe Inc. email confirmation February 14, 2022 (resolved
2026-10-22, backlog item: Sharpologist's "Williams shut down" claim =
TRUE for the product, with exact dates).** Brand-of-record: **Williams**,
owned by **Combe Inc.** (White Plains, NY; privately held, founded 1949
by Ivan Combe). Ownership chain: J.B. Williams (est. 1840s) → Nabisco
1971 → SmithKline 1982 (→ SmithKline Beecham → GSK) → Combe acquired
North American rights 2002 (mfg moved NJ → White Plains) → Combe bought
the brand outright from GSK end of 2009 and distributes Williams (and
Aqua Velva) worldwide. **Discontinuation evidence:** B&B thread 620643
(started 2015 on the rumor): post #648 (Feb 2022) — a community member
phoned Combe (1-800-431-2610, ref #776418): rep confirmed discontinuation
**effective November 2021**, Walmart stock through ~March 2022; post #657
(Feb 14, 2022) quotes the Combe email verbatim
(consumer.resources@combe.com, "Cesar, Consumer Resources Consultant",
3:40:15 PM EST): "We are sorry to inform you that it has been
discontinued." Dataset curve (post counts mentioning Williams):
27/33/122/229/157/216 hits 2016–2021 → 91/62 in 2022–2023, then a
2024 nostalgia spike (175: Lather Games "Drugstore Day" tributes, "RIP
Williams" notes), 155 in 2025, 83 through Oct 19, 2026 — all post-2021
uses are destash. **Catalog impact [catalog]:** validators date the
Williams Mug Shaving Soap entry discontinued Nov 2021; primary evidence
route = SOTD photos, retailer pages, Wayback/Combe caches — never the
current Combe site. Aqua Velva (also Combe) is UNAFFECTED — it continues
in production; do not conflate the mug-soap discontinuation with the
brand. Method note: Combe's 2015 "limited distribution, not discontinued"
call-center answer preceded the real discontinuation by six years —
call-center denials are not closure evidence either way (the recency
trap's mirror image: the boy-who-cried-wolf trap).
