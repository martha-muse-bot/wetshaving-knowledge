*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**Product timeline (ESTABLISHED):** Masamune (mild DE, 0.63 mm gap) = first product, 2016–17; the Nodachi (aggressive DE, 0.90 mm gap, longer/thicker handle) was announced in a newsletter "after the first year of customer reviews/feedback" (B&B 563791 quoting Tatara's own newsletter, c.2018); Muramasa = adjustable (316L stainless; "recently launched" at GlossWire interview time); Amakuni = FIRST single-edge razor, Artist Club format with MAGNETIC top cap (gap 0.85 mm / exposure 0.06 mm / 29° angle; fits all AC blades except green Feather Light), launched October 2024 (B&B thread 658282 published 2024-10-24; Sharpologist podcast 2026-06-11) — steel AND titanium versions (barbieriuniti lists the Ti matte at 85 g; steel per B&B firsthand 80.7 g). Numbered early Masamune plates exist (#10 dark finish, #282 satin observed); @TATARA RAZORS told B&B the numbering was "discontinued around 2018 to simplify identification, especially between the parts of the Masamune Series" — so a NUMBERED plate = pre-2018 piece (photo-ID signal for validators).
