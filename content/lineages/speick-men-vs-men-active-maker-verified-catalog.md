**Speick — Men vs Men Active, maker-verified [catalog]:** Speick Naturkosmetik GmbH & Co. KG (Leinfelden-Echterdingen, founded 1928 by Walter Rau) sells TWO parallel men's lines. Classic Men: Shaving Cream 75g (Art. 170), Shaving Soap 50g puck, Shaving Foam 200ml, Shaving Stick 50g, After Shave Lotion 100ml, After Shave Balsam Sensitive 100ml, Eau de Toilette 50ml (2012). Men Active (launched JAN 2013 — B&B PIF Jan 20, 2013): Shaving Cream 75ml (Art. 390), Shaving Soap 150g jar, After Shave Lotion 100ml — and NOTHING else in shaving (no Active balm/EdT/stick/foam). The classic line is the lavender/beeswax scent; the Active line is the citrus/reed-extract scent ("The grey 'Active' line is purely focused on citrus" — Teufelskraft, dataset note 2022-05-12). CORRECTION: the 01:06 run's "no plain-Men jar soap exists" (from Violey's list) is WRONG — the maker lists the 50g Men Shaving Soap puck live; Violey simply doesn't stock it. Retailer lists decide distribution, never product existence.
