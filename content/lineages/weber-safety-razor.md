**Weber Safety Razor — heads discontinued, handles-only; full
closure SETTLED 2026-10-23 (see the Weber closure entry below —
the "full closure = open lead" note is superseded).** Founded
2010, launched 2012 — first
modern USA-made DE razor, 316L stainless, cast heads + machined
handles. Three heads (DLC, ARC, PH), three handles (Classic,
Bulldog, Wave). Stopped making complete razors ~2019 but "continues
to produce safety razor handles" (Sharpologist, Joe Borrelli, 2019,
updated 2020-06-12); B&B "Why doesn't Weber make razors?" (~2021)
confirms handles-only, citing head QC complaints and high costs —
exactly the 2021-06-25 dataset note's trajectory ("made handles
only, then heads and handles... complaints about head QC caused them
to scale back to handles"). [catalog] (hardware brand;
Weber-head strings are pre-~2020 products).
