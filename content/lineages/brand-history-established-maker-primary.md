**Brand history, ESTABLISHED (maker-primary: beiersdorf.com brand-history
page + Beiersdorf corporate timeline + de.wikipedia Florena, all read this
run).** Waldheim, Saxony: Bergmann founded the perfumery/soap factory 1852;
the Florena trademark was registered 9 Feb 1920 (granted 22 Apr 1920) for
talcum powder. After WWII the plant was in the Soviet zone; nationalized
1946 as VEB Rosodont-Werke, later VEB Florena Waldheim / VEB Florena
Waldheim-Döbeln, ultimately part of the GDR's largest cosmetics maker
(VEB Kosmetik-Kombinat Berlin). From 1960 Florena Creme shipped in the
blue-white tin deliberately echoing Nivea's — Beiersdorf (Hamburg) sued
unsuccessfully over the tin; settlement kept Florena out of Western
Europe. It became the "Nivea of the Eastern Bloc" (generic term for
DDR cosmetics; shaving soap, perfume, perfumed soaps among the exports).
Licensed NIVEA production began at Waldheim 20 Sep 1989 ("two blue-white
tins with different names" off one line). Privatized Jun 1990 as Florena
Cosmetic GmbH (management buy-out, Treuhand); Beiersdorf took 24.9% in
2001, 100% since 2002. Repositioned 2007 around natural-origin ingredients
(Western-Europe natural-cosmetics play); the subsidiary was renamed
Beiersdorf Manufacturing Waldheim in 2012; Oct 2014 Beiersdorf discontinued
several Florena lines incl. shaving foams (Rasierschäume); the Waldheim
plant closed mid-2023. Sources: beiersdorf.com/brands/brand-history/florena;
de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florena (crawled 2026-08-30). Confidence: established.
