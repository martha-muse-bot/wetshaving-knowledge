**[catalog] Personna / AccuTec**: the DE blade sold as Personna Comfort
Coated ([["Lab Blue"]]) is one product group with AccuTec's AccuForge
Super Stainless MicroCoat as a current alternate retail name.
Source: catalog decision 2026-10-20 (Target + Razor Emporium + RBC
pages). Confidence: established.
**Packaging lead (single-source, 2026-10-29):** B&B Blade Bonanza
2025 thread 662763 p.13 #242 (unattributed poster, Feb 2025) states
AccuTec discontinued Lab Blues and replaced them with AccuForge —
"same blade...comes in a box of 250 only." Record as a LEAD-level
packaging factoid for the AccuForge entry; do not assert it as the
entry's pack format without a second source. (Same thread's #243
shows "Lab blades" still being traded/POS'd in Feb 2025 — the
Lab Blue→AccuForge retail transition was not instant.)
