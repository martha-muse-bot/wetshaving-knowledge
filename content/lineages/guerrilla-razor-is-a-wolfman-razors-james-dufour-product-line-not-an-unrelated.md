*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

Guerrilla (razor) is a Wolfman Razors ([[James Dufour]]) product line, NOT an
unrelated brand. Gen 1 (in SOTD data from 2016): stainless SB/OC sold direct
on wolfmanrazors.com, followed by an anodized-aluminum color version
(e.g. Royal Blue w/ Black Trim, $175 CAD) also sold on wolfmanrazors.com —
B&B thread "Wolfman Razors - GUERRILLA RAZOR - ANODIZED ALUMINUM," Oct 2017,
OP links the wolfmanrazors.com product page. Positioned as Wolfman's
simpler, readily available, less-finished line. In 2023 Wolfman relaunched
it as a redesigned "2023 version"/v2 (changed head geometry: blade held
closer to edge, v1's large lather slots gone; gaps .54/.69/.84/.99;
materials aluminum in anodized colors incl. Matte Black and Camo Brown,
bronze, stainless, titanium), sold via wolfmanrazors.com + email orders
through Tara AND a dedicated storefront, guerrillarazors.com (Shopify, CAD,
branded simply "Guerrilla Razors"). Same operation throughout — a
storefront/brand split, no third party. The color-anodized "Guerrilla
Razors Camo Brown .99" strings are this v2 aluminum line. Sources: B&B
530565 (2017), B&B 645197 + DFS "Wolfman Guerrilla 2023 Version" (2023),
guerrillarazors.com (fetched 2026-10-21), SOTD dataset string analysis
(1,521 posts; "Guerrilla Razors"-prefixed forms appear only from 2023).
Confidence: established (spin-off to a separate company: refuted).
