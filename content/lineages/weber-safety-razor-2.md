*Confidence: lead · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**[[Weber Safety Razor]] — FULL CLOSURE settled (2026-10-23;
quiet wind-down, no farewell announcement — same pattern as Cold
River Soap Works).** Heads ended ~2018–19 (MIM-cast QC problems —
"wonky" plates; B&B 548620 #5, #15; the 2021-06-25 dataset note
narrates the trajectory); handles-only ~2019–2021 (Sharpologist
2019–2020; the 2020-06-20 dataset note "the maker moved away from
making heads and now makes only handles"). By ~2021 the company was
selling down blemished-handle dregs — B&B 548620 #12: "Their
website links to ebay which only contains two handles. Both
blemished. The amazon link doesn't have anything at all. I'd say
they pretty much look defunct at this point"; #11/#13 report
buying blemished handles as the last stock. Sharpologist's
"Analyzing The Traditional Wet Shaving Industry 2021" lists Weber
among "razors no longer being produced" (2020 secondary-market
value spike). **weberrazor.com now serves a DreamHost "Domain is
Parked" page (observed live 2026-10-23)** — the business is gone.
(The 2026-10-22 DNS failure was a sandbox/network flap, like the
BullGoose flap; treat parked, not flapping, as the verdict.)
Wayback CDX (2026-10-23): last 200 captures of weberrazor.com run
2024-04-22 → 2026-01-23 (20260123120913); what those 2024–2026
captures served — live shop or zombie/parked page — is unpinned
(snapshot read blocked from this sandbox — live-browser job).
Dataset tail 2021–2026: destash of vintage heads + handles reused
as aftermarket handles on other makers' heads ("Frankenbradley —
Karve CB B plate and cap, Weber stainless handle," "Wolfman WR1
.61mm head w/ Weber Classic handle"). Founder "Ed" (B&B interview
2013-01-07 — surname unpinned, lead); Clayton, MO / St. Louis area
(B&B 548620 #1). **[[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]:** no new Weber
product has existed since ~2019 (heads) / ~2021 (handles); every
Weber string is legacy or destash; "Weber handle" on another head
is aftermarket reuse — never a new Weber product. Handle
inventory (Classic, Bulldog, Wave) dates ~2010–2021.
