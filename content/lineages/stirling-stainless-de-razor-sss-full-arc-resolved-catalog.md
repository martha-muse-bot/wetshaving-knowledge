**Stirling Stainless DE Razor ("SSS") — full arc RESOLVED [catalog]:** launched ~Mar 2021 (first product-page capture 2021-03-05; first dataset use Eructate 2021-03-29), 316L stainless, Rod-designed, serial-etched base plates, "Made in the USA" (batch 1 only), $159.95 single head / $179.95 both plates. Plates: **ST = Standard (43g), HA = Hyper-Aggressive (46g)** — maker-side abbreviations from the page's Specifications section. Handles Austin (57g) / Dallas (55g) / Fort Worth (50g) — the same three names as the 2017 DE3P handles, so handle alone never decides the razor. **Batch 2 (Jul 2022): the US manufacturer outsourced the job to China without Stirling's knowledge; the 'Made in the USA' etching was ground off and the razors sold greatly discounted — $70 single / $95 both** (maker's own page copy). Batch-2 SSS razors are Chinese-made; never cite US origin for a 2022+ SSS razor. Austin handle sold out by 2023-09-27; the whole SSS line is absent from the live store 2026-11-09 (DE3P line still live at $25.95). Prototype HA plates circulated in 2020 before launch (shaveSymptomatic 2020-03-26; maker's own 2019 prototype-aggressive-head post). Dataset: 193 SSS razor-field rows (PhilosphicalZombie 186), 61 HA rows / 14 authors, still shaved Jan 2026. **Catalog: SSS clusters are owed their own entry — several are mislabeled "Stainless Handle DE Razor" (the DE3P line); never fold SSS into DE3P.**
