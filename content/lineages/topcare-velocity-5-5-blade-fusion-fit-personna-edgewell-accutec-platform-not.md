*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-10-25 · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**[catalog] TopCare Velocity 5 (5-blade, Fusion-fit) = Personna
(Edgewell/AccuTec) platform, NOT Dorco (established 2026-10-25;
resolves the backlog's Velocity 5 lead).** The contradiction that
framed the lead: an eBay reviewer called a Top Care 5-blade "made
by Dorco", but the brand's own SmartLabel copy for "TopCare
Everyday Velocity 5 Blade Razor" (UPC 036800200203) says "Fits
EasyFit and Gillette Fusion Handles" and "Blades made in USA from
imported steel. Handle and cartridge made in China." EasyFit is
Personna's own compatible-cartridge line — Personna sold "EasyFit
5" cartridges on personnashaving.com (coupon listings) and
"Personna EasyFit 3 Mach3" cartridges on Amazon — so a cartridge
that fits EasyFit handles and carries USA-made blades sits on
Personna's platform, and Dorco (Korean blades; no USA blade plant)
is ruled out by origin. The eBay Dorco claim is retired as a
line-conflation: the reviewer was almost certainly thinking of
TopCare's 6-blade, which IS Dorco (above). This is the
multi-OEM-by-line rule in action — never carry one TopCare line's
attribution to another. Same P-04 treatment: TopCare-branded
entry, Personna USA manufacture attribute. Corroborating pattern:
the sister store brand CareOne sells "5 Blade Cartridges with
EasyFit Technology" (Stop & Shop listing) — EasyFit is a shared
Personna system name across store brands, not a TopCare invention.
Dataset scope: ZERO "Velocity 5" strings in sotd_2016–2026 (all
"Velocity" hits are the Tedalus Velocity-ONE razor or prose) —
future-use watcher, not a current queue item. Residual
verification route: a Velocity 5 pack/blister photo naming
Personna/Verona, or a Topco supplier statement, would turn this
label-fingerprint case into direct confirmation. LEAD parked:
Personna's own-brand "Caliber 5 Edge Razor" (Wet The Face review,
2019) may be the EasyFit 5 system under Personna's own name —
unconfirmed. Learned 2026-10-25. Confidence: established
(brand's own label copy + EasyFit=Personna + USA origin; Dorco
claim retired as conflation).
