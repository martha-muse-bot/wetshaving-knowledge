**TSM = The Shave Market (Facebook group).** Maker's own 2016-08-29 Sharpologist interview (sharpologist.com/interview-chatillon-lux/): "I originally made a limited edition fougère for The Shave Market on Facebook, named TSM Fougère, aptly enough." It was popular with non-members, so the mods blessed a transition into CL's regular lineup "coming out on October 3" (2016). Dataset: 934 "TSM Fougère" product strings, 121 authors, 2016–2026 — one of CL's higher-volume scents; the soap side was the P-16 DG/L&L collab ("DG/CL TSM Fougere", "L&L TSM Fougere" ×21, "Declaration Bison TSM Fougere" ×13 = Bison-base era). Parallel case in the same interview: Catie's Bubbles made **Le Marché du Rasage** (French for "The Shave Market") as its TSM exclusive — so repete66219's 2016 "CB TSM soap" = Le Marché du Rasage. The group's brush group-buys appear as "Wolf Whiskers TSM GROUP 'ZYDECO'" and "2017 TSM Badger"; 2016 notes chatter ("Over in TSM this new artisan...", "TSM 30 Day Straight Challenge") is the same group.
