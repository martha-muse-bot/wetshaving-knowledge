**Red Hollow ≠ Dead Hollow (established as distinct).** "Red Hollow"
has TWO independent attestations — Priusaurus's 2024-11 SOTD soap
string and a ~Mar-2026 B&B firsthand review (aged apples, slippery
elm bark, caramel-apple read) — predating the 2025-announced "Dead
Hollow" (pecan/pumpkin-forward). Different years, different notes,
one letter apart in name. Catalog: never fold Red Hollow into
Dead Hollow; Red Hollow owes an entry (no catalog cluster yet).
