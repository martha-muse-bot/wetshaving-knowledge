**[catalog] Zingari Man ← Smitten Soapery (rename, October 27, 2018).**
Founder [[Heather Melton]] launched **Smitten Soapery** in 2014 (after two
years of crafting) and renamed it **Zingari Man** on Oct 27, 2018 —
"a brand fully dedicated to high-performance men's grooming"
(maker's own origin-story blog post, "The Origin Story of Zingari
Man," zingariman.com, Mar 6, 2025; corroborated by the Oct 2019
"HELP US CELEBRATE!" post referencing "our rebranding story").
The 2021 dataset note ("The rebranding occurred in the fall of 2018,
which can be confirmed in the AMA that Heather did with us") and the
2019 mammothben note ("Zingari Man Lothario... formerly known as
Smitten Soapery") both point at the same event. ONE brand group for
the catalog: pre-Oct-2018 "Smitten Soapery" strings = Zingari Man
(name = era attribute). Established. [catalog]
