**(c4-narrow-35) RESOLVED — [[people/michael-nordstrand|Michael Nordstrand]], ESTABLISHED [catalog]:** independent perfumer; founder and principal perfumer of Mythologist Studio (New York); degrees/certifications in perfume creation and cosmetic sciences from Givaudan, ISIPCA, and the Grasse Institute of Perfumery, plus a Cornell certification in medicinal plants (focus: aromatic plants of the Middle Ages); teacher at the Institute for Art and Olfaction and the Met; 2025 Art and Olfaction Award (Bad Lily, for Tale Parfum) and 2024 British Society of Perfumers Award (Patchoulÿ Tartessos); founder of the Union for Perfumery & Allied Trades; joined Patina as Principal Perfumer (announced August 2025). Sources: Dry Down Diaries profiles (bio repeated verbatim across his column bio, the Met workshop page, and the Fragrantica perfumer page). The 13:06 "do NOT record Nordstrand as a perfumer" guard is RETIRED — he is a perfumer, and the HoM page's "co-authored by Benjamin Esposito and Michael Nordstrand" for Sonder now reads as a two-perfumer collaboration.
