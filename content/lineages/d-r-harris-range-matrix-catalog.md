**D.R. Harris range matrix [catalog]:** creams in ALL NINE scents (Almond,
Arlington, Eucalyptus, Lavender, Marlborough, Rose, Sandalwood, Twenty
Nine, Windsor — Naked Armor trial-pack copy lists "every variety of
shaving cream"; maker URL for the Almond cream tube). Soaps in six
(Almond, Arlington, Lavender, Marlborough, Sandalwood, Windsor + sticks);
Eucalyptus and Rose are cream-only. Founded 1790, St James's, London
(manandshaving brand page). The catalog has cream entries only for
Lavender and Rose; the other five scents carry cream aliases folded onto
soap entries, and Eucalyptus has no entry at all.
