*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**[catalog] Cerberus (HoM × [[Declaration Grooming]] × Noble Otter collab)**:
TWO releases, not one. Release 1 "Cerberus" dropped ~Aug 14, 2021 (second drop
Sep 18, 2021; NOT Dec 2021 — correction 2026-10-22): the "trifecta"/"Three Heads"
set = DG soap (milksteak) + Noble Otter aftershave (splash) + DG-bottled EdP, all
in HoM's Cerberus scent (sandalwood, ripe apricot/fruit, amber, resin; close to
HoM's Sandalorian). Release 2 "Cerberus Fougère" ~May 2022: a DISTINCT second
scent (green fougère — fern, florals, star anise, resinous woods — not the fruity
sandalwood), same lineup (DG soap, NO aftershave, DG-bottled EdP with its own
"Cerberus Fougere" label). Division of labor (u/Tetriside Lather Games 2023 writeup,
djudge.me/post/23/5): HoM created the fragrance, NO contributed the aftershave
formula, DG made the soap, bottled the EdP, and sold everything through DG's site.
DG also appears to have sold a Cerberus-scented "Liniment" (their balm-type
aftershave) — trythatsoap collection 2606 lists "Declaration Grooming/House of
Mammoth - Cerberus - Liniment" and 100+ SOTD posts name it, but no bottle photo
found; treat as likely-real, evidence-thin. Attribution per P-16 (user ruling
2026-10-22, applied to all six entries): joint collab credit, House of Mammoth
primary — soap "HoM × DG", aftershaves "HoM × NO" (NO's) and "HoM × DG" (DG's),
EdP "HoM × DG". OPEN TENSION for re-review: the EdP bottle label reads
"Declaration Grooming — Cerberus — Eau de Parfum" (DG first; SOTD photo evidence)
and DG bottled/sold it — label/retail presentation points to DG, while community
naming (HoM-first ~3:1) and scent creation point to HoM. Sources: SOTD dataset
string/note mining 2026-10-22; djudge.me/post/23/5; fraganceswiki.com/
declaration-grooming/cerberus (brand listing). Confidence: established (release
history, products); lead (DG liniment bottle/label details).
