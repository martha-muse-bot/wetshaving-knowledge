**Disambiguation note [catalog]:** Krasimir_ 2020-09-05 (soap "Florena";
Gillette 40s Super Speed) is almost certainly NOT the Canadian product:
his other 2020 soaps are Karo Sensitive and Barbon (Bulgarian/Hungarian
drugstore creams) — he reads as an Eastern-European poster whose "Florena"
is the contemporary European Florena line (still a live Beiersdorf brand
there in 2020). European Florena men's shaving products (Energy line,
ASB, Rasiercreme) were discontinued in the 2010s — gut-rasiert.de's
"Florena Men After Shave Balsam" forum thread (posts through ~2014, a
later post ~2020: "10 Jahre später sind die Rasurprodukte von Florena
längst Geschichte, sowohl ASB als auch RC usw." = "10 years later the
Florena shaving products are long history") and the Oct 2014 production
discontinuation incl. shaving foams. Post-2014 European "Florena" shaving
strings are NOS/destash by definition. Confidence: discontinuation era
established on two independent community/company sources; Krasimir_'s
sourcing = lead (single inferential chain, never cite as certain).
