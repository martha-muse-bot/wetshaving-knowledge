**[catalog] Catalog guidance.** ONE brand group for the SOTD
"Pinaud"/"Clubman"/"Pinaud Clubman" strings — the US Clubman line
under AII. No rename, no split entry: "Clubman" is the line name, not
a separate brand, and the French house is not a second brand group
for dataset purposes. Era attribute worth recording on Clubman
entries: the 1990s move to plastic bottles forced a reformulation
(Bespoke Unit) — glass-bottle-era vs plastic-bottle-era Clubman is a
real formulation break, and vintage glass bottles still circulate as
destash/collector items. Learned 2026-10-25.
