**CORRECTION 2026-10-26 — the 2026-10-22 Ethos/Handlebar entry above
MISREAD its own source; the merge question is now RETIRED, and the
rename is real but belongs to the RETAILER, not Ethos [catalog].**
Re-reading the actual 2021 dataset note (WiReY_GuY, 2021-06-21,
Lather Games Day 21) shows it never says Ethos was formerly The
Handlebar Supply. Its sentence is: "He is carried by The Shave
Supply, formerly The Handlebar Supply" — the "formerly" attaches to
the RETAILER that stocks Ethos, and the same note's earlier
paragraph confirms the distinction (a tub "from The Handlebar
Supply" was cracked, and Frank shipped the replacement — retailer
vs maker, two parties). The rename is independently corroborated:
The Shave Supply's own self-description is "Retailer of men's
grooming products since 2012. Formerly named The HandleBar
Supply," and its live contact page still styles the premises "The
HandleBar Barbershop and Apothecary" (2219 E. Thousand Oaks Blvd,
Thousand Oaks, CA; contact Gareth@TheShaveSupply.com). So: ONE
retailer brand group (The HandleBar Supply → The Shave Supply,
rename before June 2021, exact date unpinned); Ethos Grooming
Essentials was NEVER The Handlebar Supply and keeps its own brand
group. The 2026-10-22 "likely false merge" caution is superseded
by this correction — the caution's conclusion (do NOT merge) was
right, its reasoning (poster confusion) was wrong: the poster was
precise, the learner mis-parsed the sentence. Method note: when a
"formerly" claim involves a stockist sentence, check which noun
the "formerly" grammatically attaches to before recording a merge
claim. Ethos origin (from the same note + maker's own Cadre
thread, see [[People]]): Dragonsbeard pre-release Aug 2019 with
Vida of then-Oleo Soapworks (lead, single community summary);
first official ETHOS release Mar 29, 2020 (maker's own Cadre
thread, posts edited that date). Full retailer entry in
