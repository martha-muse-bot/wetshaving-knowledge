**[[ADDENDUM 2026-10-25]] — Taconic Twin cartridges: product confirmed,
maker still UNPINNED (Personna-leaning lead, NOT established).** The
backlog's open question was whether the injector attribution carries
over to Taconic's other blade line. Answer: it does NOT carry over
automatically — but the circumstantial case points the same way, and
it is worth recording precisely so a later run does not over-claim
it. (1) PRODUCT: the Taconic Twin is real and current — Trac
II/Atra/Vector/Contour-compatible twin-blade cartridges with a
Vitamin E + aloe lubricating strip, sold in 10/100/200-count
(10-per-tray × 10 trays) packs; retailer copy (Amazon-derived,
mirrored by Ubuy/Carrefour) says "Made in the USA"; first Amazon
availability April 22, 2014 (Ubuy product data). The matching
hardware is the Taconic Heavyweight All-Metal Non-Pivoting Trac II
handle (model T2RHWB, ASIN B0036PX5NU, 10 cartridges included),
plus a Taconic ATRA-compatible heavyweight handle. BONUS SCOPE:
Taconic also sells a TRIPLE-blade Atra-compatible cartridge
(30-count, aloe strip) — a second cartridge line validators should
not confuse with the Twin. (2) DATASET: zero Taconic cartridge
strings in sotd_2016–2026_full.json — all 15-16 Taconic blade-field
hits are the injector (see addendum above); Taconic's dataset
presence is otherwise soaps/creams (Bay Rum, Lime, Tequila Lime,
Urban Woods, Eucalyptus Mint), pre-shave oil (168 hits, mostly one
poster redwingzfandave) and aftershaves. So the Twin is a
future-use watcher, not a current queue item. (3) MAKER CASE
(lead): the USA-made claim, if true, narrows the field sharply —
the current Trac II-compatible cartridge makers are Gillette (own
brand), Personna/AccuTec Verona VA (Twin II, Twin Pivot Plus),
Dorco (South Korea) and Schick Japan (Super II); only Personna
makes them in the USA, and store brands in this space are believed
Personna-made (B&B 583007: TopCare Twin Pivot Plus "manufactured
by Personna"). Supporting pattern, not proof: Taconic's ATRA
handle listing ships with Personna Pivot Plus blades (Amazon
listing title "…ATRA Compatible Twin Blade Razor & 10 Personna
Pivot Plus Blades"), and Taconic already bundles Personna
cartridges in the same product family — the same
distributor-sourcing pattern as the injector finding. AGAINST
over-claiming: no direct statement, no pack-marking photo, and no
forum comparison of Taconic Twin vs Personna Twin Pivot Plus
surfaced in 2026-10-25 searches; "Made in USA" is retailer copy,
not a pack photo. [[Catalog guidance]]: Taconic Twin stays its OWN
Taconic entry (brand of record, P-04) when strings appear, format
Cartridge; do NOT record Personna as manufacture attribute yet.
Verification routes (in order): Taconic Twin tray/pack photo
(Verona VA? AccuTec markings? Personna-style tray identical to
Twin Pivot Plus 10-packs?), a side-by-side shave comparison vs
Twin Pivot Plus, or Taconic/Parker confirming the supplier.
Learned 2026-10-25. Confidence: product + USA claim established
(retailer listings); Personna attribution LEAD only.
