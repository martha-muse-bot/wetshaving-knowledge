**Laboratoires Osma company profile, ESTABLISHED (maker's own company page +
multiple French retailer/press relays of the maker copy, all consistent).**
Founded 1957, Lyon; hand-shapes natural potassium-alum stones in France
(workshops at Craponne, Lyon area); all production still French. Positions
itself as the last French alum-stone maker ("dernier façonnier français de
pierre d'alun"). Until the early 1980s it sold essentially shaving products
(Bloc Osma was the barbers' block); then added a haemostatic pencil, alum
deodorants (150/250 g, then 75/100 g formats), and the Osma Tradition /
Osma Bio / Aluna cosmetic lines. Sources: laboratoiresosma.com/en/company/
(fetched via search 2026-10-30); artdubarbier.com "Zoom sur Laboratoire
Osma"; unjourunhomme.com; manitself.fr (Pierre d'alun Osma Tradition 100 g,
"taillée et polie à la main en France"). Confidence: established on the
maker page; the "only production facility in France that extracts and
processes alunite crystal" phrasing is a single retailer's (lantale.com.ua)
— lead-grade, never cite as maker-stated. Learned 2026-10-30.
