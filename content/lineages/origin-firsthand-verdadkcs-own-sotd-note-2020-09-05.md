**Origin (firsthand, verdadkc's own SOTD note, 2020-09-05):** during [[glossary/austere-august|Austere August]] 2020, several participants formed "Team Mammoth"; one member, u/Secret_Squirrel2, passed the TBAS exam (USAF pilot-selection test). verdadkc, u/relided and u/ConquerorChromatic secretly commissioned u/mammothben (Ben, the maker) to create a special soap to recognize the accomplishment. **"Mammoth Cirrus was born"** — one tub was designated **"Flying Squirrel"** and awarded to Squirrel; the rest sold as Cirrus, with generated funds donated to charity. Scent brief: celebrate flight, inspired by clouds and the sky; "light, and subtle, as befits the name" (verdadkc); "a very fresh and clean scent. True to the cloud theme" (ConquerorChromatic, same day). Base: Tusk.
