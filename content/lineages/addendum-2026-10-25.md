**ADDENDUM 2026-10-25 — Taconic=Personna (injector) RESOLVED, with a
format correction.** The backlog item said "Taconic DE blades" — wrong
format. The dataset holds ZERO Taconic DE blade strings; all 15 Taconic
blade-field hits are injector blades, all from one poster (Greyzer,
Parker Adjustable Injector, 2021: "Taconic shave Injector" ×7,
"Taconic Shave Injector blade" ×4, "Taconic Shave Injector Blade" ×3,
"Taconic Injector" ×1). The attribution question is therefore about
the INJECTOR blade, and it resolves as follows. B&B thread 605298
"Anybody Tried Taconic Injector Blades? Any Good?" (Apr 2021, multiple
independent firsthand voices, not one person's understanding): #2 the
Taconic blades are the blades that ship with the new Parker Adjustable
Injector (consistent with the Taconic↔Parker distribution link above);
#11 a UK poster finds the Taconic cartridge IDENTICAL to the
UK-sold "Personna USA" injector cartridge (plastic body, metal key
and slider) and concludes 20-count Taconic = rebranded Personna USA;
#15 "Taconic injector blades are definitely just Personnas"; #20 the
thread's summary: "Ted Pella, Taconic and Barber Supply blades are
all Personna blades." The injector-thread general rule (B&B 323369)
independently states the structural reason: "Any injector blade that
is not branded as a Schick is made by Personna in the US" — current
injector production is a duopoly (Schick China vs Personna USA), so a
US-sold third brand can only be one of the two, and the dispenser
evidence points to Personna. The one counter-signal is also on the
record: the eBay seller refused to name the maker ("company policy"),
and one poster guessed Chinese — outweighed by the dispenser match
and the thread consensus. **The backlog's "which Personna format:
Comfort Coated? Israeli?" sub-question is MISFRAMED for injectors:**
those splits are DE lines; the Personna injector is a single US-made
line (AccuTec, Verona VA — the same plant behind Lab Blue/AccuForge;
community shorthand "Personna PTFE injector blades"). [catalog]
[[Catalog guidance]]: Taconic Shave Injector Blade stays its OWN Taconic
entry (brand of record, P-04 — same pattern as the Ted Pella ruling);
record Personna USA (AccuTec) as manufacture attribute, format
Injector; do NOT merge into any Personna DE entry and do NOT create
a Taconic DE blade entry. LEAD left: Parker-branded INJ20 injector
blades (retailer copy: "Made in USA") are presumably the same
Personna stock under Parker branding — same inference, unconfirmed.
Learned 2026-10-25. Confidence: established (multi-voice community
corroboration + dispenser match + duopoly structure; no maker
admission — the seller's refusal is the only gap).
