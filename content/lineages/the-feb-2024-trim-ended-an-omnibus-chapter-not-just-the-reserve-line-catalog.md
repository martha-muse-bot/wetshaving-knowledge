**The Feb 2024 trim ended an OMNIBUS chapter, not just the Reserve line [catalog]:** all five trimmed scents had Omnibus-base soap reissues. Naked Armor's B&M Omnibus soap listings were created in one Apr 3–10, 2023 bulk batch — Cool 2023-04-06; Cologne Russe 2023-04-06; Fougère Classique, Lavender, and The Full Measure of Man 2023-04-10 (all sold out now). CAVEAT: created_at dates the retailer listing and the Apr-2023 cluster is a bulk import — "listed by Apr 2023", never a launch date. Dataset Omnibus tags prove earlier circulation: Lavender Omnibus in hands Jan 2022 (Sp00kyP00tis ×3), Cool Omnibus Jul 2022 (Madflava81). Omnibus chapter: base launch Dec 2021 → trim Feb 2024. The Omnibus reissue dropped "Reserve" from the title (Naked Armor Cool page: "Omnibus reissue of Barrister's Reserve Cool").
