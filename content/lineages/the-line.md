**The line:** Duke Cannon Supply Co. launched **Proper Cologne** (EdP, 1.7 fl oz, apothecary bottle; "made with clean, naturally derived fragrance oils, crafted in small batches"; "meant to be discovered, not announced") in **July 2019** — The Manual launch article (2019-07-22) names **six scents in three groups**: woodsy Prescott + Sawtooth; aquatic Huron + Seneca; musk Grant + Randolph. Fragrantica lists 9 Duke Cannon perfumes, earliest 2019, newest 2026.
