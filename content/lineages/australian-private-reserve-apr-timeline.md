**Australian Private Reserve (APR) timeline — ESTABLISHED (Vintage
Shaving Australia 5-part APR series, vintageshaving.au, read
2026-11-03; B&B "Australian Private Reserve Soap" threads 2018–2019)
[catalog]:** Australian artisan, maker first name **Dan** (B&B
community, multiple). Ran roughly **2017–2020 (~3 years; "came and
went over the course of about 3 years or so" — VSA)** with
international attention (Maggard, West Coast Shaving, Pasteur
carried it). Timeline: Summer 2017 lineup → 2018 major releases
(F1 base; Mycroft, Fougère Rose Bourbon) → F2 base from the
Ozymandias/Fenchurch era (creamier; B&B firsthand) → TEXAUS LE
collab with Noble Otter (Sept 2018) → Fougère Trois (Feb 2019;
Storybook Soapworks made the SOAP, APR the splash + EdT — P-16
joint-credit pattern) + Torchwood (Feb 2019) → **Raconteur** (Nov
2019, the last full-set non-collab release: soap + aftershave + EdT
+ extrait; notes coffee/whiskey/leather/civet/sandalwood/tuberose;
this is the "APR - Raconteur" soap/aftershave in the Oct 2026
dataset posts) → Highland Springs collab (Nov 2019; "Reserve
Privée" nomenclature) → Noble Otter extrait collab (May 2020) →
Southern Witchcrafts collab (June 2020; SW + Dogwood produced, APR
perfumed) → **wound down mid-2020** (VSA part five is the "final
instalment"; last collabs May–June 2020). Complex-scent reputation
(ambergris accord, animalics). 2026 dataset uses = destash
(tomorrows_LG_today + _walden_ both Oct 2026). Catalog: APR is a
closed brand; Raconteur is a 2019 product, not a 2026 one; P-16
applies to the Storybook/Noble Otter/SW collab releases.
**LEAD (single community source, do NOT assert):** _walden_
2026-10-29 claims APR "is also closed due to the death of the guy
who made it. He was, and still is, infamous in the community."
No web source mentions a death; the VSA series is silent on cause.
Keep the closure cause open.
