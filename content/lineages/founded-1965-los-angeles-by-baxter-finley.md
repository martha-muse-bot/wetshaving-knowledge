**Founded 1965, Los Angeles, by Baxter Finley.** Finley, a New York
advertising executive who had moved to California in the early 1960s,
launched with one product, **Super Shape** — a skin conditioner/
moisturizer marketed specifically to men — because the moisturizers on
the market were made for women. By the early 1970s Baxter was one of
the first complete men's grooming lines (hair, skin, anti-aging,
fragrance, shaving). Sources: maker's own legacy page
(baxterofcalifornia.com/pages/the-legacy-trio, "LIFE LIVED TRUE. SINCE
1965"); MR Magazine interview with owner/CEO Jean Pierre Mastey
(2012-08-14, updated 2015-04-06). Confidence: established.
