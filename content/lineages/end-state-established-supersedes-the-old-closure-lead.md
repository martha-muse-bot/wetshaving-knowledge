*Confidence: established · Source: [[lineages|Lineages]]*

**End state (established, supersedes the old "closure lead"):** YPM did NOT close — it was **acquired by Razor Emporium**, announced **Feb 24, 2025** (razoremporium.com blog, primary source; Sharpologist Wet Shaving Talk 2025-03-10 confirms: "announced at the Annual Meet Up at RE will acquire Yates Razors and produce them onsite"). Deal included the name, designs, tooling, website, and socials. Jake personally completes outstanding orders from his PA shop; Razor Emporium begins production only after Jake's backlog is fulfilled; lifetime warranty on existing models honored; Merica, Winning, and 921 to continue. RE's About-page timeline (2025 entry): "another boutique razor manufacturer was looking to exit the market so a deal was made to acquire their product designs and manufacturing equipment... continuation of the legacy Yates razor designs."
