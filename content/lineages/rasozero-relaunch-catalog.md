**Rasozero relaunch [catalog]:** discontinued 2019; 'reborn with new
formulas and fragrances, accompanied by completely renewed packaging'
(Super Safety Razors, retailer copy). Original trio: Spiffero
(mint/eucalyptus), Agrumella (lemon), Barbacco (tobacco, 'smells like a
marshmallow'). Relaunch trio: Sensitive Skin / Pelli Delicate, Refreshing /
Rinfrescante (citrus + menthol, green), Protective / Protettivo
(anise/licorice, root-beer note). Relaunch names are NOT the old names.
