**Contract maker — Music City Suds (established, maker-side) [catalog]:** every house-soap page carries "handcrafted exclusively for The Shave Mercantile by the 'Unofficial Soap Makers of Country Music', Music City Suds" (musiccitysuds.com). Brand of record stays The Shave Mercantile (P-04); Music City Suds is the manufacture attribute — the same pattern as Nice Pack → Asylum Levante. **Music City Suds itself:** Nashville bath-soap maker founded ~2012 by **Rachel Turner** — country-music pun bar soaps (\"Friends in Aloe Places\", \"I Walk the Lime\", \"Stand By Your Mandarin\") wrapped in reclaimed sheet music, vegetarian/all-natural/food-grade ingredients, local sourcing (Deep South Magazine 2012-05-16; robinwadefurniture.com 2012-07-03). **Surname discrepancy — record both:** shopmadeintn.com (2020) calls her "Rachel Mullen"; the 2012 sources say "Rachel Turner". Likely a married-name change, unpinned — do NOT assert one surname. **[[aliases/music-city-suds-has-its-own-shaving-line|Music City Suds has its OWN shaving line]] too** — "Tennessee Whisker" (see [[aliases|Product aliases]] run section) — it is not only a contract maker.
