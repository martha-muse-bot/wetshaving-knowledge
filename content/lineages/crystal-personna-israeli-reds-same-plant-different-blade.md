**[catalog] Crystal ≠ Personna Israeli Reds — same plant, DIFFERENT
blade.** Resolves the backlog item "Crystal blades = rebranded Personna
Israeli Reds" (Greyzer 2021/2026, Impressive_Donut114 2025): the claim
is TOO STRONG. Both were made at Edgewell's Nazareth, Israel plant, but
the community consensus (B&B "Question about Israeli blades" #2, "Looking
for info on Personna" #1/#6, "Personna Israeli Reds" #4) holds they are
different blades: Israeli Reds = Personna Platinum Chrome; Crystal =
"Israeli Personnas" (a.k.a. "Super+" family, PTFE-coated stainless,
patent-number-stamped, rated slightly less sharp than the Reds but
excellent). [[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]: keep Crystal as its OWN entry
with Edgewell/Personna Nazareth as the manufacture ATTRIBUTE; do NOT
merge into Personna Platinum. Per P-04 this is the private-label/plant-
mate pattern, not the rename pattern. Learned 2026-10-24. Sources:
badgerandblade.com threads 47145, 603804, 476121, 584633 (multiple
firsthand users). Confidence: established.
