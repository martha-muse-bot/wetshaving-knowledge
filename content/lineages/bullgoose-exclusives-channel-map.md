- **Barbaros razors: maker site CLOSED ~early May 2024, still dead
2026-10-24.** B&B thread 629705 #35 (post last edited May 7, 2024,
firsthand): "The Barbaros website has been permanently closed as of
this week (It was online last week!)" — the poster bought one of the
last TR-3s from Turkey via the maker's eBay/Etsy stock as the site
went dark; #39 corroborates ("looks like this is likely the end of
the Barbaros razors"). Residual stock at closure: maker's eBay +
Etsy stores (one unit left per #35) and Bulgarian reseller Split7
(blemished combos, per #40); BullGoose was clearing TR-3s at $200
(#34). Live check 2026-10-24: barbarosrazors.com fails DNS
(browser error page, reload same) — no storefront, no parked page.
No maker farewell announcement found; the closure is dated by
community observation, not a maker statement, so record it as
established-site-death with the May 2024 bracket, NOT as a
maker-announced business closure. Catalog implication: Barbaros
product evidence = retailer/Wayback/SOTD photos only; the
Rocnel × Barbaros Sailor lineage (above) is unaffected — Sailor
production is Rocnel's. Dataset context: Barbaros strings are a
thin tail (123 hits 2016–2025, peak 2021) — a small, mostly
pre-2024 maker, not a destash wave.

maker's own 2024-09-26 DFS post selling Diamondback Ti razors
direct ("please go to paradigmshaveware.com", $380 retail) and
fetched LIVE 2026-10-24 (homepage: "Paradigm Salient On Sale!
Just $99!"). Maker = [[Andrew ("Andy")]] — "Andrew of Paradigm
Shaveware" (B&B 646543 #23) and "Andrew at Paradigm Shaveware"
(DFS Salient test drive). Still releasing in 2025: Sharpologist
Apr 14, 2025 podcast notes the Copper Diamondback, "available
now", limited to one run. Machining partner for the Diamondback
Ti: Jake Yates (YPM) — B&B 646543, maker-adjacent posts (Yates
shipped finished razors to Andy for polishing). Catalog
implication: Paradigm is NOT BullGoose-dependent — the
maker-direct channel survives any BullGoose closure; validators
route Paradigm evidence to paradigmshaveware.com, not BullGoose.
Surname for Andrew still open; the Salient's $99 sale banner may
be stale homepage copy (site fetched live, sale currency
unverified — do not cite the price as current without a
