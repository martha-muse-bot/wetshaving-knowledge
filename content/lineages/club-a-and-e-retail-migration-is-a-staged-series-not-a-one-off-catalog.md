**Club → A&E retail migration is a STAGED series, not a one-off [catalog]:** the pattern is Club VIP first, A&E retail years later, usually as an Ultima 2 re-release; the live stores pin the sequence (Shopify created_at = the current listing — see the re-listing caveat below):
- Signature: Club VIP soap/splash Oct–Nov 2018 → A&E-only strings Jan 2021 (TTS 2222) → A&E Ultima 2 soap/splash Feb 2024 → EdP Jun 2025. The "more modern rendering" (Engineered_Shave 2026-10-25) is the Ultima 2 era; era/channel discriminates VIP-original vs retail.
- Novelist: Club VIP splash listing Feb 2017 → A&E Ultima 2 Oct 2024.
- Khalifa: Club VIP splash May 2018, soap Aug 2018 → A&E Ultima 2 Dec 2024.
- Vanille Vendetta: Club VIP Feb 2021 → A&E soap Jul 2022, splash Dec 2022 → EdP Jun 2025.
- Chasing the Dragon: Club VIP Sep 2017 (earliest Club listing found) → A&E Ultima 2 Oct 2024 → EdP Jun 2025.
- Baklava: dataset 2022 → Club VIP re-listing Nov 2023 → A&E shave cream + splash Jan 2025.
- **Big Top: Club VIP soap Apr 2019 → A&E retail Oct 11, 2026** (soap + splash, Ultima 2, both available:true) — the NEWEST migration, last month. Catalog's verified A&E Big Top entries are the retail rendering.
- EdP batch: Signature + Vanille Vendetta + Chasing the Dragon EdPs created Jun 12, 2025 at A&E retail (Club VIP EdP listings Jul 4, 2025). **Wake the F' Up EdP created Aug 17, 2026 — Club VIP only, NOT at A&E retail.**
