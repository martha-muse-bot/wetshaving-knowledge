**The Canadian Florena shaving cream → Nivea shaving cream switch,
dated ~2008–2009 with a REFORMULATION refinement, ESTABLISHED on
firsthand community side-by-side.** B&B thread 42578 "Nivea Products For
Men" (published 2008-04-02; page-2 posts read live 2026-10-30), Canadian
posters: #27 "My understanding is Nivea owns the Florena brand name and
is removing or no longer going to distribute the Florena product shaving
cream. It is to be replaced by their 'Nivea' brand shaving cream. The two
do not smell the same. I smelled them both and they are different. The
Florena 'barbershop' is gone. The Nivea cream is much more subtle. The
ingedient list is different."; #28 "the new Nivea shaving cream available
to us here in Canada... I wish they didn't discontinue Florena after all
these years"; #29 "I will be buying a tube or two of the Florina though
as it will soon be a thing of the past"; #37 "Florena apparently had
chamomile which the new Nivea doesn't... quite different in terms of
lather. Nivea gives a smooth lather which is quite rich, but Florena gave
a silky lather and felt slicker than Nivea does"; #31 "So today I went to
the grocery store and opened a bottle of the new Nivea (Florena)... quite
a difference in smell... As for the texture it seems the same." So
USS-SpongeBob's "rebranded" is true at BRAND level but is NOT a
same-formula rename: the 2008–09 switch was a reformulation with a
different scent and ingredients. [catalog] Validators: Canadian pre-~2009
"Florena" shaving strings are their OWN product (Florena shaving cream,
barbershop-scented, chamomile); NEVER fold them into a Nivea Sensitive /
Nivea for Men shaving-cream entry, and never cite USS-SpongeBob's note
for formula continuity. Dataset scope: exactly 3 Florena strings
(USS-SpongeBob 2019 note; Krasimir_ 2020 soap field "Florena";
Engineered_Shave 2022 video-title bonus "Florena Shave Cream" — not a
product field). Learned 2026-10-30.
