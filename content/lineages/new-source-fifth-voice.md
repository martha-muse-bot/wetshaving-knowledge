**NEW SOURCE (fifth voice):** B&B "The Official Guide to Israeli Made
DE Blades" p.12 #226 (verbatim): "Tesco, Boots, ASDA, and on the
continent, HEMA, seem to sell the same blades." #227: "The same blades
you can get in Germany called 'Streamline' in a supermarket chain.
1,89€, distributed by Personna UK. You can't compare them to the Red
Personna." Pins the supermarket-generic family AND the NOT-Red
distinction directly — the HEMA blade is the plain generic store-brand
spec (the ASDA blister-pack variant in the same thread holds "plain
single wrapped blank blades" in a Tesco-style box), not the Platinum
Chrome "Red".
