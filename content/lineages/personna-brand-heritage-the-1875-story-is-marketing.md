**Personna brand heritage — the 1875 story is marketing.** B&B "Help
demystify Personna" #31 (2022): the Personna brand was acquired by the
American Safety Razor Co. only in 1951; earliest known Personna blades
are the 1943 "Hollow Ground" line (expensive, 10 for $1, endorsed by
Groucho Marx and Bob Hope). The "since 1875" heritage refers to Star
Safety Razor (one of ASR's 1919 merger ancestors), not to Personna.
Today the brand is fragmented — different companies hold the name in
different markets, and the Lab Blue line is morphing into AccuTec's
"Accuthrive/AccuForge" branding. Learned 2026-10-24. Confidence: lead
(single B&B post, but internally detailed and consistent with the
Lab Blue/AccuForge catalog precedent).
