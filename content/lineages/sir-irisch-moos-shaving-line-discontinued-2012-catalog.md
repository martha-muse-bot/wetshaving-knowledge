**Sir Irisch Moos shaving line — discontinued ~2012 [catalog]:** B&B
561600: the shave soap "was discontinued in 2012"; the CREAM went with
it (B&B 66837 firsthand 2008 tube review; 2014 restraint lists "Irisch
Moos Cream, Tube | 100 ml"; a 2024 Lather Games post narrates a B&B-forum
raid of Moos cream tubes). The AFTERSHAVE continued (B&B 427653: "It
never made sense to me why M&W still makes an IM aftershave but the soap
was discontinued"). Exact end month unpinned (lead 330). Mäurer & Wirtz
still makes Tabac; Irisch Moos shaving products are legacy-only. Dataset
strings 2016–2026 are destash/TTS. CORRECTION to the 244 note: the cream
was never "(current)".
