**[catalog] Mitchell's Wool Fat — company ALIVE; the Sharpologist
"MWF shut down" claim is FALSE (a formula discontinuation conflated
with a brand death; resolved 2026-10-22, backlog item).** Mitchell's
Wool Fat Soap (Bradford, Yorkshire; founder Fred Mitchell, formula
dating to 1893) reformulated: the classic **tallow formula was
discontinued ~fall 2022** (Connaught Shaving stock-outs with 6-puck
order caps, B&B 640011 #1721) and replaced by a tallow-free (palmate)
formula — 2023 version (poor reception) → improved 2024 version; the
soap **retains lanolin** (the "wool fat" in the name; never "mutton
based"). Maker's own statement, quoted via a community member who
contacted Mitchell's (B&B 640011 #337/338): "The quality and
consistency remains at the forefront of all we do, and the change in
base formulation, brought on in part by changes in the use of some
common preservatives, will not detract from performance or the feel,
whilst maintaining a more consistent availability of supply and
stability of the market." **Alive-and-producing evidence:** Connaught
Shaving stocked 62 pucks of the NEW formula (~2025, 10% off — B&B
640011 #1721); retailers worldwide sell it new today (nebrito,
vshod); dataset usage rebounded 20 hits in 2024 → 131 in 2025 → 183
through Oct 19, 2026. **Kent shaving soap = rebranded MWF** (long
community consensus, B&B threads 22979/566290/654723 — "the exact same
product with different wrapping," incl. the wooden-bowl presentation);
the Kent-branded version's current retail status is a lead (appears
NOS-only). **Domain note:** mitchellswoolfatsoap.co.uk is a PARKED
placeholder ("THIS SITE IS BRAND NEW", live-checked 2026-10-22) and
mitchellswoolfatsoaps.co.uk doesn't resolve — the maker's web presence
is dark while production continues, and that gap is the plausible
seed of the "they're gone" rumor (alongside hairmaker.gr's "The
original ... has been discontinued by the manufacturer" wording).
**Catalog impact [catalog]:** MWF entries keep brand-status alive;
record formula as an era attribute (tallow = pre-fall-2022, tallow-free
= 2023+; lanolin in both); validators must never treat a lapsed maker
domain as a closure by itself — require retail/announcement evidence.
