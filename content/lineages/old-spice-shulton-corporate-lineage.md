**Old Spice / Shulton corporate lineage — ESTABLISHED (Wikipedia Old
Spice + B&B community threads + cosmeticsandskin.com company page,
all read 2026-11-03) [catalog]:** William Lightfoot Schultz founded
the Shulton Company (1934; Old Spice introduced June 19, 1937 as
Early American Old Spice for women; men's line 1938, nautical theme,
clipper-ship packaging, Clifton NJ). **Dec 21, 1970:** American
Cyanamid acquired Shulton (deal announced that day; B&B's detailed
timeline); the Shulton name continued as "The Shulton Group"
banner under AC. **June 1990:** P&G purchased the Old Spice brand
from American Cyanamid (transaction finalized 9/11/90); Shulton name
dropped, scent changed, logo changed (clipper ship → sailboat/yacht
in 1992); P&G took over worldwide (incl. India) 1993. FireDragonMonkey's
2026-10-29 note (which prompted this) is accurate to the year
(1971 vs Dec 1970 announcement — trivial). Catalog: vintage
"Shulton" Old Spice shaving products are a pre-1990 era marker;
FireDragonMonkey's own aftershave field "Shulton - Old Spice After
Shave Lotion Ship Decanter" = vintage Shulton-era stock.
