**Dreiring caution — do NOT fold the Dreiring closure into M&W
history.** Dreiring-Werk Krefeld (Dalli → Kappus, insolvent Sept
2018, plant closed Jan–Sept 2020) is a separate contract-soap
story; a detailed B&B correction (2022, citing the Dreiring
timeline) states explicitly that "the manufacturer of Tabac (and
4711 and others, Mäurer & Wirtz) didn't went insolvent" and that
the Dreiring connection is only via the Wirtz family/Dalli. Any
validator note blaming a Tabac/Irisch Moos product end on
Dreiring is wrong. Confidence: caution established.
