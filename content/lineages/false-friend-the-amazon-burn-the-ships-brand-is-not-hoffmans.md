**False friend: the Amazon "Burn The Ships" brand is NOT
Hoffman's.** Amazon's "Burn The Ships Inferno Shave Soap" (goat
milk, Himalayan cedar/vetiver/grapefruit/frankincense/myrrh —
product copy verbatim) is a separate men's-grooming brand that
took the phrase as a brand name; Hoffman's "Burn the Ships" is
the Shulton-Old-Spice-dupe scent. Never attribute between them.
