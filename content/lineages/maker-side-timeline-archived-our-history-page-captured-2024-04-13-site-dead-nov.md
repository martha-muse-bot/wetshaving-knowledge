**Maker-side timeline (archived "Our History" page, captured 2024-04-13; site dead Nov 2026):** first razor made 2013 (hand tools, WWII-era metalworking machines); 2015 moved to automated CNC machining and produced more refined designs under the Charcoal Goods shaving-razor brand; 2016 offered the first commercially available 3D-printed stainless steel safety razor; by 2016 producing complete razors in brass, copper, and stainless steel; **the Charcoal Goods brand and its premium-priced collector's razors were retired in 2023**, and work began on an affordable, high-quality, unique razor — the Stinger. The page's slideshow names "Stinger Safety Razor in Solid Copper", "Bishop Safety Razor in Stainless, Brass, and Copper", and "Arrow Safety Razor in Solid Brass" (CG-era models). The history page does NOT name Brian Twilley — the Twilley link still rests on the B&B-quoted company email alone.
