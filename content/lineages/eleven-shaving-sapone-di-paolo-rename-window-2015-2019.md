**[catalog] Eleven Shaving ← Sapone di Paolo (rename, window
2015–2019).** Paolo's brand launched as **Sapone di Paolo** Mar 6,
2015 (announced on The Shaving Nook; Charleston SC — Wet The Face,
Jul 2015). A 2024 Wet The Face review states: "Its new name is
called Eleven"; Sharpologist interviewed "Paolo of Sapone di Paolo"
(~2017) and "Paolo from Eleven Shaving" (Aug 5, 2019) — same founder,
two names. "Eleven" in dataset notes by 2020 (MMCZ86,
youarebreakingthings). ONE brand group; exact rename date unpinned
(no maker statement found). Established (corroborated, non-primary).
