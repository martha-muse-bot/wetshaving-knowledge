**Founders (full names, ESTABLISHED — co-founder Luis Oliveira's own words):** João Gomes (innovation engineer), Luis Oliveira (product-design specialist), André Guimarães (CNC machining expert, leads manufacturing). Source: GlossWire interview "BeautyTalks with Luis Oliveira, Tatara Razors Co-Founder" (page carries no publication date; read 2026-10-30). All three studied Mechanical Engineering at the same university; the brand concept grew out of their university's "School of Startups" after a katana-manufacturing video fixed the Japanese identity. The name came from the ORIGINAL plan to cast razors in a foundry — "tatara" (鑪) = the clay furnace of Japanese sword-smithing. Early arc: all three worked day jobs and built razors nights/weekends, starting in André's parents' garage → Porto office; Masamune pre-orders "sold out in a few hours"; Cosmoprof attendance; founders' Japan trip 2018. Date-dependent claims are LEADS: João "returned from Belgium in 2020 to work full time"; Luis "left my job this year" — interview undated, likely ~2021–22 (post-Muramasa launch); pin via Wayback for the exact publication date.
