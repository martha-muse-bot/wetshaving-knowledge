**[catalog] Long Rifle Soap Co. — NOT renamed (brand name unchanged);
2025 reorganization = scent RENAMING + lanolin-base reformulation
(resolved 2026-10-23, backlog item).** Founded **2014** by **Amanda**
(first name per wetshaving reviews; surname not pinned — lead);
**Sturgeon Bay, WI** (Door County; maker's contact page lists
3888 Sargeant Rd, Sturgeon Bay WI; a 2018 dataset note calls her
"Local WI Artisan"). Operating Status: Active (live-checked
2026-10-23). Maker's own announcement "Big Changes, Same Great
Scents" (longriflesoap.com/blogs/the-powderhorn/big-changes-same-
great-scents, published 2025-01-25): she is "heading to work full
time, so I need to streamline the business." **NO brand rename** —
the name Long Rifle Soap Company is unchanged; the B&B-2024 lead
claiming a "new brand name" is WRONG (corrected against the maker).
The changes: (a) all shaving soaps reformulated to the **Black
Label wool-fat formula** (lanolin; maker notes "as a shepherd, I
approve") — replacing the old locally-sourced-beef-suet tallow base
(maker's 2020 Earth Day post: "Locally-sourced beef suet from small
farms transforms into the amazing tallow used in our bar and shaving
soaps"). The B&B-2024 "sheep rather than beef tallow" gloss is also
wrong — the change is to wool fat (lanolin), not sheep tallow.
(b) **All scents renamed by key scent note + number** (numbers are
arbitrary — "rollout, rank, because I like the number" — NOT
versions): Tabac No. 01 ← Hunting Lodge; Cèdre No. 06 ← 1776;
Tabac No. 04 ← Brown Bess; Épicé No. 07 ← Bay Rhum; Chypre No. 03 ←
Voyageur; Boisé No. 09 ← Saranac; Menthe No. 05 ← Hivernant (plus
future Oud, Ambre, Santal, Fougère names teased). Three scents go
year-round (Tabac No. 01, Chypre No. 03, Cèdre No. 06); others on
limited-edition rotation with a share of sales to environmental
charities. Rollout: changes apply as old stock sells out; the maker
said she'd reference old names in website copy for a year. Shave
tonics → Black Label formula; beard balm/oil, mustache wax unchanged;
candles renamed on restock with partner **Big White Yeti**. Dataset
corroboration (2025–2026): "Long Rifle Soap Co. – 06-Cèdre" (2025),
"Long Rifle Soap Co. - Cèdre No. 6" ×27 (2026), "Tabac no. 3 beard
balm" (2025), "Long Rifle - Bay Rum: Tobacco & Leather" (2026);
June 2025 Hivernant ingredients still list locally-sourced tallow =
transition in progress. **[[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]:** current names
are canonical per the canonical-name rule (Tabac No. 01 etc.),
old story names fold as aliases; base = era attribute (old tallow
vs Black Label wool fat); AMBIGUITY WARNING — "Tabac No. 01" is Long
Rifle's Hunting Lodge, NOT Mäurer & Wirtz Tabac (matcher caution).
