*Confidence: lead · Source: [[lineages|Lineages]]*

**P.160 arc refined [catalog]:** Elberel Italiana made the original tallow P.160 (formula: Aqua, Stearic Acid, Tallow, Cocos nucifera, Potassium Hydroxide, Sodium Hydroxide, Parfum, Potassium Carbonate, Tetrasodium EDTA — B&B 392937 #12); post-2004 reformulation replaced tallow with vegetable substitutes for retail sizes and slightly reformulated P.160, "Figaro" name removed from the professional 1kg pack, produced until 2009, discontinued. Dataset P.160 strings: hawns 2017 'Figaro P160 Tipo Duro'; Hyvasuomi79 2018 'Figaro P160' / 'Figaro P.160'; NoLogonServAvailable 2018 'Figaro P.160' (production stopped ~2009/2010). **RazoRock Classic = the P.160-formula revival:** Joe (Italian Barber) "bringing back the original P160 formulation under the Razorock name in May" (B&B 392937 #20, 2014, relayed from Facebook); "RazoRock Classic, which is literally Figaro in a different tub" (B&B 412939 #11, Jul 2014); "RR Classic = Fitness for Men / Figaro" (B&B 412939 #14 + B&B 383523 #6, Jan 2014, both read verbatim). RazoRock ALSO sells a separate "P.160" product ("copy of P160 Tipo Morbido" — B&B 412939 #14; 2025 B&B 657517: "P160 shaves the same as Classic in spite of a trace amount of tallow (otherwise same base)"). Catalog: RR Classic stays its own entry with the Figaro/P.160 lineage noted, never folded into Figaro. RR Classic's maker stays UNPINNED — TFS is plausible but unsourced; B&B 392937 #7 hedges "some of the original folks may be involved" (new lead (413)).
