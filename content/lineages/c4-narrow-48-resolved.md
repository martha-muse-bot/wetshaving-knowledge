**(c4-narrow-48) RESOLVED — the audit is complete, and the stamp is wrong as often as right [catalog]:** Fragrantica carries exactly NINE House of Mammoth perfume pages. Scorecard of their "launched in YYYY" stamps against maker/dataset evidence: TRUE ×2 (You & I 2024 — maker Oct 4, 2024; DinoS'mores 2025 — maker created 2025-04-07, published 2025-05-19, first use 2025-05-24); WRONG ×3 (Beloved "2026" — true Apr 2022, established 19:06; Santa Noir "2024" — true Nov/Dec 2021, see below; Fú Dào "2025" — true Jan 2022, see below); NO STAMP ×3 (Tobacconist, Everett, Indigo — no year sentence on the page at all); DECADE-VAGUE ×1 (Embrace — "launched during the 2020's", consistent with the true 2021 launch and useless as a date). The stamp tracks the page-addition/submission year, not the launch: it is right only when the scent was new at addition. **Standing rule (hardened from the 19:06 "needs a maker-side date" note): NEVER cite a Fragrantica launch year for a House of Mammoth scent — or any late-added artisan house — without a maker-side created_at or a dataset first-use. The rule now covers all nine pages, not just Beloved.**
