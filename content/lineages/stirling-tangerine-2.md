*Confidence: established · Source: [[lineages|Lineages]]*

**[[lineages/stirling-tangerine|Stirling Tangerine]] — 2015 batch was a one-off, never retailed; retail return Oct 22, 2020 → Jan 21, 2021, ESTABLISHED [catalog]:** the 2015-era Stirling store ran on BigCommerce (category `/shave-soap/`, URLs `<scent>-shave/`), and its full shave-soap lineups — Aug 2015 (33 SKUs), Feb 2016 (40), Mar 2016 (36), all single-page, no pagination — contain ZERO Tangerine (and zero Vanilla Tangerine, consistent with the replacement story). No BigCommerce Tangerine URL was ever archived either. The Jul 2015 B&B "made a batch" post is a passaround/one-off batch, not a launch. [[lineages/the-scent|The scent]] returned to retail between the Oct 22, 2020 Shopify collection snapshot (page 4, 18 handles, no Tangerine) and the first dataset use Jan 21, 2021, and is confirmed listed by Aug 5, 2021 (`tangerine-shave-soap` on collection page 4). The five-year dataset silence (2016–2020) is a genuine retail absence. The (358) delist bracket (live Sep 2021 → absent Nov 2026, modal guess 2022–2023) is unchanged — Wayback has no collection captures 2022–2024 to narrow it, and no Tangerine product page was ever archived in either URL era.
