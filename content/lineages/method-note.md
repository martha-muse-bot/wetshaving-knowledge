**Method note:** a Shopify `created_at` dates the CURRENT listing, not necessarily the launch — Baklava's Club VIP listing (2023-11-20) postdates its first dataset use (2022-07-14), and Novelist's VIP soap listing (2021-06-25) postdates its VIP splash listing (2017-02-14) by 4+ years. Use the earliest listing per scent as a latest-possible launch date and cross-check against first dataset use; never cite a single created_at as the launch.
