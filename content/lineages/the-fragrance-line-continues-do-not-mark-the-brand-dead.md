**The fragrance line CONTINUES — do not mark the brand dead.**
Current retailer listings (The Perfume Hunter, shop pages
crawled 2026) carry Sir Irisch Moos After Shave Lotion, Eau de
Toilette Natural Spray, and Deo Spray; a 2023 ShavingEdge review
bought the aftershave at retail ($25–40/100 ml); dataset
aftershave/fragrance uses run through Oct 2026 (46/44 in 2026),
consistent with a living fragrance line rather than a destash
tail. A "Sensitive After Shave" variant also exists (Parfumo
listing). Scent notes per M&W's own description (via
ShavingEdge): top lemon, orange, rosemary, coriander, green
foliage; heart lavender, geranium, carnation, sandalwood; base
patchouli, moss, tonka bean. Name note: "Irisch" is branding,
not origin — a dataset poster observes it has "nothing to do
with Ireland (or moss for that matter)" (kaesees 2020).
Confidence: fragrance line current established (retailer
listings + 2023 retail purchase + dataset curve).
