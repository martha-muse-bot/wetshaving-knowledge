**[catalog] L.A. Shaving Soap Co. wind-down + Chiseled Face scent
continuation (Black Rose, Woody Lavender).** The "CF bought the
scents from LA" lead (OnionMiasma's 2025-02-01 SOTD note, the backlog
item) is CONFIRMED as a real lineage, with one detail caveated:
- **Black Rose — ESTABLISHED.** CF's own live product page
(chiseledface.com, fetched 2026-10-23): "I love the rich scent of
rose absolute... This is an all-natural scent originally made
popular by the Los Angeles Shaving Soap Co in their vegan shave
products. Now available in our tallow formulation." CF's version is
rose absolute + cedar + geranium + other EO, charcoal-tinted.
Dataset: first CF-labeled "Chiseled Face - Black Rose - Soap" use
2021-04-13 (seventiesfro; trythatsoap collection 2444); the LA
original ("LA Shaving Soap Co. - The Black Rose - Soap (Vegan)")
was still in use 2021-03-30, so the handoff began as a COEXISTENCE,
not a closure fire-sale. The CF page even cross-sells: "Pairs
perfectly with LA Shaving's Black Rose Aftershave."
- **Woody Lavender — ESTABLISHED (continuation; purchase detail a
lead).** CF's live page (chiseledface.com, fetched 2026-10-23):
"Introducing Woody Lavender... lavender with the grounding aroma of
ho wood and amyris... West Indian Bay Leaves... The fragrance is
100% essential oils" — the same note stack as LASSCo's Woody
Lavender (WCS sample-pack era, B&B 535388, 2018). Dataset: first CF
"Chiseled Face - Woody Lavender" use 2023-06-14 (RedMosquitoMM);
2025-02-01 frankieIVfingers pairs the CF SOAP with the ORIGINAL
"Los Angeles Shaving Soap Company - Woody Lavender - Aftershave"
(destash), proving the scent survived the brand change intact in
the community's nose. The "CF bought the scents from LA" claim
itself is single-community-sourced (OnionMiasma's 2025-02-01 note);
whether a formal formula purchase or an amicable handoff is
unpinned — tag the PURCHASE detail as a lead.
- **LASSCo wind-down — bracketed, not pinned.** Dataset LA uses by
year: 19/19/39/27/52/71/79/12/19/34/14 (2016–2026) — the cliff is
2022 (79) → 2023 (12). No farewell announcement found this run;
2023–2026 uses are destash. Closure-date lead parked below.
- **Scope:** of LASSCo's lineup (Blackfern, The Black Rose, Santa
Monica Bay Rum, Bespoke #1, Topanga Fougère, Myrkviðr, Woody
Lavender, Vanilla/Eucalyptus/Mint, Vert Noir, Lime), ONLY Black
