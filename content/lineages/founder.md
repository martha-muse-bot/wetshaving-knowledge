**Founder:** **Gregory Khan** (adjustablerazors.co.za; Classic Shaving spells "Greg Kahn" — record both spellings), living in Thailand at the time; production in Southeast Asia, finishing/assembly in Austin, Texas. First razor handmade on a milling machine/lathe, stainless billet head, 316L handle (Classic Shaving, Mike Sandoval). Bulldog launched 2010 ($80, stainless); H20/H2O 2011 ($149, T6 aluminum head, dual-comb).
