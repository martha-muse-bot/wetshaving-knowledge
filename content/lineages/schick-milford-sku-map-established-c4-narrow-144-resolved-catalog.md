**Schick Milford SKU map — ESTABLISHED (c4-narrow-144 RESOLVED) [catalog]:** the Milford plant's US-made output is the **Hydro family + Xtreme3 Eco disposables** — NOT the whole Schick line. Blade-vs-cartridge split matters: Milford stamps/hones the blades; cartridge assembly and handles are split across plants.
- **Hydro 5 / Hydro 3 / Hydro Silk — blades made in Milford, USA.** Retailer origin data (Giant Food Stores, Hydro Silk Sensitive Care: "Blades Made In USA... Cartridge Made In China With Blades Made In The USA From Imported Steel"); maker copy via Dollar General (Hydro Skin Comfort 3-blade: "Razor blades are crafted and honed in the USA"); review consensus ("The handle is made in China, but the actual razor cartridge is made in the United States... one of the only razors we've found that's made in the United States comes on the Schick Hydro 5"). Blade steel strip is IMPORTED (China, per the 2017 ITC record's Mr. White declaration) — the "Made in USA" claim rests on US honing/crafting, not US steel.
- **Hydro Connect 5 cartridges — manufactured AND assembled in Milford** (Schick's own counsel, 2017 ITC letter, present tense: "manufactured and assembled in Schick's plant in Milford, Connecticut... the entirety of the cartridge, including the blade unit and connecting member, is manufactured and assembled in the United States"). Assembly moved China → Milford by 2017: the pre-move boxes carried "assembled in China" leaflets, which Schick's counsel called "misstatements" — Gillette's letter disputes that framing and notes Schick never stated no Connect 5 was ever assembled in China, so the move date is bracketed to ≤ Oct 2017, not pinned.
- **Hydro Silk cartridges — assembled in CHINA with US-made blades** (Giant origin data above) — same blades, different assembly line from Connect 5. **Hydro 5 standard cartridge assembly — CONFLICTING, lead-grade:** review consensus says USA; one source says "Schick Hydro® blades made in the USA, assembled in China" — treat as mixed/era-dependent, never cite either way without a pack-origin read.
- **Xtreme3 Eco disposables — Milford-made** (Chain Drug Review, Feb 2, 2012, company statement: the Xtreme3 Eco "is made and shipped out of the company's 99.9% landfill-free facility in Milford, Conn."). Consistent with the EPA record (plant does plastic injection molding) and with "Schick Twin Blade Disposable Razors 5 ct MADE IN USA" marketplace titles (lead-grade corroboration only).
- **NOT Milford — Quattro: Germany.** Blades made in Germany by Wilkinson Sword GmbH (Solingen); razor assembled in China or Mexico (Skintimate origin data, formerly Schick Quattro You: "Blades Made In Germany, Razor Assembled In Mexico"; allamerican.org list: "Schick Quattro – Made in Germany"). **NOT Milford — Schick injector blades ("Chick"): China** (B&B firsthand ×multiple; dataset "Chinese Schick" 71 rows + a 2020 note: "the current production schick blades from china are pretty good"). **NOT Milford — Schick DE blades: Germany (Solingen)**, same factory as Wilkinson Sword (dataset "schick - double edge made in germany" ×3; B&B 371440: "made in solingen Germany there the same as the wilkinson's... German Schick blades from Solingen"). **Proline B-20/P-30: Japan** (prior established fact, not re-derived this run).
- **Dataset grounding (full corpus, 241,282 rows):** blade field is injector-dominated (Schick Injector 1,075; Proline 857; bare "Schick" 769; "Chinese Schick" 71; "Japanese Schick" 13) with only **54 Schick DE rows** (incl. 3 explicitly "made in germany"); razor field 4,906 Schick rows, almost all vintage (Injector types, Krona, Hydro-Magic) — modern systems are negligible (Xtreme 18, Hydro 5: 2, Hydro 3: 2, Quattro 1, Intuition 2). The Milford map therefore matters for **era guidance, not matching volume**.
- **[[lineages/catalog-guidance-catalog|[[lineages/catalog-guidance|Catalog guidance]] [catalog]]]:** (1) the Dec 31, 2027 Milford closure ends US production of Hydro-family blades + Xtreme3 Eco disposables — post-2027 "Made in USA" Hydro strings are old stock; (2) **CORRECTION to the 13:06 run:** the podcast's "injector blades/cartridges unaffected (already made in China)" is CONTRADICTED for cartridges — Hydro Connect 5 cartridges are Milford-assembled and Hydro blades are US-honed, so the closure DOES hit cartridge-blade production; only injector blades (China), Quattro (Germany), DE (Germany) and Proline (Japan) are unaffected. The 13:06 "consistent with" reading is retired. (3) Schick DE strings (54 rows) = German-made; never fold into a US-made Schick blade entry. Learned 2026-11-15. Established (ITC letter + retailer origin data + trade press + dataset census); Hydro 5 assembly + Twin Blade disposables stay lead-grade.
