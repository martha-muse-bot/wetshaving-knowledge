*Confidence: lead · Source: [[lineages|Lineages]]*

**HoM fragrance-descriptions page harvest (maker-side primary) [catalog]:** the page is a first-class source for HoM scent-name etymologies and statuses — validators should read it before citing a HoM launch date or name origin. Harvested this run:
- **Sonder co-authored by Benjamin Esposito AND [[people/michael-nordstrand|Michael Nordstrand]]** (new person — who is he? lead (c4-narrow-35)).
- **Smash "was created by William Carius from [[lines/barrister-and-mann|Barrister and Mann]]"** — a HoM scent by B&M's Will; posters knew it as a collab from launch (Nov 2022): dual-attribution strings from day one ("House of Mammoth/Barrister and Mann - Smash", "House Of Mammoth x Barrister And Mann - Smash - Soap"). Dataset: 633 genuine rows / 71 authors, 2022-11-03 → 2026-11-05. Maker page marks Smash discontinued (soap + aftershave only); 2026 rows are destash. **Catalog flag:** verified Smash entries exist under BOTH brand slots (`soap/house-of-mammoth-barrister-and-mann-smash` AND `aftershave/house-of-mammoth-smash` + `fragrance/house-of-mammoth-smash` + parallel HoM×B&M fragrance/aftershave entries) — validators check whether the HoM-only and HoM×B&M entries are the same products duplicated across brand slots (one product, two brand slots); the perfumer credit (Will Carius) belongs on the entry either way.
- **Indigo:** "Mood Indigo" is the Duke Ellington tune — corroborates the HoM "Mood Indigo → Indigo" rename note; maker page marks Indigo discontinued.
- **Uitwaaien (discontinued):** Dutch idiom, "a walk in the wind, as a means of refreshing and clearing the mind"; rocky-beach childhood inspiration. Dataset 162 genuine rows, first 2023-11-23; still in the Tusk sample program.
- **You & I (Will Die):** "the latest perfume from House of Mammoth"; People's Choice at the 2025 Art & Olfaction Awards. Dataset 82 genuine rows / 30 authors, first 2024-10-05 — launch ~Oct 2024, predates the award; pin the exact launch date (lead (c4-narrow-33)). **Catalog gap [catalog]:** 64 soap-field rows but NO soap entry exists (aftershave + fragrance verified) — `soap/house-of-mammoth-you-i-will-die` is owed, promotion-ready.
- **Kryptonite 2:** EdP "never been available as a perfume until now (May 2025)"; dataset 43 rows, first 2025-06-21 (incl. a "Kryptonite 2 Soap (green)" string, socialkittielynx — single author, lead-grade). **Validator check:** no "Kryptonite 2" entry exists, but `fragrance/house-of-mammoth-kryptonite` IS verified — and the original Kryptonite was "soap and aftershave only" per the same maker page, so a fragrance entry under plain "Kryptonite" is likely the Kryptonite 2 EdP mislabeled (or tester-era strings — "Kryptonite Tester EdP" rows run Jul 2024, pre-dating the May 2025 launch). Re-read the fragrance entry's evidence before citing it as either.
- **DinoS'mores:** 2025 LE; dataset 100 genuine rows, first 2025-05-24. All three entries verified — no gap.
- **Beloved DATING CORRECTION [catalog]:** Fragrantica's "launched in 2026" is WRONG as a launch date — dataset: 407 genuine rows / 47 authors, first 2022-04-10 by mammothben (Ben Esposito himself). Beloved is a 2022-era scent; 2026 is a re-listing/EdP date at most. Never cite 2026 as its launch. All three entries verified — knowledge note only.
- **Ausflug** = "custom project, no longer available"; **Dammusi** = Pantelleria island houses (a place name, but a specific one); **Damocles** = wine/spices up front (a wine-spice scent predating Mull); **Voices** = HoM × Lather Talk podcast collab, 2023 Art & Olfaction Artisan winner.
