**(c4-narrow-277) RESOLVED as SHOP TAG, not a discontinuation signal
[catalog]:** the maker descriptions page does NOT contain the word
"final" anywhere — no "final formulation/version/batch", no
tester/prototype graduation story ("tester", "prototype", "graduate" all
absent). Store-side census (HoM co, 69 products): "final" on 43 of 69.
It sits on live current products (Beloved, Embrace, Rumble, Alive,
Shire 2, You & I, Voices, Fu Dao, Santa Noir, Snippid, Mull, Kryptonite
v2) AND on discontinued scents' lingering balms (Dammusi, Smash — both
also "clearance"), so it cannot mean final batch / final sale /
discontinuation. It is not scent-level (Everett splash + balm = final,
Everett soap = no final; Hygge v2 splash = final, Hygge soap + balm =
no final; Z splash + balm = final, Z soap = no final) and not format-level
(soaps/splashes/balms both carry and lack it). It NEVER appears on any
EdP/perfume (16 products), samples, gift card, merch, Permafrost cooling
solution, or the almond-leather balm (tags ['almond leather',
'clearance']). The five shaving-format exceptions (Everett soap,
Hygge soap + balm, Z soap, almond-leather balm) are stable across the
whole snapshot series (same three soap exceptions in the 2026-11-12
snapshot) — long-standing listing variance, not a recent edit. VERDICT:
"final" is a Shopify store tag marking the settled/final listing of a
shaving product (maker-internal, vs earlier/tester-era listings); no
maker-side sentence explains it. [[lineages/catalog-guidance|Catalog guidance]]: IGNORE the "final"
tag when judging whether a HoM product is current, discontinued, or
final-batch — discontinuation evidence stays the descriptions-page
"(discontinued)" heading + per-format store presence, as in (267)/(271).
