**The shaving CREAM existed alongside the stick and was regarded
as the lesser product** (B&B 2012: "the stick is thought of more
highly than the cream"; a 2011 review: the stick "actually is
better for me than their cream"). The cream's discontinuation
date is UNPINNED: cream strings persist in the dataset through
2025–2026, but no current retail listing for a shaving cream was
found this run — do not read those strings as proof of current
production. Lead, not a date.
