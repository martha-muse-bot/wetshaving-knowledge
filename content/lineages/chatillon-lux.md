**[catalog] Chatillon Lux — production history + the [[Declaration Grooming]]
turn (resolved 2026-10-21; answers the backlog "Chatillon Lux —
status/collaborations/what the label became" thread).** Founder: **Shawn
Maher**, St. Louis, Missouri — musician and ad-agency copywriter turned
self-taught perfumer; started Chatillon Lux in **2015** after a bad
store-bought balm annoyed him into making his own. Brand named after
explorer/trapper **Henri Chatillon** (Melville's "gallant man");
pronounced "Cha-tee-ohn LOOx"; every scent is St. Louis-inspired.
Sources: fragrantica.com news piece (founder + founding story + name
origin); stephanmatthews.com Lamplight Penance review (2015 founding,
self-taught). Confidence: established.
- **Phase 1 (2015–mid-2020): Maher made everything himself** — shaving
toners/aftershaves in his own scents — and ALSO manufactured
aftershaves for **Declaration Grooming's** soap scents: B&B 2018
threads show the standard pairing of a DG soap (e.g. TSM Fougere,
Yuzu/Rose/Patchouli) with the *Chatillon Lux* aftershave in the same
scent. Catalog caution: same-scent DG-soap + CL-aftershave pairs are
**cross-brand by design** — do NOT fold them into one-maker set
cross-validation.
- **Phase 2 (mid-Aug 2020–Jan 2026): DG makes everything.** Maker's own
announcement post (chatillonlux.com, dated June 29, 2020): "from here
on out, Declaration Grooming will be the sole provider of Chatillon
Lux fragrances in shaving product bases. We plan on making this
transition by mid-August" — Maher's day job had made small-batch
production unsustainable; he recast Chatillon Lux as a fragrance
maker, keeping only unscented aftershave/toner production himself.
The DG-produced aftershaves **carry the Chatillon Lux name on the
label** (B&B community confirmation) — so dataset "Chatillon Lux X"
strings from ~2020 on are DG-manufactured but brand-of-record =
Chatillon Lux; manufacturer era is an ATTRIBUTE, not identity (per
the brand-of-record/user-intent rule). Sources: the announcement
post itself (primary); B&B thread 603567 "Declaration Grooming
Aftershaves?" (Mar 2021 — arrangement already in place: "Chatillon
is now out of the scented aftershave market... DG has an
arrangement"). Confidence: established.
- **Phase 3 (2026): production ends with DG's closure.** Declaration
Grooming shut January 31, 2026 (lineage entry above); Chatillon Lux
no longer sells ANY shaving products — chatillonlux.com fetched
2026-10-21 lists 9 eau-de-toilettes + gift card + sampler only
(products.json): Lost Valley, Treget, Weinstrasse, Confluence, La
Petite Prairie, Rose Santal, and FOUR titled "...To Be
Discontinued" (Admiral, Envoi, Can't Stand the Rain, Sunrise on
LaSalle). Maher's perfumery continues separately
under **Maher Olfactive** (linked from the CL site nav). 2026
dataset uses of CL shaving products (339 hits Jan–Oct) are therefore
destash, not live retail. Validator evidence routing: CL shaving
entries cannot be verified on the maker site — use retailer pages
(Maggard), Wayback snapshots (pre-2020 for Maher-made era), or SOTD
photos. Source: live storefront fetch 2026-10-21 (primary).
Confidence: established (brand alive as fragrance house; shaving
line production ended with DG's closure).
- Side note (identity/misspelling): B&B posts show "Chantillon Lux" /
"Chatillion Lux" typed variants — harvest into [[Product aliases]].
Awards data point: Weinstrasse named best perfume of 2019 by
CaFleureBon editors + Mark Behnke (maker's own awards post, Jan
2020) — corroborates the brand's fragrance-first arc.
- **Retail/label addendum (resolved 2026-10-22, collab-presentation
research).** The DG-made CL soaps were sold primarily through
**declarationgrooming.com** (release threads: Santal Auster,
Yuzu/Rose/Patchouli, Unconditional Surrender, La Forêt de Liguest —
all "released at Declaration") plus retailers **Maggard Razors**
(shipped the Y/R/P soap; Bon Vivant noted "out of stock
everywhere"), Top of the Chain, West Coast Shaving; NO evidence the
soaps were ever sold on chatillonlux.com — CL's blog consistently
routes the soap to DG ("soap in the Icarus base from Declaration
Grooming", Nefertiti post 2019; "Declaration Grooming will also be
making a matching shaving soap", Sni Mato post Jan 2019), and the
June 2020 announcement says customers/retailers would "order our
shaving products from one source". **Tub branding:** DG's
milksteak-era illustrated label artwork with **Chatillon Lux
prominently featured** (djudge Lather Games 2023 SOTD note on 88
Chestnut Street; Thirsty Badger Apr 2020: "kudos to Declaration
Grooming on the milksteak labels"); B&B confirms the CL name was on
the label of DG-sold collab products. Whether the DG bull logo also
appeared is unverified (photo sources blocked). **Aftershave split:**
pre-Aug-2020 aftershaves/toners/salves/EdTs were CL-made ("our own
aftershave and toner", Nefertiti 2019); post-transition DG made the
scented CL aftershaves too (CL announcement "sole provider of
Chatillon Lux fragrances in shaving product bases"; B&B Mar 2021:
"Chatillon is now out of the scented aftershave market"; DG aftershave
lineup ~30% CL, CL name on label) — but brand of record stays
**Chatillon Lux** throughout (dataset: aftershave strings 100%
CL-alone; soap strings 3,144 joint CL/DG vs 2,142 CL-alone,
2016–2026). Customer-facing: CL announced releases (Shawn's
emails), DG ran the soap sales (buyers emailed Scott for release
schedules); collab soaps were limited runs ("will not be
replenished"). Confidence: established (maker's own posts +
community threads); bull-on-label detail: lead.
