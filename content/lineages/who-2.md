**Who:** French artisan donkey-farm soapery in **Giat (63620), Combrailles, Puy-de-Dôme** — CORRECTED 2026-11-06 (was "Pontaumur"; the maker's own site says Giat, Pontaumur is the nearby town tourism listings use). Soapmaker **Franck Jeandaleix**; his partner **Karine** handled orders, made the confipeaux (jams) and did packaging (maker's own 2015 "Qui sommes nous" page; CORRECTED 2026-11-06 — was "contact/first-name: Karine"). Pitch = all-natural, donkey-milk, handmade; old site `savonneriedubonberger.com` (referenced live 2017). Scents observed: Cedar Almond "Ass Milk", Rosemary Mint, Orange Lavande, Lavender Orange, Auvergne, Cedar/Mandarin, Lavender Rosemary. Imported to the US c.2017 by Phil at Bullgoose (B&B "Donkey!" thread: "overnight he added Savonnerie du Bon Berger!").
