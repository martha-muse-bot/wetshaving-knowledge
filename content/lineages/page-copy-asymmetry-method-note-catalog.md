**[[aliases/page-copy-asymmetry-method-note|Page-copy asymmetry (method note)]] [catalog]:** not every splash page carried the cooled "synthetic menthol" line. Everett's 2024-09-12 capture and You & I's 2024-11-10 capture carry NO cooling copy at all — neither the menthol line nor the note (the formula section is the generic Mammoth Magic text). Z's 2024-10-11 and Hygge's 2024-09-12 captures DID carry the menthol line; the note replaced it (Z Feb 2025: note present, menthol line gone). Consequence for the method: on a page that never carried cooling copy, absence of the note proves only that the page hadn't been edited — it says nothing about the formula. The note-appearance method brackets flips only where the cooled line existed (Z, Hygge) or where the note's arrival is itself the signal (You & I). Do not read "no note in capture" as "still cooled" on Everett-type pages.
