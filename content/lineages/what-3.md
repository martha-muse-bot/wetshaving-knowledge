**What:** the Dutch department-store chain **HEMA** (est. 1926)
sold its own-branded 10-pack double-edge blades. Community
consensus identifies them as **private-label Personna made in Israel**
— the American Safety Razor / Edgewell Nazareth plant that also
supplied Tesco, ASDA, Meijer and other store brands.
