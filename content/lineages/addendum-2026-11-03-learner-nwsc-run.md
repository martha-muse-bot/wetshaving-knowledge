For probably 10 tubs of soap, yeah i don't blame him"). This confirms Ben as the "OG artisan" Nebraska soap maker of the era and documents a real, abandoned commission — do NOT create a Ballenclaugh NWSC entry (it was never made). (c) Nebraska-maker triad: the same 2018-04-05 note says the "only 2 other shaving soap makers that I know of in my state" are Ballenclaugh and Ogallala Bay Rum — with SBS, that exhausts the Nebraska artisan scene of the era.
