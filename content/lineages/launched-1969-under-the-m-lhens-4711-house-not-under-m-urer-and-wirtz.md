**Launched 1969 under the Mülhens (4711) house, NOT under Mäurer &
Wirtz.** Three secondary sources agree on 1969 (trend-traveller's
4711 brand profile: "Sir Irisch Moos (1969)" among the Muelhens
portfolio; cosmeticcheck.app brand page: EdT "largely unchanged
since its introduction in 1969"; ClassicShaving: "Established in
1969"). ClassicShaving's "produced by Maurer & Wirtz" gloss is only
true for the post-2007 era — see the acquisition below.
Confidence: established (convergent secondary sources).
