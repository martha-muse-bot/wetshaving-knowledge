*Confidence: established · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**"Same base as Tabac" — community consensus, not a maker
statement.** ClassicShaving's review: the stick "performs
similarly to the Tabac Shaving Soap because it is produced by the
same company and has many of the same ingredients"; dataset
notes repeat it ("Sir Irisch Moos is the same base as Tabac,
with a different scent", vigilantesd; "the same [old version]
base, but with a greener scent", vigilantesd). The "[old
version]" qualifier matters: the comparison is to the tallow-era
Tabac base, and posters rate the stick's scent greener than
Tabac's. Do NOT cite this as a manufacturer-confirmed shared
formula. Confidence: consensus established; maker confirmation a
lead.
