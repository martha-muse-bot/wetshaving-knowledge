**CORRECTION — "Italian Fern" is a TFS scent, never BdF [catalog]:** the 17:06 run's BdF scent list (Mistero/Classico/Esclusivo/Raffinato/Italian Fern, from B&B 346381's "the BdF soaps are the same as the TFS soft soaps with different scents" — which names no scents) is CORRECTED: (a) no BdF listing ever carried Italian Fern — the 2016–2026 category captures show only the five scents + sets; (b) the dataset's ONE "Italian Fern" row (2019-07-18) attributes it to TFS directly in both fields ("[[lineages/tcheon-fung-sing|Tcheon Fung Sing]] Italian fern" soap + aftershave). The five-scent BdF list stands; the correction is annotated on the 17:06 entries in [[lineages|Lineages]], [[lines|Product lines]], [[aliases|Product aliases]], and [[glossary|Glossary]].
