**Schick / Edgewell Milford, CT blade plant — CLOSURE ESTABLISHED (c4-narrow-139 RESOLVED) [catalog]:** the Jan 20, 2026 podcast claim now has two independent news sources. Edgewell filed a CT WARN notice Nov 13, 2025 and announced the closure Nov 17, 2025 (Nov 13 earnings call): the Schick manufacturing facility at **10 Leighton Road, Milford, CT** closes permanently **by Dec 31, 2027**; **293 jobs** cut, first layoffs March 2026 (Mar 2–16 per the WARN letter), all cuts Dec 13–31, 2027; plant director Jeff Wilson's letter to the CT Dept. of Labor. Part of Edgewell consolidating four North American manufacturing sites into a single automated plant (location undisclosed); a Knoxville, TN facility closes too (132 layoffs). Sources: Norwich Bulletin Nov 18, 2025 (full read) + Manufacturing Dive Feb 3, 2026 (Edgewell spokesperson confirms Milford among the four sites; sale of the North American feminine-care business to Essity for $340M in the same restructuring). History pinned: Schick manufacturing in Milford **since 1961** (CT governor's office 2011 press release; Science Museum Group collection — moved from Bridgeport 1961), so the podcast's "about 50 years" understates it (64 years at announcement). EPA RCRA record: the plant manufactures "wet shaving products, razor blades and handles" (stamping, heat treating, sharpening). **[[lineages/catalog-guidance|Catalog guidance]]:** US-made Schick blade production ends Dec 31, 2027 — "Made in USA" Schick blade strings after that date are old stock; the podcast's "injector blades/cartridges unaffected (already made in China)" is consistent with the knowledge base's standing note (current injector production = Schick China vs Personna US) but remains podcast-attributed, not independently verified. Learned 2026-11-15. Established (news + WARN + state/museum sources).
