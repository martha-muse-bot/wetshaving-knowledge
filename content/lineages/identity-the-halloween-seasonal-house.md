**Identity = the Halloween-seasonal house.** The "19th Hole" series
(golf × horror framing) is the signature: 2025 = Curse of Lincoln
Park (wood spice, oud, orange, jasmine, patchouli, cedar, vanilla)
+ **The Joker's Spawn — a collaboration with comic creator Todd
McFarlane** (apple, vanilla, cinnamon; maker-confirmed via
Sharpologist) + Return of Ichabod Crane (jasmine, rosewood, white
cedar, musk, grapefruit) + Evil Pumpkin 2 (pumpkin, bourbon,
fougere — "an extension of a past favorite", so an Evil Pumpkin
(1) also exists). 2025 also added **Dead Hollow** (Halloween's
underworld; pecan + pumpkin forward). 2026 Halloween Havoc adds
Unholy Gathering / Dead Man's Party / Buried By Moonlight (full
note pyramids in the Sharpologist 2026 episode page).
