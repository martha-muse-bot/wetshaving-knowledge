**Wet Shaving Products (WSP) — base system + Olympus launch, ESTABLISHED [catalog]:** founded 2011 by **Lee Tyau**, handcrafted in-house in Chandler, AZ (maker's About page; already in the Asylum addendum above). Three lather formats, maker-documented since the Dec 21, 2018 explainer "Rustic vs. Formula T vs. Shaving Cream": **Rustic** (vegan, 7 ingredients), **Formula T** (tallow, 9 ingredients — "The only difference is in the T"), **Shaving Cream** (13 ingredients, brush or brushless tub). **Olympus** launched June 2018 in Formula T AND Rustic from day one (maker year-in-review; Sep 28, 2018 blog; B&B 545771 firsthand Rustic ownership June 2018); the cream line relaunched 2018 in a new formula after 2 years R&D (Sharpologist review). Scent = Aventus-inspired fruity chypre. Current live Olympus range (maker products.json, live 2026-11-23): Formula T soap $24.99, Rustic soap $24.99, Shaving Cream $29.99 (7.44 oz), Aftershave Splash $29.99, Cooling Aftershave Splash $34.99 (new, created 2026-04-26), Cooling Aftershave Balm $29.99, EdP $64.99/$89.99, Pre-Shave Oil, Beard Oil, Beard Balm, Mustache Wax. **Catalog:** the verified Olympus soap entry is the Formula T soap (split design in [[lines|Product lines]] run section); cream entry owed; Rustic entry owed only on first dataset strings. **Trap:** Dec 2025 created_at dates on the Rustic/cream handles are a store re-platform, never launch dates.
