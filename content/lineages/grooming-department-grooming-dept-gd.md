*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-10-22 · Source: [[Lineages]]*

**[catalog] Grooming Department ("Grooming Dept", GD) — history, naming,
and base system (resolved 2026-10-22, backlog item). NO RENAME, NO
SUCCESSOR — the naming question is settled: "Mallard", "Kairos",
"Nai", "Janus", "Lusso", "Angel", "Fortis", "Primus", the donkey-milk
lines, etc. are FORMULA BASES (soap lines) inside ONE brand group,
not sub-brands; brand stylings in the wild: "Grooming Dept",
"Grooming Dept.", "The Grooming Department", "GD". Founder/maker:
**[[Mohammad Abed]]** (signed his own consolidation email "Mohammad
Abed" — B&B GD club thread; the community calls him "Mo";
Northern California artisan per West Coast Shaving retailer copy;
posts as "GroomingDept" on Damn Fine Shave, e.g. 2018-09-30). Timeline:
beta/tester phase in 2016 ("Grooming Dept v34" noted as "soon to be
released... vegan based and 100% palm free," dendj55 2016-08-10;
"The Grooming Dept. Tester," hawns 2016-12-20/22 — all dataset
primary evidence); retail from ~2017 (West Coast Shaving: "started
Grooming Dept in 2017"; dataset curve 3/23/60/457/621 2016–2020,
peak 2020, then 306/317/336/280/153 2021–2025 and 77 through
2026-10-19 = alive, no destash cliff). One-man shop notorious for
tiny/sold-out drops, fulfillment slowness, and constant formula
experimentation ("genius... experimental with us as the voluntary
beta testers" — B&B club thread). ~2020 CONSOLIDATION (maker's own
email, quoted verbatim by ackvil on B&B GD club p.110, #2,185 —
email date not shown in the fetch; bracketted ~2020 by the DFS
post of 2020-07-23 discussing the upcoming Mallard release):
formulas reduced to THREE — **Nai** (vegan), **Kairos** (tallow),
**Mallard** (duck fat) — with one last **Angel** release (lamb
tallow) and one final unscented DonkeyBase **Asinus**. Post-2020
releases include Mallard Amore and Kairos Lavender (dataset 2021+);
Asinus shipped (secondary-market "Asinus - Spring and Fall" posts
on DFS). **Fortis** = affordable/value base (2020+, scents incl.
Incense & Oud, Ingress, Boomer; sold via GD + West Coast Shaving —
one poster's hope that Fortis get licensed to Oleo/WCS is an
UNVERIFIED lead). **Addendum 2026-10-27 — Fortis licensing verdict:
NOT LICENSED (lead retired, [catalog]).** The licensing idea's
SOLE source is that one poster's own wish (B&B GD club p.110
#2,189, replying to the consolidation email: "I am still hoping
Fortis is kept available in some way... (maybe licensed by GD to
Oleo to produce and West Coast Shaving to sell...)") — a hope,
never a report. Against it: Mohammad's consolidation email kept
only Nai / Kairos / Mallard (+ Angel + Asinus finals) — Fortis is
not in the kept list; no Oleo- or WCS-branded Fortis product,
listing, or announcement exists anywhere in search (the Oleo×WCS
relationship that does exist is the Oleo-made WCS house duck-fat
soap, Canard base — a different product); and the dataset's
Fortis links point at groomingdept.com product pages, i.e.
GD-made stock. Fortis therefore stayed a GD-made value line and
died at the ~2020 consolidation; post-2020 dataset Fortis uses
are destash. Dating correction: Fortis was already in the dataset
in 2019 ("Grooming Dept Spa soap in Fortis base"), so the "2020+"
tag above is too late — Fortis = 2019–~2020. Re-open only on an
Oleo- or WCS-branded Fortis listing or a maker statement.
**Collaborations [catalog]:** **Maol Grooming** —
NOT a GD successor/rebrand (a separate Canadian aftershave brand,
Hanover, Ontario; collab'd also with Tallow + Steel) — the
**Frankenlime/Frankinlime** release (~Oct 2018, announced on the GD
site by Mohammad): soap by Grooming Dept in the DONKEY base +
matching aftershave by Maol Grooming (B&B 570551; DFS 2018-09-30
thread). Dataset strings [["Grooming Dept / MAOL - Frankenlime"]] are
this collab — do NOT create a MAOL brand entry for it. Retailer
channel: West Coast Shaving stocks GD (Before the Fire tallow &
duck fat, etc.). STATUS: active through Oct 2026 — no closure
announcement, no farewell found; one-man-shop watch (flaky
fulfillment is the risk, not closure). Sources: dataset notes
mining sotd_2016–2026_full.json (v34 tester, curve); West Coast
Shaving product page (founding 2017, Mohammad, N. California);
B&B GD club thread pp.105–110 (base rankings, consolidation email
verbatim, Fortis value line); DFS threads (Frankenlime collab
announcement, Maol collab, Asinus posts). Confidence: established
(founder name via own signature; beta+launch window; bases-as-lines;
consolidation + Angel/Asinus finals; Maol collab); exact email date
= lead; Maol maker's name = lead. Validator note: [catalog] product
identity for GD is SCENT + BASE — the same scent in Janus vs Mallard
vs Kairos is a different product; posters routinely include the base
("Grooming Dept - Amare - Kairos base", "- Charbon - Janus Tallow",
"Mallard Rose Chai"). [catalog]
