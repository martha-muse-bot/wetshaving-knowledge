CL discontinued the scented post-shave salve line on **August 1, 2018** ("Saving the Salves", chatillonlux.com blog) and re-launched in September 2018 as **Skin Salves** in only two scents + unscented: **Pure Lavender** and **Fresh Citrus** (2 oz $15 / 4 oz $28). Implication: scented CL salves (LQV salve, GLS salve, etc.) are pre-Aug-2018 products; post-2018 "CL salve" strings are overwhelmingly the Skin Salve line (or old stock).
