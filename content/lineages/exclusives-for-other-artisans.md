**Exclusives for OTHER artisans — NONE (established by complete-store read) [catalog]:** the only "exclusive" language in the whole store is the Music City Suds house-soap copy (5 "exclusively for" hits, all house soaps). Everything else in the ~109-product catalog is standard retail stock (Rockwell, Parker, Dovo, Omega, Merkur, Gillette, Goodfellas' Smile, Bison). [[lineages/the-retailer|The retailer]] has never done an exclusive for another artisan — the TSM narrow lead (b) is answered in the negative. (Contrast: Catie's Bubbles' Le Marché du Rasage was an exclusive FOR The Shave Market group — a different TSM; do not conflate.)
