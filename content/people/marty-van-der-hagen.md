**Marty van der Hagen** — CEO of van der Hagen Enterprises as of 2015
(quoted in the company's Men's Health Grooming Award announcement for
Shave Butter; chaindrugreview.com). Same surname as the brand — the brand
origin story claims a Dutch Van Der Hagen family (Lith, Holland), so this
may be genuine continuity rather than a bought name; unproven either way.
The family company was sold to Universal Beauty Products in 2016 (per
Sharpologist). See [[Lineages]]. Established for the CEO role; family
lineage = lead.
