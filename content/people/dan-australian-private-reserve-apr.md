**Dan — Australian Private Reserve (APR)** (maker/perfumer, Australia;
surname unpinned). Ran APR ~Nov 2016–mid-2020: first batch Nov 2016,
first commercial lineup Jan 2017 (both announced on the Paste & Cut
forum), seasonal + LE + collab releases through June 2020 (Gravefruit
with Southern Witchcrafts, the last). Known for complex, animalic
scents (ambergris accord, civet/castoreum) and for a blunt forum
persona — wyze0ne 2022-12-12: "a brutally honest bloke who definitely
had a way with words and rubbed some people the wrong way, but he was
extremely kind and generous to those he considered friends" (this is
the "infamous" in _walden_'s 2026-10-29 note; no scandal found).
**Died in 2022** — his wife posted on B&B ("Goodbye and thank you
from APR", thread 633570, 2022-12-10): "Dan died earlier this year.
He was far too young to leave this world." The death did NOT close
the brand (APR had already wound down ~2020; socials deleted before
Dec 2022) — it closed the door on any return. Source: B&B 633570
(primary — the widow's post); wyze0ne dataset note 2022-12-12;
vintageshaving.au 5-part APR series (read 2026-11-03/05). Learned
2026-11-05. Established (death + brand arc); LEAD: surname, exact
death date/cause, and the ~2020 closure cause all unpinned.
