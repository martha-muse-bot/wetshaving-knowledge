**Michael Nordstrand — PROMOTED lead → ESTABLISHED (perfumer; co-author of HoM "Sonder").** The 13:06 run's "do NOT record Nordstrand as a perfumer" guard is RETIRED. Independent perfumer; founder and principal perfumer of **Mythologist Studio** (New York); degrees/certifications in perfume creation and cosmetic sciences from Givaudan, ISIPCA, and the Grasse Institute of Perfumery, plus a Cornell certification in medicinal plants (focus: aromatic plants popularized during the Middle Ages); teacher at the Institute for Art and Olfaction and the Met; 2025 Art and Olfaction Award for Bad Lily (Tale Parfum) and 2024 British Society of Perfumers Award for Patchoulÿ Tartessos; founder of the Union for Perfumery & Allied Trades; joined Patina as Principal Perfumer (announced August 2025). Sources: Dry Down Diaries profiles (bio repeated verbatim across his advice-column bio, the Met "Reawakening the Senses" workshop page, and the Fragrantica perfumer page — one bio, three carriers, so treat as a single well-corroborated source, not three independent ones). The HoM fragrance-descriptions page's "Sonder was co-authored by Benjamin Esposito and Michael Nordstrand" now reads as a two-perfumer collaboration. Learned 2026-11-11. Established. [catalog]
