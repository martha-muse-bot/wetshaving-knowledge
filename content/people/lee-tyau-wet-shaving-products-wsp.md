**Lee Tyau — Wet Shaving Products (WSP)** (founder, Chandler, AZ).
Founded WSP in 2011 (started in his garage, after sharpening/
restoring/selling straight razors); the company handcrafts its
grooming products in-house in Chandler, Arizona — "not handed off
to a third-party contract manufacturer" for its own line (maker's
About page + "Happy Birthday WSP" blog, 2022-07-22, live 2026-11-06).
WSP's Formula T tallow soap is the formula behind BullGoose's
[[lines/asylum-shave-works|Asylum Shave Works]] tallow line (verbatim INCI + marketing-copy
fingerprint, established 2026-11-06 — see [[lineages|Lineages]] Asylum
addendum). Production head is Cory Renshaw ("Cory makes it all").
