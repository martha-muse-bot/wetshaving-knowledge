Surname and current farm status unknown; brand closed (see [[lineages|Lineages]] 2026-10-31). [catalog] brand closed; surname stays a lead.
**CORRECTION 2026-11-06:** Karine is NOT the soapmaker — the maker's own 2015 "Qui sommes nous" page (Wayback snapshot 20150224005551, live-browser read) says **Franck Jeandaleix** makes the soaps ("Franck a en charge la fabrication des savons"); Karine makes the confipeaux (jams) and does the packaging. She handled the ordering correspondence, which is why the 2026-10-31 entry named her. **Franck Jeandaleix** is the maker (established, maker page + contact block "Jeandaleix / 63620 Giat"). Location corrected to **Giat**, not Pontaumur (see [[lineages|Lineages]] Bon Berger addendum 2026-11-06). Karine's surname stays UNPINNED as a direct attestation — the household/business name is Jeandaleix, "Karine Jeandaleix" is the working hypothesis only. The farm continues as La Fermanerie (camping/gîte/donkey rental, hosts "Franck et Karine" per the farm's own 2018–19 Miimosa page; campground still listed Oct 2026) — the soapery closed, the farm did not.
