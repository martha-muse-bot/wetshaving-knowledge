**Rudy Vey — custom brush maker / restorer** (established 2026-11-01).
B&B username "Rudy Vey" (tagline "Shaving baby skin and turkey
necks"); runs his own "Rudy Vey Custom Shaving Brushes and
Restorations" vendor thread (badgerandblade.com, thread 160924, still
crawled 2026). Made the B&B **2012 LE brush** (keyhole design, aged
ivory resin, 100 numbered brushes with B&B + RV engravings, sold out
2012-02-15) and the **2014 LE brush** (tortoiseshell resin, 50
brushes, sold out in ~24 hours), plus the 2012 Christmas Horn Set
(lather bowl + brush + razor handle; B&B wiki Product Releases page).
Signature practices: laser-engraved RV logo on the handle bottom
(his own process posts: sticker alternatives rejected, 2–3 laser
passes); universal praise on craftsmanship and service ("top tier
brushes, meticulously crafted"). Still active at least through
2022–2023 ("Rudy Vey's 2022 Contributor handle" threads; @Rudy Vey
active in synthetic-knot threads). Mozingo link: Brett Mozingo
provided knotting services for Vey handles (B&B 628985, 627538).

threads (fetched 2026-11-01). [catalog] own brand entry owed;
