**Maria Arman** — maker behind **Through the Fire Fine Craft**
(TTFFC), Alanson MI; also made the Maggard-brand and Blades Grim
soaps (her own email, quoted in B&B's "Through the Fire Fine Craft
Soaps" thread). **Rebranded ~2022–2024 to "Through the Fire Fine
Crafts" (plural), ttffcrafts.com** — shaving line still in production,
now sold through Dr. Mike's Shaving Emporium (retail partner; maker's
own site, live 2026-10-23); Maggard house-soap relationship continues.
Established (maker's own email via B&B + live maker site + live Maggard
page). [catalog]
