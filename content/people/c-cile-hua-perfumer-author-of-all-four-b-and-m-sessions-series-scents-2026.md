**Cécile Hua — perfumer, author of all four [[aliases/b-and-m-sessions-series|B&M Sessions Series]] scents (2026).** Maker copy on every Sessions Series soap/splash page (barristerandmann.com, live 2026-11-11): "authored by perfumer Cecile Hua." A first for B&M — the house's scents are normally founder Will Carius's own compositions, so an outside master perfumer authoring a whole line is a line-level fact, not a footnote. Background (Silloria perfumer profile, live 2026-11-11): French + Vietnamese heritage, grew up in Marseille; trained at the Givaudan Roure Perfumery School in Grasse, graduated first in her class; joined Givaudan as a Junior Perfumer in 1996, seven years in the program; fifteen years in luxury fine fragrance (Paris → New York); first recipient of the Fashion Group International Rising Star Award for Fragrance Design; today a senior perfumer at MANE, in close partnership with Arielle Shoshana (the nose behind Arielle Shoshana's Sunday and Monday, and Paris Hilton's Fairy Dust). Also credited on Cypress Cove "An Evening At The Resort" (cafleurebon, July 2025). **LEAD (not established):** the "Cécile Krakower" name — Silloria carries a separate Krakower profile (Jean Patou 1997 → Givaudan → Mane) and one secondary wiki lists "Cécile Hua a.k.a. Cécile Krakower"; the two profiles' career timelines do not cleanly match, so do NOT record Krakower as her former name until a primary source ties them.
