**Shawn Maher — identity-line restoration + [[Maher Olfactive]] addendum
(dated correction, 2026-10-31).** The earlier entry's header line was
lost (text begins mid-sentence "musician + ad copywriter..."); the
content is intact. Restored identity: **Shawn Maher** —
founder/perfumer of **[[Chatillon Lux]]** (2015) and **Maher Olfactive**
(launched April 28, 2020; first release Crystal Moon), St. Louis MO;
musician + ad copywriter, self-taught perfumer; posts as
**@ChatillonLux** on Damn Fine Shave; writes the Scent Notes blog.
Maher Olfactive rationale (cafleurebon, launch coverage): "a separate
space where the goal was Art for Art's Sake — unconstrained by
budgetary concerns such as cost of materials"; the line's scents are
"edgier and riskier" with more naturals (Fragrantica interview).
Earlier contract-perfumery lead now CLARIFIED (not withdrawn): he
composed for other brands/shop lines — "Madame Chouteau" for
**American Perfumer** (St. Louis retail shop; his blog Feb 5, 2020)
— and Madame Chouteau now also appears as a **Maher Olfactive**
scent in dataset strings (2024–2025), so the scent exists under both
attributions; attribution caution stands (maker of the juice ≠ brand
on the bottle). See [[Lineages]] (Maher Olfactive brand entry).
Sources: Fragrantica "History as Perfume" interview; cafleurebon
launch reviews; scent-notes.com (Maher's blog); dataset mining
sotd_2024–2026_full.json. Established (identity + MO line + rationale).
