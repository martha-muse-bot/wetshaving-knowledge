**Brett Mozingo — Mozingo Brushworks** (maker, Youngsville NC).
Custom shaving-brush maker and hand-tied badger knot maker:
ZT3/ZT4-series fan knots (natural, untreated/ungelled badger —
owner-described as the opposite of gel-treated, tips soft
without treatment), custom resin handles (Peacock, Molten Metal
M7, Flower Power, Tie-Dye — e.g. "Mozingo Peacock", "Mozingo
Flower Power"), synthetic M7 line, and a knotting service
installing his knots in other makers' handles (Rudy Vey, Chisel
& Hound examples in B&B 627538). Posted in r/Wetshaving as
**u/Cousin-Eddie** (30 authored SOTD posts 2016–2020; Tuxcats'
2016 note attributes his new "Mozingo Flower Power" brush to
u/Cousin-Eddie; pppork's 2020 "Mozingo Sumohive" beehive custom
built by him). Also did resin-pour collab handles (B&B
NomadicDave thread: "Mozingo Brushworks/Jesse Chapman pour").

"Mozingo is shutting down"; B&B 627538 ~2024: Etsy on "taking a
break", mozingobrushworks.com up for sale, order email bouncing,

mozingobrushworks@gmail.com; B&B 661515 (2026) lists Mozingo
