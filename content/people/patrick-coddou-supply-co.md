**Patrick Coddou — Supply Co** (founder; wife Jennifer Coddou, co-founder). Aerospace engineer (ex-Lockheed Martin Aeronautics) who started Supply after a Razorpedia review site led him to vintage Schick injectors; bootstrapped the brand on Kickstarter (2015/2017), a 2019 "Shark Tank" appearance (Herjavec deal never closed), until the Foundry Brands acquisition (Sept 2022, entire team stayed).
