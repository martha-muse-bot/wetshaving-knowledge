design house (Vullion Group Pty Ltd, Melbourne; Sharpologist 2019).

founded by "Julian Vue" (an anglicised moniker — the maker's own site
says it protected the family from identity theft/e-commerce hackers;
he has since died), a 75-year-old ex-army captain, who started with
shaving creams/soaps; his son added safety razors and brushes in
1985 and the family bought its own manufacturing company for quality
control; now led by fourth-generation Robert Vu. Community shorthand:
VB. Source: bestmensshaver.com Chieftain review; vikingsblade.com
myth-debunk page (fetched 2026-10-22). Learned 2026-10-22.
