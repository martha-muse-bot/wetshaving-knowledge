**Models:** **Bulldog** (2010, $80, stainless, smooth safety bar + OC option; Classic Shaving 2010-12-27) — also the standard handle name. **H20/H2O** (2011, $149, T6 aluminum head, dual-comb OC one side + SB other; Classic Shaving 2011-11-01). **Tech/Tek** (Gillette Tech homage; Fendrihan labeled "closed comb" instead of "ikon tech"). **B1 Standard = solid bar** (B&B verbatim: 'the "solid bar" Ikon B1 is called the B1 Standard'), **B1 Deluxe = OC**; **B1 Slant**; B1 = coating/finish on Standard/Deluxe heads (Nusquam-Humanitus 2017-01-28 "iKon Standard / B1 Coating w/Bulldog Handle"; B1 Standard has 4 corner notches torquing the blade). **Shavecraft 101/102/103/104** (101 dual-comb, 102 slant, 103 short comb, 104 Tech — from catalog cluster names). **X3 slant** (largest iKon head in dataset). **SBS** (attested head, expansion unpinned). **OSS = long handle** (vs Bulldog 80mm handle). **El Jefe** (corroborated cluster).
