**[catalog] Personna "New Angle 70" = the community's "BBS-0" — SOLVED
dataset mystery (2026-10-21).** The discuss queue's "Personna 'New
Angle 70 / BBS-0' — identity unsettled" entry is a REAL vintage
product, not a launch. Dataset profile (the ANTI-recency trap):
336 razor-field uses spread 2020–2026 (77/33/39/108/51/22/6), 12
distinct authors, first use 2020-06-21 — well past the 3-author
attestation threshold. Dominant dataset strings: `Personna -
New Angle 70 (BBS-0)` (97), `Personna "BBS-0"` (150 across
HTML-escaped/unescaped spellings), `Personna BBS-0` (19+18), plus
`Personna New Angle 70 (BBS-0)`, `Personna New Angle 70: BBS-0`.
Real name established 2022-03-02: Sharpologist's Wet Shaving Talk
podcast host found a NOS packaged example and read the package —
the name is the **Personna "New Angle 70"**; the community calls it
the **"BBS-0" / "BBS Zero"** because it is reported to be an
inspiration for the famous BBS-1, and its parts are reportedly
interchangeable with the BBS-1 and the Gillette Tech (Sharpologist,
firsthand handling + eBay research). Construction (same source):
aluminum handle, plated brass, galvanized-steel top cap. Made in
England, 1960s (per B&B Vintage April 2021 post). LEAD (single
community source, hedged "from what I have read"): it was provided
to first-class airline passengers flying out of England. Variant
note (lead, from ONE 2020 dataset post by Enndeegee): `person a
travel (aka bbs-0)` — likely "Personna travel", suggesting a travel
variant also carries the BBS-0 nickname. **TRAVEL-VARIANT QUESTION
SOLVED 2026-10-21: "Personna travel" is NOT a variant — it is the
poster's provenance descriptor for his New Angle 70 (see the
[[Product aliases]] entry), folding INTO this product; no separate
travel product exists.** Sources:
Sharpologist Wet Shaving Talk 2 Mar 2022 podcast
(https://sharpologist.com/podcast/wet-shaving-talk-for-2-mar-2022/);
B&B "Focus on Vintage DEs" thread post 2021-04-03
(https://www.badgerandblade.com/forum/threads/focus-on-vintage-des-for-2-weeks-4-1-4-15.605269/page-4);

**[[ADDENDUM 2026-10-26]] — the "inspiration for the BBS-1" claim:
DOWNGRADED, effectively retired (backlog lineage thread resolved).**
The claim traces to exactly ONE source: B&B 605269 #71 (2021-04-03),
doubly hedged — "nicknamed the 'BBS-0' because, from what I've
read, it was reported to be an inspiration for the famous BBS1"
(same post carries the airline story). Against it stands the
STRONGER source, Sharpologist's BBS-1 history article (mid-2018,
written with thanks to both [[James Dufour]] and [[John Brown]], i.e.
near-primary): its "Similar Razors" section names a DIFFERENT razor
as the inspiration — the **BR 131 Basic Safety Razor: "This is the
razor that inspired the BBS-1. It's a Chinese knock-off of a 3 pcs
Gillette Tech"** — and calls the Gillette Tech "inspiration razor
number one" (the BR131 being a copy of it). In that same article
the Personna is introduced only as "Nicknamed the BBS-0" with
"dimensions are similar and the parts of this razor are
interchangeable with the BBS-1" — similarity + interchangeability,
NOT inspiration. So: (a) the nickname "BBS-0" originates in the
2018 Sharpologist article, where it means "similar to / interchangeable
with the BBS-1"; (b) the "inspired the BBS-1" reading is a later
community back-formation from the "0" in the nickname (the 2021 post
links "more info and a review" — i.e. the very Sharpologist article
that says otherwise); (c) the documented BBS-1 lineage is
**Gillette Tech → Baili BR131 → BBS-1 → Wolfman WR2**, with the
Personna New Angle 70 a PARALLEL Tech-family razor, not an ancestor.
Corroboration for the Tech root: the contemporary 2014 B&B BBS-1
review ("John from LA Shaving likely used the tech as a starting
point... the BBS-1 very much emulates a tech"). What STANDS from
the original entry: identity = New Angle 70, nickname BBS-0, and
parts interchangeability with the BBS-1 (Sharpologist firsthand,
owned both). What is RETIRED: never cite the Personna as a BBS-1
design inspiration, and never use the Personna↔Wolfman link as a
lineage (ancestor) link — it is a similarity/interchangeability link
only. Catalog entries unchanged (BBS-0 remains the community alias;
the BR131 is already the Baili 131 entry). RESIDUAL LEAD: the
article says John sent James "two safety razors to try" — the second
razor is unnamed; do not guess it was the Personna.
