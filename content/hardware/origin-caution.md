**Origin caution:** "Handmade in Isle of Man" is stated ONLY for the vegan brush. Do NOT transfer it to the boar brush (whose manufacture attribute stays Omega 50014, Italy). The two brushes share a title — distinguish by handle (`travel-shave-brush` vs `vegan-travel-shave-brush`), fiber, price ($25 vs $48), and weight (40 g vs 71 g).
