**Named editions:** every razor carries a unique serial number or a unique NAME (maker gallery). Named one-offs/small runs: Fuso (Mokume), Apex, Atlas, Red, G2, Utah, 7Q-89 (serial-name convention), Black, Blue (1-of-1), Crown, Kojin, Ronin, Lowe, V42 (Devil's Brigade knife), Templar (Knights Templar), Hada (Japanese sword grain, brass/copper mokume cap), Atrum, Niwa, Takeda, Avem, GII, Iliad, Avem, Sparta, Osiris (Damascus deity-conflict series), Marine, Carbide, "One Black, One Blue". The "X of 6" six-piece series (Atrum 2/6, Templar 3/6, Hada 4/6, V42 5/6, Lowe 6/6). **Catalog rule:** ALL fold to `razor/carbon-shaving-cx` as LE/variant attributes; never create entries on LE names (P-17); maker also offers inlays (18K red/yellow gold, one with G VS diamond) and 2-tone finishes — cosmetic attributes.
