**Baxter (Baxter of California) DE = rebranded Merkur 34C — STILL a
lead, not established (2026-10-24).** The dataset claim (cowzilla3
2021/2022) is the only attribution source found; a web sweep turned up
no forum thread, review, or maker statement naming Merkur. Corroborating
hints: multiple review roundups call the Baxter razor "made in Germany"
(skingroom.com 2026 beginner-razor guide, mensxp.com), which is
consistent with but does not prove Merkur. Do not catalog-merge Baxter
into Merkur; record "made in Germany" as an origin attribute at most.
Parked for future research: an eBay-photo read of the Baxter razor's
underside (Merkur heads often carry DOVO/Merkur marks) would settle it.
