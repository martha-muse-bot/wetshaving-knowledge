*Confidence: established · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**ST = Standard, HA = Hyper-Aggressive — maker-established, not poster shorthand.** The 2021 page's Specifications section reads verbatim: "Standard (ST) Head Weight: 43g / Hyper-Aggressive (HA) Head Weight: 46g / Austin Handle Weight: 57g / Dallas Handle Weight: 55g / Fort Worth Handle Weight: 50g." Both heads: aggressive blade gap, positive blade exposure, shallow blade angle, "ride the cap"; HA "one of the most efficient and aggressive razors to come to market"; ST "super efficient and smooth... still an aggressive razor and not for beginners."
