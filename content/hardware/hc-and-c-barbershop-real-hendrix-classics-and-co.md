**"HC&C Barbershop" = real Hendrix Classics & Co. scent, distinct from
"Barber's Invasion".** 32 soap-field strings 2020–2024 (EldrormR 28,
Delttaz 5, plus menthol-doctored variants) + catalog's
unverified/soap/hendrix-classics-and-co-barbershop-soap. "Barbershop by
Hendrix Classics & Co" is a standalone fragrance listing (fraganceswiki:
basil/bergamot/oakmoss/patchouli) — the "HC&C" abbreviation is the
poster's brand shorthand, not a typo of HAGS. The miner's
"hags-barbershop" match was wrong. [catalog] keep Barbershop and
Barber's Invasion as separate scent entries.
