**[catalog] Leaf Shave — the half-DE / pivoting bracket, FULL model
sheet (RESOLVED 2026-11-01, backlog item).** Leaf Shave (Pittsburgh PA),
founded 2016 by engineers Adam Hahn + Adam Simone (Blue Belt
Technologies surgical-robotics colleagues; Simone Northeastern ME '09,
former Gillette co-op — sources: Northeastern COE interview, Lochtree
brand page, Mama Earth Talk podcast ep 023). Two PRODUCT LINES, one
PIVOTING and one FIXED — the distinction posters routinely blur:
- **PIVOTING line (multi-blade): The Leaf (original) → Leaf Two.**
The Leaf: Kickstarter summer 2016 ($115,328 from 1,400+ backers vs
$100k goal; Northeastern), deliveries 2017, official retail launch
March 2018 post-KS fulfillment (intersectmagazine interview, Adam
Simone). Holds 1–3 half-DE blades, pivoting cartridge-like head,
world's first multi-blade pivoting plastic-free razor (Sharpologist).
RETIRED 2026: maker's FAQ — "Leaf Two replaces the 3-blade original
Leaf Razor" — and the Korean namu.wiki shaver article pins the
discontinuation to 2026-01-21 (single community-compiled source,
lead-grade for the date; the replacement itself is maker-established).
**Leaf Two** (2026): 2 half-DE blades, guarded blade positions,
pivoting head, all-metal; $109 kit. "The Leaf" dataset strings =
the ORIGINAL, never the Two.
- **FIXED line (single-edge): Twig → Thorn → Branch.** All use ONE
half-DE blade, fixed head held "at an angle similar to a cartridge
razor" (maker copy) — NOT pivoting; twist-to-open via the handle
base, magnetic load assist, back-weighted handle.
**Twig** (2021): Kickstarter Sep 2020 (geeky-gadgets 2020-09-09;
deliveries expected Nov 2020; >$155k raised per Sharpologist),
retail from early 2021 (dataset: first razor-field "twig" hits
2021, 32). Mild/lighter-hair/sensitive-skin positioning. 2.5 oz
(~68 g), 102 mm; maker blade gap 1.0 mm (per Leaf, via Sharpologist
Branch review). Sharpologist Twig review (updated Feb 2023)
confirms fixed-head + prototype history back to 2016.
**Thorn** (2021): the more aggressive sibling — in reviewers'
hands by June 2021 (luvmy07subie's "Leaf Thorn Copper" 2021-06-05,
Twig-vs-Thorn duel videos Jul/Sep 2021; B&B "Twig Thorn v Twig"
published 2022-02-01). Same body as Twig; ID MARKER: a raised dot
on the base-plate center marks the Thorn (maker's answer to the
B&B poster — the dot does not exist on the Twig; the only visible
difference, so same-color Twig+Thorn pairs are visually
indistinguishable assembled). Maker blade gap 1.2 mm; medium+
aggressiveness, "coarser hair" positioning.
**Branch** (2026): consolidates the fixed line — Sharpologist review
~Aug 2026; maker's single-edge-razors page (live 2026-11-01):
"Branch is our all-new... the new version of Twig and Thorn,
blending both models into one razor"; Twig = "mild legacy razor",
Thorn = "aggressive legacy razor", both "available while stock
lasts". Blade gap 1.1 mm (0.1 above Twig, 0.1 below Thorn);
hex-grip handle, silicone travel head cover. Dataset: "branch"
first appears 2026 (5 hits); "thorn" 2026 = 18 hits vs 3 in 2025 —
the resurgence is Branch-launch chatter/comparison, watch whether
it persists. **Catalog: Branch = NEW entry (2026); Twig/Thorn =
legacy (phased out 2026, stock-sell-through); Twig/Thorn/Leaf Two
all coexist in the 2026 dataset.**
