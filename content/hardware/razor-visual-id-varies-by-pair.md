Razor visual ID varies by pair: Karve Christopher Bradley (scalloped guard, chunky machining, often brass, plate aggression letter AA–G) vs Wolfman WR2 (slim head, mirror polish, smooth bar) is separable in a clear photo; lookalike 3-piece razors and vintage Gillette variants often are not. Conservative rule stands: clearly identifiable or no photo verification.
