*Confidence: unknown · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**Dates:** brass original existed by **Dec 2015** (Sharpologist Fendrihan article 2015-12-11 calls the SBS "limited edition" and describes it: "a comb and bar, and a baseplate that flipped to offer mild and aggressive sides"). Aluminum reissue in circulation by **2019** (B&B 571174 p.4 "Tradere Is Back," Jul 2019: "the new SBS (which is some kind of aluminum or aluminum alloy...) is a completely different beast... INSPIRED by the Tradere and the original SBS"). Connaught sold SBS heads at £20 ex VAT (same thread). Brass original: no serial numbers, quantity unknown ("nobody actually knows exactly how many were made and sold"), $200+; aluminum reissue: "made of the same material that the Shavecraft 101" per Greg (same thread) — proprietary hardened aluminum. Patent-infringement stop stands (Tradere-identical parts down to tool markings; reversible baseplate).
