**179 = the butterfly/butterfly-TTO (Rockwell R1 mold).** BD179 ≡
BR179 — SAME razor under two prefix codes (RESOLVES the earlier
"likely duplicates" flag, which is now ESTABLISHED). Evidence: B&B
586713 #1 identifies the R1 as a "Baili BD179"; Maggard sells it as
the "Baili BR179 TTO" (B&B 616190 #45); a 2023-07-05 SOTD firsthand
note chains them explicitly: "The Baili BR179 is currently being sold
at CVS [under their Goodline brand]... you may also recognize this
razor as the Rockwell R1." Dataset: BR179 dominates (87 + 59 chrome +
28 chrome + misc, 2020–2026) vs "Baili BD179 TTO" ×4. Catalog: fold
all BD179/BR179 strings into ONE Baili 179 entry; canonical name form
= "Baili 179" (prefix omitted; number is the identity).
