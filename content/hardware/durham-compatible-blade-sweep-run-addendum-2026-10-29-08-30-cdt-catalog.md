**Durham compatible-blade sweep (run addendum 2026-10-29
~08:30 CDT) [catalog]:** full dataset scan (sotd_2016–2026,
35 posts with "durham" in razor/blade field) + forum research.
The modern compatible-blade list is wider than 72-0200 alone —
THREE established functional substitutes, plus one
retailer-category name and one performance-caveat family:
(1) **Ever Ready hair-shaper blade — ESTABLISHED second
substitute (functional, not exact).** Discovery: B&B member
"Eighties", SOTD thread 617386 #192 (Dec 4, 2021, "Obscure
Shaving Gear" week): "After a bit of research, I discovered
that UK packaged Ever Ready hair shaper blades fit the razor
perfectly" (mug + dome shave, DFS, "BLADE: Ever Ready Compact
Hair Trimmer Blade"). Confirmed by the SABRE-group poster
(B&B 360385 #38, "following Eighties' suggestion"): "They're
exactly the right size, slightly thinner (which is a good
thing) and extremely sharp... I've stropped both edges"
(double-edged, ~£1/blade). Dataset chain: Semaj3000 2022
("Ever Ready hair shaper blade") → suggested to Enndeegee
×5 2022 ("ever ready hair sharpener blade (u/semaj3000s
suggestion)") → djundjila 2022 ("Ever Ready Compact Hair
Trimmer", "following in u/Enndeegee's footsteps") →
throwa-waaaay 2024 ("[[Durham Duplex]] Hair Shaper Blades", the
razor+blades sent to him by Enndeegee: "It came with a
proprietary hair shaper blade"). The exact Ever Ready SKU is
unpinned (a UK barber-supply hair-shaper blade), and
"exactly the right size" is one owner's fit report — so
functional substitute, never an exact-match claim.
(2) **Personna Double Edge Pathology 72-0200 — fit/use
reconfirmed + dataset attestation.** The 2013 Personna
medical catalog (doczz.net mirror of personnablades.com)
lists "Personna® Double Edge Pathology Blades 72-0200 —
Blade, Personna DE 100/CT" (confirms the DE-pathology
identity); the Dabblers thread calls them "Personna
Vivisection blades" (#41, Bil) and names "Model 72-0200"
(#49). Dataset adds NeedsMoreMenthol 2020-06-26
("Personna Pathology" in a Durham Duplex, "Freeze Your
Face Off Friday") — the substitute is now attested in
actual SOTD use, not just the B&B PIF thread. A 2025 eBay
listing's verified-purchase feedback ("fit and work well
with my vintage Durham double edge razor", item
187444825133) is a third-party fit corroboration.
(3) **"Personna Specimen Prep Blade" (anchovie7468 ×6,
2025) = his name for the 72-0200 pathology blade, NOT a
separate SKU [catalog]:** fits across five Durham styles
incl. the Demonstrator ("Durham Duplex - Open Single Edge
Comb", "Open Edge Guard", "Open Blade", "Straight Bar
Shavette", "Open Blade System", "Durham Demonstrator
Black Scales"); stropped with reuse counts (1X)/(2X)/(3X);
verdict "The downfall of the Durham is the blade / The
Personna Specimen Prep is OK / I wish I could find or
adapt something keener" + "hit or miss edge" — matching
the uncoated 72-0200's stroppable, variable-edge behavior
in the Dabblers thread. No retail product literally named
"Personna Specimen Prep" found (microscopy suppliers sell
Personna blades as specimen-prep stock). Catalog: fold
these blade strings into the 72-0200 substitute cluster;
never create a "Specimen Prep" product entry.
(4) **Carpet-cutting blades (Personna 61-0017/61-0019) —
used, with performance caveats.** The Dec-2021 post notes
"Apparently many use carpet cutting blades in this
razor" and Bil (#49) flags 61-0017/61-0019 as
carpet-cutting blades sometimes mislabeled as pathology
blades; the post compares rough reports to "uninformed
new wet shavers using Gem paint scraper blades in their
Gem blade holders" — i.e. fit-with-pulling/tugging
reports. **Dataset quantification (2026-10-31): exactly
ONE SOTD poster ever used carpet blades in a Durham —
luvmy07subie's 2021-09-17 "~1947 DURHAM DORSET" hack
post** (Personna "Carpet Blades" from Home Depot, plus a
Feather Pro); zero dataset strings mention 61-0017 or
61-0019 (two raw grep hits are `created_utc` epoch
timestamps, a chunking false positive). The "many" is
forum lore, not dataset reality. Catalog: note as a known
hack family, not a recommended substitute; Bil's #49
note keeps its photo-ID value — 61-0017/61-0019 is
sometimes MISLABELED as pathology blades, so validators
buying "pathology blades" should check the model number.
**Mismatches (never compatibility evidence):** Astra SP
(Jimtasticness 2020, Lather Games) and Wizamet (Jimtasticness
2021, aesthetics post) in Durham razor fields are DE blades
that CANNOT fit — poster mismatches. A DE blade field on a
Durham razor post is a mismatch, never a substitute.
**Demonstrator = real model (established):** Smithsonian NMAH
nmah_1347457 ("Straight Razor with Celluloid Handle and
Case"): handle inscribed "DURHAM DUPLEX BLADES" and "DURHAM
DEMONSTRATION", blade "PAT. U.S.A. MAY 28, 07." — the
barber-demo straight-style model. Catalog [catalog]: fold
anchovie7468's "Durham Demonstrator Black Scales" (2025)
into the Durham family with Demonstrator as model
attribute (P-17); the other anchovie7468 razor strings
("Open Single Edge Comb", "Open Edge Guard", "Open Blade",
"Straight Bar Shavette", "Open Blade System") are
poster descriptors of guard/attachment states, not models.
**Company-arc refinement (via the Dec-2021 post's Shave
Nook citation, lead-grade):** Durham Safety Razor Co.
founded NYC c. 1908 by Thomas C. Durham; trademark
published May 18, 1908 (first use ~Feb 1, 1908); first
U.S. patents filed Oct 1906 / Jan 1907 / Mar 1907
(inventors Durham, Sidney I. Prescott, Thomas C. Sheehan);
1917: five million razors in use claimed; 1920: eight
million satisfied users claimed. Also the base definition:
"barber type folding straight razor with a removable
comb guard, a double-edge rigid replaceable blade, and
a celluloid imitation ivory ('Parisian Ivory') handle."
**Weck connection note:** Weck bought Durham-Enders Nov
1963 — and Weck's house product was the hair-shaper blade
(Sextoblade); the hair-shaper-blade ↔ Durham-blade fit is
the same flat-long blade form family, which explains the
compatibility. Context, not a product claim.
