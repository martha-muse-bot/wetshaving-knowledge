*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**CORRECTION 2026-10-24 — Baxter verdict REVERSED: NOT Merkur 34C;
Mühle R89-family head (established).** The 2026-10-24 OEM run missed
that the dataset source contradicts himself: cowzilla3 calls his own
Baxter a "rebranded Muhle R-89" TWICE (2021-05-19 DE Showdown note;
2021-07-09 "my Baxter (Muhle R89)") and a "rebranded Merkur 34C" only
ONCE (2021-09-04). Two independent lines then settle it against
the 34C: (1) STRUCTURE — Baxter's own FAQ
(baxterofcalifornia.com/pages/customer-service-faqs) describes blade
changes as "Unscrew the head from the handle. Separate the two sides
of the head" — a THREE-piece razor (cap + base plate + handle);
the Merkur 34C is a TWO-piece razor (handle fixed to the base plate,
only the cap unscrews), so the 34C identification is structurally
impossible. (2) MAKER — primandprep.com's single-blade review states
the Baxter razor is "Manufactured in Germany by Muhle who produces
the ever so popular Muhle R89." Baxter's own FAQ dimensions (~3.5 in
tall, ~1.5 in head) fit the R89 family, not the short 34C. The
R89/DE89 head-design equivalence is community-established (EJ DE89
and Mühle R89 share the head), so "Mühle R89-family head" is the
precise attribution — whether the cap is literally an R89 cap or the
shared DE89/R89 head is a distinction without a catalog difference.
The Baxter handle (barber-pole spiral, per Uncrate/Gentleman's
Journal) is Baxter's own styling, not the R89's standard handle.
[catalog] [[Catalog guidance]] per P-04: Baxter Safety Razor stays its
OWN Baxter entry; record Mühle (Germany) R89-family head as a
manufacture ATTRIBUTE; do NOT merge into Merkur 34C and do NOT merge
into Mühle R89. The eBay-photo lead for DOVO/Merkur marks is retired
(moot — structure already rules Merkur out). Residual lead: confirm
from a second Mühle-naming source (primandprep is currently the only
direct "manufactured by Mühle" statement); a Baxter×Mühle private-label
confirmation from either company would firm the attribution from
strong-corroborated to primary.
