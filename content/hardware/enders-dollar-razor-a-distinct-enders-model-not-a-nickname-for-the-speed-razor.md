*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[["Enders' Dollar Razor"]] = a DISTINCT Enders model, NOT a
nickname for the Speed razor [catalog].** Established by
case/pack evidence, not by the dataset: a Proxibid auction
lot (Hawker Services #597, lot 150) describes a cased
"Vintage Enders Razor Co. Dollar Razor" — signature
engraved on the razor, case stamped inside "Enders Razor
Co, New York City, U.S.A., None Genuine Without The
Signature, Trade WmEnders Mark" and outside "Enders
Dollar Razor", 4 1/4 in razor with one blade in package.
Auction lots elsewhere list "Enders Speed & Dollar Razors"
and "Enders Dollar Safety Razor" as items SEPARATE from
Speed Shaver lots — the trade treats them as two models.
The NYC imprint dates it to the pre-Mystic era (HQ moved
to Mystic CT by 1941). The name is a PRICE point: a
Feb 28, 1931 Collier's ad (Enders Razor Co., 105 W. 40th
Street, New York, N.Y.) offers "trade a dollar for an
Enders razor and six [blades]" — contemporary with the
~1930 Speed razor launch, so Dollar is the early-1930s
budget/entry model, not a later rename.
