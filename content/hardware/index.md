Resolves the backlog "Rockwell 2C provenance" item. The 2C is a
Rockwell own-design razor: the SAME chromed-zinc-alloy 3-piece body as
the 6C, shipping with only ONE reversible base plate instead of three
— so it offers 2 shave settings (R1 + R3 on the one flippable plate)
where the 6C offers 6 (R1–R6 across three plates) and the 6S the same
6 settings in stainless steel (made in USA; the chrome 6C/2C are made
in China). The system is Rockwell's patented adjustable base-plate
design, originating with the 6S Kickstarter (2014) — not a rebrand,
and not a Baili-type OEM buy like the R1 TTO was.
Naming decoded (all three letters explained, no R1-line inheritance):
**2 = two settings, C = chrome** — exactly parallel to 6C (6 settings,
chrome) and 6S (6 settings, stainless). The "R1" inside "2C (R1/R3)"
is the mildest PLATE SETTING of the 6-series system, not the R1 TTO
razor — a different product that merely shares the setting-name
family. The backlog's "does the 2 follow from the R1 line" half is
therefore answered NO; the "2 plates" half is answered "one plate,
two settings."
Launch: the Chrome Series (2C + 6C) was crowdfunded autumn 2016
(Gadget Flow: campaign ending Oct 22, 2016; $25 for the 2C with the
R1/R3 plate vs $40 for the full six-size set; Gear Diary Dec 13,
2016 pre-order piece, delivery estimated March 2017). Dataset first
2C string 2017-05-08 — consistent, no earlier strings. Maker's
current FAQ confirms the 2C still ships with the R1/R3 plate
("The 2C includes the R1/R3 plate"; published gaps R1 0.20 mm,
R3 0.48 mm). The 2C is CURRENT (retail listings live Oct 2026).

Baili/Weidi (Guangzhou Weidi Technology Co., Ltd.; first DE razor 2007
per B&B 647716) sells the same physical models under at least three
two-letter prefix conventions: **BR** (dominant in Western retail),
**BD** (the maker's own "BAILI Classic" line + AliExpress/older stock),
**BT** (oldest scheme — Vikings Blade's own page calls the BT131 the
"unrelated ancient BT131 model"). No community source decodes the
letters; the letters are demonstrably NOT a product-type code: 179 is a
butterfly TTO under BOTH BR and BD; 131 is a 3-piece under BOTH BR and
BT; 171 appears under BOTH BR and BT; 176 under BR and BD; 191 under
BR and BD. The working reading (lead): the prefix = SKU series / sales
channel, the number = the product. Caution for the validators: posters
TRANSPOSE the prefix letters freely ("Baili DB179", B&B 651308 #29 —
see below), so normalize BR/BD/BT + number before matching, and treat
prefix-letter order variants as the same code.
