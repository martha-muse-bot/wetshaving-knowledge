**Decode:** the number-letter code on WCS handles is **number = weight in grams; letter = finish**. Evidence: Sharpologist "West Coast Shaving Razors" (2016-12-02): "175B" Midnight handle, "four inch handle and a weight of 175 grams"; Classic = 303 stainless steel, Midnight = 303 stainless with black powder coating; six handle styles mated to scalloped SB or OC heads (zinc alloy, chrome-plated).
