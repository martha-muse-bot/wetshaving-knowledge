**Windsor re-launched (product created 2024-06-24, published 2025-06-25).**
Maker copy: "In 2025, more than a decade after its first launch, we've
updated the Windsor line with new materials, improved finishes, and new
handle designs... machined and finished in-house at our San Diego shop."
Plates: **M (Mild) / R (Regular) / H (High)** — the ORIGINAL plate names
are back, exactly what the community asked for at acquisition time
(backlog: "bring back the Windsor M and H plates as opposed to the more
recent SSMR & SSRH plates"). Materials: Stainless Steel / Titanium ×
finishes Classic / Mirror Polish. NO aluminum, no copper/bronze
mixed-metal, no Windsor Pro. Handles: 90mm Atlas (classic) / 102mm
**Colossus** (semi-hollow, NEW Blackland-era design). SS Classic from
$129; Windsor Base Plate from $45; Windsor Collection bundle = all 3
plates + stand.
**[catalog] Validator rule:** a 2025+ "ATT Windsor M/R/H" string is the
NEW Windsor line (Blackland-era attribute), not the old M/H plates —
keep old (pre-2024) Windsor strings and new-era strings in the same
brand group with an era attribute, never as different products, but do
not treat a 2022 "SSMR" string and a 2026 "M" string as the same plate.
"Colossus" = the 102mm semi-hollow handle (Blackland-era ATT), never
an old handle name.
