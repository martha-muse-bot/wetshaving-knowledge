**The razor (B&B thread 648098, "Everyday Shaving Stinger", published 2024-01-06, updated 2024-03-08):** DE safety razor; magnets hold the blade cover in place; DOUBLE-SIDED, not plate-swapping — the solid-bar side has neutral blade exposure for everyday use, the scalloped side positive exposure and a larger gap for a closer shave (B&B firsthand #37: one side 0 exposure / 0.66 mm gap, the other 0.1524 mm exposure / 0.762 mm gap); two-piece, blade held by 4 corner posts + magnets; 40 g / 1.4 oz (very light); square/rectangular aluminum handle with hex/honeycomb design + bee engraving. Maker copy: CNC machined in the USA from lightweight aerospace aluminum, precision-ground alignment pins, lifetime warranty, 30-day returns, free razor stand. Maker-side product title is "Magnetic Safety Razor" — "Stinger" is the design name. **[[lineages/correction-2026-11-05|CORRECTION 2026-11-05]] (browser Wayback read):** this entry's earlier "interchangeable base plates" framing was wrong — the maker page describes a double-sided razor, no plate swapping. JoboozeRum's 2025-08-29 note ("interchangeable magnetic plates... scalloped and roller bar version") conflicts with maker copy; the "roller bar" variant is UNPINNED (possibly a second comb variant, possibly a misdescription) — do not cite interchangeable plates. Price: UNRESOLVED DISCREPANCY — the archived maker page (Feb 28, 2024) lists $34.99, sold out, while B&B reported $70 at launch → $95 by early 2024; the history page also embeds an "American Made Safety Razor" card at $64.95 with no matching product URL. Possible explanations (different configurations, e.g. the solid-copper Stinger; a clearance price) are unpinned — cite the price only with its source and date, never as a single figure. Ships from Bend, Oregon (USPS origin, B&B #30).
