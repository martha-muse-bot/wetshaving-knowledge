*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**Company arc (established, B&B wiki + Smithsonian):**
Durham Safety Razor Co., New York City, probably 1908; trading
as [[Durham Duplex]] Razor Co. with a Jersey City, N.J. factory by
1910. Early razors resembled open-comb shavettes ("transition
razors" between straights and safety razors); "hoe-type"/T-type
safety razors (Du Barry, 1922; Dorset; T-Type) followed, all
taking the same proprietary Durham blade — Du Barry handles
share Gillette three-piece threading but the blades are NOT
Gillette-compatible. Claimed 9 million users / 1/3 of the US
market in 1920. Succeeded to the Enders Razor Co. business
March 13, 1935; Mystic, Connecticut aircraft/torpedo-parts
factory 1937; HQ + razor manufacture moved to Mystic by 1941;
Durham-Enders Razor Corporation by 1947. Mailman brothers
bought Durham-Enders at auction 1957 ($208,000); sold to Edward
Weck & Co. November 1963 (25 employees, Mystic plant, closed
1979). UK subsidiary ceased male-grooming production 1970; a
successor Durham-Duplex Co. Ltd, Sheffield (formed c. 1970,
bought by Edward Turner and Son Ltd 1974, name shortened to
Durham-Duplex Ltd) still sells industrial blades/knives from
Sheffield — modern industrial Durham-Duplex blades are NOT
shaving products, and Sheffield-boxed blades with Personna
blades inside have been reported (B&B Typology thread), so a
"Durham Duplex" box alone proves nothing about its contents.
