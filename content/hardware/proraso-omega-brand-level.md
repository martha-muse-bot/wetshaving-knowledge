beyond the B&B threads the 10-24 run cited).** justshaver.com ("The
Proraso Professional is natural boar, made for Proraso by the
Italian brush house Omega", twice, Oct 2026); Gentleman's Gazette
("you can also purchase the Proraso shaving brush which is produced
by Omega"); shavemasters.com ("Crafted by Omega for Proraso"). Plus
B&B 490963: the 48/49/Proraso are "all the same brush, but at
different lofts" (49 5mm shorter than 48; Proraso 5mm shorter than
49) — Proraso Professional = 27mm knot (my-beard.com spec: 27mm
knot, 6.8cm handle, 12cm total), 57mm loft (B&B 609731 knot
