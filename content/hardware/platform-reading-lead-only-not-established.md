**Platform reading (lead only, NOT established):** the form is
most consistent with the generic Chinese-made three-piece
closed-comb DE platform of the Baili type — NOT an Edwin Jagger
DE89 or Merkur 34C (normally chrome, different handles) and
NOT a Weishi/Gillette butterfly (no TTO mechanism). A single
distant view cannot pin a specific Baili or rebranded model,
so do NOT cite Baili manufacture for Oberon, and do NOT carry
this reading over to the Dali (no Dali photo has been read).

under Handlebar Shaving Company; the photo alone does NOT
