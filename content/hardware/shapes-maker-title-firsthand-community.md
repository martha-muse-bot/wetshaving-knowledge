**Shapes (maker title + firsthand community):** G5C = **fan** (maker's own title says "G5C Fan"); G5A = **bulb** (B&B 647823 questioner + guide image); G5B = **bulb** (B&B 666592 firsthand: "My 24 G5C fan and 24 G5B bulb splayed out to the same diameter when set to the same loft"). So A vs B is NOT a shape difference — both bulbs; the difference is fiber/density/performance.
