**Nymph = the Souplex ladies' set (established, period ads).**
C&D Sept 28, 1935 ad: "SOUPLEX Ladies' Razor!" — a dainty
3" × 2" mottled-Bakelite case containing razor, brush, soap
and a "Souplex Nymph blade," retail 3/6. A June 27, 1936 ad
calls the Nymph "the most popular of ladies' shaving sets"
and says it had "been on the market only a few months" —
loose copy against the Sept 1935 ad, so date the Nymph to BY
Sept 1935, not to 1936. The same 1936 ad signs off
"Manufactured by Souplex Ltd., Morecambe — makers of Souplex
Razors and Blades" and offers "a list of Souplex Bakelite
products" — the Bakelite line was a range, not one razor.
Dataset: ischiapp's "Souplex Nymph Lady" (2023, noted "Green,"
8 g) is the same product family.
