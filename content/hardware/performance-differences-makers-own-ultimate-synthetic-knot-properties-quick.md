**Performance differences (maker's own "Ultimate Synthetic Knot Properties Quick Guide", live 2026-11-04; maker warns "use this as a guide, not a bible"):** G5A — Softness 90%, Backbone 90%, Splay 30%, Scrub 15%; G5B — Softness 85%, Backbone 95%, Splay 25%, Scrub 50%; G5C — Softness 95%, Backbone 80%, Splay 40%, Scrub 25%. Read: B is the backbone/scrub knot of the three (highest backbone, by far the most scrub, least splay, slightly less soft); C is the softest and splays most; A sits between on scrub/splay with high backbone. Community agrees on the direction: maker Andrew (via B&B 644568) says G5B "has a lot more backbone than either the G5A or G5C" with tips "not quite as soft, but still good"; painters report G5C splays best of A/B/C (B&B 612243 p.18); firsthand fiber measurement: G5C fibers ~0.1 mm vs Mühle STF 0.04–0.05 mm, and G5A fibers smaller than G5C's (B&B 638171 p.2 / 647823).
