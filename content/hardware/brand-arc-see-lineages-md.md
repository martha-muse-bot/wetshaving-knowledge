**Brand arc (see [[lineages|Lineages]]):** founded **2009 by Gregory Khan** (adjustablerazors.co.za; Classic Shaving spells "Greg Kahn" — record both spellings), living in Thailand at the time; production in Southeast Asia, finishing/assembly in Austin, Texas. First razor handmade on milling machine/lathe, stainless billet head, 316L handle (Classic Shaving, Mike Sandoval). **Defunct ~2018:** B&B "iKon Tech: where to buy in the US?" (2018-09-14): "they don't seem to be made anymore. Hoping they make a comeback."
