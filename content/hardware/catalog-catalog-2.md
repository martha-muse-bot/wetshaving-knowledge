**[[hardware/catalog-catalog|Catalog [catalog]]]: ~100 iKon clusters, ALL unverified/corroborated/attested, NO verified target.** Examples: `unverified/razor/ikon-b1` (corroborated), `ikon-b1-deluxe`, `ikon-b1-oc`, `ikon-b1-standard`, `ikon-b1-slant`, `ikon-tek` (attested), `ikon-101`, `ikon-102`, `ikon-x3-*`, `ikon-shavecraft-101/102`, `ikon-el-jefe`, `ikon-sbs-*`. The B1 Standard/Deluxe mapping above is the promotion key for the B1 family — validators should not need a separate model hunt for it.
