*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**Parker PTB/PTW push-type shavette = Lalwani Shaver — UPGRADED
2026-10-27 from bare speculation to a single-source factory
LEAD (not established; do NOT merge the brands).**
What the thread actually says, in order: (1) SABRE #4,144 floats
"speculation earlier in the thread that the Parker is a rebranded
Lalwani (or vice versa)"; (2) mjclark (SABRE p.128, owner voice)
calls his Lalwani "an amazingly packaged rebranded Parker PTB"
and describes the retention in detail — two metal tabs the blade
slips under + four small metal nubs holding it "ice cold tight"
— plus a decorated spine, good weight/grip/head geometry, and
box copy "Get ready for scorching, awesome and thrilling shave"
/ "History Begins Every Morning"; (3) ERS4 (SABRE #4,145,
~Jul 2023) supplies the mechanism that reframes (1) and (2):
"Parker's shavette is manufactured by an Indian factory 'Single
Sons', and in order to distribute to different regions, that
factory also established several brands with different names and
sold shavettes with the same appearance. Lalwani should be one of
them." His corroborating marker: his own variant is branded
"Taiwan" (Arabic script "Taiwan Shaver") for Islamic regions,
and "the same 'Taiwan' stickers and tags occasionally appear on
Parker products, which may be due to the confusion of the
production line."
The Parker side, for matching: PTW is the push-to-load (push-fit)
model — plastic scales, decorated spine, two tiny raised detents
at either end securing the blade (SABRE #4,143), aluminum head,
~28 g total with the head under 10 g (#4,159), wider tang than the
swingback style (#4,141). PTB is its sibling push-type variant
(a poster plans to "pick up a PTB" alongside his PTW, #4,154);
the PTB-vs-PTW distinction (handle color? material? — PTBMETAL
also exists) is UNPINNED, so do not fold PTB into PTW on the
letter code alone. Note the PTW/PTB push-type family is distinct
from Parker's clip-type SR family (SR1/SRW/SRB/SRX) and from the
adjustable PTABK and the kamisori-style Director.
[[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]: keep Parker PTB/PTW and Lalwani as
SEPARATE brand entries. Under ERS4's account "rebranded Parker"
is backwards — Parker is itself one factory brand, not the
origin — so shared manufacture, if firmed up, is a manufacture
ATTRIBUTE (P-04 pattern), never a merge. The "Single Sons"
factory name is itself single-source: it appears nowhere else in
web search (no company record, no second mention — possibly a
transliteration), so it stays a LEAD, not an established maker.
"Taiwan Shaver" is a third factory-brand label to watch for in
dataset strings. Re-open/establish only on: a Parker/Lalwani pack
or blade-arm marking naming the factory, import records, or a
second factory-brand identification.
Dataset scope (sotd_2016–2026_full.json, all fields): ZERO
"Lalwani" strings and ZERO "Parker PTB/PTW" razor strings.
Parker's dataset shavette presence is entirely clip-type
(SR1/SRW/SRB/SRX + generic "Parker Shavette", 238 hits) — so this
thread has near-zero current catalog impact; it is a lore
clarification for future PTB/PTW/Lalwani strings, not a queue
item. Sources: B&B 360385 p.128 (mjclark), p.208 #4,141–#4,159
(PTW owner reports + ERS4 #4,145/#4,147), dataset field scan
2026-10-27; negative checks: "Single Sons"/"Singla Sons" web
search (no corroboration), Lalwani retail search (no maker
listing surfaced).
