**Speed Razor vs Speed Shaver (lead, not an entry split):**
community usage distinguishes the 1930s "Speed Razor"
(square handle end) from the 1940s "Speed Shaver"; one
poster claims 1930s-model blades do not always fit 1940s
shavers, while two experienced users report using Enders
and Proline blades in both without issue (B&B 576746 #7–#8).
Treat as ONE family with model/era as attributes until a
head-geometry difference is documented; never split the
catalog entry on the Razor/Shaver wording alone.
