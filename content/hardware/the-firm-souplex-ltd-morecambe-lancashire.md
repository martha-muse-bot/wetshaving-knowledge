**The firm: Souplex Ltd, Morecambe, Lancashire — a Bakelite
toiletries maker, not just a razor maker (established).**
Period trade-press ads in The Chemist and Druggist (Internet
Archive scans, primary): Souplex safety razor blades advertised
Sept 19, 1925 ("standard pattern," 2s. 6d. per dozen, wholesale
only — "Souplex, Ltd., only supply wholesalers"); a Jan 9, 1926
ad repeats the blade at 2/6 per dozen with the puff claim that
sales "increased over 1,000 times in the past year." So the firm
is DOCUMENTED by Sept 1925 and already scaling — but no founding
year has surfaced; do NOT cite 1925 as the founding date.
The 1936 ads show the wider Bakelite line: a "Souplex
Toothbrush in a neat handy container of mottled bakelite, for
the traveller" and "Souplex the Shaving Senior" (razor, brush,
blades, soap in a compact Bakelite container whose "lid makes a
splendid shaving mug," C&D July 25, 1936). Validators meeting a
Souplex toothbrush or shaving set are meeting the same firm.
