**Antica Barbieria Colla (ABC) boar ≈ Zenith B03-A26 ("Zenith B2") —
STRENGTHENED LEAD, now multi-voice:** B&B 129553 ("I am 99% sure that
the ABC brush is manufactured by Zenith, the only difference is the
loft and the ABC brush fibers are slightly more bleached");
B&B 591103 ("A Zenith B2 boar brush to be exact. Most folks believe
this is the same brush as the Antica Barbieria Colla boar brush which
retails for $90. The Zenith runs about $30"); B&B 245241 ("Isn't the
ABC made by Zenith?"). Leisureguy's firsthand review (Aug 2023, Oct
2024) owns both and compares them without confirming the OEM link.
Community-corroborated, still lead-grade: no maker statement, no
side-by-side teardown. Decoder note: the "B2" = B03-A26 = aluminum
boar 26×57 — matches the ABC's aluminum handle. [catalog] ABC boar
stays its own entry; Zenith manufacture = lead-grade attribute (P-04),
never a merge; never fold Zenith B2 strings into ABC.
