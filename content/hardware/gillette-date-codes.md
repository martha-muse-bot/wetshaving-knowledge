*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] Gillette date codes — full letter→year table (RESOLVED
2026-10-24, backlog item).** US Gillette razors (and blades) carry
a letter + digit stamped under the guard / on the underside of the
base plate (between the Gillette logo and the handle on adjustables).
The letter is the YEAR, the digit is the calendar QUARTER
(1 = Jan–Mar, 2 = Apr–Jun, 3 = Jul–Sep, 4 = Oct–Dec): "O-1" =
1969 Q1, "D3" = 1958 Q3, "A1 Tech" = 1955 Q1. The alphabet skips
**Q** (25 letters), and the cycle repeats every 25 years. ONE
continuous cycle, three sources in full agreement (B&B wiki "US
Gillette Dating Information", vintagerazorshop.eu dating page,
Shave Dad date-code guide, updated ~Sep 2026):
- Blade-code era 1930–1949: blades only were coded (A = 1930,
B = 1931 … P = 1945, R = 1946, S = 1947, T = 1948, U = 1949);
razors of 1930–49 carry NO code ("NDC" years) and are dated by
model/features. A blade code dates the BLADES, not the razor
(blades may not be original) — validator caution.
- Razor-code era from 1950 Q3: coding begins with **V-3**
(B&B wiki note — V1/V2 razor stamps should not exist).
1950 V, 1951 W, 1952 X, 1953 Y, 1954 Z, 1955 A, 1956 B, 1957 C,
1958 D, 1959 E, 1960 F, 1961 G, 1962 H, 1963 I, 1964 J, 1965 K,
1966 L, 1967 M, 1968 N, 1969 O, 1970 P, 1971 R, 1972 S, 1973 T,
1974 U, 1975 V, 1976 W, 1977 X, 1978 Y, 1979 Z, 1980 A, 1981 B,
1982 C, 1983 D, 1984 E, 1985 F, 1986 G, 1987 H, 1988 I (one of
the last batches of USA-made DE razors).
- AMBIGUITY RULE: a bare letter repeats every 25 years, so the
MODEL narrows the cycle. V–Z are the classic double cases
(1950–54 vs 1975–79: a V4 is Q4 1950 or Q4 1975 — a ball-end
Super Speed points early, a Black Beauty points late, per
Shave Dad). A–I also repeat into 1980–88, but only for late
DE models (e.g. Super Adjustable) — most DE posters' A–U codes
are the 1955–74 turn.
- Q ANOMALY — RESOLVED 2026-10-26 as UNLIKELY (retired lead,
supersedes the earlier "treat a Q stamp as a 1971 anomaly
marker" guidance). The entire pro-Q evidence is two uncited
community notes: the B&B wiki's 1971 row ("Some razors
produced with Gillette code-Q", wiki last updated ~2017) and
mgbbrown's 2015 parenthetical ("there's examples of Super
Adjustable razors with a Q prefix", B&B 473605 #119) — no
photo, no quarter, no model beyond "Super Adjustable", in a
sentence whose main claim is that Q was skipped. Against it:
Shave Dad's current guide (updated ~Sep 2026) states flatly
"Gillette never used Q", that an apparent Q is "almost
certainly a worn O or G", and that a well-ranked chart lists
both Q and R as 1971 — that chart is the B&B wiki note itself,
i.e. the anomaly may be a chart error, not a factory batch;
vintagerazorshop.eu's table gives 1971 = R only;
razorlookup.com (2026-10-15) confirms the 25-letter alphabet;
Mr-Razor's blade evidence (via mgbbrown #117) shows Q already
skipped in 1946 (R1 blades), so the skip is systematic, not a
1971 slip; collector inventories show 1971 razors R-coded
(R4 Lady Gillette, R3 Super Adjustable short handle,
Scottydoint). Dataset check 2026-10-26: ZERO Q-letter code
strings across sotd_2016–2026 — every Qn hit is a
calendar-quarter gloss ("I2 - 1963 Q2", "D3 - Q3-1958") or a
joke, never a claimed stamp. No factory-error-batch story, no
attested quarter, no photographed Q stamp surfaced in
targeted searches. CATALOG GUIDANCE: never date a razor to
1971 on a Q reading alone; re-read a "Qn" stamp as a worn O
(1969) or G (1961) — R (1971) only if the model forces it —
and date by model/features. Do NOT create a Q-year entry.
RE-OPEN ONLY on a clear macro photo of a Q stamp on a
1971-plausible model. NEW LEAD spun off: WHY was Q skipped
at all — mgbbrown (2015) hoped someone would answer the
motivation; candidates (visual confusion with O? die-set
practice?) are all speculation until a Gillette-internal or
collector source says so.
- DATASET USAGE (verified 2026-10-24 across sotd_2016–2026):
posters cite codes exactly this way — "Y3 Tech" (259),
"H4 Black Beauty" (229), "E4 Fat Boy" (139), "K4 Slim" (139),
"Z1 Flare Tip" (90), "A1 Ball End Tech" (67) — so a
letter+digit token attached to a Gillette model is a DATE, not
a variant, and must never spawn a product entry. MATCHER
CAUTION: bare "Q1–Q4" in dataset strings (119 hits) is almost
always plain-English "quarter N" ("Super Adjustable Q2 1983",
"1966 Q3 Gillette Tech", "C4 --> Q4 1957" = a poster converting
code C4 to Q4 1957) — never read Qn as a date-code letter.
