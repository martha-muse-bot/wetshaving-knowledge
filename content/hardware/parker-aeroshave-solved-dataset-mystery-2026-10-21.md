**[catalog] Parker AeroShave — SOLVED dataset mystery (2026-10-21).**
The discuss queue's §13 "identity UNKNOWN" entry is a REAL product:
Parker Safety Razor launched the AeroShave in the first week of
January 2026 (per Sharpologist's Wet Shaving Talk podcast, 20 Jan
2026 episode). It is Parker's lightweight aluminum DE razor: CNC
machined from 6061 aluminum, 3-piece closed comb, mild geometry
(0.59 mm blade gap, +0.015 mm exposure), ~39–42 g, sandblasted matte
silver finish with a green-anodized safety bar plate, plastic washer
press-fitted in the handle to protect the baseplate. Sold in two
handle lengths — AeroShave-S / AeroShave Aluminum Safety Razor-S
(classic, 96 mm) and AeroShave-L / Long Length. Retail ~$65–$86.
