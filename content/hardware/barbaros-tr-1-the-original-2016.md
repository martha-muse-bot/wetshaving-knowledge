**[catalog] Barbaros TR-1 — the original (2016).** Turkish-made
machined stainless DE (316L per Sharpologist), launched via
Kickstarter (B&B 629705 #18: original backer). Sharpologist first
look June 2, 2016: $160 from barbarosrazors.com (until June 5,
2016). Handle = an AUTHORIZED version of Triad's Helix Twist
design (Barbaros asked Triad's permission; the Barbaros version
is polished smooth where Triad's is grippy/pointed). Cap uses
alignment RIBS (Wolfman/iKon-style, not pins); base plate solid
underneath (Above the Tie-like construction). Shave character:
efficient, more than mild but not wildly aggressive, less blade
feel than the Above the Tie R1; "about equivalent to a PL-602
with the handle loosened 1/4 turn." Maker = [[Hasan Kose]].
Confidence: established (Sharpologist + B&B firsthand).
