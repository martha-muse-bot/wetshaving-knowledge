**Speed Razor vs Speed Shaver (RESOLVED 2026-11-06; see
addendum):** community usage distinguishes the 1930s
"Speed Razor" (square handle end) from the 1940s "Speed
Shaver"; one poster claimed 1930s-model blades do not
always fit 1940s shavers, but that was a second-hand relay
(Ron R, B&B 576746 #6) against two multi-unit owners'
firsthand denials (#7–#8), and narrow blade beds are
per-unit tolerance variance cutting across both models
(#31–32). No head-geometry difference documented. ONE
family, model/era as attributes; never split the catalog
entry on the Razor/Shaver wording alone.
