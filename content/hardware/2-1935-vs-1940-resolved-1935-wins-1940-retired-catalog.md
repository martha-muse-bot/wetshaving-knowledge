**(2) 1935 vs 1940 — RESOLVED: 1935 wins, 1940 retired [catalog].**
The 6th Circuit opinion, *Enders Razor Co. v. Christy Co.*, 85 F.2d
195 (June 30, 1936), states verbatim: "On March 13, 1935, the
Durham-Duplex Razor Company succeeded to the business and rights of
Enders Razor Company, Inc., and was made a party in this court to
the appeal." That is a primary legal source, and it agrees with the
B&B wiki's footnoted timeline (1935 succession; 1937 Mystic factory;
HQ + razor manufacture moved to Mystic by 1941; "the two companies
became the Durham-Enders Razor Corporation by 1947"). The
streettotheleft 1940 date is a conflation of the 1935 succession with
the 1941 Mystic move and the 1947 corporate rename — and it wrongly
places an "Enders Razor Co. of Mystic Connecticut" in 1940 (Enders
was NYC-based until the succession; Mystic is Durham's move). Never
cite 1940 as the merger/succession date; the standing timeline in the
Enders entry above is confirmed, not merely preferred.
