**SLIM Edition = distinct base-plate geometry:** the .5 mm Slim plate is a slim-profile plate (maker dims 41.275 × 22.6 × 5.6 mm; SB plate 11.7 g in Ti; exposure 0.025 mm), marketed as easier under the nose/neck. It FITS the standard Timeless cap and handle ("they're the same" — maker), but the plate profile itself is different geometry. Sold in Titanium (TISLIM) and Stainless (SSSLIM), Solid Bar or Open Comb, fixed .5 gap.
