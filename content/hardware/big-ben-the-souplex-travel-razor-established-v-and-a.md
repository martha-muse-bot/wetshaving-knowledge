**Big Ben = the Souplex travel razor (established, V&A).**
V&A accession M.59-1984: "Small Bakelite case containing
aluminium and stainless steel travel razor; Souplex, Morecambe,
about 1935" — six-sided mottled-brown Bakelite case (8.1 × 3.5 ×
1.8 cm), Art Deco ring-and-bars lid, "BIG BEN" moulded inside
the lid and marked on the razor head, "Made in England"; V&A
production note: "Big Ben appears to have been a brand name
used for razors by Souplex." AMBIGUITY WARNING [catalog]: bare
"Big Ben" in the dataset's BLADE field is Lord's Big Ben Super
Stainless blade (Egypt) — never fold blade-field "Big Ben"
strings into the Souplex razor. The Souplex Big Ben has ZERO
razor-field dataset strings to date.
