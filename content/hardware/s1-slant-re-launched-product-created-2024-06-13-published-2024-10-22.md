2024-10-22)** — one year after the acquisition. Maker copy: "Since
2014... In 2024, a decade after its first launch, the S1 design got an
upgrade. We introduced a more efficient design, fully covered the blade
tabs, and elevated the hand feel with more lavish finishes." Materials:
Stainless Steel / Titanium × finishes Classic / Mirror Polish × plates
**Safety Bar AND Open Comb** (from $199; OC variants $210+ SS).
**[catalog] The S1 line now spans SB + OC.** The Blackland S1 Open
Comb occupies the OLD S2's market slot, but it is a NEW design under
the S1 name (covered blade tabs, reworked efficiency per the maker).
Validator rule: 2024+ "ATT S1 OC / S1 Open Comb" strings fold to the
S1 line with a Blackland-era attribute — NEVER fold them into the
pre-2024 S2 entry (different build lineage: old S2 = R2-OC-plate +
slant cap), and never read "S2" into a new S1-OC string. Handle on the
new S1 is a fixed Kronos-style with engraved lines (firsthand B&B
662650), not a handle option.
