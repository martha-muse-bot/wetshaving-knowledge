**Dataset scope (established, full scan sotd_2016–2026):**
The exact phrase "Enders Speed Shaver" appears 73 times,
72 by a single author (120inna55, 2019–2021 plus one 2024
post) and 1 by jrhunter25 — and 120inna55's posts are
notes-template posts ("Durham-Enders | Enders Speed
Shaver | Schick Proline B-20"), which is why structured
razor-field scans miss most of them — validators matching
notes templates must look here. His practice CONFIRMS
the modern workaround in the wild: every one of his posts
pairs the Speed Shaver with Schick Proline B-20, never
with a Durham blade. But the FAMILY is not single-author:
structured razor-field "Enders Speed razor" strings add
38 more posts across sapat1 (2020, Schick P20),
thebruhestmoments (2020–2021, Schick injector), Tetriside
(2023, Schick injector), jrhunter25 (2024, Chinese Schick
+ Proline B20, incl. "with Injector Adaptor"), and
esa1975 (2022, "Enders Speed with Last Shadow's injector
blade adapter"). The universal blade pairing is Injector
— with two oddities parked as leads (luvmy07subie 2021
pairs a Speed Razor with Gillette Permasharps / "1/2
Permasharp"; Jimtasticness 2020–2021 runs an "Enders'
Dollar Razor" / "The Enders single edge" with an "Enders'
razor blade"). [[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]: Speed Shaver

Injector, NOT [[Durham Duplex]]; the "Durham-Enders" brand
slot is the corporate-era name, not a blade-format claim.
