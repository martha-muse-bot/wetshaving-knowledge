**Speed launch refined (court + patent dates) [catalog].** The
Speed was filed Jan 1930 / granted Feb 1931, but the court says it
was only "started to put out" around July 1933, when Christy
stopped supplying Simmons/Enders ("until about July, 1933, when
these companies contracted to buy the articles from another
manufacturer. At that time also the Enders Company started to put
out a razor manufactured under a different patent, called the
'Enders Speed' razor"). Refine the entry above: Speed = patented
1931, on sale ~mid-1933 — not "developed around 1930" as a market
date. Inventor Thomas W. Bigoney (Philadelphia), assignor to
Enders Razor Co., Inc. — people-thread color. Pre-Speed model
timeline now runs: Improved Enders (~1928) → Dollar Razor ($1
price point, Feb 1931 Collier's ad) → Speed (~mid-1933). The
razor was still being sold in 1955 (Bonham Daily Favorite ad,
Jul 17, 1955 — jmudrick's "last days" ad), so the Speed family's
retail life runs ~1933–1955.
