**[[lineages/what-it-is|What it is]]:** iKon's Artist Club (AC) single-edge razor head. B&B 517908 "Can someone compare the RX and [[lineages/el-jefe|El Jefe]]?" (2017-04-26): head $20, "Very smooth and efficient. Slightly more aggressive than the General and Hawk"; "made of some sort of mystery metal, possibly zamak... cast rather than precisely machined"; "Ikon isn't planning to work on the corrected El Jefe head for another couple of months (if at all)... the corrected version will probably be $40." B&B 555279 (2018-11-23): "MR-SE (el jeffe v2ish)" — a v2 exists; head ~30 g; alignment fiddliness ("adjustable" is a joke about the blade play, B&B 502199 p.29: "It was tongue in cheek. Many refer to it as the 'adjustable' due to the alignment and play issues"). Blem heads sold ("El Jefe Blem"). Channel: iKon direct only ("only Ikon is selling it on their site") + eBay resales ($24). **NOT on the live store** (26 products, no El Jefe) — discontinued by the Jan 2023 rebuild.
