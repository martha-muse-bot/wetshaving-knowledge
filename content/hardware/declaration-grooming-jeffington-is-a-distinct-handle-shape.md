*Confidence: established · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] [[lines/declaration-grooming|Declaration Grooming]] "Jeffington" is a DISTINCT handle shape — a hybrid of Washington and Jefferson (established 2026-11-19, correcting the 2026-11-19 harvest error that carried it as a Jefferson alias).** Maker's own product copy, verbatim: "Jeffington is a hybrid between *The Washington* and *The Jefferson*. I don't have any witty copy to write about it, I just combined my favorite parts of both shapes into one shape." 8 distinct maker listings (Know It All, Arctic Sun, Battery, Cold Brushed Finish, Envy, Go Beyond B15, Van Gogh B15), B14/B15, 28mm knot, 48mm loft, $405 (Know It All B14); Wayback captures 2021-11-28 to 2025-10-09, earliest the Know It All page. Dataset: 152 brush rows / 13 authors name Jeffington. Catalog: verified entry `handle/declaration-grooming-jeffington` (created 2026-11-19); "Jeffington" and "Declaration Jeffington" removed from `handle/declaration-grooming-jefferson` the same day per Jim's correction 2026-11-18. The Sharpologist Dec 29 2025 episode names Jefferson/Washington/Theodore as the iconic shapes but does NOT name Jeffington — the maker pages are the evidence. Jeffo/Jeffos remain Jefferson owner shorthands.
