**Barbaros TR-4 — LEAD, single community source.** One B&B
post (647878 #12) claims a TR-4 with "a massive 1.4mm gap and
neutral exposure," all stainless, ~$200. No second source, no
dataset strings, no retailer listing found this run. Do NOT
catalog a TR-4 until corroborated (a second forum/review
mention or a listing). If it firms up, note the pattern: TR-4
would break the TR-2/TR-3 plate logic (a new model number
rather than a plate), so its comb type and relationship to
TR-2 need pinning too.
