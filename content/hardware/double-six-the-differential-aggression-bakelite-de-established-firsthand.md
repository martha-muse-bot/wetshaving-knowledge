**Double Six = the differential-aggression Bakelite DE
(established, firsthand collector sources).** B&B Souplex
Owners Club thread 636564 p.6 #106 (firsthand, with photos):
the Double Six (Bakelite cap) has an asymmetrical baseplate —
one side a short square guard, the other a wider guard bar
curving down more — plus an asymmetrical cap; cap pins and
baseplate holes are cone-shaped and handed, so the cap only
sits flush one way. Shaving effect (same thread + thread
383550): curved-guard side = aggressive/very aggressive,
square-guard side = mild/medium — the "trademark differential
aggressiveness." The **Double Six Minor** shares the exact
baseplate but with drilled cylindrical holes and a symmetrical
metal cap, giving only a "minor" differential effect
(#106); a Minor Mk1 is collector-dated to the late 1940s
(#145, single firsthand dating — treat the decade as lead).
A 1944-dated Double Six in a Red Cross POW parcel (thread
383550 #38, single firsthand) proves pre-1945 circulation;
the magnetised-blade-as-escape-compass anecdote on the same
post is colour, not catalog data.
