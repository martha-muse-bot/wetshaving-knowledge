**Bronze is the SAME head system, NOT its own plate system:** the maker states on the TRBR38 page "Our Bronze razor parts are interchangeable with both our Stainless Steel and Titanium razor pieces." Bronze (C954) is sold only as fixed razors — TRBR38 (.38 Solid Bar, exposure .05 mm) and TRBR78 (.78 Open Comb) — and "for our Bronze Razors, we do not make custom parts." Difference = material + gap menu only.
