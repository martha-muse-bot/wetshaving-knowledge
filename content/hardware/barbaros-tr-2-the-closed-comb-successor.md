*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] Barbaros TR-2 — the closed-comb successor.**
Replaces the TR-1's alignment ribs with PINS (Sharpologist B&R
article: "Hasan is working on updating it to a new model named
TR-2 with pins instead of alignment ribs"). Gap 1.00mm, neutral
exposure (B&B 629705 #2/#3, firsthand). Character: smooth and
efficient, "like a non-adjustable Taiga" (~Taiga setting 2.5–3);
polishing "on par with Wolfman" (B&B #7). Sold in stainless AND
titanium (B&B 628010 #40: "either in SS or Ti"). One B&B
firsthand source (#18) says the TR-2/3 geometry is the same as
the TR-1's — treat the TR-1→TR-2 change as alignment mechanism
(+ materials/finishes), not a new head geometry, pending a
second source. Release cadence: small batches every 2–3 years
(B&B #11). Confidence: established (gap/exposure/pins/SS+Ti);
geometry-continuity = lead (single source).
