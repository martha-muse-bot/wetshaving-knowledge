**Standard head system (one geometry):** Stainless Steel "Build Your Own" (SSBYOR) and Titanium (TIBYOR) are the same head: caps smooth/scalloped; base plates Solid Bar / Open Comb / Scalloped / Dual Comb; gaps 0.68 (exposure zero) and 0.95 (exposure .05 mm) — identical spec language on both maker pages. SS and Ti parts are freely mixed by owners (B&B).
