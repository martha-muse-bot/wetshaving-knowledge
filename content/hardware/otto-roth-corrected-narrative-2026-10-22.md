**Otto Roth — corrected narrative (2026-10-22).** The dataset claim
(Dogcowsaysmoof 2017: "Otto Roth in New Jersey designed the original
Gillette long comb; Gillette bought them out when they became a
threat") is BACKWARDS. Per B&B collector research (Kevin, "Otto Roth"
thread): Gillette bought a controlling interest in the German
**Roth-Büchner** company (Rotbart) in **1926** and purchased its Otto
Roth division in **1927** — the "serious competition" was Rotbart in
Germany, not the NJ company. The US "OTTO ROTH, INC." NEW long-comb
razors (mid-1930s) were **Gillette-made** NEW designs sold under the
Otto Roth brand (two variants: Otto Roth stamped vs. overstruck with
diamonds + Gillette logo). Roth/Büchner "had no razor related
patents... only produced blades before the merge" (collector
romsitsa). Separately, Otto Roth Inc. of Newark NJ was a blade maker
(Radium blades, ca. 1926–1938 — ORAU health-physics museum).
Established (B&B collector thread + ORAU). The "threat → buyout"
framing is narrative gloss, not sourced — do not repeat it.
