**B1 OSS vs OG1 — same design, different lines (guard):** both carry the identical maker "dual function head" copy, but OSS = stainless B1-coated ($89.99 razor / $79.99 head, "Our best-selling iKon Razor") and OG1 = aluminum Shave Craft ($44.99 / $35). Never fold OG1 strings into the B1 OSS entry or vice versa; material/line is the discriminator. The OSS dual head dates to 2012+ (B&B 360562 ranking uses "Ikon OSS (SB)" and "Ikon OSS (OC)" sides).
