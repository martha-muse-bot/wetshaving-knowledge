**"Magnets. Seriously?" blog post (captured 2024-04-13 05:27 UTC) READ:** four alignment pins position the blade; TWO N52 magnets hold the blade cover (2.8 lbs holding force); the handle is permanently affixed to the blade guard; when a larger magnet was tested users needed a tool to pry the top off; blind tests (epoxy-held vs magnet-held razors) — the testing panel could not determine a difference. Maker rationale for the magnet choice, in their words: "Certified rare earth magnets are expensive and difficult to work with. We chose the best solution, not the easiest or the cheapest."
