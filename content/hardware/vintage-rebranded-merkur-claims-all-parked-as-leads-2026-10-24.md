*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**Vintage "rebranded Merkur" claims — all parked as leads
(2026-10-24).** Adams razor (Lob-Star 2025, "says Adams on the head but
has the Mercury logo on the end of the handle") and Pomco Chicago
mid-century razors (Tonality 2020) each have exactly one dataset source.
This is the classic mid-century US barbershop-supply private-label
pattern (cf. Baxter); without a second source, treat each as lead, and
per P-04 keep them as own-brand entries with Solingen/Merkur as a
manufacture attribute IF ever corroborated — never fold.
- **Adams addendum (2026-10-25) — dataset scope pinned + nmrs
34C mapping CHALLENGED [catalog].** Field-specific scan of
sotd_2016–2026: exactly ONE razor-field string, "Adams/Merkur
Open Comb" (Lob-Star, thread 1ph5x6p, 2025-12-08; note verbatim:
"First go with this new-to-me razor. It's really mild but enjoyed
the outcome. Is it a rebranded Merkur? It says Adams on the head
but has the Mercury logo on the end of the handle." + imgur album
m1OLywj). No independent "Adams" DE razor source exists anywhere
in web search: the iknifecollector German-makers list for Emil
Hermes (Merkur-Werk) names Artemia/Diana/Fossa/Hermes/Hermes-*
/Imperiola/Longlife/Merkur/Polarstern/Populah — no Adams; and
Charles Adams & Co. (Rochester NY, 1889–1957) is a straight-razor-era
firm, do NOT conflate with this DE razor. The Mercury-head mark on
a handle end IS Merkur's standard handle-end logo (shopmerkur.ca
history: "The bottom of the razor handle has the symbol of the Roman
god Mercury") — so Lob-Star's handle observation is consistent with
a Merkur handle, but proves nothing about the head; a parts razor
(Merkur handle + Adams-stamped head) stays live. CATALOG CAUTION:
the catalog entry `unverified/razor/adams-merkur-open-comb` sits at
corroborated with product "34C" on the SOLE basis of nmrs
correct_matches mapping "adams/merkur open comb" → Merkur 34C — and
that mapping is internally inconsistent: the 34C is a CLOSED-comb
(safety-bar) razor, while nmrs itself routes open-comb strings to
open-comb models ("merkur 25c open comb" → 25C, "merkur 41c open
comb" → 41C). The Adams→34C mapping reads as a curator's
"commonest Merkur" guess, not an identification. Validators: do NOT
cite 34C for this string; treat the corroborated tier here as
UNRESOLVED pending the photo read. If the head is truly open comb,
the candidates are Merkur open-comb heads (11C/15C/25C/41C family)
or a Merkur-made private-label open comb — never 34C. Decisive next
step: read Lob-Star's imgur album m1OLywj (photo read dispatched
2026-10-25 via browser recovery; text fetch blocked on imgur.com) —
head profile + stamp legibility settle comb type and whether the
"Adams" stamp is real.
- **PHOTO READ RESULT (2026-10-25, same run — nmrs 34C mapping
DISPROVEN, Adams confirmed real) [catalog].** The imgur album
(m1OLywj, dated 2025-12-08) shows three photos of a silver/chrome
vintage DE razor: (1) the head's underside/base plate, rectangular
with a central screw post and two studs, is unambiguously OPEN COMB
— a row of spaced teeth along both long edges; the plate's lower
edge is engraved "ADAMS GERMANY" in capitals; (2) the round handle
end cap carries a raised winged Mercury-style head in profile
(winged head facing right, small circular medallion at the temple);
(3) full-length view: long vertically ribbed/fluted handle, smooth
narrower neck, small round bottom cap, no legible shaft lettering.
CONSEQUENCES: (a) the nmrs Adams→34C mapping is now DISPROVEN, not
merely suspect — the 34C is closed-comb and this head is open comb,
so the catalog entry's corroborated tier does NOT resolve this
string's identity; validators should re-examine the entry (its
product field "34C" is wrong for this string). (b) "Adams" is a REAL
German open-comb razor mark — "ADAMS GERMANY" engraved on the plate
— not a poster invention or misreading. (c) Merkur manufacture of
the HEAD is still NOT established: the Mercury-head cap is
consistent with a Merkur handle (or Merkur-made handle), but the
head itself is Adams-stamped; parts razor vs Merkur-made private
label remains open. Do NOT record "Merkur-made" for the head on this
evidence. [[Catalog guidance]]: Adams stands as its own razor entry
(German open comb, "ADAMS GERMANY" plate mark); the Lob-Star string
belongs to it, not to Merkur 34C; the Mercury-cap handle is a
handle attribute to note, not a maker attribution for the head.
- **Pomco addendum (2026-10-28) — RESOLVED: Merkur-made,
ESTABLISHED as a manufacture attribute [catalog].** The 2026-10-24
parking ("exactly one dataset source") is superseded: the claim now
rests on FOUR independent community sources plus parts
interchangeability, meeting the same bar as the Rockwell R1 = Baili
verdict. (1) Tonality's 2020 dataset note, flat and detailed: "The
Pomco brand was a Chicago based brand that sold rebranded Merkur
razors from Germany in the middle of the 20th century. I'm sure a
number of you have seen the silver plated regular Pomco slant or
even the original Merkur branded one." (2) B&B 582368 #5, explicit:
"The Hoffritz, Cole, and Pomco razors are all re-badged Merkurs, so
if you try one and it doesn't work for you, chances are the others
won't either." (3) B&B B.O.S.S. thread p.22: "Merkur's slant of the
1950's and 1960's used by Hoffritz, Coles, Pomco and others" — which
also brackets the platform era. (4) B&B 2008, earliest: "an old
Pomco Diagonal (Murkur slant)" [sic]. Supporting, not decisive:
TheManWhoDoneIt (2017) ran a POMCO top cap on his Apollo slant head
and then a Merkur 37C top cap on the same head ("this 37c top cap
fits perfectly... slimmer profile than the POMCO top cap I was
using") — cap interchange across the Merkur slant family. Mechanism
plausibility: Merkur patented the slant in 1916 and sold under
numerous trade names (B&B 261553 history thread). Dataset scope
(sotd_*_full.json, all years): exactly 4 razor-field strings, 3
authors — "POMCO Slant" + "POMCO slant" (TheManWhoDoneIt, 2017),
"vintage Pomco slant" (LathaLife, 2019), "1946 Lady Pomco SLant"
(Tonality, 2020) — plus a 2021 rChewbacca note ("way over paid for
a lady pomco at an estate sale"). Catalog guidance per P-04: Pomco
stays its OWN brand entry (the existing `unverified/razor/pomco-slant`
cluster); record Merkur (Solingen) as a manufacture ATTRIBUTE; do
NOT fold Pomco strings into Merkur 37C/39C — the vintage slant head
is its own 1950s/60s-era platform, and the modern 37C is a later
iteration, not the same product. QUALIFIERS: (a) "Chicago based"
rests on Tonality alone — keep the Chicago base as a single-source
lead, not established; the company behind the Pomco name is
unpinned. (b) Tonality's "1946" is poster dating for the Lady
Pomco, never a production date. (c) The Lady Pomco is a
slant-LIKE women's variant, not the regular slant: per Tonality's
note it has two different sides, "one straight [side], and a
second that has a frown shaped curve," with a slim hourglass
handle — treat as a Pomco model/variant, never a separate brand;
the linked imgur album (hCbwo42) has NOT been photo-read, so the
two-sided description is note-sourced only. (d) "Pomco Diagonal"
(2008) is an alternate model name for the regular slant until a
second source says otherwise — do not create a separate Diagonal
entry on one 2008 mention.
- **Hoffritz + Coles slants addendum (2026-10-28) — RESOLVED:
Merkur-made, ESTABLISHED as a manufacture attribute for BOTH
[catalog].** This closes backlog lead (3) from the Pomco run
("Hoffritz + Cole slants need the same treatment check").
The two names were already named together in the Pomco
sources — B&B 582368 #5 ("The Hoffritz, Cole, and Pomco razors
are all re-badged Merkurs") and B&B B.O.S.S. p.22 ("Merkur's
slant of the 1950's and 1960's used by Hoffritz, Coles, Pomco
and others") — and this run adds independent corroboration
for each name separately, so neither rides on Pomco's verdict:
HOFFRITZ — (1) B&B "On the Slant" #29, flat: "The Hoffritz is
made by Merkur"; (2) B&B corrosion thread 207495 #36 (2011):
"a vintage one such as a Hoffritz, Coles, or Pomoco. All of
them are re-branded Merkur"; (3) B&B "new slant" #19: "Merkur
made slants for other companies... You'll find Hoffritz slants
which are basically slightly older style Merkur slants";
(4) Leisureguy 2020-10-12: Hoffritz "chose [the Merkur 37] as
the razor to carry the Hoffritz name... they're excellent, and
they are basically Merkur 37s"; (5) firsthand owner LRod
(B&B 628836 #52), Hoffritz vs new 37C side-by-side: "almost
identical", Hoffritz "built to a higher standard by Merkur
for Hoffritz" in the 1960s, with small differences (Hoffritz
slightly more gap/aggression, no lather channels on the cap,
perfect blade alignment vs slight play on the 37C);
(6) B&B 583111 #36, relaying Dovo Stahlwaren: the vintage
slant is a "Merkur 37" (not 37C) made before the ~1970 tooling
change that brought in scalloped caps — "grandfather" of the
modern 37C; (7) dataset note djundjila 2023 (Lather Games):
"Hoffritz are rebranded Merkurs", model produced since the
1960s until the late 1970s/early 1980s scalloped-cap switch.
COLES — thinner, but the same pattern: the two B&B sources
above name Coles alongside Hoffritz/Pomco, Haiku (quoted in
B&B 207495 p.3) says "many were made by Merkur such as the
Coles and the Hoffritz", and the dataset posters say it
themselves: geneaut "Coles Slant (1970s rebadged Merkur 37)"
and "Merkur - 37C (Coles)", hairykopite "Coles/Merkur –
Slant DE". No maker document was found for either name —
the verdict rests on the community-source bar used for
Pomco/Rockwell R1, not on a Merkur admission.
Dataset scope (sotd_*_full.json, all years): Hoffritz 107
razor-field posts / 18 authors, slant-dominated ("Hoffritz
Slant" 64, "Hoffritz Travel Slant" 5+ variants, "Vintage
Hoffritz Slant" 4) — but Hoffritz is a RETAILER brand, not a
slant-only brand: the same scope holds "Hoffritz of New York
OC" (7 posts: OC/DE OC), "Hoffritz 5/8" straights (3),
"Hoffritz Finest" (1), and a Frankenrazor handle post.
Coles: exactly 5 posts / 4 authors, all slant ("Coles slant
w/Feather", "Coles/Merkur – Slant DE", "Vintage Coles
Slant", geneaut ×2). Catalog guidance per P-04: Hoffritz
and Coles slant entries stand as their OWN brand entries
(catalog already holds unverified hoffritz-slant and
coles-slant clusters); record Merkur (Solingen) as a
manufacture ATTRIBUTE on the slant entries only; do NOT fold
slant strings into Merkur 37/37C — the vintage head differs
from the modern 37C (gap, cap channels, alignment) and the
vintage name is "37", not "37C". Spelling: dataset and B&B
both use "Coles" (also "Cole" in 582368 #5 and "Coles NY"
in the catalog) — normalize Cole/Coles to one Coles slant
cluster; the company behind Coles (a New York retailer?)
is unpinned. QUALIFIERS / DO NOT EXTEND: (a) the Merkur
attribution covers SLANT strings only — the Hoffritz OC
strings get no Merkur attribution on this evidence (a
Merkur open comb exists, but that is not evidence for
these); (b) Hoffritz straights (5/8, Finest) are a separate
retailer-brand question, never slant evidence; (c) era
marker (community-sourced, hedged): smooth cap = pre-~1970
vintage 37, scalloped cap = later, toward the modern 37C;
do not date a Hoffritz slant by cap alone without a photo.
