*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**Vintage "rebranded Merkur" claims — all parked as leads
(2026-10-24).** Adams razor (Lob-Star 2025, "says Adams on the head but
has the Mercury logo on the end of the handle") and Pomco Chicago
mid-century razors (Tonality 2020) each have exactly one dataset source.
This is the classic mid-century US barbershop-supply private-label
pattern (cf. Baxter); without a second source, treat each as lead, and
per P-04 keep them as own-brand entries with Solingen/Merkur as a
manufacture attribute IF ever corroborated — never fold.
- **Adams addendum (2026-10-25) — dataset scope pinned + nmrs
34C mapping CHALLENGED [catalog].** Field-specific scan of
sotd_2016–2026: exactly ONE razor-field string, "Adams/Merkur
Open Comb" (Lob-Star, thread 1ph5x6p, 2025-12-08; note verbatim:
"First go with this new-to-me razor. It's really mild but enjoyed
the outcome. Is it a rebranded Merkur? It says Adams on the head
but has the Mercury logo on the end of the handle." + imgur album
m1OLywj). No independent "Adams" DE razor source exists anywhere
in web search: the iknifecollector German-makers list for Emil
Hermes (Merkur-Werk) names Artemia/Diana/Fossa/Hermes/Hermes-*
/Imperiola/Longlife/Merkur/Polarstern/Populah — no Adams; and
Charles Adams & Co. (Rochester NY, 1889–1957) is a straight-razor-era
firm, do NOT conflate with this DE razor. The Mercury-head mark on
a handle end IS Merkur's standard handle-end logo (shopmerkur.ca
history: "The bottom of the razor handle has the symbol of the Roman
god Mercury") — so Lob-Star's handle observation is consistent with
a Merkur handle, but proves nothing about the head; a parts razor
(Merkur handle + Adams-stamped head) stays live. CATALOG CAUTION:
the catalog entry `unverified/razor/adams-merkur-open-comb` sits at
corroborated with product "34C" on the SOLE basis of nmrs
correct_matches mapping "adams/merkur open comb" → Merkur 34C — and
that mapping is internally inconsistent: the 34C is a CLOSED-comb
(safety-bar) razor, while nmrs itself routes open-comb strings to
open-comb models ("merkur 25c open comb" → 25C, "merkur 41c open
comb" → 41C). The Adams→34C mapping reads as a curator's
"commonest Merkur" guess, not an identification. Validators: do NOT
cite 34C for this string; treat the corroborated tier here as
UNRESOLVED pending the photo read. If the head is truly open comb,
the candidates are Merkur open-comb heads (11C/15C/25C/41C family)
or a Merkur-made private-label open comb — never 34C. Decisive next
step: read Lob-Star's imgur album m1OLywj (photo read dispatched
2026-10-25 via browser recovery; text fetch blocked on imgur.com) —
head profile + stamp legibility settle comb type and whether the
"Adams" stamp is real.
- **PHOTO READ RESULT (2026-10-25, same run — nmrs 34C mapping
DISPROVEN, Adams confirmed real) [catalog].** The imgur album
(m1OLywj, dated 2025-12-08) shows three photos of a silver/chrome
vintage DE razor: (1) the head's underside/base plate, rectangular
with a central screw post and two studs, is unambiguously OPEN COMB
— a row of spaced teeth along both long edges; the plate's lower
edge is engraved "ADAMS GERMANY" in capitals; (2) the round handle
end cap carries a raised winged Mercury-style head in profile
(winged head facing right, small circular medallion at the temple);
(3) full-length view: long vertically ribbed/fluted handle, smooth
narrower neck, small round bottom cap, no legible shaft lettering.
CONSEQUENCES: (a) the nmrs Adams→34C mapping is now DISPROVEN, not
merely suspect — the 34C is closed-comb and this head is open comb,
so the catalog entry's corroborated tier does NOT resolve this
string's identity; validators should re-examine the entry (its
product field "34C" is wrong for this string). (b) "Adams" is a REAL
German open-comb razor mark — "ADAMS GERMANY" engraved on the plate
— not a poster invention or misreading. (c) Merkur manufacture of
the HEAD is still NOT established: the Mercury-head cap is
consistent with a Merkur handle (or Merkur-made handle), but the
head itself is Adams-stamped; parts razor vs Merkur-made private
label remains open. Do NOT record "Merkur-made" for the head on this
evidence. [[Catalog guidance]]: Adams stands as its own razor entry
(German open comb, "ADAMS GERMANY" plate mark); the Lob-Star string
belongs to it, not to Merkur 34C; the Mercury-cap handle is a
handle attribute to note, not a maker attribution for the head.
