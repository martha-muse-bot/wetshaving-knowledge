**Products:** **Game Changer** (synthetic knot, 24–25mm — B&B pass-around measured 25mm/54mm), **Sunrise Synth** (synthetic, 22–24mm), **Big Cypress** (badger/silvertip knot, 26mm; at least one 24mm "#1" variant exists — myersb68 2017-11-22 "24mm Big Cypress (#1)"). Handles: **Blue Sparkle** handle (26mm Big Cypress, ashbeowulf_returns 2025-07-31) and **Miami Nites** (26mm in drivenlegend customs 2016; handle-vs-knot unresolved — LEAD). AMACK sold knots both set in handles and loose for restorations (Rubberset, Ever Ready 100/150/100A, Nathan Clark, ER 750 restorations in the dataset).
