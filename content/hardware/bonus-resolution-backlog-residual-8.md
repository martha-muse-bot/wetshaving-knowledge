**Bonus resolution, backlog residual (8): luvmy07subie's
Speed Razor + 1/2 Permasharp posts are a DOCUMENTED MOD,
not a mis-tag [catalog].** Two posts, both luvmy07subie:

and 2021-09-15 "1930'S Enders Speed Razor" + "1/2

half-DE mod. Catalog: a Speed post with a DE blade field
is this mod, never evidence the Speed takes DE blades
natively — and never fold these posts into a DE-blade
entry for the razor.
