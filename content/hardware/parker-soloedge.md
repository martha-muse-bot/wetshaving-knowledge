**[catalog] Parker SoloEdge — 2024 half-DE launch thread (RESOLVED
2026-10-30, backlog item).** Parker Safety Razor launched the
SoloEdge in **February 2024** — its FIRST safety razor for half-DE
blades; Parker's earlier half-DE models were all barber-straight
SHAVETTES (SR1/SRW/SRB/SRX clip family, PTB/PTW push family), so this
is a new product line for them, not a shavette variant. Sharpologist
review (pre-launch sample, timed to launch, published 2024-02-09;
updated 2024-02-19) + Amazon "Date First Available Feb. 1 2024" +
B&B thread "Parker SoloEdge in stock" (649286, started Feb 2024).
Design: TWO-PIECE (head only comes off), 94 g / 9.8 cm, solid brass
handle with electroplated satin-chrome finish, straight knurling;
head molded from a metallic alloy (zamak), chrome plated. The half
blade sits FLAT (no curvature) in slots with built-in alignment
notches; exposure is automatic — no adjustment; the head angle is
deliberately cartridge-like. A small loading magnet was
DISCUSSION-only at pre-launch (never confirm a magnet in the shipped
head without a disassembly source). Ships with **100 Parker Premium
Platinum pre-cut half blades** (~6–12 months' supply per maker copy).
BLADE CAUTION: Sharpologist firsthand established the pre-cut half
blades are a DIFFERENT, noticeably more aggressive edge spec than
Parker DE blades — the pre-cut half blade is its own blade product;
validators must never fold half-blade strings into the Parker Premium
Platinum DE entry. Reception: B&B owners gave it an "early vote for
razor bargain of the year for '24" — two-pass BBS, full end-to-end
blade support with corner alignment stops (owners explicitly favor
it over the Leaf Twig, 2.5 oz, which launched 2021 after a
crowdfund, as better engineered); some owners found it more
aggressive than expected (~4–4.5/10 shave, vs Leaf Thorn milder).
wyze0ne's own note (2024-04-23): "mild, but effective... the blade
just sits in the head and is not bent in an arc." Launch channel:
initially Super Safety Razor only (Howie's US Parker retail outlet;
Parker is co-owned by Taconic Shave, Yorktown Heights NY — see
[[Lineages]]). Retailers: Razor Company page live Oct 2026.
Half-DE bracket for context: Leaf Twig (2021, crowdfund) / Leaf
Thorn / Supply Co are the other mainstream half-DE razors. Dataset:
13 strings (wyze0ne ×7 + jrhunter25 ×1 in 2024; Ok-Yak-3950 ×3 in
2025; RAZR-540 ×1 in 2026) — still in circulation; spellings "Solo
Edge" (spaced) and "Soloedge" are poster styling only.
