*Confidence: unknown · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**[[lineages/method-note|Method note]]:** a pre-announcement ("getting ready to launch") is not a launch — Sharpologist's Sept 2018 post is the ONLY source for the Holy Grail campaign, and it explicitly says "I'll be sure to pass along more information when the campaign starts" (no follow-up ever came). When a product exists only in a pre-announcement, the absence of any launch/follow-up coverage after 8 years is itself the verdict — record it as never-shipped, not as "unconfirmed."
