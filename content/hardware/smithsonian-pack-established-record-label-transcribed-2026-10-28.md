**Smithsonian pack (established record, label transcribed
2026-10-28):** NMAH holds a cataloged Durham-Duplex blade
pack — ID number / catalog number 2012.0165.416, record
nmah_1449107, maker [[Durham Duplex]] Razor Company, place made
Mystic, Connecticut, measurements 1 in x 2 1/2 in x 1/4 in,
currently not on view, with FOUR object images (ids.si.edu
NMAH-AHB2013q075741–744, viewed at full 3000×2250 IIIF size
plus enlarged crops). Legible text, and ONLY this text:
front (image 0, red pack, cream border, gold star) — "DURHAM"
/ "DUPLEX" in gold, plus a crest scroll reading "CHACUN EST"
/ "SON PROPRE" / "BARBIER" (French: each is his own barber);
reverse (image 1) — "DURHAM-ENDERS RAZOR CORP." /
"MYSTIC, CONN." / "MADE IN U.S.A." in gold. The two wrapped
views (images 2–3) yield NO legible text (faint pink
show-through under the wrapping, unreadable even cropped).
NO blade count and NO patent wording is legible in any of the
four images; small shield lettering/monogram and faint marks
above "DURHAM" are likewise illegible — do not guess at them.
The white "2012.0165.416" label visible below the object in
full frames is a museum photograph label, NOT pack text.
DATING NOTE [catalog]: the reverse names the Durham-Enders
Razor Corp., so this is a LATE-era pack (post-1947 merger),
not an early Durham Duplex pack — validators must not cite
this pack's wording for the pre-1947 era, and must not cite
it for a blade count or patent (neither is legible). The
backlog's "readable vintage pack label photo" want is now
satisfied as far as this record goes.
