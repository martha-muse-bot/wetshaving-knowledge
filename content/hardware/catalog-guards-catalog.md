**Catalog guards [catalog]:** (1) "Stinger" alone is ambiguous — the CG Stinger HANDLE (2018–2023 strings, e.g. "CG lvl2 Stinger") predates the Everyday razor by six years; only "Everyday (Shaving/Razors) Stinger" / [[glossary/magnet-razor|"Magnet Razor"]] strings fold to the Everyday entry. (2) Bare "Everyday Razor" is ambiguous too — CG's "Everyday" baseplate-level razor ("Charcoal Goods - Everyday Razor" ×14, 2020+) vs the Everyday Shaving razor; brand signal decides. (3) False friends: straight razors whose steel is called "Magnetic" — Genco Magnetic Steel, Yankee Cutlery Magnetized #910, Böker Damascus Magnetic Steel, Griffon "Carbo-Magnetic 60", Lion Razor Works "The Magnetic Razor", Licht Werk Magnetic Blue Steel — are steel-grade descriptors, never the Stinger. (4) NOT the Mag T Delta — the backlog's earlier note is confirmed: the dataset's magnet razor is the Stinger, and no Mag T Delta strings exist in the corpus.
