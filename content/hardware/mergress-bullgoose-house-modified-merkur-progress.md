**[catalog] Mergress = BullGoose-house modified Merkur Progress.**
BullGoose-exclusive adjustable ("Bling" finishes, e.g. "Mergress
'Bling'" in 2017 SOTD/wegianwetshaving strings); listed among
BullGoose's exclusive razor brands (accio profile). UPDATE
2026-10-23 — the MOD MAKER is identified: the "Mergress" is named
for **"Mer,"** the forum username of the man who invented the
modification (Mer + Progress = Mergress); Sharpologist's "Merkur
Progress Modifications": "called a 'Mergress' after the forum
username of the guy who thought it up (Mer). However he's apparently
been inactive for some time." The mod = metal (chrome) replacement
for the Progress's plastic adjustment knob, with settings
recalibrated for consistency (scale starting at 0 instead of 1/1.5).
Community recollection gives his first name as "Eric" (B&B threads
515685, 509 — hedged single-source each, LEAD: verify before
recording in [[People]]). BullGoose sold the mod as a house-exclusive
line, including the Mergress XL. Catalog: brand of record stays
Asylum/BullGoose (P-04); "Mer" is the modification's originator, an
attribution note, not an identity merge. With the shop
winding down, the Mergress has NO known alternate retail channel —
the mod dies with BullGoose unless its maker resurfaces (lead: who
did the Mergress modification work for BullGoose?). Dataset strings
reading "Mergress" are the BullGoose product. Sources:
wegianwetshaving.com 2017-06; accio BullGoose profile.
Confidence: established (house-exclusive status).
