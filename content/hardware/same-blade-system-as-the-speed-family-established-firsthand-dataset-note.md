**Same blade system as the Speed family (established,
firsthand dataset note).** The dataset's ONLY structured
Dollar string is Jimtasticness, 2020-06-29 thread
(notes header June 28, 2020): razor "Enders' Dollar
Razor", blade "Enders' razor blade". His note (same post)
is decisive: the razor came from u/fuckchalzone, who told
him "I could use an injector blade, just needed to mod it
a bit" — the same injector-workaround as the Speed family
— but it "came with several NOS Enders' blades", and he
shaved with a wiped-down NOS Enders blade. So: Dollar
takes the proprietary Enders blade; modern use = modded
injector, same as Speed. Whether the Dollar HEAD geometry
equals the Speed head is UNPINNED (no head comparison
found) — keep Dollar as a MODEL attribute inside the
Enders family entry until head evidence appears; never
fold a Dollar string into a "Speed Shaver" name.
