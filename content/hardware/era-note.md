**Era note:** the slant is Stirling's EARLY hardware line (2016–2017). The later Stirling razor line (2022+ strings: "Stirling Stainless Steel" / "SSS", Austin handle, ST standard + HA hyper-aggressive plates, serial ST-01-084) is a different, stainless line — never fold the 2017 slant strings into it.
