**Side discovery — C&H runs a used-gear resale section [catalog]:** "trifecta" listings are used bundles: "House of Mammoth Shire 2 Trifecta" (C&H capture 2024-07-18, $80) = soap + balm + EdP, "Used several times. Cannot be shipped outside US... All used gear is sold AS IS." Its TITLE reads "Embrace Trifecta" — a copied listing; cite the body, never the title. C&H "trifecta" = 3 products (soap+balm+EdP, no splash) — distinct from the dataset's "full trifecta" phrasing for a 4-product line. Sibling resale listings: Beloved trifecta (captured 2023-03-23), Fu Dao trifecta (2022-11-28).
