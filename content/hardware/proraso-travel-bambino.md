**Proraso travel = Bambino — THIRD MEASUREMENT (addendum to the
established entry).** B&B 638394 ("The smallest shaving brush
ever"): an owner's Proraso-branded small boar measures **17×46mm**,
posted in a thread comparing it directly against the Bambino (notes
18×44) — within poster-measurement tolerance of SkyWarrior21's ID
and the 19mm "Proraso Travel Boar" dataset string. Same thread: the
Omega 10086 "Bigger Bambino" is the same 18mm knot in a taller
handle (B&B 622164: "This is Omega 10086 with 18mm knot (the answer
of Omega on Boar discipline to Simpson Wee scott)").
