**Karve Christopher Bradley ("CB") plates — full lineup AUDIT 2026-11-03.**
Eight aggression levels, **AA through G** (not AA–F — the earlier note
missed G; see correction below), each level sold in **SB (solid/safety
bar) and OC (open comb)** plate types (maker's own plate-selection copy,
relayed on B&B: "two types of plates... 8 different levels of OC plates
and 8 [of SB]"). Maker-published gap/exposure table (maker chart via
Sharpologist; community corroborates against the maker chart):
- AA – 0.47mm / −0.07mm exposure; A – 0.60 / −0.02; B – 0.73 / 0.00;
C – 0.85 / +0.09; D – 0.98 / +0.13; E – 1.10 / +0.17; F – 1.23 / +0.22;
**G – 1.36 / +0.25**.
- **G is a LIMITED flash run, never a standard SKU**: Chris Bradley made
"a few" SB-G plates and sold the first five in a single Instagram flash
post (~2019); "no plans to make more" (busy with the stainless/aluminum
models); waiting-list only since. One B&B poster's "1.36 (G plate 1.23)"
aside is a confused F/G conflation — the maker-side numbers are
G = 1.36/0.25, F = 1.23/0.22. G OC never documented; dataset G strings
are SB or bare (see census).
- Community guidance (B&B, Chris via owners): OC plates shave ~one level
more aggressive than the same letter in SB — "drop down a plate" when
switching SB → OC (SB-C ≈ OC-B); and the thick bar edge means gap alone
under-predicts Karve aggression (one multi-plate owner: "Karve plate G
has larger gap and exposure than the Lupo .95" but never got closer
shaves — thick-bar geometry, not raw numbers).
- Dataset census (sotd_2016–2026_full.json, razor field, 2026-11-03):
14,876 Karve razor posts; bare letters dominate (C 4,106/175a,
B 4,100/116a, D 2,052/104a, A 834/57a, E 459/35a, F 212/18a, AA 47/9a,
G 37/10a). Comb-tagged: C-SB 249/14a, D-OC 232/3a (EldrormR-heavy),
B-SB 92/10a, A-SB 58/4a, C-OC 46/3a, D-SB 39/6a, A-OC 27/2a,
B-OC 20/5a, E-SB 13/4a, B "safety bar" spelled out 10/2a, E-OC 6/2a,
F-OC 6/2a, F-SB 2/2a, G-SB 2/1a (gfdoto 2022). E-F-G skew vocal:
maker-attested sales were 88% B–D (B&B: "E Plate 6%, F Plate 6%").
- **[catalog] Validator rule (P-17: plates are attributes):** every
plate/comb string folds to the ONE `razor/karve-christopher-bradley`
entry (exists, VERIFIED) with plate letter + SB/OC as attributes —
never split by plate, and never fold "G" strings into the F cluster
(G owners own both; it's a real plate). Bare letter = certain for the
level, no comb inference (a bare "C" is never assumed SB).
- Maker status 2026-11-03 (karveshaving.com homepage, live): Chris
Bradley's one-man Edmonton shop is ACTIVE but the lineup has moved
past the CB flagship — current razors are the **Bison** (aluminum,
2024, own single-plate head, 0.71/0.06), the **Overlander**
(heads/plates still listed), and the **KMOD Alpha** (experimental
