*Confidence: established · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**Maker — Everyday Shaving, Brian Twilley / [[lineages/charcoal-goods-link|Charcoal Goods link]] (established with a caveat):** the company's own email, quoted verbatim on B&B #22: "Our company is owned by Brian Twilley... One of his previous consumer products companies was called Charcoal Goods... Our Stinger Safety Razor is the only consumer product that we manufacture. It's made on the same equipment and by the same machinists as components for our aerospace and medical customers." Corroborated in-thread: #37 "The shop it's made in is CG's shop, I messaged the company, they said Brian is their machinist." The naming is a deliberate CG callback: "Everyday" was the official nickname of a CG baseplate level and "Stinger" was a CG handle option (B&B #31; the CG Stinger handle is a 2018+ dataset fixture — "Charcoal Goods Level 2 with Stinger Handle" ×111). CAVEAT: community skepticism exists in the same thread (#26: no direct confirmation from Brian himself; the email says "owned by" while another poster says "business partner and machinist" — owner vs machinist role unpinned). Record Twilley as the maker link with the role caveat; never assert sole ownership.
