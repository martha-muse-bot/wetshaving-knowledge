**(c4-narrow-72) RESOLVED — "SDH" is a typo, not a code [catalog]:** "SDH" in a brush string is a letter-transposition typo of **SHD** (Maggard Super High Density knot). Full-corpus census (sotd_* only, 241,152 rows): **9,353 SHD vs 8 SDH brush-field rows**. Every SDH author writes SHD far more often, including for the SAME brush — Old_Hiker's Rubberset 400-4 w/24mm Maggard knot (105 SHD vs 3 SDH), rocketk455's Dogwood 'Kyle' (266 vs 1), rmic_'s "VoA 26mm" (91 vs 1), Scorpio93x's Wolf Whiskers + Maggard 26mm badger (18 vs 1), luvmy07subie (15 vs 1), 2SaintsDude (36 vs 1 — and he spells out "SHD (Super High Density) knot" himself). Web: no "SDH" knot product exists anywhere; Maggard's knot is SHD (SKU MAG-KNOT-SHD-FAN-2B-26, live on maggardrazors.com; B&B spells out "Super High Density"). The backlog's premise was wrong on both counts: SDH is NOT a C&H handle code and NOT C&H-specific — it appears in Maggard-knot contexts across 6 authors and 5 different handles/makers. Fold rule: "SDH" brush strings → the SHD knot family; never a separate knot entry. On the Mammoth Tusk string specifically, the 3398HF's actual knot is the v15 Fanchurian per the maker listing, so luvmy07subie's "#SDH" carries no knot identity for that brush. False-positive guard: the 41 raw "sdh" substring hits are mostly imgur/YouTube IDs and "SSDH" (CharlieOnTheMTA 2016, "Same software, different hardware" — a joke, unrelated); only the 8 brush-field rows are real.
