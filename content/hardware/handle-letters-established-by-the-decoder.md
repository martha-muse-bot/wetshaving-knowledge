*Confidence: established · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**Handle letters (established by the decoder):** N = nero/black resin
(507/M/N/L = "Black Resin"; HORSE/506/N/L = "barber black"),
A = white ("barber white": 506A/M/L, HORSE/506/A/L), U = olive wood,
ALL = aluminum, MC = metal chrome. **Correction to the 2026-10-31
glossary note:** B is NOT "bianco" — the decoder never glosses B as a
word; B is the 80-series' cream/white PLASTIC handle letter
(Sharpologist: solid white plastic 80B XSE; salononline "80b Cream
Handle"). The Italian-color-word hypothesis (bianco/nero) is refined:
nero holds for N; A = white resin; B = cream plastic of the 80 line.
Do not cite "bianco" until a maker source says it.
