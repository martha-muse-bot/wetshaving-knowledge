The 2021–2022 "Rockwell 2C with a 5 plate" / "2C 5/6 plate" strings
are ALL ONE AUTHOR (loudmusicboy, 2021–2022, 26 strings): a
single owner running a 6C R5/R6 plate in his 2C body (the bodies
are identical, so plates interchange). NOT a 2C variant, NOT a
maker plate change — the maker FAQ still specifies R1/R3. Fold
into the 2C entry; plate = attribute.
