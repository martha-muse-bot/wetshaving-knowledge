**Hoffritz OC = the James Bond razor — RESOLVED 2026-10-29
(Bond-OC run):** cowzilla3's 7 "Hoffritz of New York OC"
posts (2023–2026) are the literary James Bond razor, a
Merkur-made heavy-toothed open comb — NOT a slant, and NOT
the slant entry the catalog keys them to. Evidence:
(1) Fleming, "007 in New York" (1963): "Hoffritz on Madison
Avenue for one of their heavy, toothed Gillette-type razors,
so much better than Gillette's own product"; (2) Fleming,
The Man with the Golden Gun (1965): "He always used a
Hoffritz safety razor patterned on the old-fashioned
heavy-toothed Gillette type. His American friend Felix Leiter
had once bought him one in New York to prove that they were
the best, and Bond had stayed with them" (via thejamesbond-
dossier.com, two articles); (3) literary007.com's Bond-
branding survey, flat: "Hoffritz razors (stamped with the
company name) were made for them by Merkur of Solingen,
Germany"; (4) the poster's own quest note — cowzilla3,
2023-03-29: "I picked up this Hoffritz as a birthday present
for myself as I'd spent a good while looking for the
'heavy-toothed' non-slant version that James Bond uses in
the novels"; (5) B&B 128566 #13/#14 (James Bond shaving
thread): "it was probably not a slant, that Hoffritz is
famous for"; (6) owners confirm vintage Hoffritz OCs exist
(B&B 558985 #6: "My mildest OC is a vintage Hoffritz"; B&B
60996 #2, in the open-comb thread). Maker-model note: the
Merkur open-comb head is a clone of the Gillette Old Type
thick-cap model (B&B 550655 #8) — so "patterned on the
old-fashioned heavy-toothed Gillette type" = the Merkur OC
head, and "heavy-toothed" is period language for the open
comb itself. The exact vintage Merkur OC model code behind
the Hoffritz OC is UNPINNED (period rebrand catalogs show
Hoffritz as a rebrander — see B&B 621143 below — but no
model-level ID surfaced); never cite a model number.
Dataset scope (sotd_*_full.json): exactly 7 cowzilla3 posts —
"Hoffritz of New York OC" ×4 (2023), "Hoffritz of NY OC" ×1
(2023), "Hoffritz of New York DE OC" ×2 (2025, 2026) — plus
djundjila's 2021-12-29 Lather Games post "Hoffritz – Open
comb (cursed)", a second owner of a Hoffritz OC but in a
challenge context. **Catalog correction [catalog]:** the
catalog's `unverified/razor/hoffritz-of-new-york-de-oc`
(corroborated) has product: "Slant" — inherited from nmrs
correct_matches routing the OC strings to the Hoffritz Slant
cluster. That product field is WRONG: these strings describe
the Merkur-made heavy-toothed open comb, a different razor
from the Merkur-37 slant. Validators: split the OC strings
into their own open-comb entry (Merkur manufacture
attribute), never fold into the slant entry; see the 2026-
10-28 addendum's qualifier (a), which this run supersedes —
the OC now HAS Merkur attribution on literary007 + Fleming
evidence. NOTE: the 2023 "Hoffritz of New York OC" ×4 and
2023 "Hoffritz of NY OC" ×1 strings sit in the catalog's
hoffritz-slant family (the 2025/2026 "DE OC" strings hold the
de-oc cluster) — the correction covers all 7.
