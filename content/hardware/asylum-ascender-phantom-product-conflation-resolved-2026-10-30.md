**[catalog] "Asylum Ascender" — PHANTOM PRODUCT, conflation RESOLVED
2026-10-30.** No BullGoose-house "Asylum Ascender" ever existed. The
March 2026 Sharpologist podcast (BullGoose closure announcement) said
only "one final release of Ascender razors" (clipzag transcript of the
2026-03-09 episode) — "Asylum" was never in the announcement; the
"Asylum Ascender" gloss was this learner's earlier editorialization and
is retracted. The "Ascender" is **Ascender Razors, Ross Hjelmgren's own
brand (est. 2025)**, which BullGoose carried as a RETAILER: B&B 671932
("The Ascender Eagle R1 is AVAILABLE NOW!", published 2025-07-11)
records a BullGoose email release of 15 Ascender Eagle R1s in July 2025
("Now down to 8... All gone on Bullgoose now"). The March 2026 "final
release of Ascender razors" = a final BullGoose run of Ascender Razors
stock, consistent with the existing retailer relationship; whether it
shipped is unpinned (no BullGoose Ascender product page or forum
ownership post found post-announcement). Dataset: exactly ONE Ascender
string ever — "Ascender Razors Skyline R1" (J33pGuy13, 2026-04-27,
post-announcement, channel unpinned). Catalog: never create an Asylum

entry, BullGoose as retailer. Sources: B&B 671932 p.2 #34/#35 (read
