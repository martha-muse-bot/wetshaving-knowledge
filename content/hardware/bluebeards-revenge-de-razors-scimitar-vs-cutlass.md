**Bluebeard's Revenge DE razors: Scimitar vs Cutlass — PARTIALLY
RESOLVED 2026-10-25 (backlog item "The Ultimate Cutlass").**
The Cutlass IS a real Bluebeard's Revenge DE razor, not a phantom
retailer string: it is the razor in BBR's Shaving Cream + Cutlass
Double-Edge Razor starter set (gift guides 2020-02 PackThePJs,
2021-02 evenangelsfall, 2021 Christmas mehimthedogandababy),
listed as "The Bluebeards Revenge Cutlass Double-Edge Safety
Razor" in OPUMO's best-safety-razors roundup (pub 2022-11-16,
upd 2023-12-27: "gunmetal finish to aluminium handle"), and
retailed as "THE ULTIMATE cutlass double-edge razor" (Beautiefy,
€33.95 — "Ultimate" is BBR's own marketing word, cf. the 2010
launch's "ultimate shaving experience", so treat "Ultimate" as
retailer SEO, not part of the maker name). Dataset: ZERO
"Cutlass" razor-field strings in sotd_2016–2026 — the name
lives in retail/gift-guide copy, not in SOTD posts, so no
dataset folding is needed yet; a future "Cutlass" string folds
to Cutlass, never to Scimitar, until the relationship below is
settled. The Scimitar is BBR's ORIGINAL DE razor: launched
Nov 15, 2010 (maker press release via Newswire: "dubbed the
Scimitar", laser-etched logo, 67 g, 9.5 cm), described 2021 by
Wegian Wetshaving as "a bog-standard, straightforward brass
and zinc three piece" sold by BBR, and in the dataset exactly
ONCE — delicate_, 2017-02-06: "Bluebeard's Revenge Scimitar
(=R89 head)." That parenthetical is the ONLY head-platform
evidence for either BBR DE razor: read it as R89/DE89-FAMILY
closed-comb head design (the shared EJ DE89 / Mühle R89 head
geometry used by countless private labels), NOT as "made by
Mühle" — no maker statement names Mühle, and the brass/zinc
three-piece construction points at the generic OEM head.
**Cutlass vs Scimitar relationship: UNRESOLVED — do NOT merge.**
Against a simple rename: the names circulated in OVERLAPPING
windows (Cutlass in 2020–2021 gift guides while Wegian still
called the current razor "Scimitar" in Dec 2021) and the finish
descriptions differ (Scimitar: chrome, all-metal, 67 g;
Cutlass per OPUMO, single source: gunmetal aluminium handle).
For a rename/restyle: BBR has never listed two DE razors side
by side in any source found, and the set razor is the line's
only DE offering in the 2020–2023 sources. Either a restyle
under a new name or a parallel variant fits the evidence;
a maker-site or product-photo comparison (head profile,
handle, logo etching) is the deciding evidence. [catalog]
[[Catalog guidance]]: keep Scimitar and Cutlass as SEPARATE
entries; record the Scimitar head as R89-family (attribute,
single-poster source, flagged as such); record the Cutlass
head platform as UNPINNED — the backlog's Meridian/EJ
suspects found no support, and the R89-family claim must not
be carried from Scimitar to Cutlass without its own evidence.
SISTER-BRAND NOTE (lead, spec-identical): BBR's US-market
sister brand Dreadnought (same founder David Hildrew, same
Decelerine line, same press contact) sold the DE 'Spartan'
with IDENTICAL specs to the Scimitar launch — 67 g, 9.5 cm,
laser-etched logo, chrome (Cision Christmas-gift release,
via the Bluebeard's Revenge press channel) — very likely the
same OEM razor under the US brand name; needs a photo
