**The TTO adjustable (RazoRock Adjust / Stirling adjustable).** B&B
647716 #1: the maker's "Adjustable Butterfly Razor (Razorock Adjust)"
$3.46 wholesale; B&B 610394: TRC sent the wrong "Baili butterfly
adjustable"; B&B 651308 #29 calls it "a rebranded Baili DB179" (note
the DB transposition) and says "Not sure why Stirling calls it the
BD191 though it's the same razor" — CONFLICTING single-source coding
(DB179 vs BD191), and BD191 is independently the Classic 3-piece
(manuals.plus), so do NOT assign any BD/BR code to the TTO adjustable
until a maker or clear retail source does. Maker blog (B&B 647716
#1): the adjustable-butterfly with the Gillette adjustment mechanism
was introduced 2021.
