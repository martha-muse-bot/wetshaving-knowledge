*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**The blade is injector-width, NOT Durham-width
(established, scanner measurements):** razors.click's
flatbed-scanner measurements of a real Enders blade give a
37.37 mm cutting edge, 44.00 mm back width, 15.00 mm depth,
0.35 mm thick. The [[Durham Duplex]] blade is 2 1/2 in
(63.5 mm) long — nearly twice as wide. The two blade
systems are dimensionally incompatible, full stop. Modern
workaround, established by firsthand use (razors.click
diary; B&B 576746; B&B 453079): Schick Proline B-20
injector blades (37.95 mm) fit the 38.00 mm head gap,
propped by a dulled original Enders blade as carrier;
Personna injector blades (38.10 mm) do NOT fit, and
Proline P-30 blades are a different size entirely. Some
Enders examples have slightly narrower blade beds and
need the injector blade's edges filed down (B&B 576746
p.2 #32). Blade generations: older blades carry a
greyhound etching, newer ones are unetched (B&B 576746
#8); a substitute unbranded Enders blade with fitment
issues is also reported.
