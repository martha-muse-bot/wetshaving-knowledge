**Bluebeard's Revenge Cut-Throat shavette — Osma claim REJECTED, Parker
SR lead instead (lead, 2026-10-24).** The backlog item ("suspected
rebranded Osma", djanovshaving 2025, no proof) found NO corroboration
anywhere: BBR's own Cut-Throat review (shavingadvisor.com) lists it as
"Made in the UK", and a firsthand B&B SABRE-thread owner (#32,
thread 360385) calls it "pretty sure it's just a re-branded Parker SR"
(he collected it; the shape matches Parker's SR shavettes). The Osma
claim is uncorroborated gossip; the Parker-SR claim is one firsthand
source — park BOTH as leads, Parker-SR weighted slightly heavier.
Catalog: no action; do not create an Osma-linked entry.
[[ADDENDUM 2026-10-25]] (Parker-SR second-source hunt — verdict: DOWNGRADED
to unlikely, do NOT record Parker as manufacture attribute):
- The single source is weaker than the 2026-10-24 entry implied: B&B
SABRE thread 360385 #32 says "pretty sure it's just a re-branded
Parker SR" — hedged ("pretty sure") AND pre-ownership: at that point
the poster had only put the BBR shavette on an Amazon wishlist to
"break into straights"; he later shaved with it (half-Shark, then
half-Personna Lab) but never revisits the Parker comparison. A
hedged wishlist guess is not a both-owner identification.
- No second source names Parker: the two other community voices found
speak of SIMILARITY, not identity — B&B 615634 #21: the BBR is
"very similar to more expensive Parkers"; B&B 601025 #6 calls it a
"swing-arm Parker-style shavette" (Parker-STYLE = the pattern, not
the maker) and notes his BBR also takes AC blades as well as half-DE
(friction-held; single-source blade-compat note).
- Structural counter-evidence: the BBR Cut-Throat has HARD PLASTIC
scales and is "very light... almost feel breakable" (ShavingAdvisor
review, full spec list: stainless fan-style 3-part blade housing,
closed 6 in, open 9 in, half-DE; listed "Made in the UK" — single
review source, not a maker claim). The Parker SR flagships are
ALL-STAINLESS and Made in India (SR1 48 g / 5.75 in; SRX 65 g /
5.25 in; SRX Black likewise stainless). A rebranded SR would share
the stainless scales; it does not. Price tier agrees: BBR shavette
£9.99 (maker press via Cision, 2012 Skyfall piece) vs Parkers as
the "more expensive" comparator.
- Shared mechanism is NOT OEM evidence: the three-part fan/clip
holder is a commodity swing-arm pattern (SRX review uses the same
"fans out into three pieces" wording; SABRE discusses Sanguine
tear-drop swingbacks, Barbury's, and Parker PTB/PTW push-to-load
models in the same family, and even floats Parker PTW as a
rebranded Lalwani — Parker itself is not a clean OEM anchor).
- Both-owner counter-note (weak, recorded as such): SABRE 360385 #848
owns "My Dreadnought (Blurbeard's Revenge) and my Parker" as two
separate go-to shavettes, never as the same razor.
- [[Catalog guidance]]: BBR Cut-Throat stays its OWN entry; record the
holder as "swing-arm fan-holder family (Parker-style pattern)" and
NEVER as Parker-made. Re-open only on a photo comparison of
blade-arm geometry/etching, or a Parker plastic-scaled SR-family
model matching the BBR's construction.
- SISTER-BRAND BONUS (established, maker press + owner corroboration):
the Dreadnought cut-throat shavette IS the BBR shavette under the
US sister brand — maker press release via Cision (2013 Father's
Day): "Dreadnought cut-throat Shavette... Laser etched with
eye-catching Dreadnought logo... length of 24 cm when fully opened...
RRP $20... takes snapped double edged razor blades", David Hildrew
'Chief Dreadnought'; SABRE #848's "Dreadnought (Blurbeard's
Revenge)" confirms the equivalence from an owner. 24 cm ≈ the
BBR's 9 in open (22.9 cm) within measurement variance. Fold any
"Dreadnought shavette" dataset string into the BBR Cut-Throat
family, not into a separate entry.
- Dataset scope (sotd_2016–2026_full.json, razor field): exactly TWO
BBR shavette strings, both generic ("Bluebeard's Revenge Shavette",
2017 + 2024), ZERO "Cut-Throat" strings — near-zero catalog
impact; this is a lore clarification, not a queue item. Sources:
