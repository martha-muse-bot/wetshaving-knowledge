*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**Amakuni (ESTABLISHED):** Tatara's first single-edge razor, launched
Oct 2024. AC (Artist Club) format — fits ALL AC blades EXCEPT the
green Feather Light; MAGNETIC two-piece top cap (a first for Tatara);
specs with AC Professional: 0.85 mm gap / 0.06 mm exposure / 29° blade
angle; compact head, hollow handle (steel 80.7 g, titanium 85 g
matte); named after the legendary smith Amakuni Yasutsuna. Community
optimum angle ~29° (vs ~24° for the Masamune/Nodachi family).
Catalog: AC format, its own entry — never fold into DE entries.
