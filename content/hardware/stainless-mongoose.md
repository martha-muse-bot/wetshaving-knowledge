**Stainless Mongoose:** 316 SS, satin or polished; head ~50 g, stock handle ~90 g; $169 (2015). Production ran in named BATCHES — Batch 1 = 45 units (late summer 2014), Batch 2, Batch 3 (B3 polished heads documented). NO product was ever found sold under the name "Mongoose II" — the name appears only as poster shorthand in SOTD strings; the SS line's real variation is batch + finish.
