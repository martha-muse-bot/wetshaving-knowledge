*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] Barbaros TR-3 — the open-comb plate, NOT a
standalone razor.** The TR-3 is the TR-2's OPEN-COMB base plate,
sold as a plate (and as a set with the TR-2) sharing the TR-2's
top cap and handle (B&B 629705 #1: "a Barbaros Tr-2 razor along
with the Tr-3 OC plate"; #10: "the fact that those are sold as
a whole set means that the top cap works with both plates").
B&B p.2 #22/#23: "TR3 is TR2OC? Essentially, yes, but with a
slightly larger gap and blade exposure." Specs (single firsthand
B&B source, rate-the-shave p.2360): 304 stainless, sandblasted
matte, 12-tooth open comb, 1.30mm gap, 1.15mm exposure, head
40g; total razor 115g. Character: very efficient with real
blade feel — "Blackbird territory" (B&B #4, #34) yet smoother
than the Blackbird OC per p.2 #21 ("smoother... less shallow
shaving angle... BBS effortlessly, doesn't feel aggressive");
one owner calls it a daily-usable "miracle" despite the specs
(B&B 626728). RARITY: intended run 50; polishing problems meant
only 18 passed Hasan's QC; the other ~32 went out as disclosed
"blemished"/seconds via eBay, Etsy, and Bulgarian reseller
Split7 (B&B #9, #35, #40; BullGoose cleared TR-3s at $200,
B&B #34). Hasan stated he would not produce polished versions
in future (B&B #9). [[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]: TR-3 is a
PLATE/VARIANT of the TR-2 line, not a separate razor model —
validators should key it as TR-2 + OC plate (or TR-3 plate
entry linked to TR-2), never as a standalone razor; and
"blemished"/"seconds" TR-3 strings are the same product, not a
separate SKU. Confidence: established (plate relationship,
rarity/seconds channel); TR-3 gap/exposure/steel grade = lead
(single firsthand source each).
