**Aluminum ALSB45 = fixed complete razor, separate product:** scalloped cap + Solid Bar plate, .45 mm gap, exposure 0.002", 6061-T6, parts NOT sold separately, and the maker makes NO interchangeability claim for it (pointed contrast with the Bronze and Slim pages). Sharpologist's hands-on review describes a scalloped head with raised-tab (vs pin) blade retention; owners describe a taller cap. Treated as a distinct head design; weakest-documented of the Timeless geometry calls (maker publishes no head dims for it).
