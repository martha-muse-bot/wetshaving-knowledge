**GEM Feather Weight generations vs the "Slim-V Flat Top" claim —
CAUTION (2026-10-22).** A 2024 dataset note (djundjila's 14-generation
GEM timeline) claims: "1950: New GEM Feather Weight, renamed to
'Slim-V Flat Top' in 1953; British version sold as 'Natural Angle'
by Ever-Ready." External check: the GENERATION split is real — B&B
collectors (Ron R, "Gem Razor Models" thread) distinguish the 1st-gen
Feather Weight (1950–52, slimmer set-back top cap) from the 2nd gen
(1953–54), matching the 1953 boundary — but NO collector source
corroborates a rename to "Slim-V Flat Top." Likely a conflation with
GEM's own "Natural 'V' Angle" MARKETING copy (the built-in shaving
angle printed on Feather Weight / Heavy Flat Top packaging — B&B
"help with the GEM shaving system" thread). "Natural Angle" on
Ever-Ready British packaging is the same marketing copy, not a model
name — one UK poster owns "NOS ER Natural Angle" (an Ever-Ready
Featherweight). Do NOT create a "Slim-V Flat Top" or "Natural Angle"
alias. Lead/caution; djundjila's full 14-generation timeline is
otherwise a useful collector reference (1906-1953: 1912/Star Cadet/
Junior/Damaskeene → 1914 → Shovelhead → Micromatic OC Gen 1/Gen 2 →
Clog-Pruf → Clog-Pruf Peerless → Flying Wing/Bullet Tip →
Jewel/Streamline/Ambassador → Feather Weight → ...).
