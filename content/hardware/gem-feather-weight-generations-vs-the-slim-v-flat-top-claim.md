**GEM Feather Weight generations vs the "Slim-V Flat Top" claim —
CAUTION (2026-10-22).** A 2024 dataset note (djundjila's 14-generation
GEM timeline) claims: "1950: New GEM Feather Weight, renamed to
'Slim-V Flat Top' in 1953; British version sold as 'Natural Angle'
by Ever-Ready." External check: the GENERATION split is real — B&B
collectors (Ron R, "Gem Razor Models" thread) distinguish the 1st-gen
Feather Weight (1950–52, slimmer set-back top cap) from the 2nd gen
(1953–54), matching the 1953 boundary — but NO collector source
corroborates a rename to "Slim-V Flat Top." Likely a conflation with
GEM's own "Natural 'V' Angle" MARKETING copy (the built-in shaving
angle printed on Feather Weight / Heavy Flat Top packaging — B&B
"help with the GEM shaving system" thread). "Natural Angle" on
Ever-Ready British packaging is the same marketing copy, not a model
name — one UK poster owns "NOS ER Natural Angle" (an Ever-Ready
Featherweight). Do NOT create a "Slim-V Flat Top" or "Natural Angle"
alias. Lead/caution; djundjila's full 14-generation timeline is
otherwise a useful collector reference (1906-1953: 1912/Star Cadet/
Junior/Damaskeene → 1914 → Shovelhead → Micromatic OC Gen 1/Gen 2 →
Clog-Pruf → Clog-Pruf Peerless → Flying Wing/Bullet Tip →
Jewel/Streamline/Ambassador → Feather Weight → ...).
**ADDENDUM 2026-11-06 — RESOLVED: "Slim-V Flat Top" is djundjila's
own construction, not a GEM model name [catalog].** Traced to its
source: his 2024-11-17 note states all his dates come from the B&B
"American Safety Razor Dating Chart" thread (mata_66, 566585) and
that he "hasn't double-checked anything." His timeline uses BOTH
"Slim-V Flat Top" (1953, Feather Weight rename) AND "V-Slim 'Heavy
Flat Top'" (1955, G-Bar) — the same "V-Slim" element transposed
and attached to two different models. Exact-phrase web searches
return ZERO wetshaving hits for "Slim-V" and for "V-Slim" anywhere
outside his own notes (searched 2026-11-06); the chart's text
renderings quoted across three B&B threads carry "E-Bar", "G-Bar",
"Heavy Flat Top" but no "Slim-V"/"V-Slim". The real ingredients
are all genuine: "Heavy Flat Top" IS GEM's marketing name for the
G-Bar ("It's actually the Heavy Flat TOP, which was how it was
marketed by Gem back in the day," B&B 549277); "Natural 'V' Angle"
IS GEM's marketing copy for the Feather Weight / Heavy Flat Top
shaving angle ("Hence the Natural 'V' Angle as they marketed both
these razors as they have identical heads," B&B 459575, verbatim).
The 1953 boundary is real: 2nd-gen Feather Weight 1953–54, whose
head is "very similar... or almost identical" to the G-Bar's (B&B
554376 p.32). "Natural Angle" as a British model name is likewise
unsupported: the floating ASR chart lists only "1950s ER
Featherweight (British)"; djundjila's own razor field "Ever-Ready -
Natural Angle a.k.a British Featherweight" (2024-11-26) shows he
treats the packaging copy as a name — his note the next day says
"the 'Natural' Angle in the name isn't all marketing speak."
CORRECTION to the caution above: "E-Bar" is NOT djundjila's
coinage — it is genuine community currency for the Ever-Ready
Heavy Flat Top, from the same floating chart ("1933 ER 'E-Bar',"
B&B 554376 p.18; "The elusive Ever-Ready 'Natural Handle Razor
Deluxe' AKA golden 'E-Bar'," p.15), though the chart's 1933
E-Bar dating is itself disputed by a collector on the same page.
**[[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]:** never create "Slim-V Flat Top" or
"Natural Angle" entries; fold "GEM Slim V Flat Top" strings to the
GEM Feather Weight family (2nd-gen 1953–54 era reading — the thing
djundjila's timeline means by it). The string now circulates
beyond its author: anchovie7468 used "GEM Slim V Flat Top" as a
clean-up razor 2025-07-13 (GEM JULY), eight months after
djundjila's series — almost certainly downstream of it, but a
second author all the same, so validators will meet the string.
Residual: the mata_66 chart itself is an image attachment in B&B
566585 (text fetch returns only the Zinn-history first post) — a
live-browser image read could confirm no "Slim-V" row exists in
the chart; the verdict does not depend on it. G-Bar dating tension
noted, not resolved: chart says 1956 (US), another thread says
1949–1959, a poster says circa 1949–1950s.
