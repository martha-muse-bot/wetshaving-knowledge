*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-10-22 · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] [[Vikings Blade]] is NOT a Baili rebrand — resolved 2026-10-22
(backlog item).** The dataset notes' recurring claim ("Vikings Blade
Godfather = rebranded Baili", sgrdddy 2021; "$40 for a rebranded $5
Baili BD191", rocketk455 2017) is denied by the maker itself and
nuanced rather than settled by the evidence: Vikings Blade is a design
house (Vullion Group Pty Ltd, Melbourne, Australia) that contracts
factories — Baili INCLUDED — to build molds from its own CAD designs.
VB's own account (vikingsblade.com/pages/…chieftain-is-not-the-same-
as-baili, fetched live 2026-10-22): Baili refused at first to build the
Chieftain mold unless it could use it too; after the Chieftain became an
Amazon bestseller, Baili "accidentally" broke the mold, proposed a
simplified replacement mold, and SIMULTANEOUSLY launched its own
near-identical BD179 and invited hundreds of OEM re-branders —
the re-branders' "buy the BD179, 3x cheaper" marketing created the
myth. Corroboration (independent, firsthand): B&B threads report the
Weishi/Baili/Vikings razors shave VERY differently from each other
(Jay21 2023, "I have used both plenty"), and the VB Emperor Augustus
is reported by B&B to be made to VB's own spec (copper-plated silo
doors, cognac electroplate finish — no Baili equivalent). **Catalog
impact:** do NOT merge Vikings Blade into Baili or alias Chieftain/
Godfather → BD179/BD191. Per the brand-of-record / user-intent rule
(P-04), manufacture is an ATTRIBUTE: Vikings Blade entries stand with
maker "Vikings Blade"; Baili is the contract factory behind (at least
some of) the original molds — recorded as a note, never as identity.
The BD179 is Baili's own derivative razor, a separate entry.
Confidence: established (maker's own page + corroborating firsthand
forum comparisons; the maker's version is self-serving but the
catalog rule makes the fine point moot — identity follows the brand
on the box either way). Method note: "looks identical" is not
"rebranded" — a shared CONTRACT factory can produce both the design
house's original and its own lookalike.
Addendum 2026-10-27 — Emperor Augustus QShave-OEM claim RESOLVED
(backlog item; verdict: NOT a Qshave rebrand, and the backlog's
gloss on the source post was backwards). B&B 564238 #30 does NOT
float Qshave as the Emperor's OEM: it quotes VB's own plating
specs (#25, maker email: Augustus cognac = multiple electroplated
applications; silo doors electroplated in copper, sealed in oil
lacquer) and concludes VB is "finally out of the shadow of the
whole rebranded Chinese razor rumor mill — I just can't see Baili,
Qshave or even Weishi going through that much effort." Qshave,
Baili, Weishi appear there only as the rumor-mill foil, and the
poster rejects them. The platform facts: Sharpologist's Parthenon
2.0 review places Parthenon (Qshave), Crusader (VB), and Weishi
Nostalgic on "the same basic TTO head design," all launched
~2018 — Crusader is VB's member of that platform family
(continuous, no-marking adjustment band), NOT its rebrand. The
Emperor (2019) is VB's upgrade on the family: numbered adjustment
dial, anti-misalignment trap mechanism, dual comb retained
(scalloped + smooth sides), 134 g / 110 mm, brass/Zamak
(Sharpologist Emperor review). B&B #22's "mechanism very close
to the 3000S" is platform-mechanism resemblance (internal spring,
baseplate drops with gap increase, no Gillette collet), not an OEM
identification — and "Qshave" itself is a marketing name, not a
manufacturer (B&B Futur-clone thread: Qshave sells razors of
different manufacturers under one name), so "Qshave as OEM" is a
category error on top of a misreading. Emperor family for
validators [catalog]: Emperor = frosted-chrome base model;
Augustus = the cognac/bronze cosmetic edition of the same razor
(NOT a separate model); Meiji = the same Emperor head on a short
handle (95 mm, 124 g; newer units in Raven Black coating per
Sharpologist). Dataset: Emperor strings are few and Meiji-heavy
(51× "Vikings Blade - Emperor Meiji" in 2020; only one
"Emperor Augustus" razor-field string, 2026). Catalog: VB Emperor
entries stand under Vikings Blade; do NOT alias Emperor/Augustus
→ Qshave Parthenon or Weishi Nostalgic. Manufacture attribute =
Chinese contract production to VB's spec (as with the Chieftain);
the specific Emperor factory is UNPINNED (lead).
