*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**Model family (ESTABLISHED):** Masamune (mild DE, 0.63 mm gap, closed
and open comb), Masamune Nodachi (aggressive DE, 0.90 mm gap, longer
108 mm / 12.5 mm-Ø handle vs Masamune's 93.7 mm / 11 mm), Muramasa
(adjustable, 316L), Amakuni (single-edge, AC format — see below). All
Masamune/Nodachi parts INTERCHANGEABLE (maker's "unique fitting
system"); Tatara publishes a "Razor Scale" mildness chart for the
combos.
