*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] [[Weber Safety Razor]] — trajectory + hardware notes
(2026-10-22).** Founded 2010, launched 2012 — the first modern
USA-made DE razor (316L stainless, cast heads + machined handles).
Three head designs — **DLC, ARC, PH** (PH = the 0.82mm-gap head in
the dataset, e.g. MalthusTheShaver 2021-06-25 "Weber 0.82mm PH w
bulldog handle") — and three handles — **Classic, Bulldog, Wave**.
Stopped making complete razors ~2019; handles-only after (head QC
complaints + costs; B&B "Why doesn't Weber make razors?" ~2021).
Full closure uncorroborated (weberrazor.com DNS-dead as of
2026-10-22 — suggestive, not proof). Source: Sharpologist 2019
(updated 2020-06-12) "The Weber — the first modern day American
safety razor" + B&B thread + dataset note. Confidence: established
(heads-discontinued/handles-only); closure = lead. Recorded so
validators date Weber-head strings pre-~2020 and never expect a
post-2020 Weber razor launch.
**UPDATE 2026-10-23: full closure SETTLED** (see the Weber closure
entry in [[Lineages]] — heads ~2018–19, handles-only ~2019–2021,
quiet wind-down, no farewell; weberrazor.com now a DreamHost
parked domain). [[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]: every Weber string is
legacy/destash; "Weber handle" on another head (Karve, Wolfman
pairings in the 2023–2024 dataset notes) is aftermarket reuse,
never a new Weber product — and the handles themselves are the
old Classic/Bulldog/Wave stock, last sold ~2021.
