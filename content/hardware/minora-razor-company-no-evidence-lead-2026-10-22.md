*Confidence: established · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**Minora razor company — NO evidence (lead, 2026-10-22).** A 2022
dataset note claims Gillette bought out "the Minora company" (maker
of NEW long-comb variants) in the 1930s–40s. No collector or
historical source documents such a company or buyout. What IS
established: Minora is a Gillette-owned brand name used in some
markets (Wikipedia's Gillette article: Tech razors sold as Minora),
and the catalog already loose-aliases bare "Minora" to the Egyptian
Minora Stainless blade. The note's OTHER half is true (British 7
O'Clock was acquired by Gillette). Keep the Minora-company claim as
an unresolved lead; never catalog fact.
**ADDENDUM 2026-11-05 (learner run) — RESOLVED with a reframing +
an origin CORRECTION [catalog].** [[hardware/the-source|The source]] note is VisceralWatch,
2020-06-24 (the backlog's "2022" dating was wrong): "this particular
one has 'Minora' stamped on the base plate, which was one of the
many razor companies that Gillette absorbed back in the day."
(a) **The buyout is TRUE IN SUBSTANCE, WRONG AS FRAMED.** Two
independent B&B voices agree Minora was an old SOUTH AFRICAN blade
brand Gillette acquired — B&B 598248 #17 ("Minora was an old South
African brand that Gillette acquired... Gillette started
manufacturing operations in South Africa in 1954") and the "Around
the World in 80 Blades" journal, Blade #48 ("before Gillette bought
the brand, and were made in Gillette's factory there in the
1950s"). It was a BLADE brand, acquired in the 1950s era — NOT a
1930s–40s razor company, and NOT the maker of NEW long-comb
variants. The Minora-stamped English NEW razors are Gillette-made
razors carrying the Minora brand stamp (same regional-stamp pattern
as the Polish "Grom" NEW — B&B "What is your favorite Gillette
NEW?" p.14). VisceralWatch's "absorbed" conflates Gillette's real
1930s absorptions (Autostrop/Probak 1930, Rotbart 1926–27) with the
1950s Minora brand acquisition. Exact acquisition date unpinned
(by the 1950s at latest). (b) **ORIGIN CORRECTION: the modern
Minora Stainless blade is made in RUSSIA (PPI, St. Petersburg),
NOT Egypt** — the 2026-10-22 "Egyptian Minora Stainless blade"
gloss is retired. Established by the catalog's own evidence source
(Razor Blades Club, live 2026-11-05: "double edge blades made in
Russia by Gillette") + B&B 598248 ("Stainless (yellow) Minoras
made in Russia"; "The pink-packaged Minoras were made by PPI
too") + B&B Russian Blade Games list ("[[world/gillette-minora|Gillette Minora]] (PPI)") +
B&B Blade Sharpness Research ("Gillette Minora Stainless Russia")
+ kind_simian's dataset notes 2021 ("Minora Stainless (0.1,
Russia 🇷🇺)"). The "Egyptian" gloss likely conflated Minora with
Shark/Lord (Egyptian Gillette blades) — never cite Egypt for
Minora. (c) **Blade geography:** France in the 1950s, Latin
America, Russia (PPI, current era) per B&B 598248 #17; Canada
"supposedly" per the same voice — dataset-attested but
date-uncertain (USS-SpongeBob 2019: "a box of about 600 WW2-era
Canadian-made Minora blades" from his grandfather's drugstore
stock; poster-dated WW2, which tensions the 1950s acquisition
story — treat as unpinned, never cite as a 1940s date). Yellow-pack
Stainless is the NEWER product (B&B 598248, published 2020-11-12:
"Trickling onto the market"; pink Platinum "have been around for
years"); Razor Blades Club still lists the Stainless InStock
2026-11-05 ($0.39). Post-2022 PPI wind-down: B&B 648167 lists
Minora among the brands that "might not move at all" (Asian/
African markets); whether current stock is still Russian-made is
unpinned — a lead. (d) **Dataset census (full corpus, 2026-11-05):**
razor-field Minora strings = 22: "Gillette Minora Flat Bottom NEW"
×13, "British Minora (Gillette) T-pin NEW" ×4, "Gillette NEW LC
(Minora)" ×2, 'Minora "t-pin NEW"' ×1, "Gillette Super Speed +
Gillette Minora (1)" ×1 (combo), "Gillette Tech Minora OC" ×1.
Blade-field = 1,093 rows. T-pin is a rare British NEW head
variation (B&B, same thread). (e) **Catalog gaps [catalog]:**
"Gillette Minora Flat Bottom NEW" (13 uses — the largest Minora
razor string) and "British Minora (Gillette) T-pin NEW" (4 uses)
have ZERO catalog presence — fold both into verified

brand-stamp attribute); fold `unverified/razor/minora-t-pin-new`
(1 use, ahjoyc2 2018) into gillette-new too. "Gillette NEW LC
(Minora)" is already an alias on gillette-new; "Gillette Tech
Minora OC" already an alias on gillette-tech. (f) **Author-scoped
tags (see [[aliases|Product aliases]]):** "Minora OG" = scribe__ only (94
