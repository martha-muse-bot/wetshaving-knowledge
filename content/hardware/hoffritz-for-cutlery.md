**Hoffritz for Cutlery — retailer identity ESTABLISHED
2026-10-29 ([[World notes]] entry):** founded 1932 in New
York City by Edwin Jay Hoffritz (trademark first use
1931-11-30, Class 008 covers razors); 23 stores by the
mid-1970s; peak 110 stores in 33 states early 1990s;
bankruptcy 1994; defunct 1996; successor Lifetime Hoan
(Lifetime Brands owns the HOFFRITZ mark today — Justia
trademark 73464510, registration 1985). Sources: Wikipedia
"Hoffritz for Cutlery"; USPTO/Justia trademark records.

shop and cowzilla3's "Hoffritz of New York" strings.)
