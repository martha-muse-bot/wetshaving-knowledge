**It is a real Stirling product, not a mislabel:** Stirling sold a line of its own-branded DE razors by July 2016 (Sharpologist Stirling badger review: "they now offer a lot more hardware... especially a line of their own branded DE razors"). Price ~$12 (B&B "Slant razors are cheating!" firsthand: "The cheap Stirling slant at like $12 was the best of the bunch for me, more comfort and just easier to use"). Two authors own BOTH it and the Maggard Slant and treat them as different razors: Matuhg (first razor Maggard V3/MR11 Jan 2017; Stirling Slant his second razor, "in the mail" 2017-01-25, first used 2017-01-28; Maggard Slant from 2017-03-16 — "much more intuitive and comfortable than my Stirling Slant"); mpontiff (Maggard Slant Nov 2016–2017 AND Stirling Slant Sep 2017–Jun 2018). Never fold Stirling Slant strings into the Maggard Slant entry.
