**The source:** Sharpologist "Current Crowdfunding Projects" (2018-09-19, Mantic59): Pereira Shavery was *"getting ready to launch a crowdfunding campaign for 'The Holy Grail Shaving Bowl'"* — pre-announcement, quoting the maker's email. The planned rewards: a golden-anodized-aluminium Grail-shaped bowl (personalised engraving), a 23k-gold-particle soap in a gold-anodized aluminium dish, and a choice of *"a brass safety razor, a gold plated shavette with finger ring for full DE blades, and a limited edition gold plated metal handled shaving brush set with a badger hair knot of the finest quality"* — plus an embossed towel.
