**Mammoth coin serial heuristic [catalog]:** coin numbers rise monotonically with time — 2513 (Abbraccio, ~mid-2021) → 2966–2969 (Damocles, Oct 2021) → 3329–3358 (Beloved, Jul 2022) → 3505–3508 (Voices, Oct 2022) → 4083 (Uitwaaien, ~2023) → 4861–4869 (Ausflug, Jun 2024) → 5413–5423 (Kryptonite, Jul 2025) → 6239–6244 (Snippid, Nov 2026). An undated collab-brush string carrying a coin number can be bracketed by its neighbors — a dating heuristic only, never a launch date.
