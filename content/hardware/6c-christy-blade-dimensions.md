**(6c) Christy blade dimensions — PARTIALLY RESOLVED: the Massage
Bar took the 1907-pattern ear-located DOUBLE-edge blade [catalog].**
US 1,502,615 (Christy, filed Jun 16, 1921, granted Jul 22, 1924)
states the Massage Bar razor is "identical with that of my prior
Patent No. 853,960, of May 21, 1907" except for the massage-bar
device, and its blade C carries ears at its upper rear corners.
US 853,960 (Christy, filed May 3, 1906, granted May 21, 1907):
"The blade C comprises a flat rigid plate of practically uniform
thickness, having a front cutting edge c and a rear cutting edge
c', while its ends are provided with ears or projections c2 which
bear against the ends of the lips or ears b of the supporting
plate" — a double-edged blade located by end ears, held by a
spring clamping plate, in a hoe-type razor with the blade at ~45°
to the handle. That is the SAME blade family as the old-style
Enders/Keen Kutter blades. No mm dimensions appear in either
patent's text (drawings only). Contrast the Speed system
(US 1,791,552, Bigoney, filed Jan 23, 1930, granted Feb 10, 1931):
single front cutting edge + rear BLUNT edge + spring side-tongues
that flex on the holder's inclined slideways to tension-hold the
blade — a different blade, not an evolution of the ear-located
plate. The 1936 court's one-way compatibility follows from that:
Speed blades fit old-style razors, old-style blades do NOT fit
Speed razors.
