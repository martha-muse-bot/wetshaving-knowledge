**Corporate arc vs blade system (established, B&B wiki):**
"On March 13, 1935, the Durham-Duplex Razor Company
succeeded to the business and rights of Enders Razor
Company, Inc."; HQ + razor manufacture moved to Mystic,
CT by 1941; "the two companies became the Durham-Enders
Razor Corporation by 1947." So post-1935 Enders products
were sold under the Durham-Enders umbrella — which is
why the dataset poster writes "Durham-Enders | Enders
Speed Shaver" — but the Speed Shaver kept its own blade
system throughout. TIMELINE (resolved 2026-11-06; see
addendum): the 1935 succession is confirmed by the 6th
Circuit opinion (85 F.2d 195, June 30, 1936, verbatim
quote above) — the Shaveden-derived 1940 "merger" date is
RETIRED as a conflation of the succession with the 1941
Mystic move and the 1947 rename. Never cite 1940.
