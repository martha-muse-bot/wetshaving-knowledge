The mammoth ivory was provided by Heritage Fossil... sold complete with a v15 Fanchurian knot and the matching stand." "Mammoth" here is the material, NOT House of Mammoth. The C&H CDX "mammoth" hits (3399hf/3401hf/3403hf-mammoth-brush-and-stand, Feb 2023) are the same material line. Consequence: the single "Mammoth Tusk" dataset row (luvmy07subie, 2022-06-16, "Chisel and Hound - Mammoth Tusk #FAN #SDH") is dated the SAME DAY as the 3398HF capture — the material-line reading is now the FAVORED one (lead upgraded, not established: no owner confirmation his brush is the 3398HF). Never fold a "Mammoth [material]" string into a HoM collab entry on the word "Mammoth" alone.
