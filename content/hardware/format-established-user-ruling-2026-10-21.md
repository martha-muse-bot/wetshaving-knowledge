*Confidence: established · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**Format (established, user ruling 2026-10-21):** [[Durham Duplex]]
is its own blade format in the catalog (entry
`Durham Duplex/durham-duplex`, needs-review at ruling time; 6
uses folded). It is NOT a DE, SE, or hair-shaper format: the
Durham blade is a flat, rigid, single-edge-length blade (2 1/2
in long per the Smithsonian pack record below) held flat, not
bent for rigidity the way a wafer-thin DE blade is (B&B
Dabblers thread #58). Dataset strings put it in both the razor
field ("Durham Duplex", "Durham Duplex (USA)", "Durham Duplex
(Made in Sheffield, Eng.)") and the blade field ("Durham
Duplex", "Durham - Duplex", "Personna Durham" ×2, both
NeedsMoreMenthol 2019, each paired with a Durham Duplex razor).
