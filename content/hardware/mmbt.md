**MMBT** = **M**icromatic **B**ullet **T**ip; **MMFW** = **M**icromatic **F**lying **W**ing. The 1947 third Micromatic variant (after Open Comb 1930 and Clog-Pruf 1941), also called the "Guiding Eye" (the indented circle on the top plate). Mildest of the three. Sources: B&B "GEM Micromatic Help" (264678), B&B "The Daily Double" 2023-02-27 ("Gem Micromatic Flying wing(AKA=MMFW, MMBT, guiding eye....)"), DFS "Review: The GEM MicroMatic Family".
