**The razor (established, razors.click + B&B):** the Enders
Speed razor was developed around 1930 and sold by the Enders
Razor Company as a "one piece razor" — it cannot be taken
apart to change blades. Two patents cover it: US1791552
(head and blade) and US1791553 (handle). The proprietary
blade's side tongues bend slightly on insertion and act as
springs holding the blade against the cap underside.
Corporate origin: Enders Razor Company was a Simmons
Hardware subsidiary built on Russ J. Christy patents
(Enders Razor Co. v. Christy Co., 6th Cir. 1936); pre-lawsuit
"Enders by Christy" razors (c. 1935) exist in collections
(B&B 466447 #1).
