**The 104 "Tech" IS the Tek head (same product, era name):** B&B thread 466182 "Ikon Shavecraft Tech vs Spitfire R41: Godzilla vs Mothra" (2015): the poster bought "the 104 'Tech' head with one of those lush OSS handles"; "The top grade aluminium head... the baseplate of the Tech is just, well... a Tech isn't it? And how weird to take design inspiration and the name of a famously friendly and mild Gillette razor, the Tech, for what must be the fiercest DE currently on sale!" The head: aluminum, big gap/exposure/curvature, "the fiercest DE currently on sale" (2015). Whether the maker originally sold it as "Tech" and later re-spelled it "Tek", or the community spelled it "Tech" all along, is UNPINNED — the 2015 thread calls it Tech, the 2021 maker URL says Tek. Either way the catalog canonical is **Tek** (maker's current spelling, also the dominant dataset spelling 2021–2025).
