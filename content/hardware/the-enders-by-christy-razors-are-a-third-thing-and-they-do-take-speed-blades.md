**The "Enders by Christy" razors are a THIRD thing, and
they DO take Speed blades (established, firsthand).**
B&B 466447 #1: the poster's pre-lawsuit Enders "by
Christy" razor (c. 1935) shaved with a pack of Speed
Razor blades. Mechanism per the 1936 case summary
(Studicata, Enders Razor Co. v. Christy Co., 6th Cir.
1936): Simmons Hardware acquired Russ J. Christy's
patents and marketed razors/blades under the Enders
name; the Christy Company kept producing similar
products after patent expiry using the "Enders" and
"Keen Kutter" names, and lost. So: IF a future dataset
string ever says "Enders by Christy", it folds to the
Enders Speed family (Speed-blade system); the existing
bare "Christy" strings do not. The three-way split —
Enders Speed family / Enders-by-Christy (Speed blades)
/ Christy Co. Massage Bar SE (half-DE/injector hacks) —
is the guard against the obvious matcher conflation.
