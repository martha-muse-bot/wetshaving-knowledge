*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-10-22 · Source: [[hardware|Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] Van Der Hagen TTO = rebranded WEISHI 9306 (NOT Baili) — resolved
2026-10-22 (backlog item; the lead itself was mis-attributed).** The
dataset notes' lone "VDH TTO = rebranded Baili" claim (chefkoolaid 2021,
twice) is outvoted by overwhelming community consensus that VDH's razors
are Weishi-made: B&B 520948 #3 ("They are rebranded Weishis"); B&B 520948
#4 ("rebranded Weishi 9306... it's the Weishi F", on a 63 g chrome unit);
B&B 623218 #10 ("the Van Der Hagen DE is just a relabeled Weishi 9306
razor"); B&B 644308 #23 (Jay21, firsthand both-owner: "The razor there is
also known as the 'Van der Weishi.' That razor is made by Weishi (relabeled
as Van der Hagen)"); Sharpologist's long-handle review (2019-08-30,
updated 2023): "typical OEM import from the Far East (to me it looks
similar to a Weishi long handle razor), though the Van Der Hagen name is
etched on top." [[aliases/method-note|Method note]]: even B&B 586713 — the SAME thread that
spawned the Rockwell R1 = Baili BD179 claim — says "Van der Hagen short/
long handle which seems to be a Weishi short long handle razor." When a
single dataset note contradicts the whole rest of the community including
firsthand both-owners, the single note is the error. **Variants:**
standard short-handle 85 mm TTO (the Walmart/Target drugstore unit; dataset
"Van Der Hagen 85mm Safety Razor" = this) and long-handle 4 in / ~74 g
TTO. **Catalog impact:** do NOT merge VDH into Baili; per P-04
brand-of-record, "Van Der Hagen" stays its own VDH entry with Weishi as a
MANUFACTURE ATTRIBUTE. [[lineages/dataset-presence|Dataset presence]] is large — VDH is the entry-level
drugstore razor/soap/brush brand (Walmart, Target, Walgreens, CVS, Rite
Aid). LEADS: (1) VDH blades are "manufactured in Solingen, Germany" per
VDH's own site (Sharpologist quotes it; speculates Merkur — the only
Solingen DE-blade maker anyone can name; verify before recording — cf.
catalog's Wilkinson Sword Classic US-stock convention); (2) B&B 520948
#40 claims the VDH mug and brush are rebranded **Colonel Conk** products
("the puck is made by the same people that make Col Conk") — single
~2016 community source, verify; (3) VDH launched a "V Razor" (2025,
third-news.com press piece — "Available Now", premium-at-affordable
positioning) — watch for dataset strings; (4) parentco is Universal Beauty
Products (bought Van Der Hagen Enterprises 2016; see [[lineages|Lineages]]).
Confidence: **established** for Weishi-9306-platform attribution
(multiple independent community sources + firsthand owners + Sharpologist).

**[[hardware/addendum-2026-11-06|Addendum 2026-11-06]] — the Conk direction is REVERSED; brush/mug OEM;
TWIST Adjustable; kit variants; V Razor watch [catalog]:**
- **The 2026-10-22 lead ("VDH mug and brush are rebranded Colonel Conk
products") is WRONG on direction and object, and is RETIRED.** B&B
520948 #40's actual words are only: "Seem to remember that the puck
is made by the same people that make Col Conk" — the PUCK, a shared
maker, direction unspecified. The direction is settled the other way
by three independent threads: B&B 62695 #8 (Dave, 2008): "Van Der
Hagen Enterprises makes Col. Conk soaps to Conk's specifications";
B&B 139331: "IIRC, a representative of Van Der Hagen has said that
they do indeed make the soap for Col Conk"; B&B 70533: "I heard from
Van Der Hagen that they make Col. Conk soaps to Conk's
specifications." B&B Wiki (quoted in 139331): "The current company
does not manufacture its own products, and merely sells rebranded
products." **VDH is the MAKER, Conk the rebrander.** Catalog: Conk
soap entries carry VDH as a manufacture attribute (P-04); never fold
Conk into VDH (different scents/formulas — Conk's are glycerin soaps
to Conk's specs, and posters report performance differences) and
never cite VDH products as Conk-made.
- **Brush:** the VDH boar brush is NOT a Conk rebrand either. B&B 16801
p.2: "Burma Shave, Ever Ready and Van Der Hagen boar brushes are all
made by the same manufacturer" — "made in China by the same
manufacturer and sold to ASR and VDH for their own branding" (single
poster with mechanism; lead-grade for the shared-manufacturer claim).
The Conk brush is separately a rebranded **Vulfix** per one voice
(B&B 139331 p.3: "My brush is Col. Conk as well, but it's just a
re-branded Vulfix") — also lead-grade. Dataset: "Col. Conk Badger"
brush strings are real (24 rows / 12 authors, 2017–2026;
Environmental-Gap380 ×11) — a small live line, own entry.
- **Mug:** a generic Chinese apothecary mug (B&B 520948 p.2: "Very
similar or identical mugs (no doubt also Chinese-made) can be found
online for less... VDH pucks fit inside of them perfectly"). No Conk
link.
- **"Country Uncle" CORRECTION:** not a Conk brand. Sharpologist
2014-08-06 (Mantic59): Country Uncle is the house brand of
AmazingShaving.com (Texas retailer); the puck carries the VDH logo
on the bottom; retailer Brian confirmed "Country Uncle Deluxe shave
soap is made for them by VDH... slightly different 'recipe' than VDH
Deluxe, including lanolin and aloe vera." The B&B 70533/65782 posts
claiming Conk sells the VDH line as Country Uncle are wrong. Zero
dataset strings — knowledge-only.
- **VDH TWIST Adjustable is REAL:** "Van Der Hagen Matte Black TWIST
Adjustable Razor", dial 1–6 (retail copy 2024; B&B 646712 p.4
firsthand 2024-07: "The Vanderhagen adjustable, so far. At 6 it's
pretty close to a shavette"). Dataset "VdH Adjustable" = 8 rows, 1
author (404-Runge-Kutta, 2021) — predates the 2024 attestations, so
whether his 2021 strings are the TWIST is unpinned; launch date
unpinned. Never fold into the fixed TTO entries.
- **Kit variants (dataset):** "Van Der Hagen 85MM Solid Brass" 45
razor rows; "Van Der Hagen Safety Razor Kit 110MM, Gun Metal" 47
razor rows (AndImLars 48 total incl. variants, drnickfury 3;
2018–2024). Same Weishi TTO platform in longer-handle/kit form —
handle/finish attributes, not separate models.
- **V Razor watch:** ZERO genuine dataset strings through the refreshed
2026 file (16,863 rows) — the 3 "v razor" hits are false positives
("fav razors"). Watch continues.
