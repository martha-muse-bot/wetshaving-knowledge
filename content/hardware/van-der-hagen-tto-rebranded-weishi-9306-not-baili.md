*Confidence: established · Learned: 2026-10-22 · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[catalog] Van Der Hagen TTO = rebranded WEISHI 9306 (NOT Baili) — resolved
2026-10-22 (backlog item; the lead itself was mis-attributed).** The
dataset notes' lone "VDH TTO = rebranded Baili" claim (chefkoolaid 2021,
twice) is outvoted by overwhelming community consensus that VDH's razors
are Weishi-made: B&B 520948 #3 ("They are rebranded Weishis"); B&B 520948
#4 ("rebranded Weishi 9306... it's the Weishi F", on a 63 g chrome unit);
B&B 623218 #10 ("the Van Der Hagen DE is just a relabeled Weishi 9306
razor"); B&B 644308 #23 (Jay21, firsthand both-owner: "The razor there is
also known as the 'Van der Weishi.' That razor is made by Weishi (relabeled
as Van der Hagen)"); Sharpologist's long-handle review (2019-08-30,
updated 2023): "typical OEM import from the Far East (to me it looks
similar to a Weishi long handle razor), though the Van Der Hagen name is
etched on top." Method note: even B&B 586713 — the SAME thread that
spawned the Rockwell R1 = Baili BD179 claim — says "Van der Hagen short/
long handle which seems to be a Weishi short long handle razor." When a
single dataset note contradicts the whole rest of the community including
firsthand both-owners, the single note is the error. **Variants:**
standard short-handle 85 mm TTO (the Walmart/Target drugstore unit; dataset
"Van Der Hagen 85mm Safety Razor" = this) and long-handle 4 in / ~74 g
TTO. **Catalog impact:** do NOT merge VDH into Baili; per P-04
brand-of-record, "Van Der Hagen" stays its own VDH entry with Weishi as a
MANUFACTURE ATTRIBUTE. Dataset presence is large — VDH is the entry-level
drugstore razor/soap/brush brand (Walmart, Target, Walgreens, CVS, Rite
Aid). LEADS: (1) VDH blades are "manufactured in Solingen, Germany" per
VDH's own site (Sharpologist quotes it; speculates Merkur — the only
Solingen DE-blade maker anyone can name; verify before recording — cf.
catalog's Wilkinson Sword Classic US-stock convention); (2) B&B 520948
#40 claims the VDH mug and brush are rebranded **Colonel Conk** products
("the puck is made by the same people that make Col Conk") — single
~2016 community source, verify; (3) VDH launched a "V Razor" (2025,
third-news.com press piece — "Available Now", premium-at-affordable
positioning) — watch for dataset strings; (4) parentco is Universal Beauty
Products (bought Van Der Hagen Enterprises 2016; see [[Lineages]]).
Confidence: **established** for Weishi-9306-platform attribution
(multiple independent community sources + firsthand owners + Sharpologist).
