**Bigfoot Shaves kits = rebranded Baili (Atty_Clouds 2019) — UPDATED
2026-10-25: business identified, Baili link STRENGTHENED but still a
LEAD (single-owner chain, not established).** [[Dataset scope]] is exactly
one owner: Atty_Clouds, July 2019 (4 posts; razor strings "Bigfoot
Shaves butterfly open" + "Bigfoot shaves (rebranded Baili Classic
TTO)"; blade strings "Bigfoot Shaves" ×2 in fields, +2 in notes).
The chain matters: on 2019-07-10 he ASKED in-note "does anyone know
what razors/blades bigfoot shaves rebranded?"; on 2019-07-12 his razor
string carries the answer "(rebranded Baili Classic TTO)"; by
2019-07-17/28 his notes call the razor simply "Baili tto" — the
community answer, adopted by the owner, not a maker admission. The
blade half is weaker still: 2019-07-28 hedges it ("which I am thinking
are a rebranded Baili product as the razor was as well") and reports
slight neck tugging that vanished on switching to Gillette Platinum.
What is now ESTABLISHED about the business itself: Bigfoot Shaves was
a real Amazon kit brand (2018–2019 review-roundup era; Facebook page
id 689138541206952, "started by men for men who love shaving the old
school, classic way"; maker domain bigfootshaves.com, phone
+1-855-924-4366 on the directory mirror). Kit (retailer/roundup copy):
TTO butterfly DE, chrome-coated zinc alloy, 10 stainless blades, travel
case with in-lid mirror; a Gold Razor variant ($13.99, theluxuryspot)
and a separate 30-count "Super Stainless Steel" DE blade pack (mild,
uncoated per hairworldmag; 3–7 shaves per blade per the Amazon copy).
WHY the Baili link is now likely rather than merely asserted: (1)
Baili/Weidi's documented OEM model — [[Vikings Blade]]'s own page says
Baili "invite[s] hundreds of OEM re-branders" to sell the BD179, and
the Rockwell R1 and CVS Goodline are established BD179 rebrands;
(2) the kit's spec profile matches the Baili 179 butterfly exactly
(chrome-plated zinc alloy TTO + mirrored travel case, cf. the
FashionBeans Baili Butterfly listing: 94 mm, chromed zinc alloy,
travel case with built-in mirror); (3) Baili does sell its own DE
blades (BP007 Platinum, Weidi Baili, first available Apr 28, 2017),
so a Baili blade in the kit is at least possible. AGAINST firming it
up: one owner, one community answer, no pack/head marking, and
Atty_Clouds's "Classic TTO" wording does not pin a Baili model code
(Baili's "Classic" BD191 is a 3-piece, not a TTO — do NOT cite BD191;
the TTO platform, if Baili, is the 179 family). [[Catalog guidance]]
[catalog]: Bigfoot Shaves stays its OWN brand group (razor entry
`unverified/razor/bigfoot-shaves-butterfly-open`, blade entry
`unverified/blade/bigfoot-shaves`, both single-author 2019); record
Baili as a SUSPECTED manufacture attribute only, never a merge; the
blade entry must NOT be folded into Baili BP007 or any Baili blade
on this evidence. Maker-site status (RESOLVED 2026-10-25, live-browser
read): bigfootshaves.com does NOT load as a usable site — HTTP
variants give a blank Chrome error page, HTTPS gives an SSL
version/cipher mismatch; no store content, parked page, product
names, or prices visible in any attempt, so the site cannot be
classified as live, parked, or dead from this check alone — only as
UNREACHABLE as of Oct 2026. Combined with zero dataset strings after
2019, treat Bigfoot Shaves as a defunct/dormant Amazon kit brand for
validator purposes; maker-site evidence route is dead, retailer/
roundup copy + SOTD notes only. (BullGoose-flap caution noted: the
failure here is consistent across http/https/www/non-www variants,
not a single transient flap.) Re-open to established only on a
second independent owner, a Bigfoot/Baili pack or head marking, or a
Baili OEM listing naming Bigfoot.

**RESOLVED 2026-11-05 — RAZOR HALF UPGRADED TO ESTABLISHED
[catalog]; BLADE HALF STAYS UNPINNED (with new counter-evidence).**
The re-open condition is met by a SECOND, INDEPENDENT, EARLIER
attestation: B&B thread 498538 ("Viking Razor", Aug–Sep 2016) names
Bigfoot Shaves in the same-razor Amazon-name list for the Baili/Viking
TTO family — verbatim (#30, Aug 28, 2016): "It's under quite a few
names on Amazon. Bigfoot shaves, Elkaline, Oldcastle shaving,
Mensessentials, Wellkage, Drs Prochoice, GoBetter, and more." That
predates Atty_Clouds (2019) by three years and comes from a different
community, so the Baili link no longer rests on one owner. The chain
now has five independent legs: (1) the 2016 list naming Bigfoot among
the Baili-family Amazon names; (2) the same thread's firsthand "B"
stamp (#26: "Appears that it is indeed a repackaged Bali. Just looked
at mine and it has the 'B' stamp"); (3) the OEM mechanism named in
the same thread (#33: the seller "gets the Guangzhou Weidi Commodity
Co., Ltd. in Guangdong, China to print his logo on the case, pays
between $2.00 and $2.99 per piece") — Weidi is Baili's maker, and
logo-on-case is exactly the private-label route; (4) Atty_Clouds'
adopted community answer (2019-07-12, "(rebranded Baili Classic
TTO)"); (5) spec + photo match — chrome zinc-alloy TTO + mirrored
travel case + 10 blades (hairworldmag 2019 kit review) against the
Baili Butterfly 94 mm / 70 g profile, and the kit's own product photo
(hairworldmag, 2019-04; archived in the goal's hidden_files as
bigfoot-shaves-kit-2019-hairworldmag.jpg) shows the 179-family
silhouette: diamond-knurl banded handle, flared TTO knob, butterfly
head with covered blade tabs. MODEL CODE PINNED: "Baili Classic TTO"
= **BD179** — BD is Baili's "Classic" line (2026-10-23 prefix
resolution: 179 = butterfly TTO under BR and BD alike, one product).
The Gold Razor variant ($13.99, theluxuryspot) is consistent too —
Baili sells the 179 in gold/rose-gold finishes (B&B firsthand: "Rose
gold TTO, 10 bucks on eBay"). [[Catalog guidance [catalog]]]: Bigfoot
Shaves stays its OWN brand group (P-04); record **Baili BD179/BR179
as the manufacture/model attribute** on `unverified/razor/
bigfoot-shaves-butterfly-open`; never merge into the Baili entry.
The 2016 #35 dissent ("The Vikings Blade IS in fact, an original
design... The Baili and all others are definitely NOT manufactured
identically", round-pin TTO side arms) is about Vikings Blade's
premium custom run, NOT about Bigfoot: later consensus is that
Viking admitted Baili makes the Chieftain (B&B 644308 p.2: "Viking
admitted that Baili makes it for them, but claimed that they
required higher quality control than Baili does for their own Baili
179"; B&B 567304 p.2: "Viking's Blade is not a razor manufacturer.
They sell rebranded Bailis at inflated price"). Read together, the
family splits into a premium Vikings Blade custom run and the
budget 179 rebrands — Bigfoot sits in the latter, and the dissent
does not touch it. BLADE HALF: still unpinned, and now with
counter-evidence against Baili BP007 — the Bigfoot blades are
UNCOATED stainless (hairworldmag: "These blades are not coated,
therefore will be more prone to rust and corrosion"; 30-count,
double-wrapped, 3–7 shaves), while BP007 is platinum-coated; do NOT
fold `unverified/blade/bigfoot-shaves` into BP007 or any Baili blade.

blades (USA-made, platinum-coated, hairworldmag), not Bigfoot's —
never cite it for Bigfoot. Atty_Clouds' own hedge stands ("which I am
