**(1) Speed Razor vs Speed Shaver head geometry — RESOLVED as far as
text sources go: NO documented head difference [catalog].** The
"cast different" claim is Ron R (B&B 576746 #6, Nov 2019) relaying
something he read: the 1930s Speed Razor (square handle end) "would
not fit the Enders Speed 'shaver' 1940's model blades always. They
must of cast the newer shavers a little different?" Two multi-unit
owners contradict it firsthand in the same thread — jmudrick (#7):
"I am not aware of any difference. I have used various Enders and
Proline blades in both Razors and Shavers without issue"; GAW9576
(#8): "I have both shaver and razor and haven't noticed any fitment
issues or differences between the two." The narrow-blade-bed
variance is per-unit manufacturing tolerance, NOT a model split:
GAW9576 (#31) owns 3 Speed Shavers + 1 Speed Razor and exactly 1
of the 4 has the narrow bed; Flintstone65 (#32) has the reverse
ratio, 3 of 4 too narrow — the variance cuts across both models.
The square handle end remains the only documented external
difference. Keep ONE family; never split a catalog entry on the
Razor/Shaver wording, and never cite a head-geometry difference.
