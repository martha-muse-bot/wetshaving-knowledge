**(6b) Plane Talk "Enders Sales Company" — RESOLVED as collector
confusion [catalog].** Goolsby's note (Plane Talk vol. X no. 4,
1985–86, item 10, p. X-4-15/16) claims "WM. ENDERS/OAK LEAF BRAND/
ST.LOUIS, MO. also maintained 'Enders Sales Company' New York" and
that his "Enders Dollar Razor" is by that company. Contemporary
trade press says the opposite: Printers' Ink, Aug 30, 1928, verbatim
— "The Enders Sales Company, New York, manufacturer and distributor
of Enders safety razors, has appointed Edwards, Ewing & Jones, Inc.,
advertising agency, to direct its advertising account." Enders Sales
Company was the SHAVING line's own manufacturer/distributor; the
St. Louis attachment is Goolsby conflating two "Wm. Enders" names
(the shaving trademark was the "Wm. Enders" facsimile signature; the
St. Louis firm was literally Wm. Enders, Oak Leaf tools). The Dollar
Razor stays a shaving-Enders product (Proxibid case: "Enders Razor
Co, New York City"). BONUS from the same notice: a new model,
the **"Improved Enders"**, was launching ~Aug 1928 (campaign in
preparation; agency Edwards, Ewing & Jones) — a pre-Speed model
name for the family timeline. Catalog: never attribute any Enders
razor to the St. Louis tool firm on the Plane Talk note.
