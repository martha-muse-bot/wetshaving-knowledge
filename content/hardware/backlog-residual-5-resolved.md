**Backlog residual (5) RESOLVED — dataset "Christy"
strings are NOT Enders-family [catalog].** Full scan:
NO dataset string says "Enders by Christy". The only
"Christy" strings are THREE posts, all luvmy07subie,
2021: "Christy SE" (2021-04-11) + "Christy" ×2 (May 15–16,
2021). His own description identifies them as Christy
Co.'s OWN razor, not an Enders: 4 1/2 in, 41 g, open comb
with recessed runner guide, "massage bar", lather
filtering "through the back, and into a little catcher",
blade "HAS to sit on the rivets", two variants (angled
head vs flat). Smithsonian NMAH (nmah_1292573) pins the
maker: Christy Co., Fremont, Ohio — Russ J. Christy
(Christy Knife Co. 1891, R. J. Christy Co. ~1907); box
"Christy / TRADE MARK / SAFETY RAZOR / with the /
MASSAGE BAR / KEEPS YOUR FACE YOUNG"; massage bar =
Christy patent US 1,502,615 (July 22, 1924). Modern blade
pairings in the dataset confirm a DIFFERENT blade system:
half-DE ("Gillette Black Platinum and Personna
Injector", taped for length) and DE (Permasharp,
Personna Israeli Blue) — hacks, corroborated by B&B's
modern-blades guide ("Christy Massage Bar with Personna
injector. Works for me", compression-held, tape advised).
Catalog: "Christy"/"Christy SE" strings get their OWN
Christy Co. entry (Massage Bar SE), NEVER the Enders
Speed family, and never an Enders blade attribution.
