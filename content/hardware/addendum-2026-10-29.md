**Addendum (2026-10-29) — Baxter BRUSH line: origin ESTABLISHED,
manufacturer still UNPINNED [catalog].** Baxter's own FAQ
(baxterofcalifornia.com/pages/customer-service-faqs, read live
2026-10-29) states verbatim: "our chrome-plated safety razor
(Germany), our various 100% natural badger brushes (Germany) and
our premium handmade combs (Switzerland)." So the brush line is
German-made — that sub-lead is closed on maker copy, and the
razor's Germany origin is doubly pinned. The SPECIFIC German brush
manufacturer remains unpinned: no forum, review, or maker source
names one. Mühle is the lead candidate only on circumstantial
grounds (already supplies the R89-family razor head; does
private-label work; Stützengrün Germany) — never cite Mühle for
the brushes yet. Alternatives (Thäter, Hans Baier, Klenzo) are
generic, not evidenced — never assert any. Catalog: the three
Baxter brush products (holiday-set barber-pole brush, 1.2.3. kit
aluminum travel brush, standalone Silver Tip) stand as own
entries; "Made in Germany" may be recorded as an origin
attribute on them.
- COMMODITY-HEAD context: B&B's R89 TWIST review (reviews
section) states the R89/DE89 head "is the common component
paired with over three dozen custom handles from at least
three manufacturers" — the shared Mühle/EJ 89 head is a
widely private-labeled commodity head, which is exactly the
pattern the Baxter razor fits (German-made, three-piece,
own-styled barber-pole handle). This firms the mechanism of
the primandprep attribution but is NOT a second Mühle-naming
source — that sub-lead stays open (negative targeted search
again this run: no new "manufactured by Mühle" statement).
- BAN CAVEAT for validators: the backlog's 2026-10-24 note
("L'Oréal's 2021 animal-hair ban means current Baxter brushes,
if any, are synthetic") is now TENSIONED — the live maker FAQ
(read 2026-10-29) still lists "100% natural badger brushes"
with a 6-month warranty as a current product line. Either the
FAQ is stale copy, or Baxter retains badger brushes despite
the ban — do NOT use the ban as a hard dating split for Baxter
brushes without owner-side evidence.
