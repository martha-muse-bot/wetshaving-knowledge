*Confidence: lead · Source: [[Hardware lore]]*

**[[Catalog guidance]] [catalog]:** (1) Souplex entries = Big Ben
(travel razor, c.1935), Nymph (ladies' set, by 1935), Double
Six (differential Bakelite DE, pre-1944–late 1940s), Double
Six Minor (metal-cap variant), National (post-war Souplex
version of the shared design), Shaving Senior (set), Souplex
blades (DE, 1925+). (2) Knockout → Cartledge/Cooper, Fuluse →
Cooper & Sons; Nelson → same-design family with Harrison
Fisher as lead maker. (3) Never fold blade-field "Big Ben"
into the Souplex razor. (4) A future "Souplex Open Comb"
string is the mild open-comb model, not a Double Six.
